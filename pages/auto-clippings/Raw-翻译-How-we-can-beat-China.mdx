In 1946, George Kennan, who was acting as the head of the U.S. Embassy in Moscow, wrote a message to the Secretary of State, known as the "Long Telegram".  
1946年，时任美国驻莫斯科使馆代办的乔治·凯南向国务卿发送了一份被称为"长电报"的文件。  

In the Long Telegram, Kennan laid out a theory of how Soviet power could be defeated.  
在这份长电报中，凯南系统阐述了击败苏联力量的策略理论。  

He argued that Soviet leaders would always be hostile to the U.S., but that they were also cautious — they wouldn't recklessly start a world war like Germany had.  
他指出苏联领导人虽始终对美国怀有敌意，但行事谨慎——不会像德国那样轻率发动世界大战。  

He also argued that the Soviet system was weak and unstable, and that given enough time, it would collapse from its own internal contradictions.  
他同时认为苏联体制脆弱且不稳定，假以时日必将因内部矛盾而崩塌。  

This became known as the doctrine of "containment", and it ended up being exactly the strategy that the U.S. used to win the Cold War.  
这就是著名的"遏制战略"，最终成为美国赢得冷战的制胜法宝。  

It took 45 years for the strategy to work and the USSR to fall.  
该战略历经45年最终奏效，苏联随之解体。  

I am certainly no George Kennan.  
我自然无法比肩乔治·凯南。  

I've never lived in China, I haven't been there in many years, I don't speak Chinese, and I certainly don't talk to any leaders there.  
我从未在中国生活，多年未曾造访，不通中文，更无缘接触中国领导人。  

I'm also not a career diplomat or a professional geopolitics guy of any kind.  
我也非职业外交官或地缘政治专家。  

So although I'm going to write a blog post with the same general goal as Kennan's Long Telegram, I don't want to suggest that it's anywhere near as authoritative or insightful.  
因此尽管本文写作目标与凯南长电报类似，但绝不敢妄称具有同等权威性或洞察力。  

But I'm still going to write a post about "how to beat China", because I think it's useful to think through these sorts of big strategic questions.  
但我仍要撰写这篇"如何战胜中国"，因为思考此类重大战略问题颇具价值。  

This post is also an important follow-up to a series of posts I wrote about geopolitics during the Biden years, about U.S.-China competition:  
本文也是我对拜登时期所写中美竞争系列地缘政治文章的重要补充：  

(There were more, but I figure your appetite for clicking through old posts is limited.)  
（虽有多篇旧文，但想必读者无意逐一查阅）  

The basic premise of those posts was that the competition between a China-led New Axis and the U.S. and our old allies — Europe, Japan and Korea, hopefully India, etc. — would define the upcoming era of geopolitics.  
该系列核心观点是：由中国主导的新轴心与美国及其传统盟友（欧洲、日韩，或许还有印度等）的竞争将塑造未来地缘政治格局。  

And my conclusion was that we should use an updated version of the basic strategy we used against the USSR in Cold War 1.  
我的结论是应沿用冷战时期对抗苏联的基本战略并进行升级。  

I recommended that we should build up economic power within our bloc of allies, establish military deterrence, and wait for China's internal contradictions to do the heavy lifting of removing the threat.  
我建议在盟友阵营内增强经济实力，建立军事威慑，静待中国内部矛盾自行消解威胁。  

That strategy was always going to be harder than it was in Cold War 1, because China A) is a lot bigger than the USSR, B) has a better economic model, and C) is a lot more globally integrated.  
该战略实施难度将远超第一次冷战，因为中国：A)体量远超苏联 B)经济模式更优 C)全球化程度更深  

The strategy I recommended was much tighter economic cooperation between the U.S. and a maximally large set of allies, to build up a bloc capable of overmatching even the mighty Chinese manufacturing juggernaut.  
我主张通过美国与尽可能多盟友深化经济合作，构建足以抗衡中国制造业巨无霸的阵营。  

This is the approach being promoted by experts like Rush Doshi and Kurt Campbell.  
这正是拉什·多希、库尔特·坎贝尔等专家倡导的路径。

Anyway, I still think building up an anti-China economic juggernaut would be a great thing to do, and I still think the U.S. would benefit from closer economic integration with its friends and allies.  
我仍认为打造反华经济巨无霸意义重大，美国也必将受益于与盟友的深度经济融合。  

But I think the election of Trump shows why this strategy probably isn't going to work.  
但特朗普当选恰恰证明该策略恐难奏效。  

The United States is simply too internally divided to engage in the sort of far-seeing, purposeful, smart kind of international competition that we pursued so effectively in the 20th century.  
美国内部分裂严重，已无法开展20世纪那种高瞻远瞩、目标明确、手段高明的高效国际竞争。  

Americans care more about fighting other Americans than about fighting the Chinese, and that state of affairs will persist for a while.  
美国人更热衷内斗而非对华竞争，这种状态将持续相当时间。  

Donald Trump's presidency is an outgrowth of America's internal divisions; he cares much more about fighting his own internal enemies than about prosecuting international rivalries.  
特朗普执政正是美国内部分裂的产物——他更专注打击国内政敌而非应对国际竞争。  

He has willfully degraded or torn up America's key alliances, especially with Europe, and created enmity where friendship once reigned.  
他肆意破坏美国关键联盟（尤以美欧关系为甚），在昔日的友谊之地播种敌意。  

He has flagrantly trampled on every principle of human rights, mutually beneficial economic cooperation, democracy, and international non-aggression that the post-WW2 U.S.-led international order fought to uphold.  
他公然践踏二战后美国主导国际秩序所捍卫的人权、互利经济合作、民主及国际互不侵犯等所有原则。  

He has, in short, made it impossible to prosecute the sort of Cold War 2 strategy that I spent the Biden years advocating.  
简言之，他使我曾在拜登时期倡导的"第二次冷战"战略彻底失去实施可能。  

This doesn't mean America's alliance system is necessarily dead forever.  
这并不意味着美国联盟体系必然永久消亡。  

As Doshi and Campbell pointed out in a recent article, the old alliances could be reconstituted, if America credibly overcame its internal divisions and returned to being the mostly unified, stable, reliable great power that it was last century.  
正如多希与坎贝尔近期文章所言，若美国能真正克服内部分裂，重拾上世纪基本统一、稳定、可靠的强国姿态，旧有联盟仍可重建。  

But overcoming those divisions is going to take decades at the very least, and that's exactly the window of time during which China will be at its peak of relative power.  
但弥合分裂至少需要数十年——而这正是中国相对实力达至巅峰的时间窗口。  

So I think we've basically missed the chance to use that strategy effectively.  
因此我认为我们已基本错失有效实施该战略的时机。
