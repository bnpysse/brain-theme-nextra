【文章标题】：How Asia Took Over the Global Math Rankings (2003-2025)  
亚洲如何称霸全球数学排行榜（2003-2025）  

【文章正文】：  
How the Global Math Leaderboard Has Changed  
全球数学排名变迁史  

Key Takeaways  
核心发现  

In 2003, just three of the seven highest-scoring economies in PISA mathematics were in Asia. By 2025, all seven were Asian economies.  
2003年PISA数学测试前七名中仅三个属于亚洲，而到2025年全部七席均被亚洲包揽。  

Several former leaders, including Finland, the Netherlands, and Canada, have fallen substantially from their 2003 positions.  
芬兰、荷兰、加拿大等昔日领跑者排名大幅下滑。  

The United States remains well outside the group of top-performing economies in mathematics.  
美国始终未能跻身数学表现最优经济体行列。  

The global landscape for math achievement has shifted significantly over the past two decades.  
过去二十年间全球数学教育格局已发生深刻变革。

Former Leaders Lose Ground  
昔日强者失势  

Several countries that ranked near the top in 2003 have moved down considerably.  
多个2003年名列前茅的国家排名显著下跌。  

Finland, which scored 544 in mathematics in 2003, recorded 469 in 2025.  
芬兰从2003年的544分降至2025年的469分。  

Canada’s score fell from 532 to 485, while the Netherlands declined from 538 to 483.  
加拿大从532分跌至485分，荷兰从538分下滑至483分。

Math Scores Are Falling More Broadly  
数学成绩普遍下滑  

OECD data shows that the broader decline is continuing: average mathematics performance across 35 OECD countries fell another nine points between 2022 and 2025.  
OECD数据显示下滑趋势持续：35个成员国数学平均分在2022-2025年间再降9分。  

On a longer timeline, average mathematics scores fell by 22 points between 2015 and 2025, which the OECD estimates is equivalent to just over one year of learning.  
2015-2025十年间平均分下降22分，相当于损失超过一年的学习量。  

PISA also found an association between heavy digital leisure use and student outcomes. Students spending more than four hours a day on digital leisure activities outside school tended to have lower mathematics scores and a weaker sense of belonging.

China’s Result Comes With an Important Caveat  
中国成绩的重要说明  

China’s 2025 result does not represent the entire mainland Chinese student population.  
2025年中国成绩不代表全国学生水平。  

The reported score covers Beijing, Shanghai, Jiangsu, and Zhejiang. These jurisdictions recorded an average mathematics score of 612, the highest in PISA 2025.  
该分数仅涵盖北京、上海、江苏、浙江四地，其612分的平均成绩为2025年PISA最高分。
