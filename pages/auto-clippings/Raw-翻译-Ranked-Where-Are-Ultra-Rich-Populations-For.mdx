Top-Level Wealth: Ultra-Rich Populations Forecast to Rise Fastest

顶级财富：超高净值人群预计将最快增长

Key Takeaways

核心要点

Indonesia is forecast to have the highest growth in its ultra-rich population, with projected growth of 82% from 2026–2031.

印度尼西亚的超高净值人口预计将实现最高增长，2026-2031年期间预计增长82%。

Saudi Arabia and Poland tie for second place, with projected growth of 63%.

沙特阿拉伯和波兰并列第二，预计增长63%。

In comparison, the United States is set for a growth rate of 54%.

相比之下，美国的增长率为54%。

The next wave of global wealth is being led by rapidly maturing markets. Through 2031, countries like Indonesia and Saudi Arabia are projected to see their ultra-rich populations grow faster than America.

全球财富的下一波增长将由快速成熟的市场引领。到2031年，印度尼西亚和沙特阿拉伯等国家的超高净值人口预计将比美国增长更快。

In Saudi Arabia, widespread incentives across taxes, imports, infrastructure, and funding create a pro-business environment. Meanwhile, Poland has one of the fastest-growing economies in the EU and is home to tech unicorns like InPost and Allegro.

在沙特阿拉伯，税收、进口、基础设施和资金方面的广泛激励措施创造了有利于商业的环境。与此同时，波兰是欧盟增长最快的经济体之一，并且是InPost和Allegro等科技独角兽的所在地。

It’s worth noting that Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, and Poland are all starting from relatively low numbers of ultra-rich people. This makes it easier to achieve higher forecasted growth rates.

值得注意的是，印度尼西亚、沙特阿拉伯和波兰的超高净值人口基数都相对较低。这使得实现更高的预测增长率更容易。

Southeast Asia Emerges as a Wealth Hotspot

东南亚成为财富热点

Looking regionally, Southeast Asia stands out. Four of the 10 countries with the fastest-growing ultra-rich populations are in the region: Indonesia, Vietnam, the Philippines, and Singapore.

从地区来看，东南亚表现突出。超高净值人口增长最快的10个国家中有4个位于该地区：印度尼西亚、越南、菲律宾和新加坡。

Together, the number of ultra-high-net-worth individuals across those four countries plus Thailand is projected to rise from over 14,000 in 2026 to more than 22,000 by 2031. That’s equivalent to more than four people joining the $30 million club every day.

这四个国家加上泰国的超高净值个人数量预计将从2026年的14,000多人增加到2031年的22,000多人。这相当于每天有超过四人加入3000万美元俱乐部。

With new centers of wealth emerging around the world, managing and spending money across borders is becoming increasingly relevant to a growing class of wealthy consumers.

随着全球新的财富中心出现，跨境管理和消费资金对日益增长的富裕消费者阶层变得越来越重要。
