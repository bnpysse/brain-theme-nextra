【文章标题】：开源AstaBrief——Asta平台中的快速报告生成模型

【文章正文】：

Language models can already help researchers search the literature, synthesize evidence, and work through complex questions. But scientific work places particular demands on these models—answers need to stay grounded in evidence, the models need to preserve what the evidence actually supports rather than quietly broadening a study’s conclusions, and researchers need to be able to verify the final outputs.

语言模型已能帮助研究者检索文献、整合证据并解决复杂问题。但科学工作对这些模型有特殊要求——答案必须基于证据，模型需要忠实呈现证据实际支持的结论而非暗中扩大研究推论，研究者还需能验证最终输出。

We see that in how scientists use Asta, our agentic platform for scientific work. Instead of simple keyword searches, users often bring substantial context and many constraints—for example, asking Asta to compare approaches across a body of literature while accounting for a particular method, population, or setting. Many also return to generated reports later, treating them as working research artifacts rather than one-off answers.

这在科学家使用我们的科研智能平台Asta时尤为明显。用户不仅进行简单关键词搜索，更常带入丰富背景与多重约束——例如要求Asta在比较某领域文献方法时需考虑特定技术、人群或场景。许多用户还会反复查阅生成的报告，将其视作持续研究资料而非一次性答案。

We wanted to help scientists generate cited reports faster, with a model they could download and run themselves. To do that, we tested whether a small, open model trained specifically for scientific report generation could match the report quality of the proprietary models we were using, while reducing generation time and serving costs.

我们希望帮助科学家更快生成带引用的报告，并让他们能自行下载运行模型。为此我们测试：专为科研报告训练的小型开源模型，能否在保持原有商用模型报告质量的同时，缩短生成时间并降低服务成本。

We built AstaBrief 8B, a model that turns a research question and retrieved literature excerpts into a cited report. AstaBrief is available in Asta’s Generate a report feature today as Fast mode alongside Claude-powered Thinking mode, and we’re also open-sourcing it and the training data so others can study, reproduce, and build on our approach.

我们开发了AstaBrief 8B模型，能将研究问题与文献摘录转化为带引用的报告。该模型现已在Asta的"生成报告"功能中以"快速模式"上线（与Claude驱动的"思考模式"并列），同时我们开源了模型及训练数据供学界研究、复现和发展。

Developing AstaBrief required tens of thousands of real research queries, citation-focused filtering, preference data, and a redesigned report-generation pipeline that writes the full report in one pass rather than section by section. The result is nearly an order-of-magnitude reduction in report generation time compared to the proprietary models we tracked—across the full Asta pipeline, Fast mode averages 51.1 seconds per report compared with 178.5 seconds for Thinking mode, about 3.5× faster.

开发AstaBrief需要数万条真实研究查询、以引用为核心的过滤机制、偏好数据，以及重新设计的全报告一次性生成流程（非逐段生成）。最终报告生成时间较我们跟踪的商用模型缩短近一个数量级——完整流程中快速模式单报告平均耗时51.1秒，较思考模式178.5秒快约3.5倍。

Together, those efficiency gains made AstaBrief a useful test case for a broader goal: building open language models that can be adapted to the specific demands of scientific work.

这些效率提升使AstaBrief成为实现更宏大目标的试验田：构建能适应科研工作特殊需求的开源语言模型。

Open weights will also let institutions run AstaBrief on their own infrastructure, which is necessary when research questions reveal sensitive or unpublished work. Alongside the model weights, we’re releasing an example workflow that researchers can adapt to create reports from their own PDFs, providing a starting point for local report generation

开源权重还允许机构在自有基础设施上运行AstaBrief，这对涉及敏感或未发表研究的场景至关重要。除模型权重外，我们还发布了示例工作流，研究者可基于此从自有PDF生成报告，为本地化报告生成提供起点。

This post covers how we trained AstaBrief, what we learned about grounding it in scientific evidence, and which parts of our approach we think can carry forward to future models for science. Most of the training and evaluation described was completed in 2025, so the proprietary models used to generate training data and as comparison points reflect the frontier at the time. We haven’t rerun the full evaluation against today’s frontier models; the results below are best read as evidence about the particular training and system design choices we tested.

本文详述AstaBrief训练过程、科学证据锚定经验及可延续至未来科研模型的技术路径。所述训练评估多在2025年完成，故用于生成训练数据和作为对比的商用模型代表当时前沿水平。我们未针对当前前沿模型重新评估，下文结果应视为对我们特定训练与系统设计选择的验证。

Training the model

模型训练

Our goal with AstaBrief was to build an open-weights model with all the qualities that matter most for long-form scientific synthesis: answer quality, relevance, structure, and citation grounding. We started from Qwen3-8B and focused most of our effort on the post-training data, evaluation, and surrounding report-generation scaffolding.

AstaBrief的目标是构建具备长篇科研综述核心特质的开源模型：答案质量、相关性、结构性和引用锚定。我们基于Qwen3-8B，主要精力投入在训练后数据、评估及报告生成框架优化。

Adapting general-purpose models for scientific work – and training new scientific models from scratch – is something we're exploring broadly across Ai2. Through NSF OMAI, a U.S. national initiative led by Ai2 to build fully open AI infrastructure and models for scientific discovery, our researchers are working directly with scientific communities to understand what they need from future open models and where today's general-purpose models fall short. That includes studying how needs differ across scientific fields and workflows, with more findings from that research to share in the future.

通用模型科研适配与全新科研模型训练是Ai2广泛探索的方向。通过NSF OMAI（我们主导的美国国家级全开放AI科研基础设施计划），研究人员正与科学界直接合作，厘清未来开源模型需求与当前通用模型的不足，包括不同学科和工作流的需求差异，相关发现将陆续公布。

Recent work, including our DR Tulu, has shown that reinforcement-learning-based (RL) methods can improve long-form report generation for open-weights models, especially when judge models are involved in the training loop. We considered that path for AstaBrief, but ultimately focused on a simpler recipe built around supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and direct preference optimization (DPO).

包括DR Tulu在内的近期工作表明，基于强化学习（RL）的方法能提升开源模型的长篇报告生成质量（尤其在训练循环引入评判模型时）。我们曾考虑该方案，但最终选择围绕监督微调（SFT）和直接偏好优化（DPO）构建更简化的方案。

RL-based training can be unstable and expensive. We wanted to see how far we could push report generation quality with a cheaper, more operationally manageable setup—one that's also easier to debug and iterate on.

基于RL的训练可能不稳定且成本高昂。我们想探索在更经济、更易管理的配置下（也更易调试迭代）能实现多高的报告生成质量。

这使得训练数据质量尤为关键。我们未依赖复杂优化方法修正噪声样本，而是将大量精力投入样本的生成、筛选与过滤机制。
