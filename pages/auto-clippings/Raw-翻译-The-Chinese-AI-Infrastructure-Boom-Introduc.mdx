【文章标题】：The Chinese AI Infrastructure Boom: Introducing the SemiAnalysis China Datacenter Model  
中国AI基础设施热潮：SemiAnalysis中国数据中心模型发布  

【文章正文】：  
China sits at the frontier of the global model race. GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 are the latest in a run of striking open-weights releases. ByteDance's Doubao serves 345M monthly users as China's ChatGPT, and Seedance is the State-Of-The-Art video generation model.  
中国正站在全球模型竞赛的前沿。GLM 5.3和Kimi K3是近期最引人注目的开源权重模型。字节跳动的"豆包"作为中国版ChatGPT月活用户达3.45亿，而"Seedance"则是顶尖的视频生成模型。  

Every one of those models runs on a datacenter, and China has been building them at a pace that has gone largely unmeasured outside the country. The biggest tenant files no 10-K. Several of the largest landlords have never listed. Most of the primary sources are in Chinese. So the market settled on two lazy assumptions: China is big, and China is empty. Published estimates of China’s datacenter capacity differ by 15x, and reports keep citing high vacancy rates.  
每个模型都运行在数据中心上，而中国建设数据中心的速度在海外几乎无人知晓。最大的租户不提交10-K报表。几家最大的业主从未上市。主要信息来源多为中文。因此市场形成了两个草率假设：中国很大，中国很空。对中国数据中心容量的公开估计相差15倍，报告不断引用高空置率。  

As with our flagship SemiAnalysis Datacenter Model, we use building-level data to show which of the most widely cited narratives hold, starting with how large the market is.  
就像我们的旗舰产品SemiAnalysis数据中心模型一样，我们使用建筑级数据来验证最常被引用的说法是否成立，首先从市场规模开始。  

The global model tracks over 5,000 facilities across four regions. The US leads the world with 56GW of capacity as of 2026YE, followed by ~15GW for APAC ex-China, ~14GW for EMEA, and ~2GW for Latin America. Until now, it stopped at the border of China. Today we cross it.  
全球模型追踪四大区域5000多个设施。美国以2026年底56GW容量领先世界，亚太（除中国）约15GW，EMEA约14GW，拉美约2GW。此前模型止步于中国边境，今天我们跨越它。  

Our tracking of 1,000+ datacenter facilities across over 60 players shows that China alone boasts a fleet of over 24GW. Bigger than EMEA. Bigger than the rest of Asia. This excludes ~20GW of dated pipeline and another ~30GW of announced projects.  
我们对60多家企业的1000+数据中心追踪显示，仅中国就拥有超24GW容量。超过EMEA。超过亚洲其他地区总和。这还不包括约20GW老旧管道和另外30GW已宣布项目。

Growth is at an inflection point.  
增长正处于拐点。  

In 2Q26, the combined capex of Alibaba, Tencent, and Baidu (“BAT”) reached $20B, more than doubling YoY, and for the first time on record, all three posted negative free cash flow. This is the largest capex step-up in the sector’s history.  
2026年第二季度，阿里巴巴、腾讯和百度("BAT")的合计资本支出达200亿美元，同比翻倍有余，且三家企业自由现金流首次同时转负。这是该行业史上最大规模的资本支出跃升。  

That total leaves out the largest spender of all, ByteDance, which remains private. According to our China Datacenter Model, ByteDance alone occupies roughly a fifth of delivered datacenter capacity in China, and it rents nearly all of it, making it the single most important customer for all wholesale colocation players in the country.  
这个总数还未包含最大支出方——未上市的字节跳动。根据我们中国数据中心模型，仅字节跳动就占据中国已交付数据中心容量的约五分之一，且几乎全部采用租赁模式，使其成为全国所有批发托管服务商最重要的客户。

The listed players get the attention, but they are only the visible tip of the iceberg. This is also true for datacenter landlords. GDS and VNET, the only two Chinese datacenter landlords listed in the US, signed 1.3GW of wholesale orders in 1H26. But according to our China Datacenter Model, they captured barely a third of ByteDance and Alibaba orders in 2024–2026YTD.  
上市企业获得关注，但它们只是冰山一角。数据中心业主同样如此。在美国上市的两家中国数据中心企业万国数据(GDS)和世纪互联(VNET)在2026年上半年签署了1.3GW批发订单。但根据我们中国数据中心模型，它们仅获得2024-2026年迄今字节跳动和阿里巴巴订单的三分之一。  

The buildout is a nationwide effort, and the state-owned carriers are also part of it. China’s datacenter market was historically telecom-dominated. The state carriers held the majority of the market share in the 2010s, and still own a third of national capacity today. The national power grid companies invest aggressively too. Their combined capex accelerated from 2024, ending the 14th Five-Year Plan 24% over the original blueprint. The 15th plan (2026–2030) layers another 40% on top, to over $746B (¥5T).  
建设是全国性行动，国有运营商也参与其中。中国数据中心市场历来由电信主导。国有运营商在2010年代占据大部分市场份额，如今仍持有全国三分之一容量。国家电网公司也在积极投资。它们的合计资本支出从2024年开始加速，最终超额完成"十四五"规划原定目标的24%。"十五五"规划(2026-2030)在此基础上再增40%，达7460亿美元(5万亿元)。  

Largely free of power constraints, labor shortages, and public protests, China routinely delivers 100MW datacenter facilities in under 12 months. Modular DCs are the standard playbook, which Tencent deployed its third-gen modular design in 2014. What is old news in China is only now being adopted at scale in America, as we detailed in The Wild Wild West of LEGO Datacenters.  
基本不受电力限制、劳动力短缺和公众抗议影响，中国通常能在12个月内交付100MW数据中心设施。模块化数据中心是标准方案——腾讯早在2014年就部署了第三代模块化设计。这些中国的旧闻直到现在才在美国大规模采用，正如我们在《乐高数据中心的狂野西部》中详述的。  

The growth does not stop at China’s border.  
增长不止于中国边境。  

Overseas leasing by Chinese hyperscalers is set to double from 2026 to 2029 and approach ~4GW of leased capacity in our SemiAnalysis Datacenter Model. That number still understates their offshore compute, because it excludes the hundreds of thousands of GPUs they rent from Western clouds, which we covered at length in How Oracle Is Winning the AI Compute Market.  
中国超大规模企业的海外租赁预计将在2026-2029年翻倍，在我们的SemiAnalysis数据中心模型中接近4GW租赁容量。这个数字仍低估了它们的海外算力，因为它不包括从西方云服务商租赁的数十万块GPU——我们在《甲骨文如何赢得AI算力市场》中详细讨论过这点。  

However, the market also faces some dark clouds. Vacancy rates are high, and datacenter developers compete heavily on price. Chip supply is also constrained, due to export restrictions. But as explained below, neither has stopped AI datacenters from being built and filled at a remarkable speed.  
然而市场也面临阴云。空置率高企，数据中心开发商大打价格战。由于出口限制，芯片供应也受限。但如下文所述，这些都未能阻止AI数据中心以惊人速度建设和填满。
