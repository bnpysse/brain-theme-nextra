Why we made this
为何打造这款字体

We built our own typeface for a few key reasons:
我们打造专属字体主要出于以下几个关键原因：

- Personalization: we can customize the fonts to align with our preferences.
- 个性化：我们可以根据自身偏好对字体进行定制。

- Features: we can integrate advanced typography features tailored to our use cases.
- 功能特性：我们可以集成专为自身使用场景量身定制的高级排版功能。

- Sustainability: the cost of licensing commercial typefaces increases as we grow.
- 可持续性：随着我们的发展，商业字体的授权成本会不断增加。

Source Sans was the perfect foundation for Nebula Sans because it shares many primary characteristics with Whitney SSm, our previous brand typeface â both were designed to bridge the gap between American gothic and European humanist typefaces, with a strong emphasis on readability. The majority of the adjustments we made were to adapt the metrics of Source Sans to better match those of Whitney SSm, since Source Sans is smaller and narrower by default.
Source Sans 是 Nebula Sans 的完美基础，因为它与我们此前的品牌字体 Whitney SSm 具有诸多共同的核心特征——两者的设计初衷皆在于弥合美式哥特体与欧式人文主义字体之间的鸿沟，并高度注重可读性。由于 Source Sans 的默认尺寸较小且字形较窄，我们所做的大部分调整都是对其度量参数进行优化，以便更好地与 Whitney SSm 相匹配。
