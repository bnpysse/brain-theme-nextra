Responsible Release of AI-Generated Mathematics

负责任地发布AI生成的数学成果

September 29, 2026

2026年9月29日

At present, some frontier AI labs are testing advanced mathematical problems on proprietary models that remain inaccessible to the broader scientific community. Our recommendations are formulated with this practical context in mind. However, ideally, they would not do so. We want to state clearly from the start: we do not endorse this practice, and we ask them to stop testing advanced mathematical problems on proprietary models.

目前，一些前沿AI实验室正在专有模型上测试高级数学问题，这些模型尚未向更广泛的科学界开放。我们的建议是基于这一现实背景制定的。但理想情况下，他们不应这样做。我们要从一开始就明确声明：我们不认可这种做法，并要求他们停止在专有模型上测试高级数学问题。

1. Background

1. 背景

The mathematical community has long-standing norms concerning the dissemination and peer review of results. These norms have been essential for the reliability, trustworthiness, and effectiveness of mathematical work. One of the most important scholarly norms in mathematics is that the authors of a paper should understand the mathematical argument of that paper, have verified its correctness themselves, and take full responsibility for the content. Moreover, in the mathematical community, authors of works that significantly advance the field regularly give seminars at other institutions and talks at conferences, explaining their new developments and answering questions from colleagues. This all works towards developing the deepest possible human understanding of mathematics, which is one of the crowning glories of millennia of human development.

数学界关于成果传播和同行评审有着悠久的规范传统。这些规范对保证数学工作的可靠性、可信度和有效性至关重要。其中最重要的学术规范是：论文作者应当理解文中的数学论证，亲自验证其正确性，并对内容承担全部责任。此外，在数学界，推动领域重大进展的著作作者通常会到其他机构举办研讨会或在学术会议上作报告，解释他们的新发现并回答同行提问。这一切都是为了促进人类对数学最深层次的理解——这是人类数千年发展史上最辉煌的成就之一。

However, it is now the case that AI can output mathematical arguments in situations without the human who prompted it being able to understand the arguments, verify them, or take responsibility for them. We believe that human understanding of mathematics remains of paramount importance. How, in this new era, can we work towards a new paradigm that includes human understanding of mathematics as part of responsible scholarly output?

然而现状是，AI可以在人类使用者无法理解、验证或对其负责的情况下输出数学论证。我们坚信人类对数学的理解仍然至关重要。在这个新时代，我们该如何建立包含人类数学理解的新型责任学术产出范式？

We asked the mathematical community for feedback about what it would mean for AI labs to responsibly release mathematical results and received over 600 replies (the survey asked about a specific situation in which OpenAI announced the existence of many results without giving details). Informed by these responses, we arrived at a set of recommendations, supported by a clear plurality of respondents, aimed at any AI lab whose models are likely to have a significant impact on mathematics. The overarching principles underlying these recommendations are the following.

我们就"AI实验室如何负责任地发布数学成果"向数学界征集意见，收到600多份回复（调查针对OpenAI宣布存在多项成果但未提供细节的具体情况）。基于这些反馈，我们制定了一套获得多数受访者支持的建议，适用于可能对数学产生重大影响的AI实验室。这些建议的核心原则如下：

- If AI labs produce significant mathematical results, they should responsibly release the results, as outlined in this document, as soon as possible.

若AI实验室产出重要数学成果，应按照本文件所述尽快负责任地发布

- AI labs that release substantial mathematical output without immediate accompanying human understanding must take responsibility for ensuring that human understanding will follow. In particular, AI labs should provide significant support, including funding, to help develop this understanding.

发布人类暂未理解的重大数学成果时，AI实验室必须负责确保后续人类理解的形成。特别应提供包括资金在内的实质性支持

- The development of human understanding must remain organic and community led. It should not be directed by AI labs, even when the labs have produced the results.

人类理解的发展必须保持自发性并由学界主导，不应由产出成果的AI实验室来指导

We present our recommendations themselves in Section 2, and in Section 3 we make some comments about access to powerful model.

第2章详述具体建议，第3章对强大模型的访问权限进行说明

2. Responsible release of results generated by AI labs

2. AI实验室生成成果的负责任发布

We recommend two possible courses of action, depending on the level of human understanding that accompanies a result.

我们建议根据成果伴随的人类理解程度采取两种方案

2.A. Papers that a human understands

2.A 人类已理解的论文

Papers for which there is a mathematician responsible who fully understands the content should follow the academic mathematical community’s traditional norms: the mathematician(s) concerned should post a preprint, submit a paper for peer review at a journal, and give talks to explain the work to other mathematicians.

由完全理解内容的数学家负责的论文应遵循数学界传统规范：相关数学家应发布预印本、向期刊提交同行评审论文，并通过讲座向其他数学家解释工作

2.B. Papers that are not yet understood by anybody

2.B 尚未被任何人理解的论文

The recommendations below are for labs that have AI mathematical output that is not understood by the people who prompted the AI systems. They are split into two parts. The first part is a set of proposed technical norms for the release of AI-generated mathematics. The second part is a recommendation that AI labs provide support for the additional mathematical activities that are needed for humans to be able to understand and assimilate their AI-generated mathematical output and identify possible applications of it.

以下建议针对产出AI使用者尚未理解的数学成果的实验室，分为两部分：第一部分是AI生成数学成果发布的技术规范建议；第二部分建议实验室为人类理解、消化AI数学产出及识别其潜在应用提供支持

Step I: Initial release

步骤一：初始发布

1. With the help of LLMs, it is easy to make substantial improvements to the initial written version of an AI-generated result. The following actions should be carried out by the AI labs rather than left to mathematicians afterwards.

借助大语言模型可以轻松改进AI生成成果的初始文本版本。以下工作应由AI实验室完成而非留给数学家：

(a) The literature should be scoured for any ideas that are related to the ideas in the proofs of the results released. Even if the AI lab’s model discovered those ideas independently, it should follow standard mathematical practice and cite the papers in which the ideas were first introduced.

应全面检索与已发布成果证明思路相关的文献。即使实验室模型独立发现这些思路，也应遵循数学惯例引用原始文献

(b) The model, or some other model, should be prompted to produce a version of each proof that is written up in a style that follows the conventions of a traditional

应提示该模型或其他模型按照传统数学论文惯例重写每个证明版本
