Yana Welinder

is the solo founder of Yana Bana, an AI-native fashion brand she's building without an engineering team. In this episode, she walks through how she uses ChatGPT and Codex to turn hand-drawn sketches into realistic product images, operate professional 3D fashion software, research and contact manufacturers, and build a complete e-commerce site with voting and payments.

Yana Welinder

是Yana Bana的独立创始人，这是一个她正在打造的AI原生时尚品牌，且没有工程团队。在本期节目中，她详细介绍了自己如何利用ChatGPT和Codex，将手绘草图转化为逼真的产品图片，操作专业3D时尚软件，调研并联系制造商，以及搭建一个带有投票和支付功能的完整电商网站。

Biggest takeaways:

最重要的收获：

The prompt is the spec, and the spec is everything.

提示词就是规格说明，而规格说明就是一切。

Before generating a single image, Yana creates a detailed "fashion prompt" describing the silhouette, the way the fabric should behave and move, and even the sound it should make. That upfront work leads to dramatically better results. The lesson applies far beyond fashion: the more clearly someone can define what "good" looks like, the better both AI and humans can deliver it.

在生成哪怕一张图片之前，Yana都会先创建一份详细的"时尚提示词"，描述服装的轮廓、面料的质感和垂坠动态，甚至它应该发出的声音。这些前置工作能带来显著更优的结果。这一经验远不止适用于时尚领域：一个人越能清晰地定义"好"是什么样，AI和人类就越能把事情做好。

For designers, following the vision matters more than producing something impressive.

对于设计师而言，忠于设计愿景比产出惊艳的作品更重要。

Many image models can create a beautiful garment, but it often looks like something that already exists. ChatGPT Images 2.0 stays much closer to Yana's original sketches. For creative professionals with a specific point of view, accurately following the design is far more valuable than generating something flashy.

许多图像模型都能生成漂亮的服装，但往往看起来像是已经存在的东西。ChatGPT Images 2.0则能更忠实地还原Yana的原始草图。对于拥有独特设计理念的创意专业人士而言，准确还原设计远比生成花哨的作品更有价值。

AI agents can make specialized software accessible without years of training.

AI智能体能让人们无需多年训练即可使用专业软件。

Yana used Codex to operate CLO, a professional 3D fashion design tool, and create the CAD files she needed for 3D printing, even though she had never learned to use the software herself. This points to a much bigger shift: people may no longer need to master every complicated tool before they can produce professional work with it.

Yana使用Codex来操作CLO（一款专业3D时尚设计工具），并创建她进行3D打印所需的CAD文件，尽管她自己从未学过使用这款软件。这预示着一个更大的转变：人们或许不再需要先掌握每一个复杂的工具，才能用它产出专业级作品。

AI is making previously impractical ideas possible.

AI正在让以往不切实际的想法变成可能。

Yana designed a Ruth Asawa–inspired gown featuring large sculptural forms that would have been extremely difficult to produce before. The limitation was not the lack of 3D printing but the enormous amount of CAD work required to prepare the design. AI removed that bottleneck. Similar bottlenecks are hiding in nearly every industry, often preventing great ideas from being worth pursuing.

Yana设计了一款以Ruth Asawa为灵感的礼服，其特点是大型雕塑般的造型，这在以往极难制作。限制因素并非缺乏3D打印技术，而是准备设计所需的海量CAD工作量。AI消除了这一瓶颈。类似的瓶颈几乎潜伏在每个行业之中，常常让伟大的创意不值得付诸实践。

The near-term future is agents working through purpose-built software.

近期的未来是智能体通过专用软件来工作。

Codex cannot create a finished CAD file on its own. But Codex operating CLO can produce exactly what Yana needs. That same pattern appears throughout her workflow: the AI serves as the orchestration layer, while specialized software handles the execution. SaaS is not disappearing; it is gaining a new kind of user.

Codex无法独自创建一份完整的CAD文件。但Codex操作CLO却能精确产出Yana所需的结果。同样的模式贯穿于她的整个工作流程：AI充当编排层，而专业软件负责执行。SaaS并未消失，它正在迎来一种全新的用户。

Voice-first, asynchronous work is already changing what one person can accomplish.

语音优先、异步工作方式已经在改变一个人能完成的事情。

Yana can start a deep research task, step away to sew or drape fabric, then return and continue the work through voice. The AI keeps working while she focuses on the physical parts of her business. For a solo founder, this kind of asynchronous collaboration can dramatically expand what fits into a single day.

Yana可以启动一项深度研究任务，然后离开去缝纫或打褶布料，再回来通过语音继续工作。在她专注于业务中实体部分的同时，AI持续工作。对于独立创始人而言，这种异步协作方式能极大地扩展一天之内可完成的工作量。

When the best path is unclear, humans and AI can work in parallel.

当最佳路径不明确时，人类和AI可以并行工作。

One of Yana's hardest remaining challenges is turning her designs into accurate sewing patterns. Instead of betting entirely on one approach, she has human patternmakers and Codex working on the problem at the same time. It is a practical way to move quickly under uncertainty: test both paths, compare the results, and let the better solution win.

Yana目前面临的最大挑战之一，是将她的设计转化为精确的缝纫纸样。她没有完全押注某一种方案，而是让人类打版师和Codex同时解决这个问题。这是在不确定中快速推进的务实之道：同时测试两条路径，对比结果，让更优的解决方案胜出。
