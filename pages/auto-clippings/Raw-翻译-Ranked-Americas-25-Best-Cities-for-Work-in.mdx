Key Takeaways
核心发现

Boston ranks as America’s best city for work in 2026, followed by Washington, D.C., and Burlington, Vermont.
波士顿被评为2026年美国最适合工作的城市，华盛顿特区和佛蒙特州伯灵顿分列二三位。

Burlington ranks first for job opportunity, while San Jose leads the country for compensation.
伯灵顿在就业机会方面排名第一，而圣何塞在薪酬水平上领跑全美。

Smaller metros including Madison, Fargo, and Sioux Falls rank among the strongest cities for job opportunities.
麦迪逊、法戈和苏福尔斯等较小都会区跻身就业机会最强劲城市行列。

Where are the best places in America to build a career in 2026?
2026年在美国哪些城市最适合职业发展？

Indeed’s Best Cities for Work 2026
Indeed招聘网发布的《2026年最适合工作城市报告》。

Indeed evaluated metro areas with at least 200,000 residents across six pillars: job opportunity, compensation, job quality and security, career mobility, work-life balance, and economic opportunity.
Indeed对人口超过20万的都会区从六大维度进行评估：就业机会、薪酬水平、工作质量与保障、职业发展空间、工作生活平衡度以及经济机遇。

Job opportunity and compensation each account for 20% of the overall score, while the other four categories each account for 15%.
就业机会和薪酬各占总评分的20%，其余四个维度各占15%。

Why Boston Ranks First for Work
波士顿为何拔得头筹

Boston’s top ranking is driven by strength across several measures rather than dominance in a single category.
波士顿的综合优势源于多项指标的出色表现，而非单一维度的绝对领先。

By category, Boston ranks second for career mobility and fifth for economic opportunity.
分项来看，波士顿在职业发展空间维度排名第二，经济机遇维度位列第五。

"For job seekers, the Boston-Cambridge ecosystem offers unusually strong access to healthcare, biotechnology and life sciences, higher education, and technology and professional services," Indeed said in its ranking.
Indeed在报告中指出："对于求职者而言，波士顿-剑桥生态系统在医疗保健、生物技术与生命科学、高等教育以及科技和专业服务领域提供了异常强大的资源。"

Washington, D.C., follows a similar pattern, ranking first for career mobility and third for compensation.
华盛顿特区呈现相似特征，在职业发展空间维度排名第一，薪酬水平位列第三。

Smaller Cities Stand Out for Job Opportunities
中小城市就业机会表现亮眼

Burlington leads all 228 metros for job opportunity, followed by Madison, Sioux Falls, Fargo, and Manchester.
伯灵顿在228个都会区中就业机会排名第一，其后依次是麦迪逊、苏福尔斯、法戈和曼彻斯特。

Their strong showing highlights a different side of the ranking: several smaller metros offer particularly favorable conditions for finding work. Burlington and Madison also perform strongly across the broader set of measures, ranking third and sixth overall, respectively.
这些城市的优异表现揭示了排名的另一面：若干较小都会区为求职提供了特别有利的条件。伯灵顿和麦迪逊在综合评估中也表现强劲，分别位列总榜第三和第六。

Tech Hubs Lead on Compensation
科技中心城市薪酬领先

Some familiar technology centers dominate the compensation rankings.
一些知名科技中心在薪酬排行榜上占据主导地位。

San Jose ranks first for compensation, followed by Seattle, Washington, D.C., San Francisco, and Austin. But the leaders change substantially when other measures are considered.
圣何塞薪酬水平排名第一，西雅图、华盛顿特区、旧金山和奥斯汀紧随其后。但当考量其他指标时，领先城市格局会发生显著变化。

Boulder ranks first for work-life balance, Charlottesville leads for job quality and security, and Rochester, Minnesota, tops economic opportunity. No metro leads all six categories, illustrating why the overall ranking looks different from a simple list of America’s highest-paying cities.
博尔德在工作生活平衡度上排名第一，夏洛茨维尔领跑工作质量与保障维度，明尼苏达州罗切斯特在经济机遇方面位居榜首。没有都会区能在全部六个维度领先，这解释了为何综合排名与单纯的高薪城市名单存在差异。

Ann Arbor, a college town and tech hub in southeast Michigan, is a notable all-around performer, ranking second for job quality and security, third for career mobility, and fifth for work-life balance.
密歇根州东南部的大学城兼科技中心安娜堡表现尤为全面，在工作质量与保障维度排名第二，职业发展空间第三，工作生活平衡度第五。
