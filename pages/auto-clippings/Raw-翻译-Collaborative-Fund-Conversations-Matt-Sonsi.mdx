Matt Sonsini is Managing Partner and co-founder of Ravello Impact, and the former CEO of The Sobrato Organization. Before moving into investing and philanthropy, he spent almost two decades as a corporate lawyer in Silicon Valley, working with startups, venture capitalists, and public companies.

Matt Sonsini是Ravello Impact的管理合伙人兼联合创始人，曾任Sobrato Organization的首席执行官。在转入投资和慈善领域之前，他在硅谷做了近二十年的公司律师，与初创公司、风险投资家和上市公司打交道。

In this conversation, he explains why waiting to give can become its own excuse, why measuring philanthropic impact is harder than measuring investment returns, and what founders sacrifice when they chase the highest valuation. He also revisits the boardroom moment when a CEO quit on the spot and the unlikely IPO that still stands out decades later.

在这次对话中，他解释了为何“等待再给予”可能成为自身的借口，为何衡量慈善影响力比衡量投资回报更难，以及创始人追逐最高估值时会牺牲什么。他还回顾了某位CEO当场辞职的董事会时刻，以及几十年后仍令人印象深刻的意外IPO。

“Well, I guess if you’re Warren Buffett, you have a reasonable shot at compounding capital at a greater rate than the world’s problems are compounding. But for mere mortal investors, I’m personally doubtful that’s the case.”

“嗯，我想如果你是沃伦·巴菲特，你确实有机会让资本以比世界问题累积更快的速度复利增长。但对于普通投资者来说，我个人对此持怀疑态度。”

Key Takeaways:

关键要点：

Giving has its own risk-return spectrum

: Local food banks offer immediate, measurable results. Systemic change demands more time, more resources, and a willingness to accept uncertain outcomes. Sonsini approaches those choices as a portfolio, allocating across both.

捐赠有其自身的风险-回报谱系

：本地食物银行能带来即时、可衡量的成果。系统性变革则需要更多时间、更多资源，以及接受不确定结果的意愿。Sonsini将这些选择视为一个投资组合，在两者之间进行配置。

Good intentions need honest feedback

: A business with a bad product hears from the market. A donor can still get a gala and a name on a building without knowing how much good the money did. He also argues that funders, nonprofits, and government need to collaborate more to make their resources count.

善意需要诚实的反馈

：产品不佳的企业会听到市场的声音。而捐赠者可能仍能参加晚宴、让名字出现在大楼上，却不知道这笔钱带来了多少益处。他还认为，资助方、非营利组织和政府需要加强合作，让资源发挥实效。

A higher valuation can hide a worse deal

: Senior liquidation preferences and separate investor approval rights can create conflicts when growth stalls. He urges founders to resist trading away important terms to reach a headline valuation.

更高的估值可能掩盖更差的交易

：优先清算权和独立的投资者审批权可能在增长停滞时引发冲突。他敦促创始人不要为了达到一个引人注目的估值而放弃重要条款。

Venture’s failures are part of the process

: Funding booms draw talent and ideas into new markets. Many companies fail in the correction, but investors cannot know the eventual winners in advance. He sees that experimentation as necessary.

风投的失败是过程的一部分

：融资热潮将人才和创意吸引到新市场。许多公司在调整期失败，但投资者无法提前知道最终的赢家。他认为这种试错是必要的。

Optimism takes practice

: Legal training taught Sonsini to look for downside. Investing taught him that optimism is essential. The best advisers, he says, tell clients which risks matter and which they can stop worrying about.

乐观需要练习

：法律训练教会了Sonsini寻找下行风险。投资则让他明白乐观至关重要。他说，最好的顾问会告诉客户哪些风险值得关注，哪些可以不必担心。
