Why the Netherlands Ranks First
荷兰问鼎之谜

The Netherlands’ position at the top of the ranking partly reflects Rotterdam’s role as a gateway to the wider European oil market.
荷兰登顶部分得益于鹿特丹作为欧洲石油集散枢纽的地位。

Rotterdam is Europe’s largest port and a major hub for crude oil, petroleum products, storage, and refining. Oil unloaded there can be processed locally or moved onward by pipeline and barge to Germany and other parts of northwestern Europe.
作为欧洲最大港口，鹿特丹是原油、石油制品存储与精炼的核心枢纽，卸载的石油可就地加工或通过管道/驳船转运至德国等西北欧地区。

As a result, oil recorded as exported to the Netherlands is not necessarily consumed there.
因此统计为出口至荷兰的石油未必在当地消费。

Europe’s shift away from Russian oil has also increased demand for alternative suppliers. After the European Union banned most seaborne Russian crude imports in late 2022, U.S. crude became an increasingly important replacement source for European refiners.
欧洲减少对俄罗斯石油依赖也推高了替代供应需求。2022年底欧盟禁运多数俄罗斯海运原油后，美国原油逐渐成为欧洲炼油厂的重要替代来源。

The Netherlands has remained one of the largest destinations for U.S. crude oil since then.
此后荷兰始终是美国原油的最大目的地之一。

Mexico’s Oil Trade With the U.S. Works Differently
美墨石油贸易的独特模式

Mexico’s 1.09 million barrels per day reflects a different kind of energy relationship.
墨西哥109万桶/日的进口量折射出特殊的能源合作关系。

Mexico exports much of its own heavier crude oil to the United States while importing large volumes of refined petroleum products, including gasoline and diesel, from U.S. Gulf Coast refineries.
墨西哥向美出口大量重质原油，同时从墨西哥湾沿岸炼油厂进口汽油、柴油等精炼产品。

This two-way trade reflects differences in refinery capacity and configuration between the two countries. U.S. refineries process Mexican crude while supplying fuels that Mexico’s domestic refining system does not produce in sufficient quantities.
这种双向贸易源于两国炼油产能与结构的差异——美国炼油厂既加工墨西哥原油，又补充其国内精炼系统的产能缺口。
