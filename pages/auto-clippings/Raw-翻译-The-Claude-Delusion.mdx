克劳德妄想症（永久链接）  

One of the less remarked-upon aspects of becoming an atheist is how it changes the way you see a sunset.  
成为无神论者最未被言说的改变之一，是你看待日落的方式会彻底改变。  

If you believe in an almighty, omnipresent God, then sunsets are one of God's intentional creations, which means that the beautiful colors refracting through the darkling sky were chosen to produce that effect.  
若你信仰全能全在的上帝，那么日落便是神的刻意造物——这意味着暮色中折射的瑰丽色彩都是被精心挑选的。  

To gaze upon a sunset with religious faith is to encounter the intentional act of another mind.  
带着信仰凝视日落，你是在见证另一个意识的刻意之举。  

To gaze upon that same sunset without faith is to look upon something striking, beautiful, and yet empty; not empty of wonder or beauty, but empty of purpose.  
而无信仰者看到的同一片晚霞，虽震撼绚丽却空洞无物——并非缺失奇迹或美，而是丧失了目的性。  

No one hung that sun in the sky, no one chose its colors as it sank.  
无人将太阳悬于天际，无人为沉落的夕阳调色。  

It may still be beautiful, but that's a fundamentally different kind of beauty.  
美则美矣，却是另一种本质迥异的美。  

There's a sunset that sits halfway between a religious sunset and an atheist sunset: an artist's depiction of a sunset.  
存在介于宗教日落与无神论日落之间的第三种日落：艺术家的落日描绘。  

This represents the choices of another person, another mind, and at the very least, that mind was talking to itself, trying to take something from inside the mind and put it outside of the mind.  
这代表着另一个人类心智的选择——至少这个心智在进行自我对话，试图将内在思绪外化呈现。  

Very often, the mind that directed the capturing of the sunset wanted to say something to other people, perhaps even you (think of a loved one sending you a cameraphone picture of a sunset).  
那个指挥捕捉落日的心智往往试图向他人（比如给你发送手机落日照片的挚爱）传递某些讯息。  

That painting or photo may not be divine, but it is certainly intentional.  
画作或照片虽非神迹，但必然蕴含意图。  

To look upon a painting of a sunset – or even a photo of a sunset – is to look upon something someone chose to make.  
凝视一幅落日画作（甚至照片），你看到的是某人选择创造之物。  

You don't slip and accidentally create a sunset painting. Sunset paintings aren't accidents.  
你不可能失足滑倒意外画成落日图。落日绘画绝非偶然。  

The sunset painting has something to say.  
落日画作自有其宣言。  

Figuring out intentions is our minds' reflexive preoccupation.  
解读意图是人类心智的反射性本能。  

It's what keeps us from dying in traffic, it's what lets us win at poker.  
这本能让我们避免车祸丧生，也让我们在牌局中取胜。  

It's the key to love and parenting, to effective management and "managing up."  
这是爱与育儿的核心，是高效管理和"向上管理"的要义。  

It's why we get mad at people, and why we forgive them.  
我们因此愤怒，也因此宽恕。  

It's why our hearts race in sympathy with the characters in a book or on a screen.  
我们因此为书页或银幕中的角色心跳加速。  

It's the automatic and irresistible starting point for every encounter with a poem, a song, a book or a sculpture.  
这是我们接触诗歌、歌曲、书籍或雕塑时不可抗拒的起点。  

It's the most fundamental difference between a sculpture of a tree and a tree: the tree grew all on its own, while the sculpture was made, on purpose, by another person with another mind.  
这是树木雕塑与真实树木的根本差异：树木自然生长，雕塑则被另一个心智刻意塑造。  

This reflex to attribute intention explains why AI is so controversial, so compelling, and so eerie.  
这种归因意图的本能解释了人工智能为何如此争议、迷人且诡异。  

Mark Fisher defines eeriness as "when there is something present where there should be nothing, or if there is nothing present when there should be something":  
马克·费舍将诡异定义为"本应无物处有物存在，或本应有物处空无一物"：  

To look upon an AI-generated text or image is to look upon a thing that was extruded, not chosen.  
凝视AI生成的文字或图像，你看到的是被挤压产出之物，而非选择创造之果。  

You can slip and prompt an AI-generated image of a sunset, and it will embody no intentionality on anyone's part.  
你失手误触就能生成AI落日图，其中不蕴含任何人的意图。  

This isn't something we have any experience with.  
这是人类经验之外的陌生领域。  

When we see a painting, we reflexively interpret it as having a painter.  
看见画作，我们会反射性认为存在画家。  

When we read a book, we reflexively impute auctorial intent to its words.  
阅读书籍，我们会本能地将作者意图投射于文字。  

When we converse, we reflexively form a theory about the mind directing the other half of the conversation.  
与人交谈时，我们会自动构建关于对话另一端心智的理论。  

But we can't do this with AI.  
但对AI我们无法如此。  

Anyone who understands how the theory-free statistical inference systems we call "AI" (at this time) work will understand that they are systems that cannot form intent, that have nothing to form intent with:  
任何理解当前所谓"AI"的无理论统计推断系统本质的人都明白，它们既不能形成意图，也不具备形成意图的基础：  

Even so, it's hard (or perhaps impossible) to avoid the reflex to impute intention to things that seem to have intenders.  
即便如此，我们仍难以（或许不可能）克制向看似有意图者归因的本能。  

Think of your autonomic, unavoidable emotional response to the fictional characters you encounter in literature.  
试想你对文学虚构角色不由自主的情感反应。  

Feeling sorrow or joy for imaginary people is weird.  
为虚构人物悲喜本就很奇怪。  

They aren't real, and you know they aren't real, and yet you may find yourself moved to tears by their plight.  
他们不存在，你也心知肚明，却仍会为其遭遇落泪。  

That aesthetic experience  
这种审美体验
