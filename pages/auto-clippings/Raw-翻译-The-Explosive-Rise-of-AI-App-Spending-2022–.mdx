The Explosive Rise of AI App Spending (2022–2026)

Key Takeaways

Generative AI app spending grew nearly 200-fold between the first quarter of 2022 and the first quarter of 2026.

Globally, spending on generative AI apps reached nearly $2 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Spending in North America accounts for 39% of the global total.

Meanwhile, the market share of nearly every other region has increased. Europe, Asia, and Latin America have seen the largest percentage point increases in AI app spending.

More Money, and Time, Being Spent Globally

As generative AI capabilities increase, people are paying more for apps globally.

They’re also spending more time on them. Worldwide, time spent on generative AI apps reached

36 billion hours

in the first half of 2026, which is equivalent to more than four hours for every person on Earth.

And the average number of days people use ChatGPT per month reached 13.5 in April, surpassing both Reddit (12.6 days) and Threads (10.2 days).

As AI apps become more integrated into everyday life, spending on these tools could continue to grow.
