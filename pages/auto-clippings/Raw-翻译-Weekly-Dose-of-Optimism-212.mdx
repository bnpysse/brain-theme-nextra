Vironexis Turns the Liver Into a Cancer Drug Factory  
Vironexis将肝脏转化为抗癌药工厂  

Here at the Dose, we love a lot of things, but we hate cancer.  
在《乐观周刊》，我们热爱许多事物，但憎恶癌症。  

In last week’s  
上周的  

Dose  
周刊  

, we covered a drug that helps T cells attack cancer and a gene therapy that gives kids the instructions to make a missing enzyme. This week, a company called Vironexis shared encouraging results from putting those two ideas together: give the body instructions to make its own cancer-fighting medicine.  
我们报道了助力T细胞攻击癌症的药物，以及指导儿童生成缺失酶的基因疗法。本周，Vironexis公司公布了结合这两种理念的突破：让身体自主生成抗癌药物。  

On Monday, Vironexis reported that all three patients in a subgroup with relapsed or refractory acute lymphoblastic leukemia achieved complete responses after a single administration of VNX-101, with no residual leukemia detected in testing. The first patient’s response lasted 260 days, at which point their doctor proceeded with a stem cell transplant.  
周一Vironexis宣布，在复发/难治性急性淋巴细胞白血病亚组中，三名患者单次注射VNX-101后均实现完全缓解，检测显示零残留白血病。首例患者的缓解持续260天，随后医生进行了干细胞移植。  

Three is a very small number of patients, but the  
虽然三例样本量很小，但  

mechanism  
作用机制  

is very cool. An adeno-associated virus, or AAV, delivers genetic instructions to liver cells, which produce a protein called GP101. One end grabs CD19 on cancer cells while the other grabs CD3 on T cells, bridging the immune system’s killer T cells right to their targets.  
极为精妙：腺相关病毒（AAV）向肝细胞传递遗传指令，使其生成GP101蛋白。该蛋白一端锁定癌细胞的CD19，另一端抓住T细胞的CD3，将免疫系统的杀伤性T细胞直接导向目标。  

Once a patient takes VNX-101, their liver cells keep making GP101, like a little pharmaceutical factory manufacturing the treatment inside the body.  
患者注射VNX-101后，其肝细胞会持续生产GP101——犹如体内微型制药厂。  

There are a bunch of qualifications - those three patients had no pre-existing anti-AAV antibodies; the broader Phase 1/2 trial has treated nine people across several blood cancers; they’re early, company-reported results; Cytokine release syndrome and neurological toxicity occurred during dose escalation and resolved with treatment; and there is still a lot to learn about dosing, safety, and how long the responses last - but the promise is enormous. The pharma industry spends enormous effort and money figuring out how to manufacture medicines and get them into people, repeatedly, over and over. If we can safely teach people’s own cells to handle more of that manufacturing, we could give people more time living their lives, less time receiving infusions, and ultimately, longer, healthier lives.  
需注意多项前提：这三名患者体内无抗AAV抗体；更广泛的1/2期试验已治疗9名血癌患者；当前仅是早期企业自报数据；剂量递增阶段出现细胞因子释放综合征和神经毒性（经治疗缓解）；关于剂量、安全性和疗效持久性仍有大量未知——但前景广阔。制药业耗费巨资反复探索药物生产与给药方式。若能安全教会人体细胞自主制药，人们就能减少治疗时间、延长健康寿命。  

Say it with me… get fucked, cancer.  
和我一起说：去死吧，癌症。

Solcoa Raises $75M for American Rare Earth Metallization  
Solcoa融资7500万美元推动美国稀土金属化  

Not Boring readers will  
《Not Boring》读者都  

understand  
明白  

that the future depends on rare earth metals, and that China currently mogs the US in its control over that supply chain.  
未来取决于稀土金属，而中国目前在该供应链上碾压美国。  

Enter Solcoa.  
Solcoa应运而生。  

On Thursday, Solcoa  
周四Solcoa  

announced $75 million  
宣布获得7500万美元融资  

to build Solcoa One, a rare earth metalmaking plant in Nevada designed to produce 500 tonnes a year, with operations targeted for July 2027. The financing includes $45 million in equity led by Bain Capital Ventures and $30 million in debt and equipment financing anchored by J.P. Morgan.  
用于在内华达州建设Solcoa One工厂，目标年产500吨稀土金属，2027年7月投产。融资包含贝恩资本领投的4500万美元股权融资，以及摩根大通支持的3000万美元债权及设备融资。  

Solcoa works on metallization: converting refined rare earth oxides into metals that magnet manufacturers can use. It’s one more step in the chain from mine to magnet to anything that moves electrically that we’ll be able to do here.  
Solcoa专注金属化工艺：将精炼稀土氧化物转化为磁铁制造商可用的金属。这是从矿山到磁铁再到电动设备的全链条中，美国本土化的关键一环。  

The company  
该公司  

already produces  
已实现  

neodymium-praseodymium and samarium metal at a rate of about 10 tonnes a year in Alameda, but 10 tonnes doesn’t do much against a 100-kiloton-a-year NdPr market and a 2 kiloton-a-year samarium market. The money will help them 50x that, and then 6x that, and if they’re successful, hopefully unlock capital to scale some more.  
在阿拉米达年产约10吨钕镨合金和钐金属，但相较于年需求10万吨的钕镨市场和2000吨的钐市场杯水车薪。新资金助其先扩大50倍产能，再提升6倍，若进展顺利有望吸引更多资本。  

Its approach combines new chemistry with modular reactors that it builds itself. Solcoa says the process uses less energy and costs less than conventional molten-salt electrolysis. Developing new technology is the key way America will catch up to China.  
其技术融合新型化学工艺与自主建造的模块化反应器。Solcoa称该工艺比传统熔盐电解法更节能省钱。发展新技术是美国追赶中国的关键。
