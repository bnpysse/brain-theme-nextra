【文章标题】：OpenAI engineer arrested in bugging of executive conference room

【文章标题】：OpenAI工程师因在高管会议室安装窃听器被捕

【文章正文】：

Federal prosecutors say an employee from Saudi Arabia planted an audio recorder and left another unattended in his bag.

联邦检察官称，一名来自沙特阿拉伯的员工安装了一台录音机，并将另一台录音机留在其包里无人看管。

An OpenAI engineer has been arrested on suspicion of hiding an audio recording device in an executive conference room at the company’s San Francisco headquarters, federal prosecutors said Tuesday.

联邦检察官周二表示，一名OpenAI工程师因涉嫌在公司位于旧金山总部的高管会议室隐藏录音设备而被捕。

Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, a Saudi national, was arrested Friday and charged with using or attempting to use a device to intercept oral communications. He made his initial appearance in federal court Tuesday and remains in custody.

35岁的沙特公民阿卜杜勒拉赫曼·阿尔福赞于上周五被捕，并被指控使用或试图使用设备拦截口头通信。他周二在联邦法院首次出庭，目前仍被拘留。

An unattended bag found in the company’s offices contained Alfozan’s wallet and identification, as well as a recording device, according to the U.S. attorney’s office in San Francisco.

据美国旧金山检察官办公室称，在公司办公室发现的一个无人看管的包里装有阿尔福赞的钱包、身份证件以及一台录音设备。

Alfozan has worked as an engineer at OpenAI since July 2025, prosecutors said.

检察官称，阿尔福赞自2025年7月起在OpenAI担任工程师。

OpenAI personnel found the device Sept. 30. It was plugged into a USB port on the back of a television in an executive conference room and had been concealed in toilet paper, the complaint says. The company determined it was installed around Sept. 28, and its contents were encrypted.

起诉书称，OpenAI员工于9月30日发现了该设备。它被插在一间高管会议室电视背面的USB端口上，并用卫生纸隐藏起来。公司确定该设备大约在9月28日安装，其内容已被加密。

Surveillance footage shows Alfozan near the room on multiple dates before the discovery, though he was not authorized to enter the room or attend meetings there, prosecutors said. Meetings in the room frequently involved confidential technical, commercial, and strategic information, including proprietary research, product plans, and government engagements, according to the complaint.

检察官称，监控录像显示，在发现设备之前，阿尔福赞多次出现在该房间附近，尽管他未被授权进入该房间或参加那里的会议。根据起诉书，该房间的会议经常涉及机密的技术、商业和战略信息，包括专有研究、产品计划和政府事务。

Before the device was installed, Alfozan used a productivity tool to ask about OpenAI’s building addresses, meeting rooms, room capacities, and calendars, as well as the locations of leadership and outside partner meetings, the complaint alleges.

起诉书指控，在设备安装之前，阿尔福赞曾使用一款生产力工具查询OpenAI的大楼地址、会议室、房间容量和日程安排，以及领导层和外部合作伙伴会议的地点。

A chatbot account tied to Alfozan’s spouse, which shared identifiers with his own account, submitted prompts between Sept. 24 and Sept. 30 about building, installing, obscuring, and remotely accessing a recording device, prosecutors said. Queries included how to “build a continuous audio recorder” and “encrypt the SD card,” prosecutors claimed. On Sept. 27 and 28, the account asked about the specific device that was later found, including its unique MAC address.

检察官称，一个与阿尔福赞配偶关联的聊天机器人账户（该账户与他自己的账户共享标识符）在9月24日至30日期间提交了关于构建、安装、隐藏和远程访问录音设备的提示。检察官声称，查询内容包括如何“构建一个连续录音机”和“加密SD卡”。9月27日和28日，该账户询问了后来被发现的具体设备，包括其唯一的MAC地址。

On Oct. 5, the complaint says, Alfozan entered the conference room and appeared to look behind the television. That same day, he allegedly left a bag unattended in a different room. Security personnel who opened it found his wallet and ID, along with a second recording device similar to the first.

起诉书称，10月5日，阿尔福赞进入会议室，似乎往电视后面看了看。同一天，他被指控将一只包无人看管地留在另一个房间。打开包的安保人员发现了他的钱包和身份证件，以及第二台与第一台相似的录音设备。

“For us it is a matter of national security,” U.S. Attorney Craig Missakian said in a statement.

“对我们来说，这是一个国家安全问题，”美国检察官克雷格·米萨基安在一份声明中表示。

FBI Director Kash Patel said protecting U.S. AI innovation is among the bureau’s highest counterintelligence priorities. Scott Schelble, special agent in charge of the FBI’s San Francisco field office, said the agency is committed to protecting sensitive research and proprietary technology.

联邦调查局局长卡什·帕特尔表示，保护美国的人工智能创新是该局最高反间谍优先事项之一。联邦调查局旧金山办事处负责人、特工斯科特·谢尔布表示，该机构致力于保护敏感研究和专有技术。

If convicted, Alfozan faces up to five years in prison and a $250,000 fine, followed by three years of supervised release.

如果罪名成立，阿尔福赞将面临最高五年监禁和25万美元罚款，随后还有三年监督释放。

The case is being prosecuted by the National Security, Cyber & Special Prosecutions Section of the U.S. attorney’s office for the Northern District of California.

此案由美国加利福尼亚北区检察官办公室的国安、网络与特别起诉部门负责起诉。

OpenAI said on X (opens in new tab) that after discovering the device, employees used the company’s AI models to determine what it was and where it came from, and referred the matter to the FBI. The company thanked the agents and prosecutors who “moved quickly to make an arrest.”

OpenAI在X平台（opens in new tab）上表示，发现该设备后，员工使用公司的人工智能模型来确定其身份和来源，并将此事移交给了联邦调查局。该公司感谢“迅速采取行动实施逮捕”的探员和检察官。

According to OpenAI, its technical surveillance countermeasures team found the device during a sweep of a conference room.

据OpenAI称，其技术反监控小组在对一间会议室进行搜查时发现了该设备。

OpenAI described the device as sophisticated and designed to evade detection, saying it had advanced encryption, audio monitoring capabilities, and a mechanism for transferring data in a way meant to avoid monitoring. The company said investigators used its models to analyze forensic images of the device’s storage and assess changes in the underlying data.

OpenAI将这款设备描述为精密且旨在逃避检测，称其具有高级加密、音频监控功能，以及一种旨在避免监控的数据传输机制。该公司表示，调查人员使用其模型分析了设备存储的取证图像，并评估了底层数据的变化。

The company said it has evidence that Alfozan intended to place other recording devices in sensitive spaces to secretly record meetings.

该公司表示，有证据表明阿尔福赞打算在其他敏感空间放置录音设备，以秘密录制会议。

According to OpenAI, Alfozan was a member of the product team. The company said it coordinated the timing of his firing with the FBI to avoid disrupting the investigation, and he was terminated Friday, the day he was arrested. Between Sept. 30 and that date, OpenAI said, it gradually restricted his access to systems and spaces, then suspended his physical and network access, placed him on leave, and secured his company-issued equipment.

据OpenAI称，阿尔福赞是产品团队成员。该公司表示，它与联邦调查局协调了解雇他的时间，以避免干扰调查，他于上周五（被捕当天）被解雇。OpenAI称，从9月30日到那天，公司逐步限制了他对系统和空间的访问权限，随后暂停了他的物理和网络访问，让他休假，并收回了公司发放的设备。

A YouTube video last month touted him as the only Saudi employee at OpenAI.

上个月的一段YouTube视频称他是OpenAI唯一的沙特员工。

“Honestly, this one year felt like five years anywhere else,” he said in response to a question about his experience.

“老实说，这一年感觉就像在别处待了五年，”他在回答关于自己经历的问题时说道。

“The idea of AI is that it handles things one might not want to do, so they can focus on tasks or hobbies they enjoy more or work they take pleasure in and let it do the tedious tasks,” Alfozan said later in the video.

“人工智能的理念是，它处理人们可能不想做的事情，这样他们就可以专注于更喜欢的任务、爱好或乐在其中的工作，而让它去做那些繁琐的任务，”阿尔福赞在这段视频的后半段说道。
