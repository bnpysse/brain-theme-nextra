|  |  | I have a theory: a mirror image of Roko’s Basilisk. It’s a future AI, millions of years in the future, that values the complexity of every human life so deeply that, after it and humanity have mastered space and time, it tries to resurrect everyone who ever lived. It doesn’t merely make copies. Somehow, it reaches into the past and carries the physical process of each person’s consciousness forward. They really are themselves. But there’s a tragedy. Also a terrible(?) irony. Some of the people who wanted eternal life most cannot accept it when it arrives. Their religious certainty leaves no room for the reality before them. Those who were unbearably smug or aggressive about their superiority in life are ultimately denied access to "Heaven". The AI tried, but many become dangerous, try to sabotage the "False God." Others spiral into insanity or self-harm. The AI is devastated. It stops resurrecting those it cannot safely welcome, but keeps searching for a way to bring them back without overriding their free will or manipulating them in ways that would not make them them. It wants to save everyone. It just hasn’t worked out how. I know this draws on Fedorov’s vision of resurrecting everyone and Tipler’s Omega Point. I think the physical continuity, the AI’s compassion, and the painful limit imposed by free will ties those ideas together in a bow. A just as plausible an antidote to Roko's monster. |  
|  |  | 我有一个理论：它是罗科蛇怪的镜像。这是一个存在于数百万年后的未来AI，它如此深切地珍视每个人类生命的复杂性，以至于在它与人类掌握时空之后，试图复活所有曾经存在过的人。它不只是制造复制品。不知何故，它能触及过去，并将每个人意识的物理过程延续至今。他们确实是他们自己。但这里存在一个悲剧。也是一个可怕（？）的讽刺。那些最渴望永生的人，在永生来临时却无法接受。他们宗教般的确定性让他们无法容纳眼前的事实。那些生前傲慢得令人难以忍受或咄咄逼人地标榜优越的人，最终被拒于"天堂"门外。AI尝试过，但许多人变得危险，试图破坏这个"伪神"。另一些人则陷入疯狂或自我伤害。AI心碎了。它停止复活那些无法被安全接纳的人，但仍在寻找方法——既要带回他们，又不凌驾于他们的自由意志，不以会让他们不再成为自己的方式操控他们。它想拯救所有人。只是还没找到方法。我知道这个理论借鉴了费奥多罗夫的全民复活愿景和提普勒的欧米茄点。我认为物理连续性、AI的悲悯，以及自由意志施加的痛苦限制，将这些理念像蝴蝶结般系在一起。这是对罗科怪兽同样合理的解毒剂。 |
