Key Takeaways

要点

U.S. gold IRA assets rose from $2 billion in 2005 to $76 billion in 2025, a 38x increase.

美国黄金IRA资产从2005年的20亿美元增长到2025年的760亿美元，增长了38倍。

U.S. silver IRA assets grew 19x over the same period, rising from $1 billion to $19 billion as precious metals gained ground in retirement planning.

同期，随着贵金属在退休规划中的地位不断提升，美国白银IRA资产增长了19倍，从10亿美元增至190亿美元。

U.S. precious metals IRA assets climbed from $3 billion in 2005 to $95 billion in 2025, led by a 38x increase in gold IRA assets.

美国贵金属IRA资产从2005年的30亿美元攀升至2025年的950亿美元，其中黄金IRA资产以38倍的增幅领跑。

Gold Becomes the Anchor

黄金成为支柱

Gold has become the anchor of modern U.S. precious metals retirement accounts. In 2005, gold IRA assets were estimated at $2 billion. By 2025, that figure had grown to $76 billion.

黄金已成为现代美国贵金属退休账户的支柱。2005年，黄金IRA资产估计为20亿美元。到2025年，这一数字已增长至760亿美元。

Gold and Silver Gain Ground

黄金与白银占据优势

Traditional retirement assets still play a major role, but gold and silver IRAs are gaining ground.

传统退休资产仍扮演重要角色，但黄金和白银IRA正逐渐占据优势。

Gold’s rise is especially notable because it is often viewed differently from

paper assets

. It does not depend on earnings, dividends, or interest payments, which can make it appealing during periods of uncertainty, inflation, and market volatility.

黄金的崛起尤其引人注目，因为它通常被视为与

纸面资产

不同。它不依赖盈利、股息或利息支付，因此在不确定性、通货膨胀和市场波动时期更具吸引力。

Precious Metals in Retirement Planning

贵金属在退休规划中的角色

The growth of gold and silver IRA assets, from $3 billion to $95 billion, shows how physical precious metals are becoming a larger part of long-term retirement strategies.

黄金和白银IRA资产从30亿美元增长到950亿美元，这表明实物贵金属正在成为长期退休策略中越来越重要的一部分。
