What Can One Square Meter of New York Real Estate Buy?

一平方米纽约房产能买到什么？

Key Takeaways:

关键要点：

Selling one square meter of property in New York City could buy 15.3 square meters in Cuenca, Ecuador, or 9.5 in Chiang Mai, Thailand.

在纽约市出售一平方米房产，可在厄瓜多尔的昆卡购买15.3平方米，或在泰国的清迈购买9.5平方米。

New York real estate is more expensive per square meter than property in London, Tokyo, Singapore, and Hong Kong.

纽约房地产每平方米的价格高于伦敦、东京、新加坡和香港的房产。

Copenhagen is among the few cities in the dataset where the same money buys less space, at just 0.63 square meters.

哥本哈根是数据集中少数几个同样金额能买到的空间更少的城市之一，仅为0.63平方米。

How far would the proceeds from selling a tiny piece of New York City real estate go elsewhere in the world?

Why Is Property So Much Cheaper in Parts of Asia?

为什么亚洲部分地区的房产便宜得多？

The contrast is especially striking in Southeast Asia. One New York square meter buys approximately 7.8 square meters in Bali, 4.3 in Manila, or 3.0 in Bangkok.

这种对比在东南亚尤为显著。纽约一平方米在巴厘岛大约能买到7.8平方米，在马尼拉为4.3平方米，在曼谷为3.0平方米。

Lower local wages, construction costs, and differences in housing demand help explain these gaps. However, Asia is far from uniformly inexpensive: Singapore, Hong Kong, and Seoul offer less space than New York for the same budget.

当地较低的工资、建筑成本以及住房需求的差异有助于解释这些差距。然而，亚洲远非普遍便宜：新加坡、香港和首尔在相同预算下提供的空间比纽约还少。

Lower property prices do not necessarily mean greater affordability for local residents. A home that looks inexpensive to a foreign buyer may still be out of reach for many locals, whose incomes are also lower on average.

较低的房价并不一定意味着当地居民更强的负担能力。一栋对外国买家来说看似便宜的住宅，对许多当地人来说可能仍然遥不可及，因为他们的平均收入也较低。
