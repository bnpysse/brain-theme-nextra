How State Income Tax Rates Compare in 2026

Key Takeaways
核心要点

Eight U.S. states levy no individual income tax: Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, and Wyoming.
美国有八个州不征收个人所得税：阿拉斯加、佛罗里达、内华达、新罕布什尔、南达科他、田纳西、德克萨斯和怀俄明。

California has the highest top marginal income tax rate in the country (13.3%), followed by Hawaii (11%).
加利福尼亚州拥有全美最高的最高边际所得税率（13.3%），夏威夷州（11%）紧随其后。

Fifteen states, including Illinois (4.95%) and North Carolina (3.99%), levy flat individual income taxes.
包括伊利诺伊州（4.95%）和北卡罗来纳州（3.99%）在内的十五个州实行单一税率个人所得税。

Where you live can make a major difference in the state income tax rate applied to your earnings, with systems ranging from no individual income tax to top marginal rates above 10%.
居住地会显著影响您适用的州所得税率——从零个人所得税到超过10%的最高边际税率不等。

Which States Have No Individual Income Tax?
哪些州免征个人所得税？

Eight states levy no individual income tax: Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, and Wyoming.
八个免征个人所得税的州：阿拉斯加、佛罗里达、内华达、新罕布什尔、南达科他、田纳西、德克萨斯和怀俄明。

New Hampshire is a relatively recent addition to the list, having repealed its tax on interest and dividends in 2025.
新罕布什尔州是最新加入该名单的成员，该州于2025年废除了利息和股息税。

Having no individual income tax does not mean residents avoid taxes altogether. Each of these eight states levies other taxes to raise government revenue.
免征个人所得税不意味着居民完全免税。这八个州都通过其他税种增加政府收入。

Texas, for example, has some of the highest property tax rates in the country, while Tennessee’s high combined state and local sales tax shifts more of the tax burden toward consumer spending.
例如德克萨斯州拥有全美最高的房产税率，而田纳西州较高的州税与地方销售税组合将更多税负转嫁至消费支出。

Some states also benefit from revenue generated by specific industries. Nevada collects substantial gaming taxes, while Alaska taxes oil and gas companies operating in the state.
某些州还从特定产业获取收入：内华达州征收巨额博彩税，阿拉斯加州则对在本州运营的油气公司征税。

How Flat Income Taxes Work
单一税率所得税如何运作

Another 15 states use a flat individual income tax, applying a single statutory rate to taxable income rather than progressively higher rates as income rises.
另有15个州采用单一税率个人所得税，对应税收入适用统一法定税率（而非随收入增加而提高的累进税率）。

States with flat income taxes include major Midwestern economies such as Illinois (4.95%) and Michigan (4.25%), as well as Mountain West states including Colorado (4.4%) and Utah (4.5%). Idaho’s 5.3% rate is the highest flat individual income tax rate in the country.
采用单一税率的州包括中西部经济重镇（如伊利诺伊州4.95%、密歇根州4.25%）和西部山区州（如科罗拉多州4.4%、犹他州4.5%）。爱达荷州5.3%的税率是全美最高的单一所得税率。

Individual tax bills can still vary based on deductions, exemptions, credits, and other state-specific rules. Efforts to replace flat taxes with graduated systems have also failed in some states. In Illinois, for example, voters rejected a 2020 proposal to allow a graduated income tax.
个人税单仍会因扣除项、豁免额、税收抵免及各州特殊规定而不同。某些州试图改用累进税制的努力也遭遇失败——例如伊利诺伊州选民在2020年否决了采用累进所得税的提案。

Where Top Marginal Tax Rates Are Highest
最高边际税率分布情况

Most states instead use graduated income tax systems, in which higher portions of taxable income are subject to higher rates, similar to the federal tax system. Washington, D.C., also uses a graduated system, with a top marginal rate of 10.75%.
多数州采用类似联邦税制的累进所得税系统——应税收入越高部分适用更高税率。华盛顿特区也采用累进税制，最高边际税率为10.75%。

California, the largest state economy in the country, has the highest top marginal rate at 13.3%. This rate applies only to taxable income above $1 million for single filers. California is followed by Hawaii at 11% and New York at 10.9%, which also rank among the states with the highest overall tax burdens nationwide.
全美最大经济体加利福尼亚州以13.3%的最高边际税率居首（仅适用于单身申报者超100万美元的应税收入）。夏威夷州（11%）和纽约州（10.9%）紧随其后，这两个州也位列全国综合税负最高地区。

Top marginal rates do not tell the whole story. The income threshold at which each rate takes effect can significantly affect how much tax a person ultimately owes. In Virginia, for example, the state’s top rate of 5.75% begins above just $17,000 in taxable income, while California’s 13.3% top rate applies above $1 million for single filers.
最高边际税率并非全部真相。各税率生效的收入门槛会极大影响最终纳税额——例如弗吉尼亚州5.75%的最高税率仅对超1.7万美元应税收入生效，而加州13.3%的最高税率针对单身申报者超100万美元收入部分。
