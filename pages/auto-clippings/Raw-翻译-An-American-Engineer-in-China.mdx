This piece will cover my first 11 days in China. The purpose of my extended trip is to learn about and meet with Chinese energy equipment companies and factories, but I’m also curious about China’s culture and financial ecosystem.

这篇文章将涵盖我在中国的最初 11 天。我这次延长旅行的目的是了解并会见中国的能源设备公司和工厂，但我也对中国文化和金融生态系统感到好奇。

In the first section, I was an outsider getting a feel for China and getting invited to the right WeChat group chats. As I did, I began getting invited to factories and having discussions with more and more “mainlanders” who helped fill my knowledge gaps about the country. Throughout the piece, I’ll share a mix of my experiences and key takeaways.

在第一部分，我是一个局外人，正在感受中国，并被邀请加入正确的微信群聊。随着我这样做，我开始被邀请参观工厂，并与越来越多的“大陆人”讨论，他们帮助填补了我对这个国家的知识空白。在整篇文章中，我将分享我的经历和关键要点的混合。

Three Biggest Takeaways:

三大要点：

The China of 2036 will be vastly different than the China of 2026.

2036 年的中国将与 2026 年的中国截然不同。

Companies that used to manufacture luxury handbags are now becoming the brands themselves. Investors who used to copy American VCs are now running their own state-backed deeptech playbooks. Battery cell companies are becoming grid, datacenter, and asset-owning companies. If you aren’t heavily updating your mental model of China after this piece, then you will get blindsided.

曾经为奢侈手袋代工的公司现在正成为品牌本身。曾经模仿美国风投的投资者现在正在运行他们自己的国家支持的深科技策略。电池电芯公司正在成为电网、数据中心和资产持有公司。如果你在读完这篇文章后没有大幅更新你对中国的心理模型，那么你将会措手不及。

China is heterogeneous.

中国是异质的。

China has so many different dialects, dresses, jobs, climates, educational attainments, and personalities that I feel as though I could spend centuries in China learning new things. Beware of anyone who makes reductive stereotypes about the country, positive or negative.

中国有如此多不同的方言、服饰、工作、气候、教育程度和个性，以至于我觉得我可以在中国花几个世纪学习新事物。要警惕任何对这个国家做出简化刻板印象的人，无论是正面的还是负面的。

China is big.

中国很大。
