【文章标题】：Apple Caught Off Guard by AI Demand for Mac Mini and Mac Studio  
【文章标题】：苹果被Mac mini和Mac Studio的AI需求杀了个措手不及  

【文章正文】：  
Apple's unusually timed announcement of new Mac mini and Mac Studio models this week was driven by unexpectedly strong enterprise appetite for AI hardware, according to The Information.  
据The Information报道，苹果本周一反常态地宣布推出新款Mac mini和Mac Studio机型，原因是企业对AI硬件的需求意外强劲。  

Apple normally releases new Mac models in the autumn, closer to October or November, making this week's announcement unusually early, falling just before the anticipated arrival of new iPhone models. The Information says that the AI-driven boom in Mac Studio and Mac mini sales is behind the early launch.  
苹果通常会在秋季（10月或11月左右）发布新款Mac机型，因此本周的发布显得异常提前，正好赶在新iPhone发布之前。The Information称，Mac Studio和Mac mini因AI需求激增而销量大涨，正是提前发布的原因。  

Apple noticeably promoted the ability to link multiple Mac Studios together into a single, more capable system for running large frontier AI models, a feature aimed at business and developer customers rather than everyday consumers.  
苹果着重宣传了将多台Mac Studio连接成单一、更强大系统的能力，以运行大型前沿AI模型。这一功能主要面向企业和开发者客户，而非普通消费者。  

Apple highlighted the Mac mini and Mac Studio's shift toward business buyers in June, with a "Business at the Park" event involving executives from major companies Ford, Disney, and Anthropic. The Mac mini was said to be the "darling" of the event.  
苹果早在6月就通过一场名为"Business at the Park"的活动，联合福特、迪士尼和Anthropic等大公司高管，突显了Mac mini和Mac Studio向商业买家的转型。Mac mini据称是活动的"宠儿"。  

Even so, enterprise's rush toward powerful desktop Macs more broadly took Apple by surprise. The company reportedly did not possess an engineering team dedicated to business customers or staff focused on developer relations, and lacked an enterprise AI strategy.  
即便如此，企业用户对高性能Mac台式机的广泛追捧仍让苹果措手不及。据报道，苹果既没有专门服务商业客户的工程团队或开发者关系专员，也缺乏企业AI战略。  

Businesses that approached Apple asking to buy access to the company's Private Cloud Compute infrastructure were reportedly turned down. Apple is instead leaning on partners such as WebAI and Mount Thor, which provide AI tools and execution environments built on Apple hardware.  
据报道，那些希望购买苹果Private Cloud Compute基础设施访问权限的企业遭到了拒绝。苹果转而依赖WebAI和Mount Thor等合作伙伴，它们提供基于苹果硬件的AI工具和执行环境。  

A surge in demand for Mac hardware to run AI models has coincided with the global memory shortage, leaving many Mac mini and Mac Studio configurations out of stock for months. Some enterprise customers are reportedly turning to other hardware such as Nvidia's DGX Spark, a compact AI desktop launched late last year in a form factor similar to the Mac mini's, as Apple's own high end configurations remain difficult to get hold of.  
运行AI模型的Mac硬件需求激增恰逢全球内存短缺，导致多款Mac mini和Mac Studio配置缺货数月。据报道，由于苹果自家高端机型仍一机难求，部分企业客户已转向英伟达DGX Spark等其他硬件——这款紧凑型AI台式机于去年底发布，外形与Mac mini相似。
