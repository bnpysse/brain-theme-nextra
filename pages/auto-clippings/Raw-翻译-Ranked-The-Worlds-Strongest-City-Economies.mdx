How the World’s Largest City Economies Could Change by 2050
2050年全球最大城市经济体格局变迁

Key Takeaways
核心发现

New York is projected to remain the world’s largest city economy in 2050, with GDP reaching $6.4 trillion.
纽约预计将在2050年保持全球最大城市经济体地位，GDP达6.4万亿美元

Shanghai is projected to climb from 12th place in 2025 to third by 2050.
上海预计将从2025年的第12位跃升至2050年的第三位

Three Chinese cities are projected to rank among the world’s 10 largest city economies by 2050, compared with none in 2025.
到2050年，中国预计将有三座城市跻身全球前十大城市经济体（2025年为零）

The world’s largest cities have economies that rival those of entire countries.
全球顶级城市的经济规模已堪比中等国家

By 2050, several are projected to generate more than $3 trillion in annual GDP as Asian megacities climb the global rankings.
随着亚洲超级城市崛起，2050年多个城市年GDP将突破3万亿美元

Together, New York, Tokyo, and Los Angeles generated roughly $5.5 trillion in GDP, putting their combined economic output on the scale of the world’s largest national economies.
纽约、东京和洛杉矶GDP总和约5.5万亿美元，相当于大型国家经济体量

By 2050, the scale shifts dramatically: every city in the projected top 12 is expected to exceed $2 trillion in annual GDP.
到2050年，前12名城市GDP均将突破2万亿美元

Asian Megacities Climb the Global Rankings
亚洲超级城市排名跃升

By 2050, China is projected to have three cities in the global top 10, compared with none in 2025. U.S. cities remain prominent, but some of the largest GDP gains are expected in Asia.
2050年中国将有三城进入全球前十（2025年为零）。美国城市仍占主导，但亚洲将实现最大增幅

Shanghai sees the biggest ranking jump among the cities shown. Its economy is projected to grow more than fourfold, from about $780 billion to $3.3 trillion, putting it just behind Los Angeles and narrowly ahead of Tokyo.
上海实现最大排名跃升，经济规模预计增长超四倍（7800亿→3.3万亿），仅次于洛杉矶并小幅领先东京

Beijing and Shenzhen also enter the top 10, ranking sixth and ninth, respectively. Meanwhile, Paris falls from fifth to 12th, while Houston and Washington, D.C., drop out of the top 12.
北京（第六）和深圳（第九）新晋前十，巴黎从第五降至第十二，休斯顿和华盛顿特区跌出前十二

Shanghai’s rise as a global financial hub has been decades in the making. Its GDP is expected to surpass San Francisco’s as early as 2027, even though its economy was one-quarter the size of San Francisco’s in 2000.
上海作为全球金融中心的崛起历经数十年沉淀。其GDP预计最早在2027年超越旧金山（2000年经济规模仅为旧金山1/4）

Still, the gap at the very top widens. New York is projected to reach $6.4 trillion in GDP by 2050, nearly $2.8 trillion more than second-place Los Angeles.
但头部差距持续扩大：纽约2050年GDP预计较第二名洛杉矶高出2.8万亿美元
