【文章标题】：US federal budget deficit jumps 12% in one year to nearly $2 Trillion

【文章标题】：美国联邦预算赤字一年内激增12%，逼近2万亿美元

【文章正文】：

The US federal budget deficit for 2026 officially came in Friday at $1.992 trillion, a final tabulation to close out the government's fiscal year and another reminder of Washington's spiraling reliance on red ink.

【文章正文】：

美国2026财年联邦预算赤字于上周五正式出炉，达到1.992万亿美元。这是美国政府财政年度的最终结算数据，也再次提醒人们华盛顿对赤字支出的依赖正在不断加剧。

This deficit number represents about 6% of the US gross domestic product and was a result of $5.403 trillion in total revenues for the fiscal year against total spending of $7.396 trillion.

这一赤字数字约占美国国内生产总值（GDP）的6%，其成因是本财年5.403万亿美元的总收入对7.396万亿美元的总支出。

The 2026 reading is a 12% jump from 2025's $1.775 trillion deficit.

2026财年的赤字读数较2025财年的1.775万亿美元赤字跃升了12%。

These final numbers came in the Treasury Department's Monthly Statement for September, which covers the final month in the US government's fiscal calendar.

这些最终数据来自美国财政部发布的9月份月度报告，该报告涵盖美国政府财政日历上的最后一个月。

The red ink has led to dire warnings from economists that the US now routinely runs annual deficits that were previously seen only during recessions and wars. The gross US national debt passed $40 trillion in August and now sits above $40.3 trillion.

持续的赤字引发了经济学家的严厉警告，指出美国如今常规性地出现年度赤字，而以往这种赤字水平只在经济衰退和战争时期才会出现。美国国债总额已在8月份突破40万亿美元，目前超过40.3万亿美元。

The latest "Debt to the Penny" reading from the Treasury Department came this past Wednesday, putting the gross US national debt at $40,329,229,827,541.02.

美国财政部上周三发布了最新的“精确到美分的债务”数据，显示美国国债总额为40,329,229,827,541.02美元。

The independent budget hawks at the Committee for a Responsible Budget noted in a recent analysis that "the country's future fiscal outlook is even more dismal," noting that annual deficits could reach $3.1 trillion by 2036 without a change in the fiscal trajectory.

独立预算鹰派组织“负责任联邦预算委员会”在最近的一份分析中指出，“美国未来的财政前景更加黯淡”，并警告称，如果财政轨迹不改变，到2036年年度赤字可能达到3.1万亿美元。

The group noted that annual interest costs on the debt are now $1.1 trillion, a record 3.4% of GDP, making it the second-largest line item in the government's budget. Interest on the debt still costs less than Social Security, but it is now more expensive than either Medicare or the defense budget.

该组织指出，目前债务的年利息支出已达1.1万亿美元，占GDP的3.4%，创下历史纪录，使其成为政府预算中第二大支出项目。债务利息支出仍低于社会保障支出，但目前已高于联邦医疗保险或国防预算。

Members of President Trump's team have tried to downplay this year's tabulation — the highest annual deficit reading other than 2020 and 2021, when budgets were inflated by COVID-era spending — as a blip and insist that the US fiscal situation is getting under control.

特朗普总统团队的成员试图将今年的结算数据——这是除2020年和2021年（当时预算因新冠疫情时期的支出而膨胀）之外最高的年度赤字读数——淡化为一个暂时现象，并坚称美国的财政状况正在得到控制。

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has focused on the Supreme Court-ordered tariff refunds that have turned a revenue stream into a cost center for the government in recent months.

财政部长斯科特·贝森特则将重点放在最高法院下令的关税退款上，这些退款在近几个月将一项收入来源变成了政府的成本中心。

Indeed, the impact of tariff refunds has been significant: $144.6 billion in tariff refunds were given out over the past year — but analyses of the deficit also show a variety of factors that have driven up deficits.

事实上，关税退款的影响相当显著：过去一年共发放了1446亿美元的关税退款——但对赤字的分析也显示，推高赤字的是多种因素。

A Hamilton Project tracker of government expenditures shows how government spending has increased at a steady pace in 2026. The tracker looks at spending by calendar year and shows that as of Oct. 14, government spending has jumped each of the last three years.

汉密尔顿项目的政府支出追踪数据显示，2026年政府支出一直在稳步增长。该追踪器按日历年统计支出，显示截至10月14日，政府支出在过去三年中每年都在攀升。
