How U.S. Wealth Breaks Down by Generation  
美国财富的世代分布解析  

Key Takeaways  
核心发现  

Baby Boomers hold $97.4 trillion, or 52% of U.S. household net worth, despite making up just 30% of households.  
婴儿潮一代（1946-1964年出生）仅占全美家庭总数的30%，却持有97.4万亿美元财富，占美国家庭净资产的52%。  

Millennials and Gen Z account for 37% of U.S. households, more than any other generation, but hold only 11% of household wealth.  
千禧一代（含Z世代）占全美家庭总数的37%，比例居各世代之首，但仅掌握11%的家庭财富。  

American households are worth a combined $185.7 trillion, and more than half of that belongs to a single generation.  
美国家庭总净资产达185.7万亿美元，其中超半数集中于单一世代。  

This graphic compares each generation’s share of U.S. households with its share of household net worth as of Q2 2026, using data from the Federal Reserve’s Distributional Financial Accounts.  
本图表基于美联储《分配性金融账户》数据，对比2026年第二季度各世代在美国家庭数量与净资产占比。  

Net worth is assets such as homes, stocks, and pensions minus debts like mortgages and credit cards. The Fed assigns each household to a generation based on the birth year of its reference person, typically the head of household, and counts everyone born in 1981 or later as a Millennial, so Gen Z is included in that group.  
净资产指房产、股票、养老金等资产减去房贷、信用卡等负债。美联储以户主出生年份划分世代，将1981年及之后出生者统归为千禧一代（含Z世代）。  

Why Baby Boomers Hold So Much U.S. Wealth  
婴儿潮世代财富优势解析  

Decades of accumulating stocks, homes, and pension savings have left Baby Boomers, born between 1946 and 1964, with the largest share of U.S. household wealth.  
通过数十年的股票投资、房产购置与养老金积累，1946-1964年出生的婴儿潮一代坐拥全美最大份额家庭财富。  

Generation X is the only generation whose share of wealth closely matches its share of households, at 26% for each, with $47.7 trillion in net worth.  
X世代（1965-1980年出生）是唯一财富占比（26%）与家庭数量占比（26%）持平的群体，净资产达47.7万亿美元。

The Silent Generation and older now head just 7% of households, but those households hold 11% of U.S. household wealth, averaging about $2.2 million in net worth each.  
沉默一代（1946年前出生）仅主导7%的家庭，但这些家庭持有11%的全美财富，户均净资产约220万美元。  

Stock ownership helps set Boomers’ wealth apart. They hold $35.2 trillion in stocks and mutual funds, or 55% of the household total. Their net worth rose by $7.9 trillion in Q2 2026 alone, with $5.5 trillion of that increase coming from equities. That quarterly equity gain alone was equal to more than a quarter of all Millennial and Gen Z wealth.  
股票投资是婴儿潮世代财富核心——其持有的股票与共同基金达35.2万亿美元（占全美家庭总量的55%）。仅2026年第二季度，该群体净资产增长7.9万亿美元（其中5.5万亿来自股票增值），单季股票收益就超千禧一代与Z世代总财富的四分之一。  

Millennial Wealth Is Growing, but Boomers Are Adding More  
千禧世代财富增长vs婴儿潮世代增量优势  

Millennials and Gen Z have grown from 4.9% of U.S. household net worth in Q2 2020 to 11.2% today, as their wealth nearly quadrupled from $5.3 trillion to $20.8 trillion.  
千禧一代与Z世代财富占比从2020年Q2的4.9%升至当前的11.2%，净资产从5.3万亿激增至20.8万亿美元（增长近4倍）。  

Even so, Boomer net worth rose by $38.2 trillion over the same six years, compared with $15.5 trillion for Millennials and Gen Z, who make up the largest generational group in America. A smaller percentage gain on a much larger base can still add more dollars.  
同期婴儿潮世代净资产增长38.2万亿美元，远超千禧世代15.5万亿的增量——尽管后者已是美国最大人口群体。基数优势使得较小百分比增长仍能创造更大绝对价值。

The Silent Generation held 80% of household wealth in 1990 and holds 11% today. The Boomer share peaked at 55.5% in 2014 and has slipped only three percentage points since, even as Boomers’ share of households fell from 36% to 30%.  
沉默一代财富占比从1990年的80%降至当前的11%。婴儿潮世代占比在2014年达峰值55.5%后仅回落3个百分点，同期其家庭数量占比从36%降至30%。  

Millennial balance sheets also look very different. Real estate makes up 38% of their assets, compared with 20% for Boomers, and they owe $8.6 trillion, or 43% of all household debt. That is more than Boomers and the Silent Generation owe combined, and equal to 29% of Millennial assets versus 4% for Boomers.  
千禧世代资产结构显著不同：房产占其资产的38%（婴儿潮世代为20%），但负债达8.6万亿美元（占全美家庭债务43%），超过前两世代负债总和，债务资产比达29%（婴儿潮世代仅4%）。  

The generational wealth picture will continue to change as Boomers age and assets are transferred to younger generations. How quickly that reshapes the chart will depend not only on inheritances, but also on asset prices, debt, homeownership, and how wealth is distributed within each generation.  
随着婴儿潮世代老龄化与资产传承，财富格局将持续演变。其变化速度不仅取决于遗产继承，还与资产价格、负债水平、住房自有率及各世代内部财富分配密切相关。
