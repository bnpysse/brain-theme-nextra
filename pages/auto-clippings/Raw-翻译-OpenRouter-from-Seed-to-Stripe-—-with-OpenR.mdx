【文章正文】：  
From the earliest days of open-weight models to becoming the neutral routing layer for more than 10 million developers, OpenRouter is one of the clearest bets that the future of AI will be multi-model.  
从开源权重模型的早期阶段，到成为超1000万开发者的中立路由层，OpenRouter最清晰地印证了AI未来必将走向多模型共存的趋势。  

In this episode, OpenRouter co-founder & CEO Alex Atallah, with AMP’s Anjney Midha returning with swyx to unpack how OpenRouter emerged from the first wave of Llama, Alpaca, Mistral, and Midjourney...  
本期节目中，OpenRouter联合创始人兼CEO Alex Atallah与AMP创始人Anjney Midha重返对话，与swyx共同揭秘OpenRouter如何从Llama、Alpaca、Mistral和Midjourney的第一波浪潮中崛起...  

We go deep on the product and distribution lessons behind OpenRouter: why model labs can spend billions training a checkpoint and still struggle to get it into developers’ hands...  
我们深入探讨OpenRouter背后的产品与分发经验：为何模型实验室投入数十亿训练检查点，却仍难触达开发者手中...  

Finally, Anjney explains why Stripe and OpenRouter fit together, why token fraud may become one of the defining security problems of the AI economy...  
最后，Anjney阐释Stripe与OpenRouter的契合逻辑，为何代币欺诈可能成为AI经济的关键安全问题...
