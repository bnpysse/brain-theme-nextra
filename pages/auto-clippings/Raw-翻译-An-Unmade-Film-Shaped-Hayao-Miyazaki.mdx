1. Transforming

In 1980, Hayao Miyazaki turned 39 years old. He was just shy of middle age, but he’d made almost none of the work associated with his name today.

1. 转变

1980年，宫崎骏年满39岁。他刚步入中年，但如今与他名字紧密相关的那些作品，他几乎一部都还没做出来。

He felt kind of washed up. Miyazaki’s debut feature The Castle of Cagliostro (1979) had flopped at the box office, and he viewed it as a rehash of his past projects. “I don’t think I added anything new,” he admitted. He saw his creations as stuck.1

他感觉自己有点江郎才尽。宫崎骏的处女作长篇《鲁邦三世：卡里奥斯特罗城》（1979）票房惨败，而他认为那不过是自己过去作品的翻版。“我不觉得自己添加了任何新东西，”他承认。他视自己的创作为陷入停滞。1

“With every piece I made it was obvious that I was just trotting out everything I had done before [laughs],” he said. “Nineteen eighty was my year of being mired in gloom.”

“我做的每一部作品，都明显只是在重复以前做过的一切[笑]，”他说，“一九八零年是我深陷阴郁的一年。”

He’d built a style and a set of reference points. Miyazaki excelled at children’s cartoons with big emotions, zany action, slapstick, good guys and bad guys. Among his influences were The Shepherdess and the Chimney Sweep and the Soviet Snow Queen from the ‘50s, and Fleischer stuff like Popeye and Mr. Bug Goes to Town.

他已经建立起一种风格和一套参照体系。宫崎骏擅长制作情感浓烈、动作滑稽、充满打闹喜剧、善恶分明的儿童动画。影响他的作品包括《牧羊女与扫烟囱的人》、50年代苏联版《雪女王》，以及弗莱舍工作室的《大力水手》和《虫先生进城》等。

But he started to believe that his approach was too simplistic. “I wondered if it was no longer possible to make an action movie where defeating the villain was enough to make a happy ending,” he said. The times had changed: heroism and villainy seemed like murkier concepts than they once were.2

但他开始觉得自己的手法过于简单。“我想知道，是否再也不可能拍一部动作片，只要打败反派就能迎来大团圆结局了，”他说。时代变了：英雄与邪恶的概念似乎不再像过去那样清晰。2

As Miyazaki searched for other possibilities, he got into alt comics in the orbit of Métal Hurlant, a foreign magazine. That included Arzach by Mœbius. “I first saw it around 1980. It had a great impact on me,” he said.3 Meanwhile, an American comic from the same circle lit up his imagination. It was called Rowlf (1971).

当宫崎骏寻找其他可能性时，他迷上了外国杂志《金属咆哮》圈子里的另类漫画，其中包括墨比斯的《阿扎克》。“我大约在1980年第一次看到它。它对我影响巨大，”他说。3 与此同时，同一圈子里的美国漫画点燃了他的想象力。那部漫画叫《罗尔夫》（1971）。

“I chanced upon Rowlf around the time I was wondering what to do for my next work after I had completed Lupin the Third: The Castle of Cagliostro,” he wrote recently. He was so impressed that he proposed, in 1980, to adapt the story as his second feature film. That decision would change his career.4

“大约在完成《鲁邦三世：卡里奥斯特罗城》之后，我正在思考下一部作品该做什么，就在这时偶然遇到了《罗尔夫》，”他最近写道。他深受震撼，以至于在1980年提议将这个故事改编为他的第二部长片。这个决定改变了他的职业生涯。4

In many ways, Rowlf was the opposite of Miyazaki’s past loves. Author Richard Corben came from America’s underground comix scene. In their content, his violent, transgressive stories weren’t far off the exploitation films of their era.

从很多方面来看，《罗尔夫》与宫崎骏过去的喜好截然相反。作者理查德·科尔本出身于美国地下漫画圈。就内容而言，他那些暴力、越界的故事与那个时代的剥削电影相差无几。

Miyazaki wrote in 1980 that Rowlf “definitely is not for little children.” But he detected potential here:

In Rowlf, a world that appears at first to be grotesque and ironic is actually filled with faith in a healthy life. The story possesses the power of old fairy tales — the ability to encourage people, to reassure us and give us hope. It’s no exaggeration to say that it is rare to see such a work today.

宫崎骏在1980年写道，《罗尔夫》“绝对不适合小孩子。”但他从中发现了潜力：

在《罗尔夫》中，一个乍看荒诞而讽刺的世界，实际上充满了对健康生活的信念。这个故事拥有古老童话的力量——能够鼓舞人心、安抚我们、给予我们希望。毫不夸张地说，如今这样的作品实属罕见。

Rowlf unfolds in a mixed-up world. In Corben’s description, “We have fantasy elements like the castles and medieval costumes and sorcerers, and then they clash with these mutants with their sort of ‘Panzer’ tanks.” He thought it up in the ‘60s as a film (he was an amateur animator) before he retooled it as a comic.5

《罗尔夫》展开于一个混杂的世界。据科尔本描述，“我们有城堡、中世纪服装和巫师之类的奇幻元素，然后它们又与这些开着某种‘装甲’坦克的变种人发生冲突。”他在60年代最初构思为一部电影（当时他是业余动画师），后来才改造成漫画。5

Corben took from his childhood dog for the Rowlf character — the pet of Maryara, a princess. When she’s kidnapped, Rowlf turns into a kind of werewolf and goes on a rampage to save her. The narrative is basic, and its handling of its damsel in distress is flat and often in poor taste. But Corben had a knack for telling stories in images: Rowlf’s panels have a fleshy physicality and strong feeling of space. The Academy of Comic Book Arts named him the year’s top new talent.6

科尔本以他童年时的狗为原型塑造了罗尔夫这个角色——它是公主玛丽亚拉的宠物。当她被绑架时，罗尔夫变成一种狼人，横冲直撞去救她。叙事很基础，对落难少女的处理也平淡且常常品味不佳。但科尔本擅长用图像讲故事：《罗尔夫》的画面具有肉感的实体感和强烈的空间感。漫画艺术学院将他评为年度最佳新人。6

This edgy, 20th-century Beauty and the Beast story appealed to Miyazaki. Among its other qualities, he noted its “[d]ramatic staging, including the desert (upon which Rowlf’s tank casts long shadows).” But he found it “boring” to use Maryara as she was: “I wanted her to be more active in the story’s progression.”

这个前卫的、20世纪版《美女与野兽》故事吸引了宫崎骏。除了其他特质，他注意到其“戏剧性的场面调度，包括沙漠（罗尔夫的坦克在沙漠上投下长长的影子）。”但他觉得照原样使用玛丽亚拉“很无聊”：“我希望她在故事推进中更加主动。”

In the concept sketches that Miyazaki and the artist Yoshifumi Kondo did for Rowlf, Maryara appears as a bolder character, sometimes holding weapons — one author mentioned the “nobility and inner strength” in these drawings. Along the way, Miyazaki added flying machines and other oddities from Mœbius.7

在宫崎骏与画家近藤喜文为《罗尔夫》绘制的概念草图中，玛丽亚拉以更大胆的形象出现，有时手持武器——一位作者提到这些画作中的“高贵与内在力量”。在此过程中，宫崎骏还加入了飞行器和其他来自墨比斯的奇异元素。7

Although he was changing Rowlf, Miyazaki wasn’t turning it into the same type of story he’d done before. As the project evolved, he was evolving, too. Like he said:

While I was filling out Rowlf in this fashion, I moved further and further away from the original story. And as [Maryara] began to have a stronger mind and feelings, I ended up with “a princess in her castle” and “a pathetic father.”

虽然他在改动《罗尔夫》，但宫崎骏并没有把它变成自己以前做过的那种故事。随着项目演变，他也在演变。如他所说：

当我以这种方式充实《罗尔夫》时，我离原作越来越远。随着[玛丽亚拉]开始拥有更坚强的意志和情感，我最终得到了“城堡里的公主”和“一个可怜的父亲”。

This father was a little touched — while he had once been quite heroic, now he was confined to bed and completely useless. Yet despite this, he remained a very proud man. I believed a girl with that father would make Rowlf into a much more interesting story. Suitors came one after the other, their eyes on the country’s minimal wealth. … I was trying to build in a secondary theme in which she preserved her pride while rebuffing these men. …

这个父亲有点疯癫——他曾经相当英勇，如今却卧床不起，完全无用。尽管如此，他仍然是个非常骄傲的人。我相信，有这样一个父亲的女孩，会让《罗尔夫》变成一个有趣得多的故事。求婚者接踵而至，觊觎这个国家微薄的财富。……我试图加入一个次要主题：她在拒绝这些男人的同时保持自己的骄傲。……
