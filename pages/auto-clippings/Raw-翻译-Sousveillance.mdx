Sousveillance

Sousveillance (/suːˈveɪləns/ soo-VAY-lənss) is the recording of an activity by a member of the public, rather than a person or organisation in authority, typically by way of small wearable or portable personal technologies.[14]

逆向监控（Sousveillance）是指公众成员（而非权威机构或个人）通过小型可穿戴或便携式个人技术对活动进行的记录[14]。

OED (Oxford English Dictionary) defines sousveillance as "close observation or recording of authorities like the government or police by the public using personal devices like smartphones, as well as the general recording of everyday activities", citing a 2001 date[15].

《牛津英语词典》将逆向监控定义为"公众使用智能手机等个人设备对政府或警察等权威机构进行的密切观察或记录，以及日常活动的普遍记录"，该词最早可追溯至2001年[15]。

The term, coined by Steve Mann,[16] stems from the contrasting French words sur, meaning "above", and sous, meaning "below", i.e. "surveillance" denotes the "eye in the sky" watching from above, whereas "sousveillance" denotes bringing the means of observation down to human level, either physically (by mounting cameras on people rather than on buildings) or hierarchically (with ordinary people observing, rather than by higher authorities or by architectural means).[17][18][24]

该术语由史蒂夫·曼恩创造[16]，源于法语中表示"上方"的sur与表示"下方"的sous。即"surveillance（监控）"代表"天空之眼"自上而下的监视，而"sousveillance"则将观察手段降维至人类层面——既包括物理层面（将摄像机安装在人身上而非建筑物上），也包括层级层面（由普通人而非权威机构或建筑设施进行观察）[17][18][24]。

Undersight (inverse oversight) is sousveillance at high-level, e.g. "citizen undersight" being reciprocal to a congressional oversight committee or the like.[25][26][27]

底层监督（逆向监督）是高层级的逆向监控，例如"公民底层监督"与国会监督委员会形成制衡关系[25][26][27]。

Inverse surveillance is a subset of sousveillance with an emphasis on "watchful vigilance from underneath" and a form of surveillance inquiry or legal protection involving the recording, monitoring, study, or analysis of surveillance systems, proponents of surveillance, and possibly also recordings of authority figures. Inverse surveillance is typically undertaken by those who are subjected to surveillance, so it can be thought of as a form of ethnography or ethnomethodology (i.e. an analysis of the surveilled from the perspective of a participant in a society under surveillance).[28] Sousveillance typically involves community-based recording from first person perspectives, without necessarily involving any specific political agenda, whereas inverse surveillance is a form of sousveillance that is typically directed at, or used to collect data to analyze or study, surveillance or its proponents (e.g., the actions of police or protestors at a protest rally).[29][30][31]

逆向监视是逆向监控的子集，强调"自下而上的警惕性监督"，是一种涉及记录、监测、研究或分析监控系统、监控支持者乃至权威人物的监控调查或法律保护形式。逆向监视通常由被监控者实施，因此可视为民族志或常人方法学（即从被监控社会参与者的角度分析监控现象）[28]。逆向监控通常涉及基于社区的第一人称记录，不一定具有特定政治目的；而逆向监视则特指针对监控系统及其支持者（如抗议活动中警察或示威者的行为）进行数据收集与分析的形式[29][30][31]。

Inverse surveillance

[edit]

逆向监视

[edit]

Inverse surveillance is a type of sousveillance. The more general concept of sousveillance goes beyond just inverse surveillance and the associated twentieth-century political "us versus them" framework for citizens to photograph police, shoppers to photograph shopkeepers, or passengers to photograph taxicab drivers. Howard Rheingold commented in his book Smart Mobs that this is similar to the pedestrian−driver concept, i.e. these are roles that many of us take both sides on, from time to time. Many aspects of sousveillance were examined in the general category of "reciprocal accountability" in David Brin's 1997 non-fiction book The Transparent Society, and also in Brin's novels. The first International Workshop on Inverse Surveillance, IWIS, took place in 2004,[32] chaired by Dr. Jim Gemmell, (MyLifeBits), Joi Ito, Anastasios Venetsanopoulos, and Steve Mann, among others.

逆向监视是逆向监控的一种类型。更广义的逆向监控概念超越了逆向监视及其关联的20世纪政治"我们对抗他们"框架（如市民拍摄警察、顾客拍摄店主、乘客拍摄出租车司机）。霍华德·莱茵戈德在《聪明行动族》中指出这类似于行人-司机概念，即我们许多人都会不时转换这两种角色。大卫·布林1997年非虚构作品《透明社会》及其小说中，在"相互问责"的广义范畴下探讨了逆向监控的多个方面。首届逆向监视国际研讨会（IWIS）于2004年举办[32]，由吉姆·格默尔博士（MyLifeBits）、伊藤穰一、阿纳斯塔西奥斯·韦内特萨诺普洛斯和史蒂夫·曼恩等人主持。

Some research has explored why the perpetrators of surveillance are suspicious of sousveillance.[28]

有研究探讨了监控实施者为何对逆向监控持怀疑态度[28]。

Copwatch is a network of American and Canadian volunteer organizations that "police the police." Copwatch groups usually engage in monitoring of the police, videotaping police activity, and educating the public about police misconduct. Fitwatch is a group that photograph Forward Intelligence Teams (police photographers) in the United Kingdom.[33]

Copwatch是美加志愿者组织网络，致力于"监督警察"。其团体通常开展警察监控、记录警方活动及教育公众认识警方不当行为等工作。Fitwatch则是英国拍摄警方情报小组（警察摄影师）的团体[33]。

In 2008, Cambridge researchers (in the MESSAGE project) teamed with bicycle couriers to measure and transmit air pollution indicators as they travel the city.[34]

2008年，剑桥大学研究人员（MESSAGE项目）与自行车快递员合作，在穿行城市时测量并传输空气污染指标[34]。

In 2012 the Danish daily newspaper and online title Dagbladet Information crowdmapped the positions of surveillance cameras by encouraging readers to use a free Android and iOS app to photograph and geolocate CCTV cameras.[35][better source needed]

2012年，丹麦日报《信息报》通过鼓励读者使用免费安卓/iOS应用拍摄并定位监控摄像头，众包绘制了监控摄像头分布图[35][需更好来源]。

Other types of sousveillance

[edit]

其他逆向监控类型

[edit]

"Hierarchical sousveillance" refers, for example, to citizens photographing the police, shoppers photographing shopkeepers, or taxicab passengers photographing cab drivers.[36]

"层级逆向监控"指市民拍摄警察、顾客拍摄店主或出租车乘客拍摄司机等行为[36]。

The unanticipated 2013 Russian meteor event was well documented from a dozen angles via the use of dashcam devices.[37] Similarly, in February 2015, dashcams caught valuable footage of the crash of TransAsia Airways Flight GE235.[38]

2013年俄罗斯陨石事件通过行车记录仪从多个角度被完整记录[37]。类似地，2015年2月行车记录仪拍摄到复兴航空GE235航班坠毁的珍贵画面[38]。

Alibi sousveillance

[edit]

不在场证明逆向监控

[edit]

Alibi sousveillance is a form of sousveillance activity aimed at generating an alibi as evidence to defend against allegations of wrongdoing.[39]

不在场证明逆向监控是通过生成不在场证据来抵御不当行为指控的逆向监控形式[39]。

Hasan Elahi, a University of Maryland professor, has produced a sousveillance for his entire life, after being detained at an airport because he was erroneously placed on the US terrorist watchlist. Some of his sousveillance activities include using his cell phone as a tracking device, and publicly posting debit card and other transactions that document his actions.[40]

马里兰大学教授哈桑·埃拉希在被错误列入美国恐怖分子监视名单并遭机场扣留后，开展了贯穿终身的逆向监控实践，包括将手机作为追踪设备、公开借记卡消费记录等能证明其行踪的交易数据[40]。
