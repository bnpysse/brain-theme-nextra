【文章标题】：OpenAI might have stolen another major proof
【文章标题】：OpenAI 可能窃取了又一项重大证明

【文章正文】：
BREAKING: OpenAI might have stolen another major proof. 
突发：OpenAI 可能窃取了又一项重大证明。

In a detailed Mastodon post, which I report in full in the comments, Andreas Thom presents several pieces of evidence suggesting that OpenAI may have trained Astra on conversations in which he and Gábor Kun were working on Gromov’s soficity conjecture, one of the ten problems OpenAI later announced Astra had solved.
在一条详细的 Mastodon 帖子中（我已在评论区全文转载），Andreas Thom 列出了多项证据，表明 OpenAI 可能在 Astra 的训练中使用了包含他与 Gábor Kun 研究 Gromov soficity 猜想的对话；该猜想正是 OpenAI 后来宣布 Astra 解决的十个问题之一。

I know Andreas. We met several times early in our careers. He is an exceptional mathematician, a leading expert on sofic and hyperlinear groups, and one of the most respected scholars in the field. He has spent two decades working on this problem.
我认识 Andreas。我们职业生涯早期见过几次。他是一位杰出的数学家，是 sofic 群和超线性群领域的顶尖专家，也是该领域最受尊敬的学者之一。他在这个问题上已经花了二十年时间。

If his account is correct, this is not a minor dispute over attribution. It would mean that unpublished human work was absorbed into a model and then presented to the world as a breakthrough by the model itself.
如果他的说法属实，这就不只是署名上的小争议了。这将意味着，尚未发表的人类成果被吸收进模型，然后被当作模型自身的突破展示给全世界。

And if the allegations raised by Levent Alpöge, Tristan Buckmaster, and now Andreas Thom are all substantiated, we are no longer looking at isolated incidents.
如果 Levent Alpöge、Tristan Buckmaster 以及现在的 Andreas Thom 提出的指控都得到证实，那么我们面对的就不再是孤立事件。

We may be looking at one of the greatest intellectual scandals in the history of science.
我们可能正在目睹科学史上最严重的知识丑闻之一。

AI is not discovering new mathematics.
AI 并没有在发现新的数学。

AI is stealing human discovery.
AI 正在窃取人类的发现。
