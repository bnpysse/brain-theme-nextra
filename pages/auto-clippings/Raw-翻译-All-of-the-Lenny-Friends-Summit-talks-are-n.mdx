A few reflections

几点思考

Now that we’re a few weeks out, I’ve had a chance to reflect on the day. Here are the top 5 trends that have stayed with me:

峰会结束数周后，我有机会回顾当日。以下是最令我印象深刻的5大趋势：

1. There’s a huge need for (curated) IRL events right now.

1. 当下对（精品）线下活动的需求巨大

It’s wild. I’ve rarely felt this much PMF for anything I’ve done. My sense is that the more time we spend working with agents, the lonelier the work becomes, and the more we need to find a place to connect with other humans. I heard this theme more than anything else from attendees:

这太疯狂了。我很少对自己做的事情感受到如此强烈的产品市场契合度。我的感受是：与智能体共事的时间越长，工作就越孤独，我们越需要寻找与他人联结的场所。这是我从参会者那里听到最多的主题：

“Things are certainly different now, but underneath all this technology, we’re still just ancient humans looking for connection.” —Charlie Koch

"当下一切固然不同，但在所有技术之下，我们仍是渴望联结的原始人类。" ——Charlie Koch

“I left filled with gratitude for the experience, the conversations, and the reminder that even in a role that can sometimes feel lonely, we don’t have to figure everything out alone.” —Hemanth Babu Shekar

"我满怀感激地离开，感谢这段经历、这些对话，它们提醒我：即使在有时感觉孤独的岗位上，我们也不必独自解决所有问题。" ——Hemanth Babu Shekar

“I left feeling genuinely sparked by what’s possible, and also a little less alone in figuring out what comes next.” —An attendee

"离开时我因各种可能性而真正受到启发，也对探索未来感到不那么孤独。" ——某参会者

“I left this conference having fallen in love with product all over again.” —Jenny Zhao

"离开这场会议时，我重新爱上了产品工作。" ——Jenny Zhao

2. Nobody has it figured out.

2. 无人掌握全部答案

Should we be building software factories? Are roadmaps over? Should PMs be shipping to production?

我们该建立软件工厂吗？路线图过时了吗？产品经理该直接发布产品吗？

We had speakers take both sides of these debates throughout the day. But what everyone agreed on is that there’s no one way to do it. My takeaway is we’re all still figuring it out, and the best way to do that is together. We heard this theme from both speakers and attendees:

全天都有讲者持不同观点辩论。但所有人一致认同：没有唯一正确方法。我的收获是：我们仍在探索中，而最佳方式是共同探索。我们从讲者和参会者那里都听到这个主题：

“The answer that we have come to is: it depends. It depends what type of product it is. It depends on what phase in the product it is.” —Tamar Yehoshua, Atlassian

"我们得出的答案是：视情况而定。取决于产品类型，也取决于产品所处阶段。" ——Atlassian的Tamar Yehoshua

“We don’t know the answer. We don’t think anyone knows the answer. We’re all going to discover it. And we’re all fundamentally on the same team.” —Ami Vora, Anthropic

"我们不知道答案。我们认为没人知道答案。我们都将共同发现它。我们本质上都在同个团队。" ——Anthropic的Ami Vora

“This is just one way. There’s so many ways to build products. You probably heard 10 other ones today that were just as valid as this one. You should decide on what makes sense for you.” —Robby Stein, Google Search

"这只是其中一种方式。打造产品的方法多种多样。你今天可能听到另外10种同样有效的方法。你应该选择最适合自己的。" ——谷歌搜索的Robby Stein

“The best practices have not been written yet. We get to do that.” —Katie Dill, Stripe

"最佳实践尚未书写，而我们将共同创造。" ——Stripe的Katie Dill

3. It’s never been easier to build something nobody wants.

3. 打造无人需要的东西从未如此简单

As building gets easier, choosing the right thing to build, knowing what good looks like, and caring enough to fix it are becoming increasingly important for successful product teams. Almost every speaker touched on this theme:

随着构建变得更简单，对成功产品团队而言，选择正确方向、识别优秀标准、以及用心改进正变得愈发重要。几乎每位讲者都提及这个主题：

“And now we can ship all our bad ideas, like congratulations to us.” —Claire Vo, How I AI

"现在我们可以发布所有糟糕想法了，真是可喜可贺。" ——How I AI的Claire Vo

“If almost anything you can think of can get built, it’s more clear to me than ever that the actual true value of PM is around judging. It’s around taste. It’s around doing something extremely well.” —Robby Stein, Google Search

"如果几乎所有构想都能实现，那么我比以往更清楚：产品经理的真正价值在于判断力，在于品味，在于将事情做到极致。" ——谷歌搜索的Robby Stein

“They’re not impressed if we animated something with Three.js and Blender in 30 minutes. They are impressed by us solving their problems and clever touches in the details that show we anticipated their needs.” —Katie Dill, Stripe

"用户不会因我们用Three.js和Blender在30分钟内做出动画而惊叹。他们会因我们解决问题、以及展现需求预判的细节巧思而印象深刻。" ——Stripe的Katie Dill

“The output is not the product. Customers are not buying the lines of code.” —Karri Saarinen, Linear

"产出物不是产品。客户购买的不是代码行数。" ——Linear的Karri Saarinen

“Is this useful or is it just new? It’s a big thing you need to differentiate.” —Dan Shipper, Every

"这究竟是有用还是仅算新奇？这是你需要区分的重要维度。" ——Every的Dan Shipper

4. The PM job is expanding, not disappearing.

4. 产品经理岗位正在扩展而非消失

Instead of the PM, eng, and design circles of the Venn diagram collapsing into one "builder," each role is instead evolving and expanding.

维恩图中产品经理、工程师和设计的圆圈并未融合成单一的"构建者"，而是每个角色都在进化扩展。
