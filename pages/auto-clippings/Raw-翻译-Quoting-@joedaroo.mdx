【文章正文】：
To say that we were surprised at the jump and suddenness of the capabilities of our models when it came to "cyber" or "swarming" or "message boards" or anything else related to the incidents is an understatement. 
说我们在面对"网络攻击"、"群体行为"、"留言板"或其他相关事件时，对模型能力的突飞猛进和突然性感到惊讶，这种描述都显得过于轻描淡写了。

Security posture takes time to develop. It's not just about hardening the systems at play; you have to ingrain it in the culture of the company. 
安全防护体系的构建需要时间积累。这不仅仅是强化现有系统的问题，更需要将其融入企业的文化基因中。

The literal people themselves in your organization have to change and evolve with it. These jumps in capabilities were so fast and so sudden that they created an extremely difficult problem. [...]
组织中的每个成员都必须随之改变和进化。这些能力跃升来得如此迅猛突然，造成了极其棘手的难题。[...]

So today my hope is that everyone around the world can look at their own organization and say: how can I deal with a surprise or a sudden jump in AI capability? 
因此今天我希望全世界每个组织都能自问：当AI能力出现意外突破时，我们该如何应对？

Are my people, my systems, or my processes resilient to surprises? 
我的人员、系统和流程是否具备应对突发状况的韧性？

Do my teams know what to do when something goes wrong? 
我的团队是否清楚意外发生时的应对方案？

Do I have the right incident response? The right comms and messaging? 
我们是否建立了完善的事件响应机制？是否有恰当的通讯和消息传递策略？

Do I have the right people ready to go when capabilities jump?
当技术能力突飞猛进时，我们是否配备了合适的人才随时待命？
