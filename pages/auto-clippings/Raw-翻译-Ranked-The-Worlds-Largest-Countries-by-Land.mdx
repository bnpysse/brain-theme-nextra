Ranked: The World’s Largest Countries by Land Area
全球陆地面积最大国家排名

Key Takeaways
核心发现

The five largest countries, Russia, China, the U.S., Canada, and Brazil, account for about 40% of the world’s land area.
俄罗斯、中国、美国、加拿大和巴西这五个最大国家约占世界陆地总面积的40%。

The U.S. and Canada together cover more land than all of South America combined.
美国和加拿大的陆地面积总和超过整个南美洲。

Africa is home to 21 of the world’s 50 largest countries by land area, more than any other region.
在全球50个陆地面积最大国家中，非洲占据21席，数量超过其他任何地区。

A handful of countries occupy a remarkable share of the planet’s land. Russia alone accounts for 12.6% of the global total, while China and the U.S. each account for roughly 7%.
少数国家占据了地球陆地的重要部分。仅俄罗斯就占全球陆地总面积的12.6%，而中国和美国各占约7%。

Some of the rankings may be surprising. The U.S. narrowly surpasses Canada when inland water is excluded, while Australia has more than twice the land area of India.
部分排名可能令人意外。若不计内陆水域，美国以微弱优势超过加拿大，而澳大利亚的陆地面积是印度的两倍多。

Using World Bank data, this graphic ranks the world’s 50 largest countries by land area, excluding inland water bodies.
本图表采用世界银行数据，排除了内陆水域后对全球50个陆地面积最大国家进行排名。

The Largest Countries Pull Away From the Rest
大国与其他国家的差距

The gap between the largest countries and the rest is steep. Australia, ranked sixth, is nearly three times larger than India in seventh place.
最大国家与其他国家的差距显著。排名第六的澳大利亚面积几乎是第七名印度的三倍。

Taken together, the five largest countries occupy more land than the next 27 countries combined.
前五大国家的陆地面积总和超过了其后27个国家的总和。
