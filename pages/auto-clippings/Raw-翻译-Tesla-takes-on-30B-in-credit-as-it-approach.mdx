【文章标题】：Tesla takes on $30B in credit as it approaches unprofitability

【文章标题】：特斯拉背负300亿美元信贷逼近亏损边缘

【文章正文】：

Tesla opened $30 billion in lines of credit according to a regulatory filing today. The loans come as company profits have dropped in recent years, and as Tesla guides for more spending in upcoming quarters.

根据今日监管文件披露，特斯拉已开通300亿美元信贷额度。此举正值公司近年利润持续下滑，且特斯拉预告未来季度将加大支出。

For its first decade and a half of operation, Tesla sales and revenue basically did nothing but increase. The company grew and grew, at a rapid rate, and justified basically any money spent on chasing more growth with what seemed like near-infinite demand as it scaled.

在运营的前十五年里，特斯拉的销售额与营收几乎只增不减。公司以惊人速度持续扩张，凭借看似无限的需求规模，任何用于追求增长的投入都显得合理。

That lasted until 2024, when Tesla went from 38% growth the prior year to a 1% drop in 2024.

这种态势持续至2024年，特斯拉增速从前一年的38%骤降至2024年的1%负增长。

Since then, profitability has been rough for the company, with it having to book some questionable one-time profits in order to eke out profitability. Even with that, profits have remained low.

此后公司盈利状况持续恶化，甚至需通过计入存疑的一次性收益来勉强维持盈利。即便如此，利润水平仍处低位。

One downward pressure on profits has been Tesla’s increased amount of capital expenditures as it chases various pie-in-the-sky ideas like cars without steering wheels, cars that don’t gain any performance benefit from flying, and the promise of one man controlling a trillion-dollar robot army.

利润承压部分源于激增的资本支出——特斯拉正追逐诸多天马行空的概念，包括无方向盘汽车、毫无飞行性能增益的"飞行汽车"，以及单人操控万亿美元机器人军团的承诺。

CapEx more than doubled in the last quarter, and Tesla says that it will remain high in the current environment of extremely high spending by tech companies. The company expects to spend a total of $25 billion in 2026 (up from $8.5 billion in 2025), and analysts expect similar CapEx in 2027.

上季度资本支出翻倍有余，特斯拉表示在科技企业普遍高投入的现状下，这项支出将维持高位。公司预计2026年总支出将达250亿美元（2025年为85亿美元），分析师预测2027年支出规模相当。

So, given falling profits and increasing capital expenditures, Tesla is now taking out a big loan to fill the gap.

因此，面对利润下滑与资本支出攀升的双重压力，特斯拉正通过大额贷款填补资金缺口。

Today’s regulatory filing shows that Tesla has taken out a total of $30 billion in loan facilities from Citi and Wells Fargo. The loans come in terms between one and five years, and replace a previous $5 billion credit line which Tesla had previously filed for (but had no current debt from).

监管文件显示，特斯拉已从花旗银行和富国银行获得总计300亿美元的贷款额度，期限1至5年不等，取代了此前申请的50亿美元信贷额度（但未实际使用）。

The company says it doesn’t expect to have to draw from the credit line in 2026, but the end of 2026 is only three months away, and a lot of spending is planned for next year.

公司声称预计2026年无需动用信贷额度，但2026年底距今仅剩三个月，而明年已规划大量支出。

The loan amounts for quite a lot of cash, equivalent to about a quarter worth of Tesla’s revenues at current revenue levels. But compared to Tesla’s profits, which have been razor-thin in the few hundreds of millions in the last few quarters, it’s a much bigger chunk of money.

这笔贷款数额巨大，相当于特斯拉当前年营收的四分之一。但与最近几个季度仅数亿美元的微薄利润相比，这笔资金规模尤为可观。

Most worryingly, last quarter, Tesla was cash flow negative for the first time since Q1 2024, when it experienced a massive drop in sales. Prior to that, Tesla had been cash flow positive for years running.

最令人担忧的是，上季度特斯拉自2024年第一季度销量暴跌以来首次出现负现金流，而此前多年一直保持正向现金流。
