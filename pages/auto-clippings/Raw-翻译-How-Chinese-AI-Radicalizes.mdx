【文章标题】：How Chinese AI Radicalizes  
【文章标题】：中国AI如何走向激进  

【文章正文】：  
A DeepSeek researcher’s personal blog post recently went viral.  
深度求索（DeepSeek）一名研究员的个人博客文章近日引发热议。  

On September 13, after DeepSeek V4.1-Flash was released, researcher Liu Shengyu 刘胜与 reflected on how humans might live alongside AI systems with superhuman abilities:  
9月13日，在深度求索V4.1-Flash发布后，研究员刘胜与（音）对人类如何与具备超能力的AI系统共存进行了思考：  

As AI develops, future society may trend toward one of two extremes: communism, or Cyberpunk 2077.  
随着AI发展，未来社会可能走向两个极端：共产主义，或《赛博朋克2077》。  

In the former, the productive forces are enormously liberated and people’s standards of living improve markedly (let’s stop here; I’m worried this won’t clear the censors otherwise).  
前者将极大解放生产力并显著提升生活水平（就此打住，否则恐怕通不过审查）。  

In the latter, a few tech companies control the bulk of resources; only a tiny minority can access the most advanced AI and assorted technologies, achieving something close to “machine ascension,” while everyone else makes do with feeble AI.  
后者则是少数科技公司掌控大部分资源，仅极少数人能接触最先进的AI技术实现"机械飞升"，而其他人只能使用劣质AI。  

Liu specializes in kernels and is passionate about the craft.  
刘胜与专攻内核技术并对此充满热忱。  

He remarks with frank resignation that, “give it another six months or a year, and AI-written kernels will most likely be just as good as mine, if not surpass my work.”  
他坦率而无奈地表示："再过半年或一年，AI编写的内核很可能达到甚至超越我的水平。"  

So why does he keep working on this? Simply put: the race dynamic.  
为何仍坚持研究？简言之：竞赛机制使然。  

If I were to slack off at this point, or even deliberately throw sand in the gears to hold up model training, other companies’ models would develop all the same and would kill off my career in the exact same manner in the end.  
"若此刻懈怠甚至故意阻碍模型训练，其他公司的模型仍会发展，最终以同样方式终结我的职业生涯。"  

... When everyone is this fixated on destroying themselves, I have no choice but to join the cruel arms race, too.  
"当所有人都沉迷于自我毁灭时，我也不得不加入这场残酷的军备竞赛。"  

Liu deeply mourns losing his edge over AI on the kernel optimization work that he’s spent years perfecting.  
刘对自己花费多年打磨的内核优化技术即将被AI超越深感悲哀。  

He says that in the near future, he expects to have to pivot to working as a kind of “mecha pilot” 机甲驾驶员 for agents in order to remain employed.  
他表示近期可能需转型为智能体的"机甲驾驶员"来维持职业价值。  

In the meantime, he has a shockingly forthright warning for everyone else:  
同时他对公众发出惊人直白的警告：  

If Anthropic controlled the world’s most advanced AI forever, care to guess whether future society would turn out communist or 2077? Go on, guess.  
"若Anthropic永远掌控最先进AI，猜猜未来是共产主义还是2077？尽管猜。"  

So I still believe that the most frontier intelligence should be supplied to everyone, in an open and cheap way.  
"因此我坚持前沿智能应以开放廉价的方式普及。"  

I don’t trust Anthropic or OpenAI to do that. I especially don’t want Anthropic controlling the most advanced artificial intelligence or AGI.  
"我不相信Anthropic或OpenAI会这么做，更不愿见Anthropic掌控最先进的AGI。"  

To put it dramatically, the severity of that would be no less than letting Hitler get the atomic bomb before the Allies.  
"夸张地说，其严重性不亚于让希特勒早于盟军获得原子弹。"  

This is also why I chose DeepSeek and have stayed: we research powerful, fast, universally accessible AI and open-source it, and perhaps that can pull the world back a little from the 2077 end.  
"这正是我选择并留在深度求索的原因：我们研发强大、快速、普惠的AI并开源，或能将世界从2077结局拉回些许。"  

It’s obviously abhorrent to liken anyone not guilty of crimes against humanity — let alone someone with a Jewish mother — to Adolf Hitler.  
将未犯反人类罪者（尤其是有犹太血统者）比作希特勒显然令人不适。  

Liu, however, probably sees his comparison as a fair rebuke, given Amodei’s maximalist accusations against the Chinese system.  
但鉴于Amodei对中国制度的极端指控，刘可能认为这个比喻是合理反击。  

As of September 18, his article remains available on his WeChat blog and has received sympathetic coverage in prominent Chinese media outlets like Huxiu, 36Kr, and state-owned Yicai.  
截至9月18日，该文仍在其微信公众号可见，并获虎嗅、36氪、国资背景的第一财经等媒体声援。  

DeepSeek has not commented in support of its researcher, but neither has the lab asked him to take it down.  
深度求索未公开支持研究员，但也未要求删文。  

On Zhihu and Xiaohongshu, where China’s tech communities gather online, as well as in the blog’s own comment section, the tone is overwhelmingly supportive.  
在知乎、小红书等中国科技社区及博客评论区，支持声浪占绝对主流。  

People say that DeepSeek has shown the most commitment to “an inclusive vision”; that Dario Amodei’s talk of major AI risks is an alarmist dead horse; that OpenAI and Anthropic are “wantonly destroying all that is beautiful” to “undermine the foundation of Babel”;  
网友称深度求索最致力于"包容性愿景"，指Amodei的AI风险论是危言耸听，批评OpenAI和Anthropic"肆意摧毁美好"以"动摇巴别塔根基"；  

and that Anthropic, in particular, is “techno-fascist” and “barely human.”  
更直指Anthropic是"技术法西斯"且"毫无人性"。  

The very group that has the closest — and, arguably, the most humanizing and nuanced — contact with the American AI community is starting to conclude that the latter is motivated by nothing more than anti-Chinese ideology.  
这个与美国AI界联系最紧密（也最具人文关怀）的群体开始认定：对方动机纯粹是反华意识形态。  

Caravaggio’s David with the Head of Goliath.  
卡拉瓦乔画作《手提歌利亚头的大卫》。  

How did we get here?  
我们何以至此？  

Before AI governance seemingly reached a boiling point, on the sidelines of ICML this summer in Seoul, I had a casual exchange with a Chinese attendee.  
今夏首尔ICML会议间隙，在AI治理问题白热化前，我曾与一位中国与会者闲聊。  

My interlocutor, while not a technical expert, has had a career in the vicinity of AI for years. He reads machine learning papers, travels extensively, and works closely with researchers.  
这位非技术专家在AI领域工作多年，常读机器学习论文，频繁出差并与研究员密切合作。  

When the conversation moved to geopolitics and I explained Dario Amodei’s worldview to him, he was completely baffled.  
当我解释Amodei的地缘政治观时，他完全困惑。  

I told him that as far as I understood it, the CEO of Anthropic is deeply committed to liberal democracy as a preferred system to authoritarianism; therefore, since AI is possibly a technology that increases productivity and national power at exponential scale, democracies should do everything possible to ensure dominance in this technology.  
我解释道：AnthropicCEO坚信自由民主制优于威权主义，而AI可能是指数级提升国力的技术，因此民主国家应全力确保技术主导权。  

One (democracy) plus one (AGI) equals two (export controls against China).  
"民主制度+AGI=对华技术封锁"。  

“It’s this straightforward? Then … nothing else?” He found the simplicity of this idea almost too implausible to entertain.  
"就这么简单？没有其他因素？"他觉得这种线性思维难以置信。

若身处湾区/华盛顿特区，或仅从美国视角了解AI与地缘政治，你可能觉得...
