\u003Ch2\u003EThe NEC V20\u0026#8217;s slightly troubled past\u003C/h2\u003E
\u003Ch2\u003ENEC V20的坎坷往事\u003C/h2\u003E

\u003Cp\u003EIn 1982, NEC licensed the rights to produce the Intel 8088 as a second source. But in March 1984, NEC released the V20, an 8088 clone with some performance improvements. It had more than double the transistor count of the 8088 and could perform two data transfers concurrently, allowing it to complete more instructions in a given time than a real 8088.\u003C/p\u003E
\u003Cp\u003E1982年NEC获得英特尔授权成为8088第二供应商。但1984年3月，NEC发布了经过性能改良的8088克隆版V20。其晶体管数量是8088的两倍多，支持并发双数据传输，单位时间内能执行比原版8088更多的指令。\u003C/p\u003E

\u003Cp\u003EIntel sued in late 1984. NEC said the V20 was a cleanroom design. Ultimately NEC prevailed on September 22, 1986. The legal questions surrounding the V20 likely caused larger PC makers to shy away from it. By the time it was ruled legal, the market for XT-class machines was still healthy, but XTs were budget machines, so the lower transistor count of the 8088 meant a real 8088 was going to cost less in OEM quantities.\u003C/p\u003E
\u003Cp\u003E英特尔于1984年底提起诉讼，NEC坚称V20采用净室设计。1986年9月22日NEC最终胜诉。这场法律纠纷使得主流PC厂商对其敬而远之。当判决尘埃落定时，XT级计算机市场虽仍繁荣，但作为廉价机型，晶体管更少的原装8088在批量采购时更具成本优势。\u003C/p\u003E
