### 1. **New Yorkers to get very mad at their government and demand change**

### 2. **China’s choke-hold on rare earths**
- **Critical Minerals**: These include traditional metals like aluminum and nickel, along with strategic elements such as neodymium (used in magnets) and yttrium.
- **Chinese Dominance**: China controls a significant portion of refining these minerals despite not being the primary miner for many of them. This is due to their dominance in downstream industries and financial support for refineries.
- **Downstream Industries**: Chinese companies manufacture products that use these metals, making it economical to process them near production facilities.
- **Financial Support**: China’s government offers cheap loans to support refining activities.
- **Looser Regulations**: Compared to Western countries, China has more lenient environmental regulations.
- **AI and Tacit Knowledge**: Efforts are needed to leverage AI for rapid knowledge transfer and refine processes.

### 3. **India's Women Need to Work More**
The Indian economy is hindered by low female labor force participation rates compared to other developing nations. While there’s potential for economic growth through urbanization, cultural norms and social attitudes prevent many women from leaving their homes for work in factories.

- **Historical Context**: Historically, women have stayed within the agrarian sector.
- **Current Trends**: Despite improving education levels and income opportunities, women are still reluctant to leave home, likely due to traditional beliefs and community pressures.

### 4. **Murder Rates Not Really High**
The myth persists that murder rates are declining because of better trauma care in hospitals, rather than actual decreases in violent crime:

- **Data Issues**: The Harris et al. (2002) paper used flawed data: the rate of aggravated assaults was conflated with murder rates.
- **Police Reporting**: Modern police forces are more thorough and professional compared to earlier decades, resulting in fewer underreported incidents.
- **Victim Reports**: When asking people directly if they were victims of shootings or assaults, higher levels of reporting persisted even as overall rates increased.
- **Actual Trends**: Murder rates have actually stabilized since their peak in the early 2010s and mid-2020s.

- **Indian Women**: Requires addressing social stigmas and traditional beliefs surrounding women working outside the home to unlock economic growth.
- **Murder Trends**: Historical data on murders was flawed, leading to a misinterpretation that modern healthcare improvements were responsible for declines.
