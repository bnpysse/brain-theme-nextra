【文章正文】：  
What a New Car Costs Across 30 Global Cities  
全球30大城市新车价格一览  

Key Takeaways  
核心发现  

A new car costs about $156,000 in Singapore, more than four times as much as in New York City.  
新加坡新车均价约15.6万美元，是纽约市价格的4倍多  

Across most of the 30 cities shown, new car prices fall within a much narrower range of roughly $35,000 to $50,000.  
其余29个城市的新车价格集中在3.5万至5万美元区间  

Singapore stands apart largely because of policies designed to limit car ownership, including an ownership certificate that can cost close to $100,000.  
新加坡因"拥车证"等限购政策独树一帜，仅这张证书就需近10万美元  

In Singapore, the price of a new car can rival the cost of a home in some parts of the world.  
新加坡新车价格堪比某些地区的房产总价  

The car may be comparable, but the final bill isn’t. Taxes, import costs, and ownership rules can create price gaps of tens of thousands of dollars between cities.  
同款车型最终售价差异巨大，税费和限购政策可导致数万美元价差  

This graphic compares new car prices across 30 major cities, based on data from Deutsche Bank and Numbeo. Prices are shown in U.S. dollars and are based on a Volkswagen Golf 1.5 or an equivalent new car.  
数据来源：德意志银行/Numbeo，以大众高尔夫1.5或同级车型为基准（美元计价）  

Why Cars Cost So Much More in Singapore  
新加坡天价车背后的政策逻辑  

Singapore’s unusually high car prices are largely by design. The city-state tightly controls the number of vehicles on its roads, which stands at around one million.  
新加坡通过行政手段严格控制百万量级汽车总量  

Car buyers must also obtain a Certificate of Entitlement (COE), which gives them the right to own and use a vehicle for 10 years. Recent COE premiums have approached $100,000 before accounting for the vehicle itself and other taxes and fees.  
购车者须竞拍10年期限的拥车证（COE），近期COE中标价已逼近10万美元（不含车价及其他税费）

At $156,200, a new car in Singapore costs nearly three times as much as in Tel Aviv, the next-highest city at $57,900. New York and Paris, by comparison, come in at around $35,000.  
新加坡15.62万美元的车价是第二名特拉维夫（5.79万）的近3倍，纽约/巴黎仅约3.5万美元  

Beyond Singapore, prices are considerably more compressed. Cities as different as London, Milan, Los Angeles, Chicago, and Barcelona all fall within a range of just $35,000 to $39,000, even though their broader costs of living vary considerably.  
其他城市价差较小：伦敦/米兰/洛杉矶/芝加哥/巴塞罗那均集中在3.5-3.9万美元区间  

The Sticker Price Is Only the Beginning  
标价只是开始  

The purchase price is only one part of what drivers ultimately pay. In the U.S., a new vehicle costs an estimated $12,863 per year to own and operate, or about $1,072 per month.  
美国车主年均养车成本达12,863美元（约1,072美元/月）  

What you drive can widen that gap further. A half-ton pickup costs $1.10 per mile to own and operate, compared with $0.62 for a small sedan. Over a year, that amounts to a difference of more than $7,000.  
全尺寸皮卡每英里使用成本1.1美元，较小型轿车（0.62美元）年差额超7,000美元  

Together, the figures show how car affordability is shaped by two different forces: where you buy a vehicle determines its upfront price, while what you drive can significantly change the cost of owning it over time.  
购车成本受双重因素影响：地域决定购置价，车型决定长期持有成本
