Fuse’s Faeton-X Hits 1.27×10¹² Neutron Yield
Fuse的Faeton-X达到1.27×10¹²中子产额

Compared to every other fusion company, the thing I love about our portfolio company

Fuse Energy
Fuse Energy

(aside from the founder,
（除了创始人

JC Btaiche
JC Btaiche

) is that it generates revenue by selling radiation on the way to generating power from fusion.
之外）的一点是，它在实现聚变发电的过程中，通过出售辐射来产生收入。

When I
当我在

wrote about the company in May 2024
2024年5月写过这家公司

, I described a couple of paths. The TITAN path - from TITAN to Z STAR to APEIRON, is what will ultimately deliver fusion power to the grid.
时，我描述了几条路径。TITAN路径——从TITAN到Z STAR再到APEIRON——最终将为电网提供聚变电力。

The FAETON path, while not the path to fusion directly, produces valuable neutron shots: “In the near-term, Fuse actually expects to make more money from FAETON II shots than it does from TITAN II shots, because the value of FAETON II’s neutron-producing shots are twice as valuable as TITAN II’s.” Defense contractors and satellite makers pay tens of thousands of dollars a shot to blast their electronics with neutrons to find out whether they could survive a nuclear detonation or a decade of radiation in orbit.
FAETON路径虽然不直接通向聚变，但能产生有价值的中子射击：“短期内，Fuse实际上预计从FAETON II射击中赚到的钱比TITAN II射击更多，因为FAETON II产生中子的射击价值是TITAN II的两倍。”国防承包商和卫星制造商每次射击要支付数万美元，用中子轰击他们的电子设备，以确定它们能否在核爆炸或十年轨道辐射中幸存下来。

FAETON also uses some of the same components, namely the Brick, “the high-voltage capacitor and gas switch that is the fundamental building block of its pulsed power generators”
FAETON也使用了一些相同的组件，即Brick，“高压电容器和气体开关，是其脉冲功率发电机的基本构件”

All of which is a long way of introducing the importance of this breakthrough. This week, Fuse published results from FAETON-X, showing that it generated 1.27×10¹² fusion neutrons in one shot, making them the first fusion company to publicly document a yield in the 10¹² range.
所有这些都是一种引出这一突破重要性的长篇方式。本周，Fuse公布了FAETON-X的结果，显示它在一次射击中产生了1.27×10¹²个聚变中子，这使他们成为第一家公开记录10¹²量级产额的聚变公司。

Importantly, though, they did it with a higher current efficiency than the labs:
不过，重要的是，他们以比实验室更高的电流效率做到了这一点：

4.5 MA/MJ
4.5 MA/MJ

, highest current efficiency ever for a MJ-class plasma focus, against 2.35–3.25 for all published peers including government machines. It took Livermore’s MJOLNIR 1.3 MJ to produce 1.2×10¹² neutrons, where it only took Fuse 1 MJ to produce its 1.27×10¹².
，这是MJ级等离子体焦点有史以来最高的电流效率，而所有已发表的同行（包括政府机器）为2.35–3.25。利弗莫尔的MJOLNIR产生1.2×10¹²个中子需要1.3 MJ，而Fuse产生1.27×10¹²个中子只需1 MJ。

That
这

is important, because yield in pulsed-power z-pinch devices scales as the fourth power of drive current. Current efficiency is the compounding variable of which yield is the derivative. 40% more current per MJ gets you 4x more fusion, not just 40% more.
很重要，因为脉冲功率Z箍缩装置的产额随驱动电流的四次方变化。电流效率是复利变量，产额是其导数。每MJ增加40%的电流，得到的聚变输出增加4倍，而不仅仅是40%。

This is maybe the most important curve for Fuse: neutrons per megajoule of capacitor bank. On Faeton, it means delivering those valuable neutron shots more efficiently. On the TITAN path, it means that everything gets smaller and cheaper for the same current, which could mean getting to fusion power, and making it cheaper, sooner.
这可能是对Fuse来说最重要的曲线：每兆焦耳电容器组产生的中子数。在Faeton上，这意味着更高效地提供那些有价值的中子射击。在TITAN路径上，这意味着在相同电流下，一切都变得更小、更便宜，这可能意味着更快实现聚变发电，并使其更便宜。

This is the Fuse thesis, too. Instead of straight-shotting fusion like competitors, get out in the field and start selling. Iterate. Improve the brick on FAETON, and bring it to TITAN. Move fast. They went from design to first shot on FAETON-X in nine months, and from first shot to record in another nine. Keep interacting with the messiness of the real world, keep iterating, and maybe one day the iterative approach gets you to the end goal faster.
这也是Fuse的论点。不像竞争对手那样直奔聚变，而是到现场去开始销售。迭代。改进FAETON上的Brick，并将其带到TITAN。快速行动。他们从设计到FAETON-X首次射击用了九个月，从首次射击到打破纪录又用了九个月。持续与真实世界的混乱互动，持续迭代，也许有一天迭代方法能让你更快达到最终目标。

If not, there’s a lot of very useful stuff you can do with all that radiation at Q&lt;1.
如果没有，那么在Q&lt;1的情况下，你可以用所有这些辐射做很多非常有用的东西。
