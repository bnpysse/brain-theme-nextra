【文章标题】：I Went to Kyrgyzstan to Find China’s Silent Nomads  
【标题翻译】：我前往吉尔吉斯斯坦寻找中国沉默的游牧民族  

【文章正文】：  
On a rainy day at the beginning of September, I found myself standing under the giant sky at the foot of the mountains in rural Kyrgyzstan, shouting “add oil! (加油) from the sidelines of a horseback archery track.  
九月初的一个雨天，我站在吉尔吉斯斯坦乡间群山脚下辽阔的天空下，在马背射箭赛道旁喊着"加油"。  

I was back in Central Asia for the World Nomad Games, an Olympics-style celebration of traditional sports where China reluctantly lets some of its minorities compete.  
我重返中亚参加世界游牧民族运动会——这是奥林匹克式的传统体育盛会，中国勉强允许部分少数民族运动员参赛。  

As if that weren’t justification enough for a reporting trip, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization’s annual summit was happening in Bishkek, with the Games designated as a partner event open to the delegates. Putin, Modi, and nine other heads of state flew in to discuss new infrastructure megaprojects, drugs and nuclear material trafficking, market integration, and more.  
更巧的是，上海合作组织年度峰会正在比什凯克举行，这场运动会被列为面向各国代表的配套活动。普京、莫迪等11国元首齐聚于此，商讨基建超级工程、毒品与核材料走私、市场一体化等议题。

（ChinaTalk是读者支持的出版物，欲获取更新请订阅）  

When I went to Kyrgyzstan last August in search of Chinese influence, the country captivated me with its friendly people, rapidly growing economy, and a nomadic way of life still practiced by many in the countryside.  
去年八月我为探寻中国影响力初次造访吉尔吉斯斯坦时，就被当地人的友善、快速发展的经济以及乡村地区保留的游牧生活方式深深吸引。  

And now, on the 35th anniversary of Kyrgyzstan’s independence from the Soviet Union, I was honored to bear witness to the country’s moment on the world stage.  
如今在吉尔吉斯斯坦脱离苏联独立35周年之际，我很荣幸能见证这个国家站上世界舞台的高光时刻。  

Today, I’ll take you along on my journey. This piece is part travelogue and part geopolitics, and it covers a lot of ground: the massive infrastructure buildout, the Kyrgyz president’s Chinamaxxing, the muzzled Inner Mongolian athletes representing China, the lessons China hasn’t learned about sports diplomacy and inter-ethnic harmony, and what this all means for Central Asia’s future.  
今日我将带您同行。本文既是游记也是地缘政治观察，涵盖众多层面：大规模基建、吉尔吉斯斯坦总统的"中国化"倾向、代表中国参赛却遭禁声的内蒙古运动员、中国在体育外交与民族和谐方面尚未吸取的教训，以及这一切对中亚未来的意义。  

Arriving in Bishkek  
抵达比什凯克  

The World Nomad Games were first conceived in Kyrgyzstan in 2014 and are held every two years.  
世界游牧民族运动会2014年创始于吉尔吉斯斯坦，每两年举办一次。  

After being hosted by Türkiye in 2022 and Kazakhstan in 2024, the Games have finally returned home to the mountains of Kyrgyzstan. And these were the largest Games ever, with 200,000+ visitors piling into Kyrchyn Gorge, one of the Games’ six venues, by the third day of the Games to watch 3,000+ athletes from 109 countries (compared to 583 athletes from 19 countries in 2014).  
历经2022年土耳其和2024年哈萨克斯坦承办后，运动会终于回归吉尔吉斯斯坦的群山怀抱。本届规模空前——赛事第三天，六大场地之一的克尔钦峡谷就涌入了20多万观众，观看来自109个国家的3000多名运动员竞技（2014年首届仅有19国583名运动员）。  

An aerial view of some of the World Nomad Games facilities at Kyrchyn Gorge. Source.  
（克尔钦峡谷运动会设施航拍图，来源略）  

I flew into Bishkek from Tashkent on a beautifully clear day, and I spent the entire 75 minutes in the air gazing at the picturesque Tian Shan mountains (yes, that name is of Chinese origin).  
我在晴朗之日从塔什干飞往比什凯克，75分钟航程中始终凝视着如画的天山山脉（没错，这个名称源自中文）。  

I arrived a few days before the opening ceremony, hoping to sneak a peek at the SCO action in the capital before heading out to the shores of Lake Issyk-kul for the Games.  
我提前数日抵达开幕式前，打算先窥探上合组织在首都的动态，再前往伊塞克湖畔的赛场。  

With some help from the China Road and Bridge Corporation (中国路桥), Bishkek’s airport had been completely remodeled in preparation for the international arrivals.  
在中国路桥公司协助下，比什凯克机场为迎接国际来宾进行了全面改造。  

I stepped outside to find an organizer from the Games holding a sign bearing my name.  
走出机场便看见运动会工作人员举着写有我名字的接机牌。  

The organizer explained that I needed to go with him to register for my press pass, and then get on a bus to the resort where the journalists were staying… an hour and a half outside the capital.  
工作人员解释需先办理记者证，再乘班车前往距首都一个半小时车程的记者驻地度假村。  

This was probably because of the SCO — the Kyrgyz government had even recommended/requested that Bishkek residents leave the city for the weekend of the summit.  
这或许源于上合峰会——吉尔吉斯斯坦政府甚至建议/要求市民在峰会周末期间离城。  

But this made me all the more curious about what was going on in Bishkek. I knew I couldn’t go rogue immediately, so I followed the organizer back into the airport to get a Journalists’ Merit Badge.  
但这反而激发了我对比什凯克现状的好奇。自知不能立即脱队，便随工作人员返回机场办理"记者功勋徽章"。  

The official World Nomad Games reception area was tucked inside a secluded corner that smelled of fresh paint and black tea, awash with young Kyrgyz people wearing red vests emblazoned with the word “VOLUNTEER.”  
运动会接待处藏在飘着油漆与红茶香味的僻静角落，随处可见身穿印有"志愿者"红马甲的吉尔吉斯斯坦青年。  

Everyone was so excited, and no one had any idea if it was a hard rule that I needed to go to the resort rather than to the capital.  
众人情绪高涨，但没人能确定"必须前往度假村"是否铁律。  

I made myself some tea and was escorted to a Soviet-style minibus tasked with shuttling the international press to the resort.  
我泡了杯茶，随后被领上一辆负责接送国际媒体的苏式小巴士。  

The organizer who was waiting for me with the sign sat shotgun next to the driver, and the only other passenger, I soon learned, was Afzal Reza, a bureau chief of the Iranian state media organization IRNA.  
举牌接我的工作人员坐在副驾驶，车上另一位乘客 soon learned 是伊朗国家通讯社IRNA的分社社长Afzal Reza。  

I began negotiating in Russian with the organizer and driver, and Afzal sprang to life. "Oh my goodness, you speak Russian!"  
当我用俄语与工作人员和司机交涉时，Afzal突然活跃起来："天啊，你会说俄语！"  

I helped him translate for a bit before he succumbed to jet lag, and then continued negotiating with the organizer.  
我帮他翻译了几句，待他因时差昏睡后，继续与工作人员协商。  

"I have a hiking trip planned for tomorrow, and I’d really like to go shopping in Dordoi market" (all true).  
"我明天计划徒步旅行，很想去多尔多伊市场购物"（所言皆实）。  

I handed him one of my Russian business cards. "You can add me on WhatsApp, and I’ll tell you when I arrive at the resort tomorrow night."  
我递上俄文名片："可以加我WhatsApp，明晚到度假村会告知您"。  

He studied my business card and turned it over. "You are from Kansas, USA?" he asked with intrigue. "I know it from The Wizard of Oz! You are also from the steppe then, like the Kazakhs!"  
他端详着名片翻转查看："你来自美国堪萨斯？"饶有兴趣地问，"我从《绿野仙踪》知道的！那你也是草原儿女啊，和哈萨克人一样！"  

And with that, he agreed to take me to Bishkek.  
就这样，他同意带我去比什凯克。
