【文章正文】：  
Today is Claude Fable (and Mythos) 5.1 day.

Anthropic say that Fable 5.1 "sets a new standard for coding, knowledge work, and long-running problem-solving tasks".  
Anthropic宣称Fable 5.1"为编程、知识工作和长期问题解决任务设立了新标准"。

Their announcement spends a notable amount of time on scientific research, boasting of a 52.6% score on the brand new Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 benchmark (first announced on August 27th), up from 24.7% for Fable 5, 29.0% for Opus 5 and 22.4% for GPT-5.6 Sol.  
其公告大篇幅强调科研能力，炫耀在全新Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1基准测试（8月27日首次公布）中获得52.6%的分数，较Fable 5的24.7%、Opus 5的29.0%和GPT-5.6 Sol的22.4%有显著提升。

Other benchmarks show slightly improved scores, but none as impressive as the Science one.  
其他基准测试分数略有提高，但都不及科学基准的突破性表现。

But how well can it pelican?  
但它画鹈鹕的能力如何呢？

Back in July I wrote about how I was losing faith in the pelican benchmark - its connection to how good the models were at other tasks didn't seem to hold as strongly as it did back in 2025.  
早在七月份我就撰文提及对"鹈鹕基准"失去信心——该基准与模型其他任务能力的关联性已不如2025年时显著。

The most interesting insights I get from it now are comparisons within model families, and particularly comparisons for the same prompt at different reasoning effort levels.  
如今该测试最有价值的洞察是模型家族内部的横向比较，尤其是同一提示词在不同推理强度下的表现差异。

Fable 5.1 has five reasoning levels: low, medium, high, xhigh, max - and no option to turn off reasoning entirely.  
Fable 5.1设有五级推理强度：低、中、高、超高、极限——且无法完全关闭推理功能。
