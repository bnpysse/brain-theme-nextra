To explain further what this video shows: this is the results of the solar system being simulated with Newtonian, point-mass gravity from 1st January 1950 to 12th August 2026. As you can see, the eclipse is (mostly) correctly predicted after more than 76 years of simulation, starting only from the bodies' state vectors and gravitational parameters.
为了进一步解释这段视频展示的内容：这是太阳系在牛顿点质量引力下从 1950 年 1 月 1 日到 2026 年 8 月 12 日的模拟结果。正如你所见，在仅从各天体的状态向量和引力参数出发、模拟超过 76 年之后，这次日食被（基本上）正确预测了。

I've shared this project here before, but to reiterate: Ephemeris Explorer lets you build solar systems (or any gravitationally bound system) and simulate them with Newtonian point-mass gravity. You can also add spacecraft and manoeuvres to plan flight paths within these systems!
我之前在这里分享过这个项目，但再次重申：Ephemeris Explorer 让你能够构建太阳系（或任何受引力束缚的系统），并用牛顿点质量引力进行模拟。你还可以添加航天器和机动，在这些系统中规划飞行路径！

There's a lot more I plan to add to Ephemeris Explorer in the future (see the README), but it takes time as this is a hobby project I work on in my free time. Hope this helps more people who might be interested find their way to it!
未来我计划为 Ephemeris Explorer 添加更多功能（参见 README），但这需要时间，因为这是我在空闲时间做的业余项目。希望这能帮助更多感兴趣的人找到它！
