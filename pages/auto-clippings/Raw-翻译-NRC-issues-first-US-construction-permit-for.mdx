【文章标题】：NRC issues first U.S. construction permit for a BWRX-300 small modular reactor

【文章标题】：美国核管会首次颁发BWRX-300小型模块化反应堆建造许可

【文章正文】：

-

Decision advances BWRX-300 deployment in the U.S. and reinforces growing regulatory momentum across North America

该决定推动BWRX-300在美国的部署，并强化北美地区日益增长的监管势头

KNOXVILLE, TN (September 29, 2026) – GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy (GVH) today welcomed the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission’s (NRC) issuance of a construction permit to the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) for a BWRX-300 small modular reactor at its Clinch River site in Oak Ridge, Tennessee.

田纳西州诺克斯维尔市（2026年9月29日）——GE Vernova日立核能公司（GVH）今日对美国核管理委员会（NRC）向田纳西河谷管理局（TVA）颁发BWRX-300小型模块化反应堆建造许可表示欢迎，该反应堆将建于田纳西州橡树岭的克林奇河厂址。

The permit is the first issued in the United States for a BWRX-300 and represents a significant milestone for TVA, GVH and the U.S. nuclear industry as the country advances toward a new generation of nuclear power deployment.

这是美国颁发的首份BWRX-300建造许可，标志着TVA、GVH和美国核电行业在推进新一代核电部署进程中取得重大里程碑。

"This is a milestone for TVA and GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy, and an important moment for new nuclear in the United States," said Eric Gray, CEO of GE Vernova Power Segment.

"这对TVA和GE Vernova日立核能公司具有里程碑意义，也是美国新型核电发展的重要时刻，"GE Vernova电力业务首席执行官埃里克·格雷表示。

"TVA has shown strong leadership in advancing new nuclear, a commitment bolstered by the collaborative efforts of the NRC and Department of Energy. Together, and in alignment with federal Executive Orders aimed at accelerating advanced reactors, we are grateful for this collaboration as we work to support the deployment of the first BWRX-300 in the country."

"TVA在推进新型核电方面展现出强大领导力，这种承诺得到了NRC和能源部协同努力的支持。我们与旨在加速先进反应堆部署的联邦行政令保持一致，感谢各方合作共同推动美国首台BWRX-300的部署。"

The NRC’s decision follows an extensive review of TVA’s construction permit application and builds on significant engagement between TVA, the NRC and GVH. The process demonstrates how experience, early regulatory engagement and a mature reactor design can support a more efficient pathway for advanced nuclear projects.

NRC的决定基于对TVA建造许可申请的全面审查，并依托于TVA、NRC与GVH之间的深度合作。这一过程证明经验积累、早期监管介入和成熟反应堆设计能为先进核电项目提供更高效的实施路径。

The milestone also adds to growing regulatory progress for the BWRX-300 across North America. In April 2025, the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC) issued Ontario Power Generation (OPG) a License to Construct the first BWRX-300 at its Darlington New Nuclear Project site, where nuclear construction is now underway.

这一里程碑进一步推动了BWRX-300在北美地区的监管进展。2025年4月，加拿大核安全委员会（CNSC）向安大略发电公司（OPG）颁发了在达灵顿新核电项目厂址建造首台BWRX-300的许可证，目前该核电建设已启动。

In March 2026, OPG submitted its License to Operate application for the first unit, which is currently under review by the CNSC. In September 2026, Blue Energy submitted the first part of its construction permit application to the NRC for a BWRX-300 planned for its gas-plus-nuclear project in Texas.

2026年3月，OPG提交了首台机组的运行许可证申请，目前正在CNSC审查中。2026年9月，Blue Energy向NRC提交了得克萨斯州气电-核电项目中BWRX-300建造许可申请的第一部分材料。

Together, these projects are establishing a growing body of licensing experience that can be applied across future BWRX-300 deployments.

这些项目共同积累了日益丰富的许可经验，可为未来BWRX-300的部署提供参考。

"Fleet deployment is essential to scaling nuclear energy in the United States," said Jason Cooper, CEO of GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy.

"规模化部署对推动美国核电发展至关重要，"GE Vernova日立核能公司首席执行官贾森·库珀表示。

"A standard design, combined with a predictable and efficient regulatory environment, allows us to apply common technology, licensing experience, supply chain capabilities and lessons learned from one project to the next. Clinch River brings us another step closer to making that fleet model a reality."

"标准化设计结合可预期的高效监管环境，使我们能够将通用技术、许可经验、供应链能力和项目经验教训应用于后续项目。克林奇河项目让我们向实现规模化模式又迈进了一步。"

TVA submitted its construction permit application for Clinch River in April 2025. The project builds on TVA’s existing Early Site Permit for Clinch River and is located within the Oak Ridge nuclear ecosystem, near the U.S. Department of Energy’s Oak Ridge National Laboratory.

TVA于2025年4月提交了克林奇河项目的建造许可申请。该项目基于TVA已有的克林奇河厂址早期许可，位于橡树岭核能生态系统内，毗邻美国能源部橡树岭国家实验室。

The BWRX-300 is a 300-MW small modular reactor designed to reduce construction complexity by using a simplified configuration and leveraging decades of boiling water reactor operating experience.

BWRX-300是一座300兆瓦小型模块化反应堆，通过简化配置和借鉴数十年沸水堆运行经验来降低建造复杂度。
