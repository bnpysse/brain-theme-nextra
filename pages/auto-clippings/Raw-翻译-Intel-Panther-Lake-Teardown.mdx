【文章标题】：Intel Panther Lake 拆解分析

【文章正文】：
Panther Lake debuts the first commercial implementation of backside power delivery (BSPDN), introduces Intel’s first iteration of gate-all-around (GAA) transistors, and showcases their advanced packaging capabilities with its Foveros-S assembly. 
Panther Lake首次实现了背面供电（BSPDN）的商业化应用，推出了英特尔首代全环绕栅极（GAA）晶体管，并通过其Foveros-S组装技术展示了先进的封装能力。

With Panther Lake, Intel’s manufacturing arc has shifted from nebulous roadmaps to shipped silicon, a significant milestone on their long road back to competitive semiconductor manufacturing. 
随着Panther Lake的问世，英特尔的制造进程已从模糊的技术路线图迈向量产芯片阶段，这是其重振半导体制造竞争力的重要里程碑。

To evaluate the extent of Intel’s comeback, we tore down Panther Lake.
为评估英特尔的技术复兴程度，我们对Panther Lake进行了深度拆解。

Our teardown traces 18A from its four-sheet RibbonFETs (Intel’s marketing name for GAAFETs) and gate stacks through contacts, frontside and backside wiring, and the bonded carrier. 
我们的拆解从18A工艺的四层RibbonFET（英特尔对GAAFET的营销命名）和栅极堆栈开始，追踪了接触孔、正面/背面布线及载板接合的全流程。

We explain how these material and integration choices improve gate control and reduce resistance, while adding capacitance, thermal resistance, and process complexity.

Our measurements put Panther Lake’s 18A compute logic and TSMC N3E GPU logic at similar logic density. 
测量数据显示，Panther Lake的18A计算逻辑与台积电N3E GPU逻辑具有相近的逻辑密度。

However, 18A does not lead TSMC N3P, N2 or Samsung SF2 in peak density. 
但18A在峰值密度上仍落后于台积电N3P、N2及三星SF2工艺。

Panther Lake’s CPU cores are incremental updates, and the high-end GPU still uses TSMC N3E.
Panther Lake的CPU核心属于渐进式升级，高端GPU仍采用台积电N3E工艺。

Panther Lake assembles one compute tile, one GPU tile, and one I/O tile atop a passive base tile using Intel’s Foveros-S advanced packaging. 
Panther Lake采用英特尔Foveros-S先进封装技术，在无源基础晶粒上集成计算晶粒、GPU晶粒和I/O晶粒各一枚。

Both compute tile variants use Intel 18A. The Xe3 GPU options are a 4-core GT1 tile on Intel 3 and a larger 12-core GT2 tile on TSMC N3E. Both I/O tile variants use TSMC N6. [1], [2]
两种计算晶粒均采用Intel 18A工艺。Xe3 GPU提供两个版本：基于Intel 3工艺的4核GT1晶粒，以及采用台积电N3E工艺的12核GT2大晶粒。I/O晶粒则统一使用台积电N6工艺。[1][2]

我们的分析聚焦于PTL-U计算晶粒、4核/12核GPU晶粒以及12通道I/O晶粒。

PowerVia
PowerVia技术解析

In conventional chips, power and signal are routed through the same frontside metal stack towards the device frontend. 
传统芯片中，供电与信号通过同一套正面金属堆栈传输至器件前端。

Power rails consume scarce routing resources near the transistors, while tall via stacks carry VDD and VSS from the coarse upper wires to local rails. 
电源轨会消耗晶体管附近宝贵的布线资源，而高层通孔堆栈则将VDD/VSS从粗粒度上层导线传导至本地电源轨。

Backside power delivery (BSPD) moves the main power network behind the transistor layer, to the backside, separating it from frontside signal routing. 
背面供电技术（BSPD）将主供电网络移至晶体管层背面，使其与正面信号布线分离。

We covered BSPD and its impacts in 2024. [3], [4], [5] Intel’s BSPD implementation, branded as “PowerVia”, routes power through dedicated backside metals to nano-TSVs, which connect those rails to local source/drain (S/D) contacts.
我们已在2024年详述BSPD技术及其影响[3][4][5]。英特尔将其实施方案命名为"PowerVia"，通过专用背面金属层和纳米硅通孔（nano-TSVs）将电源连接至本地源极/漏极（S/D）接触点。

Implementing that separation requires Intel to build the interconnect stacks from both sides of the wafer. 
实现这种分离需要英特尔从晶圆两侧构建互连堆栈。

The frontside comprises the M0-M14 signal stack, while the backside comprises the BM0-BM5 power stack. M0 and BM0 are closest to the transistors.
正面包含M0-M14信号堆栈，背面则包含BM0-BM5电源堆栈。其中M0和BM0最接近晶体管层。

Generalized PowerVia process sequence and comparison of buried power rails, PowerVia and direct backside contact schemes. Integration details and layer counts are illustrative; the schematic is not to scale. Source: SemiAnalysis
PowerVia工艺流程概览及埋入式电源轨、PowerVia与直接背面接触方案的对比。集成细节和层数仅为示意，非实际比例。来源：SemiAnalysis

The nano-TSVs connect the two sides, but Intel patterns and etches each via from the front after forming the contacts. 
纳米硅通孔连接晶圆两侧，但英特尔在形成接触孔后从正面进行图形化和刻蚀。

A narrow via runs from the side of the contact deep into the silicon substrate. 
狭窄的通孔从接触孔侧面延伸至硅衬底深处。

Intel then completes the frontside signal metal stack, bonds the wafer to a carrier, flips it and removes the original substrate until the buried via tips are exposed. 
随后英特尔完成正面信号金属堆栈，将晶圆接合至载板并翻转，通过去除原始衬底使埋入的通孔尖端暴露。

The backside metal stack is then deposited directly on the revealed vias.
背面金属堆栈直接沉积在暴露的通孔上。

The nano-TSV and backside-via profiles taper in opposite directions because Intel forms them from opposite sides of the wafer. 
纳米硅通孔与背面通孔的锥度方向相反，因其分别从晶圆两侧形成。

The transistor structures form the FEOL. Local contacts and nano-TSVs connect them to the wiring. M0 begins the frontside interconnect stack.
晶体管结构构成前段制程（FEOL），通过本地接触孔和纳米硅通孔连接至布线层。M0标志着正面互连堆栈的起点。

The silicon carrier remains attached above the frontside interconnects. It supports the device wafer during substrate removal and backside processing and remains part of the finished chip’s thermal path.
硅载板始终固定在正面互连层上方，在衬底去除和背面加工过程中支撑器件晶圆，并作为成品芯片散热路径的组成部分。

PowerVia removes the main power distribution from the congested frontside metals, routing supply through shorter and wider backside wires. 
PowerVia技术将主供电网络从拥挤的正面金属层转移，通过更短更宽的背面导线进行供电。

Its lateral landing still occupies area in the standard cell, so it recovers less cell area than a direct backside contact. [3] 
其横向连接仍会占用标准单元面积，因此比直接背面接触方案节省的单元面积更少。[3]

Nano-TSVs beside the logic devices carry VDD or VSS from the backside power network, while signal connections continue upward through the frontside metals.
逻辑器件旁的纳米硅通孔负责从背面供电网络传输VDD/VSS，而信号连接则继续通过正面金属层向上传输。
