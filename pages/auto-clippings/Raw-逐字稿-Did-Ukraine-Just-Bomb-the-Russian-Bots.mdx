On October 11th, a member of the

European Parliament posted a chart on

Twitter showing that online activity

around Germany's AFD party had collapsed

right after Ukrainian drones hit Russian

data centers. It was viewed more than 4

million times, and it was very

convincing, not least because it

included data from 3 days in the future.

10月11日，一名欧洲议会议员在推特上发布了一张图表，显示乌克兰无人机袭击俄罗斯数据中心后，德国AFD党周边的网络活动立即崩溃。这张图表被观看了超过400万次，且极具说服力，尤其是因为它还包含了未来三天的数据。

To be fair to the poster, he did add the

words if confirmed, which is always a

sensible precaution when sharing a graph

that knows what happens next Wednesday.

公平地说，发帖人确实加上了“如果得到证实”的字样，对于分享一张知道下周三会发生什么的图表而言，这始终是一个明智的预防措施。

Now, as someone who spends a lot of time

looking at financial charts, I can tell

you that good historical data can be

hard enough to get data for next

Wednesday is where it gets really

tricky. A Bloomberg terminal costs

around $30,000 a year and it won't give

you that. This chart managed it for

free.

作为一个经常研究金融图表的人，我可以告诉你，获取可靠的历史数据已经够难了，而获取下周三的数据才真正棘手。一台彭博终端每年花费大约3万美元，它也不会给你这些数据。这张图表却免费做到了。

It was posted on a Sunday and the

plunge ran all the way through till

Wednesday the 14th. When the fact

checkers at the German broadcaster DW

took a closer look, they found that the

chart didn't name a data source, didn't

say which online platform it covered,

and didn't say what it was measuring.

这张图表是在周日发布的，而数据暴跌一直持续到14日周三。当德国广播公司DW的事实核查员仔细检查时，他们发现图表没有注明数据来源，没有说明它覆盖了哪个在线平台，也没有说明它在测量什么。

Its title was daily activity over 3

months. Activity of what? It didn't say.

In fact, the chart didn't even mention

the AFD.

图表的标题是“3个月内的日常活动”。什么的活动？它没说。事实上，图表甚至没有提到AFD。

The scaling on both axis was off. On the

vertical axis, the gap between 2,500 and

3,000 was shorter than all of the other

500 point gaps, which is one way to make

a decline looks steeper. The German

magazine Capital traced the chart back

to a threads account claiming to belong

to a 24year-old flight attendant, and DW

concluded that while AI involvement

couldn't be proven, it couldn't be ruled

out either.

两个坐标轴的刻度都有问题。在纵轴上，2500和3000之间的间隔比所有其他500点的间隔都要短，这是一种让下降看起来更陡峭的方式。德国《资本》杂志追查到图表源自一个自称属于一位24岁空乘的Threads账户，而DW的结论是，虽然无法证明有人工智能参与，但也不能排除这种可能性。

But still, if confirmed, it was a

devastating blow to the box. You can see

why people wanted to believe it.

但即便如此，如果得到证实，这对AFD来说是一个毁灭性的打击。你可以理解为什么人们愿意相信它。

That same weekend, the British prime

minister said in an interview that

Russia had interfered in the Brexit

referendum. With the US midterms just a

few weeks away, the idea that a few

well-dimemed drones could switch off

foreign propaganda and make social media

pleasant again was hard to resist.

同一个周末，英国首相在接受采访时表示，俄罗斯干预了英国脱欧公投。距离美国中期选举仅剩几周，几只精准投放的无人机就能切断外国宣传、让社交媒体重归愉快的想法，实在令人难以抗拒。

It was certainly a more decisive

approach to content moderation than

anything the tech companies have come up

with so far.

这无疑比科技公司迄今为止提出的任何内容审核方法都要果断有力。

So today, let's look at what those

drones actually hit, why the bots didn't

seem to notice, and the humrum business

of manufacturing online outrage.

所以今天，让我们来看看那些无人机到底击中了什么，为什么机器人似乎没有受到影响，以及制造网络愤怒的平淡无奇的生意。

So what

actually happened? Well, over the

previous month, Russia had been striking

data centers in Ukraine, something the

Russian Defense Ministry described on

Telegram as a digital detox. Ukraine

responded by sending drones into three

of the six major data centers run by

Yandex, which is usually described as

Russia's Google, but which also runs the

country's biggest taxi app, a food

delivery service, a payments business,

and its own AI chatbot.

那么实际发生了什么？在此前一个月，俄罗斯一直在攻击乌克兰的数据中心，俄罗斯国防部在Telegram上将此举描述为“数字排毒”。作为回应，乌克兰派出无人机袭击了Yandex运营的六大数据中心中的三个。Yandex通常被称为“俄罗斯的Google”，但它还运营着该国最大的打车应用、外卖服务、支付业务以及自己的人工智能聊天机器人。

Two of the sites were shut down completely and the

third lost several modules within hours.

People were sharing charts from

Cloudflare Radar showing Russian bot

traffic falling off a cliff. Euro news

calculated that automated traffic from

Yandex's network had dropped by about

94%.

其中两个站点完全关闭，第三个站点在数小时内损失了多个模块。人们开始分享来自Cloudflare Radar的图表，显示俄罗斯的机器人流量断崖式下跌。欧洲新闻（Euro news）计算得出，来自Yandex网络的自动化流量下降了约94%。

Now, Cloudfare is an American company

that sits between a large share of the

world's websites and the people visiting

them. They protect those sites from

attacks, and part of that job involves

guessing which visitors are human and

which are software.

Cloudflare是一家美国公司，它位于全球大量网站和访问者之间。它保护这些网站免受攻击，而其中的部分工作涉及判断哪些访客是人类，哪些是软件。

Because so much of

the internet passes through them,

Cloudfare publishes a public dashboard

called Radar, showing what they see. In

Cloudflare's world, a bot is any

automated online request. And before the

strikes, these made up more than a third

of all of the traffic they handle from

Russia.

由于大部分互联网流量都经过其网络，Cloudflare发布了一个名为Radar的公共仪表板，展示它们所看到的情况。在Cloudflare的语境中，机器人（bot）指的是任何自动化的在线请求。在袭击发生前，这些请求占他们处理的所有来自俄罗斯的流量的三分之一以上。

Most of them are search engine

crawlers and other scripts, which tend

to be a lot more boring than online

trolls. According to Cloudflare, the

traffic that collapsed was almost

entirely Yandex's search crawlers.

programs like Yandexbot, which spent

their days reading news websites so that

the search engine knows what's on them.

其中大多数是搜索引擎爬虫和其他脚本，它们往往比网络水军（trolls）要乏味得多。根据Cloudflare的说法，崩溃的流量几乎完全是Yandex的搜索爬虫——例如Yandexbot这样的程序，它们整天都在读取新闻网站，以便搜索引擎知道网页内容。

So, the internet had spent the weekend

celebrating the destruction of a search

engine's filing clerk, one that around

40% of the news sites it visited had

already blocked anyway. Yandex's entire

search network came to about 0.05%

of the world's bot traffic.

于是，整个互联网都在周末庆祝一个搜索引擎的“档案管理员”被摧毁——而事实上，它访问的新闻网站中约有40%本来就已经屏蔽了它。Yandex的全部搜索网络流量仅占全球机器人流量的约0.05%。

Cloudflare then checked the thing people cared the

most about traffic to social media sites

including the parts of a platform that

handle posting replying and liking from

Russia or from anywhere else. They found

no unexpected change. They were careful

to point out that they can only see

traffic passing through their own

network. So they couldn't rule out a

broader effect. But as far as they could

see, the troll farms hadn't missed a

single day's work.

随后，Cloudflare检查了人们最关心的东西——社交媒体网站的流量，包括平台上处理来自俄罗斯或其他地区发帖、回复和点赞的部分。他们没有发现任何意外变化。他们谨慎地指出，他们只能看到经过自己网络的流量，因此无法排除更广泛的影响。但就他们所观察到的情况来看，水军农场一天的工作都没有落下。

The drone strikes did

plenty of damage, though. According to

the FT, users of Yandex's payment app

woke up to find that their balances had

vanished. Timetables disappeared from

bus stops in some cities and residents

of many Moscow apartment blocks found

themselves locked out of their own

courtyards because the barrier gates

were run by a company that relied on

Yandex.

不过，无人机袭击确实造成了不小的破坏。据《金融时报》报道，Yandex支付应用的用户醒来后发现余额消失了。一些城市的公交车站时刻表消失了，许多莫斯科公寓楼的居民发现自己被锁在自家院子外面，因为闸门是由一家依赖Yandex的公司运营的。
