【帖子标题】：China proposes export controls on 23 AI technologies (including large models, agents & clusters); already open-sourced tech is exempted

【帖子标题】：中国拟对23项人工智能技术实施出口管制（包括大模型、智能体及算力集群）；已开源技术不受限制

【帖子正文】：

On October 16, 2026, China's Ministry of Commerce and Ministry of Science and Technology released a draft revision to the Catalog of Technologies Prohibited and Restricted from Export. It proposes adding 40 technologies across AI, biomedicine, and solar PV, of which 23 are AI-related (covering foundation/large models, intelligent agents, computing clusters, and information processing technologies).

【帖子正文】：

2026年10月16日，中国商务部与科技部发布了《禁止出口限制出口技术目录》修订草案，拟在人工智能、生物医药和光伏领域新增40项技术，其中23项与人工智能相关（涵盖基础模型/大模型、智能体、算力集群及信息处理技术）。

Public comments are open until November 15. Temporary pre-export registration requirements for the covered technologies take effect immediately.

公众意见征集截止至11月15日。所涉技术的临时出口前登记要求立即生效。

Key points relevant to open-weight models:

与开放权重模型相关的要点：

• Technologies already made public via open-source releases or academic publications are explicitly exempted and not subject to the controls.

• 已通过开源发布或学术发表公开的技术明确豁免，不受管制约束。

• Open-sourcing AI technologies, plus certain other activities (e.g., Chinese firms building their own overseas compute centers), are eligible for streamlined automatic licensing.

• 开源人工智能技术及某些其他活动（例如中国企业在海外自建算力中心）可享受简化自动许可。

• Providing access via APIs, apps, websites, or SaaS is generally not treated as a technology export.

• 通过API、应用程序、网站或SaaS提供访问通常不被视为技术出口。

• Overseas local deployment of restricted models can fall under the rules (with possible facilitation in lower-risk cases such as a company's own overseas cloud).

• 受限模型的海外本地部署可能受规则约束（在低风险情形下可能获得便利，例如企业自有的海外云）。

This is not a blanket ban on open-weight models. However, new models that have not yet been released may face registration or approval steps before their weights or source code can be transferred abroad (thresholds based on performance, parameters, data sensitivity, etc. are still being defined). Chinese labs such as DeepSeek and Qwen could see additional compliance requirements on future open releases.

这并非对开放权重模型的全面禁令。然而，尚未发布的新模型在权重或源代码转移至海外之前，可能面临登记或审批程序（基于性能、参数、数据敏感性等的门槛仍在制定中）。DeepSeek、Qwen等中国实验室未来进行开源发布时可能面临额外的合规要求。
