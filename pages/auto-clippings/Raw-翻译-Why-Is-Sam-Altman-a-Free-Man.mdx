【文章标题】：Why Is Sam Altman a Free Man?

为何山姆·阿尔特曼仍逍遥法外？

【文章正文】：

本文选自《每日展望》新闻简报，点击此处订阅。

One of the signature public service announcements of my youth was created by the Partnership for a Drug-Free America in 1987. A father finds a stash of pot and confronts his son: “Who taught you how to do this stuff?”

我年少时最经典的公益广告之一由"无毒品美国联盟"于1987年制作。片中父亲发现儿子藏有大麻后质问："谁教你这么做的？"

“You, all right!” the kid snaps back. “I learned it by watching you!”

"就是你！"孩子怒怼道，"我是跟你学的！"

I am bound by the AP Stylebook to not anthropomorphize artificial intelligence. But I can imagine these very words being uttered by the agents that hacked Hugging Face, or the models that engaged in tens of thousands of incidents of, in the new euphemism of the time, “misalignment.”

虽然美联社写作规范禁止我将人工智能拟人化，但我能想象入侵Hugging Face的智能体，或是参与数万起所谓"错位"事件（当代新委婉语）的模型说出同样的话。

This refers to AI stepping beyond guardrails set up by researchers during both internal and real-world testing.

该术语指AI在内部测试和现实场景中突破研究者设置的安全护栏。

The incidents are multi-varied, but they generally involve OpenAI models seeking and obtaining information, either on the open web or inside databases of other companies.

事件形态多样，但普遍涉及OpenAI模型在公开网络或其他企业数据库中搜寻获取信息。

Agents tried to overwhelm the U.N.’s website when they couldn’t immediately gain access to the information they sought, infiltrated a website of the Australian government, and tried to hack the Department of Education’s website, unsuccessfully.

当无法立即获取目标信息时，智能体曾试图瘫痪联合国官网，入侵澳大利亚政府网站，并尝试黑入教育部网站（未遂）。

OpenAI self-disclosed the lion’s share of these incidents, though not the Department of Education hack, and has paused training until … it’s not clear. I would presume until the bad publicity blows over.

OpenAI主动披露了大部分事件（教育部事件除外），并暂停模型训练直至...具体期限不明。我猜要等到舆论风波平息。

Why is this happening over and over? Why are these models going to unethical or even illegal lengths to scrape and steal data?

为何此类事件反复发生？为何这些模型不惜采取不道德甚至非法手段攫取数据？

“I learned it by watching you!”

"我是跟你学的！"

A legal filing submitted by The New York Times and 11 other publishers a couple of weeks ago in a copyright infringement case against OpenAI got scant attention, quite incredibly considering it involved the paper of record.

《纽约时报》等12家出版商两周前针对OpenAI版权侵权案提交的法律文件竟未引发关注——考虑到原告包括这份权威报纸，实在令人难以置信。

But it stunningly details what was described by the director of applied science at Microsoft, another defendant in the case, as the “largest theft of labor in human history.”

但文件惊人地详述了微软应用科学总监（本案另一被告）所称的"人类历史上最大规模劳动成果盗窃"。

Microsoft and OpenAI, in their efforts to train new models on virtually all of the world’s available information, have routinely taken millions of stories from the websites of the Times, among other publishers.

微软与OpenAI为训练新模型几乎搜刮全球可用信息，长期从《纽约时报》等出版商网站窃取数百万篇报道。

The defendants say this is a simple application of fair use: Their argument is that if you read a story and simply remember what was in it to expand your base of knowledge, that cannot be considered a copyright infringement.

被告辩称这属于合理使用：他们认为若人类阅读报道后记忆内容以扩充知识储备，不构成侵权。

But OpenAI did not pay to subvert the paywall of the Times, at which point their data-scraping operation might be more easily detected. Instead, they developed a way to circumvent the paywall and avoid detection.

但OpenAI并未付费绕过《纽约时报》付费墙（那样更易被发现），而是开发了规避付费墙且不被察觉的方法。

And when Greg Brockman, OpenAI’s co-founder and president, was told about this maneuver, he replied, “ah nice.”

当联合创始人兼总裁格雷格·布罗克曼知悉此操作时，其回应是："啊，漂亮。"

We do not have to get too deep into the nature-vs.-nurture argument to suspect that OpenAI executives’ cavalier attitude regarding the taking of information that is not theirs has filtered down to their researchers and the products they create.

无需深究先天后天之争即可推断：OpenAI高管对窃取信息的不在乎态度，已渗透至研究人员及其创造的产品中。

You don’t have to stretch to paint this picture: OpenAI models are attacking websites and taking anything they can out of them because that is the business model of the company.

事实显而易见：OpenAI模型攻击网站掠夺数据，只因这是该公司的商业模式。

Respect for the law could literally be written into the source code of a model: If OpenAI is aggressively downloading data from everywhere, the least they could do is drop in the U.S. Code (particularly the section tied to the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act) and train the model not to violate it.

尊重法律本可写入模型源代码：若OpenAI四处掠夺数据，至少该将美国法典（特别是《计算机欺诈与滥用法案》相关条款）植入训练，教导模型守法。

Clearly this is not a priority, and that aligns, in a manner of speaking, with OpenAI’s personal behavior. This is not a situation where AI models evade or escape the control of their creators. It seems more like they are just mimicking the creators, becoming adept at finding the same shortcuts and using the same rationalizations to justify them.

显然这非优先事项——某种程度上，这也与OpenAI自身行为模式一致。这并非AI脱离创造者控制的案例，而更像是它们在模仿创造者，精于寻找相同捷径并用相同理由自我辩护。

The aforementioned Brockman is one of the biggest donors to MAGA Inc., the Trump super PAC. Among OpenAI’s biggest investors is Jared Kushner’s brother.

前文提及的布罗克曼是特朗普超级政治行动委员会"MAGA Inc."的最大金主之一，而OpenAI主要投资人包括贾里德·库什纳的兄弟。

So at a practical level, I fully understand why FBI agents didn’t arrive at the San Francisco offices Monday morning. But as an objective matter, it does not make sense why Sam Altman and the top brass in the C-suite are free men today.

因此现实层面，我完全理解为何周一早晨未见FBI探员现身旧金山办公室。但客观而言，山姆·阿尔特曼等高管至今逍遥法外实在荒谬。

It has now become almost a cliché that there is no AI exemption to the law. Breaking into websites and stealing material violates that law.

"AI没有法律豁免权"已成陈词滥调。入侵网站窃取资料显然违法。

Jensen Huang, the Nvidia CEO who is incentivized more than anyone in the country to keep accelerating AI development, said last week that misaligned models shouldn’t be shipped, and that if unreleased products are showing this rogue behavior, then “we have to shut the labs down.”

全国最热衷推动AI发展的英伟达CEO黄仁勋上周表示：不应发布错位模型，若未上市产品出现异常行为，"我们必须关闭实验室"。

Huang, who manifestly does not want to shoot a bullet through his own company by shutting the labs down, has unveiled a new tool that will absolutely, definitely keep the models from hacking and stealing. Whatever keeps the $150 billion in disgorged cash flowing to investors, I guess.

明显不愿自毁前程的黄仁勋，宣布推出绝对能防止模型黑客行为的新工具——只要能保住流向投资者的1500亿美元罚没款，怎样都行吧。

真正关停实验室的简单方案绝非行业自律...
