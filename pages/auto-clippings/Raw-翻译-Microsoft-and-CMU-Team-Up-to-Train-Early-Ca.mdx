【文章标题】：Microsoft and CMU Team Up to Train Early Career Software Engineers

【文章标题】：微软与卡内基梅隆大学合作培养早期职业软件工程师

【文章正文】：

By Marylee Williams

作者：Marylee Williams

In between classes on machine learning and software engineering, Jace Watson isn't heading back to his dorm or finding a quiet place to study somewhere on Carnegie Mellon University's campus.

在机器学习与软件工程课程之间，Jace Watson并没有回到宿舍，也没有在卡内基梅隆大学校园里找个安静的地方学习。

That's because Watson isn't even in Pittsburgh. He's in Seattle, working at his full-time job as a software engineer at Microsoft and taking classes through the Preceptorship for AI-accelerated Software Engineering (PRAISE) program, which CMU and Microsoft launched to train early career software engineers for the rapidly changing computer science landscape.

那是因为Watson根本不在匹兹堡。他在西雅图，全职担任微软的软件工程师，并通过“AI加速软件工程导师计划”（PRAISE）项目上课。该项目由CMU与微软联合推出，旨在为快速变化的计算机科学领域培养早期职业软件工程师。

"When I started undergraduate, it was the beginning of AI being something that everybody had access to on their own computers. As I got closer to graduation, it started becoming clear how big AI tools would be in the workforce, but using AI wasn't a huge part of my education," Watson said. "When I joined Microsoft, these tools were part of my day-to-day, and I wanted to make sure I knew how to fully take advantage of them, which is why I sought out the additional education opportunity through CMU and Microsoft."

“当我开始本科学习时，AI刚刚开始成为每个人都能在自己电脑上使用的工具。随着我临近毕业，AI工具在工作中的重要性日益凸显，但使用AI并不是我教育中的主要部分，”Watson说。“当我加入微软后，这些工具成了我日常工作的一部分，我想确保自己知道如何充分利用它们，因此我通过CMU和微软寻求了额外的教育机会。”

The CMU/Microsoft PRAISE program offers early career Microsoft employees a chance to take online courses in CMU's Kenneth C. Griffin School of Computer Science while receiving hands-on mentorship at the company. At Microsoft, selected early career engineers, or preceptees, work with senior engineering preceptors to build AI-accelerated software engineering skills through structured guidance, mentorship and application to their day-to-day Microsoft work. Participants can also enroll in SCS classes focused on software engineering and artificial intelligence.

CMU/微软PRAISE项目为微软的早期职业员工提供了在CMU肯尼斯·C·格里芬计算机科学学院（SCS）学习在线课程的机会，同时在公司接受实践指导。在微软，被选中的早期职业工程师（即学员）与资深工程导师合作，通过结构化指导、辅导以及将其应用于日常微软工作，来培养AI加速软件工程技能。参与者还可以注册SCS专注于软件工程和人工智能的课程。

"This partnership with Microsoft is about providing the Griffin School of Computer Science's high-quality computer science learning to people who aren't in the traditional education system but are seeking guidance to navigate a rapidly changing landscape," said SCS Dean Martial Hebert. "SCS and Microsoft recognize that we all have a part to play in supporting and readying the nation's workforce, and the CMU/Microsoft PRAISE program is an innovative step in that direction."

“与微软的合作旨在将格里芬计算机科学学院的高质量计算机科学学习提供给那些不在传统教育体系中、但正在寻求指导以应对快速变化环境的人们，”SCS院长Martial Hebert表示。“SCS和微软都认识到，我们都有责任支持和培养国家劳动力，CMU/微软PRAISE项目正是朝着这一方向迈出的创新一步。”

The idea for the program came about following CMU alumnus Mark Russinovich's (ENG 1989, 1994) visit to SCS in October 2025. The chief technology officer, deputy chief information security officer and technical fellow for Microsoft Azure spoke about how AI tools help senior engineers, but noted that the same level of benefit isn't available for junior engineers.

该项目的想法源于CMU校友Mark Russinovich（1989年、1994年毕业）于2025年10月访问SCS之后。这位微软Azure的首席技术官、副首席信息安全官和技术研究员谈到了AI工具如何帮助资深工程师，但也指出初级工程师无法获得同等程度的益处。

"AI changes the work, but it doesn't eliminate the need for junior engineers or for human judgment and accountability," Russinovich said. "By combining Microsoft's engineering experience with CMU's academic expertise, PRAISE offers a model we're proud to lead and hope others in industry and academia will adopt."

“AI改变了工作，但并没有消除对初级工程师或人类判断力和责任心的需求，”Russinovich说。“通过将微软的工程经验与CMU的学术专长相结合，PRAISE提供了一个我们自豪地引领的模式，并希望业界和学术界其他人能够采纳。”

From that discussion, SCS and Microsoft began considering how education could help junior engineers overcome the challenges of using AI in the workplace.

从那次讨论开始，SCS和微软开始考虑教育如何帮助初级工程师克服在工作场所使用AI的挑战。

"It used to be that early career software engineers would do a lot of tasks that would build up their expertise," said Michael Hilton, a teaching professor in CMU's Software and Societal Systems Department (S3D). "Combining forces between academics and practitioners allows us to bring our expertise together and create solutions that draw on computer science research while standing up to the daily realities of software development."

“过去，早期职业软件工程师会承担许多任务，这些任务能积累他们的专业知识，”CMU软件与社会系统系（S3D）的教学教授Michael Hilton说。“学术界与从业者的联合使我们能够汇聚各自的专长，创造出既借鉴计算机科学研究又经得起软件开发日常现实考验的解决方案。”

This semester, Microsoft employees enrolled in the CMU PRAISE certificate program are taking Machine Learning in Production and Preceptorship in AI-Augmented Software Engineering I. These classes train students on how to incorporate machine learning into the software they write and to use AI tools while developing that software.

本学期，参加CMU PRAISE证书项目的微软员工正在学习《生产环境中的机器学习》和《AI增强软件工程导师课I》。这些课程训练学生如何将机器学习融入他们编写的软件中，并在开发该软件时使用AI工具。

"That judgment for when to use AI should be viewed as ongoing education, which is why we're partnering with Carnegie Mellon University on curriculum as part of the PRAISE program," Microsoft wrote in their announcement of the partnership. "The intent is to set students up for success in the workplace and accelerate preceptees with formal curricula to complement the guidance from the preceptor on how to build and deliver with confidence."

“何时使用AI的判断力应被视为持续教育的一部分，这就是我们与卡内基梅隆大学合作开发PRAISE项目课程的原因，”微软在合作公告中写道。“其目的是帮助学生在职场中取得成功，并通过正式课程加速学员的成长，以补充导师关于如何自信地构建和交付产品的指导。”

In the spring semester, students will take DevOps: Engineering for Secure Development and Deployment and the second half of the Preceptorship in AI-Augmented Software Engineering course sequence. These courses can be applied to a master's degree in software engineering, allowing students to further their learning at SCS. CMU hopes to grow the program and add more students to the certificate program, perhaps one day opening the door to other industry partners.

春季学期，学生将学习《DevOps：安全开发与部署工程》以及《AI增强软件工程导师课》系列课程的后半部分。这些课程可计入软件工程硕士学位，使学生能够在SCS继续深造。CMU希望扩大该项目，增加更多学生参加证书课程，也许有一天会向其他行业合作伙伴敞开大门。

"I am tremendously excited about the impact this program will have on junior engineers at Microsoft," said Jonathan Aldrich, an S3D professor who directs the PRAISE certificate program. "With so many companies worldwide faced with upheaval due to AI in software development, this program is a great example of how academia and industry can work together to prepare the next generation of software engineers."

“我对这个项目将对微软初级工程师产生的影响感到非常兴奋，”S3D教授、PRAISE证书项目负责人Jonathan Aldrich说。“全球众多公司因AI在软件开发中的应用而面临剧变，这个项目是学术界与工业界如何合作培养下一代软件工程师的一个绝佳范例。”
