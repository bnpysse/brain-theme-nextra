How Leaders Can Take Action  
领导者如何采取行动  

High-performing organizations don’t just develop strong leaders. They build teams that operate with alignment and shared discipline to help them execute faster on their priorities.  
高绩效组织不仅培养强大的领导者，还建立具有一致性和共同纪律的团队，以帮助他们更快地执行优先事项。  

This research demonstrates that there are specific, actionable steps leaders can take to create an environment of accountability. When team members get clarity, have a high-level view of the company’s context and strategy, and are equipped to handle tough situations, they are better able to take ownership together.  
这项研究表明，领导者可以采取具体的、可操作的步骤来创建责任感的环境。当团队成员明确目标、对公司背景和战略有高层次的理解，并具备处理艰难情况的能力时，他们更能共同承担责任。
