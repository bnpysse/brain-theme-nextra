【文章标题】：Ranked: Which Industries Have More Women Than Men?

【文章标题】：排名：哪些行业的女性多于男性？

Are Industries Male-Dominated or Women-Dominated?

各行业是男性主导还是女性主导？

Key Takeaways:

核心要点：

Women outnumber men in four of 18 industries, led by healthcare and care services (60%) and education (54%).

在18个行业中，有4个行业的女性人数超过男性，其中医疗保健和护理服务（60%）和教育（54%）位居前列。

Infrastructure (24%) and oil, gas, and mining (25%) have the lowest shares of women workers.

基础设施（24%）以及石油、天然气和采矿（25%）行业的女性员工占比最低。

Women account for 46% of entry-level workers but just 23% of C-suite executives.

女性占入门级员工的46%，但在最高管理层中仅占23%。

Women have made significant gains in education and employment, but their representation in the workforce still varies widely by industry.

女性在教育和就业方面取得了显著进步，但她们在劳动力中的占比仍因行业不同而存在巨大差异。

Healthcare and care services has the highest share of women workers at 60%, followed by education (54%), consumer services (52%), and government and the public sector (51%).

医疗保健和护理服务行业的女性员工占比最高，达到60%，其次是教育（54%）、消费服务（52%）以及政府和公共部门（51%）。

At the other end of the ranking, infrastructure has the lowest share at 24%, a 36-percentage-point gap from healthcare and care services.

在排名的另一端，基础设施行业的女性占比最低，仅为24%，与医疗保健和护理服务行业相差36个百分点。

为什么医疗保健和教育行业领先？

Why Are Blue-Collar Industries Dominated by Men?

为什么蓝领行业由男性主导？

Infrastructure, mining, manufacturing, and transportation remain heavily male-dominated. Traditional apprenticeship pathways, occupational stereotypes, and workplace culture all contribute to this divide.

基础设施、采矿、制造业和交通运输行业仍然严重由男性主导。传统的学徒路径、职业刻板印象和工作场所文化都导致了这种分化。

Representation Doesn't Guarantee Leadership

占比高并不保证领导职位

Workforce representation tells only part of the story. Across the dataset, women account for 46% of entry-level workers but just 23% of C-suite executives.

劳动力占比只说明了部分问题。在整个数据集中，女性占入门级员工的46%，但在最高管理层中仅占23%。

This gap highlights a separate challenge from workforce participation: advancement into senior leadership. Women continue to face barriers to promotion, including unequal access to career development opportunities and professional networks. These challenges can persist even in industries where women make up the majority of employees.

这一差距凸显了一个不同于劳动力参与率的挑战：晋升到高级领导职位。女性在晋升方面持续面临障碍，包括职业发展机会和职业人脉网络的不平等获取。即使在女性占员工多数的行业中，这些挑战也可能依然存在。
