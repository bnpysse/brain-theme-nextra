【文章标题】：Show HN: Koi.rest – 观赏锦鲤，重拾内心平静

【文章正文】：
My name is Paul. I’m a developer, an explorer and I’ve got ADHD. As of
          the beginning of August, I’m also unemployed. This last part, along
          with everything else this past year, has caused me a great deal of
          stress.
我是Paul。一名开发者、探索者，同时也患有注意力缺陷多动症。八月初开始，我又成了失业大军中的一员。最后这个身份，连同过去一年发生的所有事，都给我带来了巨大的压力。

While our balcony zen garden project is yet to be completed, I had an
          idea to create a virtual one that everyone can use.
          It's an idea that, unfortunately, I’ve been postponing for a while
          now, mostly because I have no fucking idea how to do it as I don’t
          know JavaScript, and I don't have the capacity to learn it right now.
          So I let perfect be the enemy of good and, well... just kept the idea
          to myself.
虽然阳台禅意花园项目尚未完工，但我萌生了一个创建虚拟禅意空间的想法。可惜这个想法已被搁置多时——主要因为我他妈根本不懂JavaScript，现阶段也没精力学习。于是完美主义成了行动力的敌人，这个点子就一直尘封在脑海里。

Then I said "fuck it" and used AI to make the thing I really
          wanted to make. I realized I didn't want "perfect". I
          wanted "good enough". I tweaked, added, removed, drew, researched,
          questioned, tested... I just wasn't the one coding it.
          So now, instead of occupying my brain, it now lives on the internet
          for others to enjoy.
后来我决定"去他妈的"，直接用AI实现了这个创意。我意识到自己不需要"完美"，只要"足够好"就行。调整、增删、手绘、调研、质疑、测试...只是这次不用亲自写代码。现在这个项目不再占据我的大脑，而是存在于网络供大家欣赏。

Yes, there’s something noble about making something entirely on your
          own, but what good is an idea that just sits in my head?
          So here I am. I made the thing. The weird, little, quiet koi pond.
          The silly project of passion. The little corner of the internet to let
          strangers watch fish quietly, together.
独立完成创作确实很酷，但烂在脑子里的点子有什么用？于是就有了这个作品：古怪、小巧而宁静的锦鲤池。这个充满热血的傻气项目，成了互联网的静谧角落，让陌生人能一起静静观鱼。

I hope this pond helps you as much as it helped me.
愿这个池塘能像治愈我一样治愈你。
