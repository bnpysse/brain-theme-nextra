Solar surpasses nuclear power globally for first time

太阳能发电量首次在全球范围内超越核电

The World Nuclear Industry Status Report (WNISR) 2026, overseen by French nuclear energy consultant Mycle Schneider, shows that wind power alone surpassed nuclear generation on a monthly basis in March 2025, followed by solar in April – the first time global monthly solar generation exceeded nuclear output.

由法国核能顾问迈尔·施奈德主持编写的《世界核工业现状报告》2026年版显示，2025年3月，仅风力发电一项就在月度发电量上超过了核电，随后太阳能发电在4月也实现了超越——这是全球太阳能月度发电量首次超过核电产出。

From March 2025 through June 2026, either solar or wind individually generated more electricity than nuclear every month. In March, April and May 2026, both technologies independently surpassed nuclear generation.

从2025年3月至2026年6月，太阳能或风能中的任意一种，其月度发电量均超过了核电。在2026年3月、4月和5月，这两种技术分别独立超越了核电发电量。

Increasing gap

差距日益扩大

According to the report, the gap between renewables and nuclear widened substantially in 2025. Global solar capacity increased by 510 GW to 2,383 GW, up 27% year on year, while wind added a record 159 GW to reach 1,291 GW, an increase of 14%. By comparison, installed nuclear capacity rose by just 1.5 GW to 399.5 GW, while operating nuclear capacity declined by 0.5 GW to 368.6 GW. The report notes that nuclear capacity has remained essentially flat for more than 25 years, while wind and, particularly, solar have expanded rapidly.

报告显示，2025年可再生能源与核电之间的差距显著拉大。全球太阳能装机容量增加了510吉瓦，达到2383吉瓦，同比增长27%；风电新增装机创纪录地达到159吉瓦，总装机容量增至1291吉瓦，增幅为14%。相比之下，核电装机容量仅增加了1.5吉瓦，达到399.5吉瓦，而运行中的核电容量则减少了0.5吉瓦，降至368.6吉瓦。报告指出，核电容量在超过25年的时间里基本保持平稳，而风电，尤其是太阳能，则实现了快速增长。

The report’s PV figures, however, are based on relatively conservative estimates. More recent data from IEA PVPS indicate that global PV additions reached around 690 GW in 2025, about 180 GW more than the figure used by WNISR. This suggests the gap between solar and nuclear capacity – and their respective growth rates – was even wider than indicated in the report.

然而，报告中的光伏数据基于相对保守的估算。来自国际能源署光伏电力系统项目的最新数据显示，2025年全球光伏新增装机容量约为690吉瓦，比《世界核工业现状报告》使用的数据高出约180吉瓦。这表明太阳能与核电之间的装机容量差距——以及它们各自的增长率差距——比报告中所显示的还要大。

“The reality of the nuclear industry on the ground is quite sobering for those who expected to find a booming sector,” Schneider told pv magazine. “Recently, U.S. nuclear company Holtec put an initial public offering (IPO) on hold just days before its planned stock market listing, through which it had hoped to raise almost $1 billion. Its peer, Fermi, has lost about 80% of its stock value since it was first listed in October 2025.”

“对于那些期待看到核能行业蓬勃发展的人来说，核工业的现实状况相当令人清醒，”施奈德告诉《光伏》杂志。“最近，美国核能公司霍尔台克在计划上市前几天暂停了首次公开募股，该公司原本希望通过此次IPO筹集近10亿美元。其同行费米公司自2025年10月首次上市以来，股价已下跌约80%。”

“The main reason? These companies have little or nothing to show on the ground to convince investors to continue providing cash,” he added. “Meanwhile, the world’s operating nuclear fleet increased output by 1.1% – with output declining outside China – while global solar power generation jumped by a stunning 30%, surpassing nuclear’s annual electricity production for the first time. One is tempted to say: It’s the growth rates, stupid!”

“主要原因是什么？这些公司在实际运营中几乎拿不出什么成果来说服投资者继续提供资金，”他补充道。“与此同时，全球在运核电机组的发电量仅增长了1.1%——中国以外的地区发电量还在下降——而全球太阳能发电量却惊人地增长了30%，首次超过了核电的年发电量。人们不禁想说：关键在于增长率，笨蛋！”

The report identifies falling battery costs as a potential enabler of continued PV expansion, particularly as grids contend with growing volumes of variable renewable generation and periods of low or negative electricity prices. Grid-scale battery deployment accelerated sharply in 2025, with 104 GW and 257 GWh connected during the year. More than 78% of cumulative battery energy storage system (BESS) capacity was installed between 2023 and 2025.

报告指出，电池成本下降是光伏持续扩张的潜在推动因素，尤其是在电网面临日益增长的可变可再生能源发电量以及低电价或负电价时期的情况下。2025年，电网级电池部署急剧加速，当年新增并网容量达104吉瓦和257吉瓦时。累计电池储能系统容量的78%以上是在2023年至2025年间安装的。

Slow expansion

缓慢扩张

The report shows that the global nuclear sector is expanding slowly, with most new activity concentrated in China and Russia. As of July 1, 2026, 411 reactors were operating in 31 countries, excluding 30 units in long-term outage, with operating capacity at 374.7 GW. In 2025, nuclear plants generated 2,703 TWh, up 1.1% year on year, largely driven by a 7.6% increase in China. Outside China, nuclear generation declined by 0.2% and remained well below its 2006 level. Nuclear’s share of global electricity generation slipped to 8.8%, its lowest level in four and a half decades and roughly half its 1996 peak of 17.5%.

报告显示，全球核能行业扩张缓慢，新增活动主要集中在中国和俄罗斯。截至2026年7月1日，31个国家共有411座反应堆在运行（不包括30座长期停运机组），运行容量为374.7吉瓦。2025年，核电站发电量为2703太瓦时，同比增长1.1%，主要受中国7.6%的增幅推动。在中国以外地区，核电发电量下降了0.2%，且仍远低于2006年的水平。核电在全球发电量中的占比下滑至8.8%，为45年来的最低水平，约为1996年峰值17.5%的一半。

Four reactors started supplying electricity in 2025, while seven closed, resulting in the first negative annual reactor balance since 2021. Between 2006 and 2025, 104 reactors started up and 106 closed. China accounted for 53 of those startups and no closures, meaning the rest of the world recorded a net decline of 55 reactors. Although global nuclear capacity increased by a net 21.5 GW over the two decades, China alone added 52.3 GW, while capacity outside the country fell by almost 30.7 GW.

2025年，有4座反应堆开始供电，而7座关闭，导致自2021年以来首次出现年度反应堆净减少。2006年至2025年间，共有104座反应堆启动，106座关闭。其中中国启动了53座，且没有关闭任何一座，这意味着世界其他地区净减少了55座反应堆。尽管在这二十年里全球核电容量净增加了21.5吉瓦，但仅中国就增加了52.3吉瓦，而中国以外的容量减少了近30.7吉瓦。

The construction pipeline has nevertheless expanded. Seventy-three reactors were under construction as of July 1, the highest number since 1987, with China accounting for 37. Russia remains the dominant international supplier, with Russian technology involved in 29 reactors under construction worldwide, including 22 outside Russia. Nine out of 10 reactors being built are in Asia or Eastern Europe.

尽管如此，在建项目数量有所增加。截至7月1日，共有73座反应堆在建，为1987年以来的最高数量，其中中国占37座。俄罗斯仍是主要的国际供应商，全球在建的29座反应堆涉及俄罗斯技术，其中22座位于俄罗斯境外。在建反应堆中，十分之九位于亚洲或东欧。

Long construction times and delays remain a feature of new nuclear projects, according to the report. None of the 16 reactors connected to the grid between 2023 and 2025 was completed on schedule, with an average construction period of 10.9 years. Of 13 reactors scheduled to start generating electricity in 2025, only four did so. Some projects have experienced exceptionally long timelines, including Slovakia’s Mochovce-4, where construction began more than 40 years ago, and Iran’s Bushehr-2, where construction originally started in 1976.

报告指出，建设周期长和工期延误仍是新建核电项目的特点。2023年至2025年间并网的16座反应堆中，没有一座按期完工，平均建设周期为10.9年。在计划于2025年开始发电的13座反应堆中，只有4座实现了这一目标。一些项目经历了异常漫长的工期，包括斯洛伐克的莫霍夫采4号机组，其建设始于40多年前；以及伊朗的布什尔2号机组，其建设最初始于1976年。
