Ancient & Classical  
古代与古典  

Classical Greek Tactics: A Cultural History, Roel Konijnendijk, 2018. Covered in an upcoming podcast. Fun to read a book where from the first five pages it’s clear the author has an absolute command of his sources.  
《古典希腊战术：一部文化史》，罗埃尔·科宁戴克，2018年。将在后续播客中讨论。读这本书很有趣，因为开篇五页就能看出作者对史料有着绝对的掌控力。  

My favorite riff was his exploration of the debate around what actually happened at the Battle of Leuctra, Epaminondas’ famous victory over Sparta in 371 BC. Prussian historians tried to make him into another Frederick the Great grand tactical genius, but the sources just don’t give you enough color to know what it was he did to win this battle, so each historian came up with their own pet theory.  
我最喜欢的部分是他对留克特拉战役（公元前371年伊巴密浓达击败斯巴达的著名战役）真相争议的探讨。普鲁士历史学家试图将他塑造成另一个腓特烈大帝式的战术天才，但史料并未提供足够细节说明他如何取胜，因此每位史学家都提出了自己的偏爱理论。  

Roel also squared a lot of circles by emphasizing just how corny all Greeks save Spartans thought hoplite training was. Xenophon acknowledges that Athenians "not only neglect to make themselves fit, but mock at those who take the trouble to do so." Beyond general fitness, weapons training was practically non-existent, and all hoplites save Spartan ones couldn’t turn. This limitation limited just how creative generals could get with their tactics.  
罗埃尔还通过强调"除斯巴达人外，所有希腊人都认为重装步兵训练很老套"这一观点，解决了许多矛盾。色诺芬承认雅典人"不仅忽视锻炼身体，还嘲笑那些认真训练的人"。除基础体能外，武器训练几乎不存在，且所有非斯巴达重装步兵都无法转向。这种局限限制了将领们的战术创意。

The World of Odysseus, Moses I. Finley, 1954. The history books that still get recommended from the 1950s are some of the best I’ve ever read. This era had writers with so much confidence and style!  
《奥德修斯的世界》，摩西·芬利，1954年。至今仍被推荐的1950年代历史书是我读过最棒的。那个时代的作家充满自信与风格！  

*Odyssey on audiobook!  
*《奥德赛》有声书推荐！  

Highly recommend listening to the Clare Danes’ Emily Wilson in parallel with the Ian McKellen’s Fagles rendition. Both bring completely different energies, highlighting their respective translators’ strongsuits. Listening gives you more of a Homeric experience and reminds you how fun and plotty this text is. Compared to the Old Testament, you get much more time for characters to breathe while still benefitting from the extreme economy you get in ancient literature.  
强烈建议同时聆听克莱尔·丹妮丝的艾米丽·威尔逊版与伊恩·麦克莱恩的费格勒斯版。两者风格迥异，各显译者所长。聆听能带来更浓厚的荷马史诗体验，让人想起文本的趣味性与情节性。相较《旧约》，它给角色更多喘息空间，同时保留古代文学特有的极致简练。

Korea & the DPRK  
朝鲜与朝鲜民主主义人民共和国  

Korean Messiah: Kim Il Sung and the Christian Roots of North Korea’s Personality Cult, Jonathan Cheng, 2026. Covered in this podcast.  
《朝鲜弥赛亚：金日成与朝鲜个人崇拜的基督教根源》，乔纳森·程，2026年。相关播客在此。  

Kim Il Sung in the Khrushchev Era: Soviet-DPRK Relations and the Roots of North Korean Despotism, 1953-1964, Balázs Szalontai, 2005.  
《赫鲁晓夫时代的金日成：苏朝关系与朝鲜专制根源（1953-1964）》，巴拉兹·萨隆泰，2005年。  

Deeply archival, it has some good lore and new perspectives on familiar political beats as it explores the question of why the DPRK got so weird while other divided communist countries like East Germany and North Vietnam didn’t grow into wacko personalist dictatorships.  
基于深度档案研究，本书在探讨"为何朝鲜变得如此怪异，而东德、北越等其他分裂的共产主义国家未发展成疯狂的个人独裁政权"时，提供了珍贵史料与新视角。

"In my view, one of the principal causes of North Korean despotism was the intense and xenophobic nationalism professed by Kim Il Sung and the former Manchurian guerrillas. Rooted in the latter’s personal experiences and reinforced by Japan’s post-1937 assimilationist policy, this nationalism greatly influenced Pyongyang’s actions toward both South Korea and the ‘communist camp.’ In turn, the goals the North Korean leadership pursued in the field of foreign policy produced a strong effect on its domestic policies. Furthermore, the excessive importance Kim Il Sung attached to direct participation in the armed revolutionary struggle against Japanese rule became a source of excessive elitism." After Stalin’s death, the purge of the Soviet and Yan’an groups in 1957–1959 subsequently "secured Pyongyang’s political independence from Moscow once and for all." (pp. 229, 246)
