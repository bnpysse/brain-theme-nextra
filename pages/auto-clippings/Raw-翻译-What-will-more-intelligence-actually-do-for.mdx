Your insights touch on several fascinating and complex topics related to technology's impact on human capabilities, knowledge transfer, and societal changes. Let me summarize and elaborate on your points:

### 1. **AI and Productivity: Beyond Raw Intelligence**
   - **Roboticization of the World**: You highlight how advances in battery technology have made it easier to store and move energy efficiently. This has enabled the transition from dumb matter (materials) to smart matter (intelligent robots). 
   - **Capital-Labor Ratio**: As we leverage physical capital, including AI and robots, alongside human labor, it's anticipated that the ratio of capital to labor will increase significantly in the long-term steady state. Humans can essentially leverage an "army" of intelligent machines.
   - **Distributed Tacit Knowledge**: You discuss how tacit (embodied) knowledge—knowledge embedded within individuals' experiences and techniques—is preserved within organizations, making it very difficult for competitors to replicate or steal.

### 2. **Leveraging AI for Technological Diffusion**
   - **AI's Capabilities**: You suggest that AI’s ability to quickly process and synthesize large amounts of distributed tacit knowledge could potentially accelerate the spread and adoption of advanced technologies compared to traditional methods.
   - **Solving Complex Problems**: The idea is that AI, through its computing power, can rapidly discover small improvements in processes and innovate more quickly. This could level the playing field between leading and lagging firms.

### 3. **AI, Cloud Laws, and Unfathomable Phenomena**
   - **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: You highlight how AI has made natural language processing seem almost a solved problem compared to traditional human approaches.
   - **Cloud Laws**: You introduce the concept of "cloud laws," suggesting that certain complex phenomena in nature follow diffuse and difficult-to-understand patterns but can be exploited by technology. This could potentially lead to new scientific insights or discoveries.

### 4. **AI as a Tool for Human Collaboration**
   - **Limitations on Group Intelligence**: While groups of humans might approach AI's level of competence, the communication bottleneck between individuals remains significant. AI doesn't face this limitation and can process vast amounts of data simultaneously.
   
### Why It Matters:
- **Reconciling Human and Machine Capabilities**: You argue that the real revolution in productivity won’t come from simply having machines smarter than humans; rather, it will involve integrating human intelligence with machine capabilities to solve problems more effectively.

### Limitations and Ethical Considerations:

1. **Human Role in AI Development**:
   - While AI can process vast amounts of data quickly, human input is still crucial for setting goals (what kind of math we want solved) and interpreting results.
   - Human oversight remains essential because the quality of solutions derived from AI will depend on initial prompts or instructions given by humans.

2. **Ethical Implications**:
   - As technology becomes more powerful, there are concerns about privacy, accountability, and how human values can be preserved in a world increasingly dominated by machines.
   - The idea that AI might discover patterns without understanding them could raise questions about whether these patterns reflect real truths or simply abstracted data.

### Conclusion:

AI's role as a tool for humans—augmenting our capabilities rather than replacing them—is likely to yield the greatest potential benefits. However, this integration must be carefully managed with ethical considerations in mind. The future promises exciting possibilities but also requires thoughtful navigation through complex ethical landscapes.
