【文章标题】：I'm Tired of the AI Tone  
【文章标题】：我厌倦了AI腔  

【文章正文】：  
I'm tired of the AI tone  
我厌倦了AI腔  

I'm tired of the AI tone.  
Not AI writing itself. AI is incredibly useful. I've probably used ChatGPT more this week than I'd like to admit.  
我厌倦的不是AI写作本身。AI非常有用——这周我用ChatGPT的次数多到不愿承认。  

I'm talking about the weird, increasingly recognizable way everything on the internet now sounds like it was written by the same extremely articulate 27-year-old who has never had a bad day.  
我指的是那种怪异的、越来越容易识别的网络文风：所有内容都像出自某个从未经历过坏日子的27岁精英之手。  

You know the tone.  
你们都懂这种腔调。  

Everything is a wedge.  
万物皆可"楔子"。  

Every idea is not just X, but Y.  
每个观点都要"不是X，而是Y"。  

Every paragraph contains an em dash — like this — because apparently the comma has been deprecated.  
每段都要用破折号——像这样——仿佛逗号已被淘汰。  

Everything is "a powerful shift."  
什么都是"重大转变"。  

Something doesn't just solve a problem. It "unlocks a new way to think about the problem."  
解决方案必须"解锁新的思考维度"。  

A product isn't useful. It "sits at the intersection of X and Y."  
产品要说"处于X与Y的交汇点"。  

A company doesn't build something. It "reimagines how we..."  
公司都在"重新构想我们..."  

And every post somehow ends with:  
每篇文章都会以这样的结尾收场：  

The future is already here.  
"未来已来"。  

Thanks, Claude.  
谢谢你啊，Claude。  

The AI vocabulary  
AI专用词汇表  

There are certain words that have become suspicious.  
有些词已经变得可疑起来。  

"Wedge" is probably the biggest one.  
"楔子"堪称罪魁祸首。  

Every startup now has a wedge.  
现在每个初创企业都有"楔子"。  

"We're starting with X as our wedge into Y."  
"我们将以X作为切入Y的楔子。"  

Nobody talks like this in real life.  
现实里根本没人这么说话。  

I've been in startups for a long time. I've sat in hundreds of product meetings. I've talked to founders, engineers, customers, and investors.  
我在初创企业摸爬滚打多年，参加过数百次产品会议，与创始人、工程师、客户和投资者都打过交道。  

I have almost never heard someone say:  
几乎从没听人说过：  

«"This is our wedge."»  
"这是我们的楔子。"  

But put the same person in front of ChatGPT and suddenly they're building a "land-and-expand wedge into the enterprise."  
但同一个人对着ChatGPT时，突然就开始打造"打入企业市场的登陆扩张楔子"。  

Other suspicious words include:  
其他可疑词汇包括：  

- unlock 解锁  
- leverage 利用/杠杆  
- ecosystem 生态系统  
- paradigm 范式  
- transformative 变革性  
- seamless 无缝  
- robust 稳健  
- thoughtfully 深思熟虑地  
- empower 赋能  
- foster 培养  
- nuanced 微妙  
- landscape 格局  
- intersection 交汇点  
- fundamentally 根本上  
- increasingly 日益  
- reimagine 重新构想  
- accelerate 加速  
- journey 旅程  

None of these words are bad.  
这些词本身都没问题。  

That's what makes it worse.  
这才更糟糕。  

They're perfectly normal words that AI has managed to make feel like corporate spam.  
它们本是普通词汇，却被AI用成了企业级垃圾话。  

And then there are the em dashes  
还有那些破折号  

Why are there so many fucking em dashes?  
为什么满屏都是该死的破折号？  

Seriously.  
说真的。  

AI loves an em dash.  
AI酷爱破折号。  

It uses them to connect thoughts that a normal human would separate into two sentences.  
正常人会分成两句话的内容，它偏要用破折号连接。  

Or a comma.  
或者用逗号。  

Or just leave disconnected because humans don't actually write with perfect transitions all the time.  
或者干脆不衔接——毕竟人类写作本就不追求永远过渡完美。  

AI writing has this strange obsession with making every sentence flow beautifully into the next one.  
AI写作有种诡异的执念：非要每句话都丝滑衔接下一句。  

Real people don't do that.  
真人不会这样。  

Sometimes we start a sentence badly.  
有时我们开头很糟。  

Sometimes we use parentheses.  
有时会用括号。  

Sometimes we repeat ourselves.  
有时会重复啰嗦。  

Sometimes we say "and" four times.  
有时连说四个"然后"。  

Sometimes we write:  
有时会写：  

«"I don't know. Maybe I'm wrong."»  
"我不知道。或许我错了。"  

And move on.  
然后继续写。  

That's writing.  
这才是写作。  

The perfect structure  
完美结构强迫症  

AI also loves structure.  
AI还痴迷结构。  

You can usually recognize an AI-generated post from about 50 feet away.  
五十步外你就能认出AI生成的文章。  

It starts with a provocative sentence.  
开头必是挑衅句式。  

Then:  
接着：  

Here's the thing:  
"关键在于："  

Then three or five perfectly balanced points.  
然后列出三五点完美对称的论点。  

Then a counterargument.  
再来段反驳。  

Then a thoughtful conclusion.  
接着是深思熟虑的结论。  

Then a sentence about how "this isn't about X. It's about Y."  
再来句"这不是X的问题，而是Y的问题"。  

Then the inspirational ending.  
最后以励志金句收尾。  

It's like every LinkedIn post has gone through the same screenwriting course.  
仿佛每篇LinkedIn文章都上了同一门编剧课。  

And God forbid you make a list.  
要是列清单就更可怕了。  

AI will give you exactly five bullets.  
AI必给你整整五点。  

Not four.  
不是四点。  

Not six.  
也不是六点。  

Five.  
必须是五点。  

Because five feels comprehensive.  
因为五点显得周全。  

The fake vulnerability  
虚假的脆弱性  

My favorite is the AI version of vulnerability.  
我最爱看AI版的"示弱"。  

"I used to think X.  
"我曾以为X。  

I was wrong.  
我错了。  

After spending years working on Y, I've come to realize Z."  
经过多年研究Y，我终于领悟Z。"  

That's not how people talk.  
真人根本不这么说话。  

That's how a machine imagines someone talks after analyzing 40,000 LinkedIn posts.  
这是机器分析四万篇LinkedIn文章后臆想的人类口吻。  

Real vulnerability is messier.  
真实的脆弱更混乱。  

"I completely screwed this up."  
"我彻底搞砸了。"  

"I thought this was going to work. It didn't."  
"本以为能成，结果凉了。"  

"I don't really know what I'm doing here."  
"其实我不知道自己在干嘛。"  

"I changed my mind."  
"我改主意了。"  

"I have no idea if this is right."  
"完全不确定对不对。"  

Those sentences are interesting because they're actually human.  
这些句子有趣，因为它们真实。  

The problem isn't that AI writes well  
问题不在于AI写得好  

Ironically, the writing is often technically better.  
讽刺的是，AI在技术上往往写得更好。  

That's the problem.  
这才是问题所在。  

AI removes all the little imperfections that tell you a human wrote something.  
AI抹去了所有泄露人类执笔痕迹的小缺陷。  

The weird phrasing.  
奇怪的措辞。  

The unnecessary joke.  
多余的玩笑。  

The sentence that goes on too long.  
冗长的句子。  

The opinion that doesn't quite fit the rest of the argument.  
格格不入的观点。  

The typo.  
错别字。  

The "I don't know how to explain this, but..."  
"不知该怎么解释，但..."  

Those things give writing a personality.  
这些才让文字有个性。  

AI optimizes them away.  
AI却把这些都优化掉了。  

And when everyone uses the same model, everyone optimizes toward the same mean.  
当所有人都用同个模型，就会优化成同一个模样。  

We are slowly converging on one universal Internet Voice™.  
我们正逐渐统一成"全网通用语音™"。  

Polite.  
礼貌。  

Clear.  
清晰。  

Structured.  
结构化。  

Nuanced.  
微妙。  

Optimistic.  
乐观。  

Slightly corporate.  
略带企业味。  

Occasionally profound.  
偶尔深刻。  

And completely fucking interchangeable.  
而且他妈的全能互相替换。  

Please sound like yourself  
请做你自己  

I'm not arguing that we should stop using AI to write.  
我不是说要禁用AI写作。  

Quite the opposite.  
正相反。  

Use it.  
尽管用。  

Have it rewrite your terrible first draft.  
让它重写你糟糕的初稿。  

Have it fix your grammar.  
让它修正语法。  

Have it make your argument clearer.  
让它理清论点。  

Have it turn your 2,000-word ramble into something people can actually read.  
让它把你两千字的碎碎念改成可读内容。  

But then put some of yourself back into it.  
但记得把你自己塞回去。  

Delete the em dashes.  
删掉破折号。  

Remove "unlock."  
去掉"解锁"。  

Kill the "not X, but Y."  
干掉"不是X而是Y"。  

Delete "wedge" unless you are actually talking about a wedge.  
除非真在说楔子，否则删掉这词。  

Say what you mean.  
直抒胸臆。  

Use shorter sentences.  
写短句。  

Have an opinion.  
要有观点。  

Be slightly weird.  
稍微古怪点。  

Don't make every paragraph perfectly symmetrical.  
别让每段都对称完美。  

And for the love of God, occasionally write a sentence that sounds like something an actual human would say.  
看在上帝份上，偶尔写句真人会说的话吧。
