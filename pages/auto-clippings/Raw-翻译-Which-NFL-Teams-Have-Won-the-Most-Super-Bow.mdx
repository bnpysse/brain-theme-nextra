The Dynasties Who Dominate
统治时代的王朝球队

Only 20 of the NFL’s 32 teams have won a Super Bowl.
NFL32支球队中仅20支曾夺冠

At the top, the New England Patriots and Pittsburgh Steelers have six titles apiece, accounting for 12 of the 60 championships played so far.
领跑的新英格兰爱国者与匹兹堡钢人各获6冠，占已举办60届超级碗的五分之一

They built those records in very different eras.
两队在不同时代建立王朝

The Steelers’ dynasty emerged in the 1970s.
钢人王朝崛起于1970年代

At the start of the 1974 season, Pittsburgh had never won a Super Bowl trophy.
1974赛季初时匹兹堡还从未夺冠

Within six seasons, the team had won four and established itself as the NFL’s dominant franchise of the decade.
此后6个赛季内豪取4冠，奠定1970年代霸主地位

The Most Successful Teams in the NFL
NFL最成功球队

Several other franchises have built impressive Super Bowl résumés.
其他多支球队也战绩辉煌

The Green Bay Packers, for example, won half of their four titles in the first two editions of the championship game, following the 1966 and 1967 seasons.
如绿湾包装工4次夺冠中，前2次来自1966-1967赛季的前两届超级碗

The Dallas Cowboys won twice in the 1970s, then added three titles in a four-season span during the early 1990s.
达拉斯牛仔1970年代两度夺冠，1990年代初四年内再添三冠

In the process, the Texas team became known as "America’s Team" and eventually became the most valuable sports team in the world.

The Cowboys’ five titles were matched by their NFC rivals, the San Francisco 49ers, who won five Super Bowls between the 1981 and 1994 seasons.

Waiting on a Miracle
等待奇迹的球队

Twelve NFL franchises have never won a Super Bowl.
