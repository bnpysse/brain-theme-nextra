【帖子标题】：EU GPU prices are up ~19% in one month, tracked the same 176 models across 3+ retailers daily to rule out cherry-picking

【帖子标题】：欧盟GPU价格一个月内上涨约19%，每日追踪3家以上零售商的同176款型号以排除选择性抽样

【帖子正文】：
hey again! I run a EU PC hardware price tracker (

pricesquirrel.com

), 25+ stores across 9 countries, and wanted to check: are GPU prices actually rising, or does it just feel that way?

【帖子正文】：
嘿，又是我！我运营一个欧盟PC硬件价格追踪器（

pricesquirrel.com

），覆盖9个国家的25+家商店，想验证一下：GPU价格是真的在上涨，还是只是感觉如此？

To make this defensible, I didn't just average "whatever's in stock" each day, that inflates the trend if cheap cards sell out and drop off the average. Instead I built a fixed basket: 176 GPU models tracked at 3+ different retailers, every single day for the last 30 days, no gaps, no swapping in or out.

为了让结论站得住脚，我没有简单地每天对“所有有货的产品”求平均，因为如果便宜显卡售罄并从平均值中消失，会虚增趋势。相反，我构建了一个固定篮子：过去30天里，每天追踪176款GPU型号在3家以上不同零售商的价格，无间断，无替换。

Result:

July 15: €808.57 average

August 14: €963.56 average

+19.2% in one month, same 176 cards throughout

结果：

7月15日：平均€808.57

8月14日：平均€963.56

一个月内+19.2%，全程同一批176款显卡

The rise wasn't sudden, it started climbing around July 24-25 and has been steady since, not one spike from a single event:

July 15-23: flat, €805-815

July 24-31: climbing, €826 → €906

Aug 1-14: continued rise, plateauing slightly in the last week, €910 → €964

上涨并非突然发生，大约在7月24-25日开始攀升，此后一直稳步上涨，并非由单一事件导致的一次性飙升：

7月15-23日：持平，€805-815

7月24-31日：攀升，€826 → €906

8月1-14日：持续上涨，最后一周略有趋平，€910 → €964

Methodology, upfront:

"price" here is each product's average price across whichever 3+ retailers carried it that day (not the single cheapest, not a blended market average across a shifting product list). Basket is fixed, same 176 SKUs day 1 and day 30, verified daily. This is EU-wide, not one country.

方法论，提前说明：

这里的“价格”是每款产品当天在3家及以上有售零售商处的平均价格（不是单一最低价，也不是基于变动产品列表的混合市场平均值）。篮子固定，第1天和第30天都是同样的176个SKU，每日核实。这是欧盟范围的，而非单个国家。

Happy to break this down by tier (5060 vs 5070 vs 5080/5090) or AMD vs NVIDIA if there's interest, this is currently the aggregate number.

如果大家感兴趣，我很乐意按级别（5060 vs 5070 vs 5080/5090）或AMD vs NVIDIA拆分数据，目前这是汇总数字。
