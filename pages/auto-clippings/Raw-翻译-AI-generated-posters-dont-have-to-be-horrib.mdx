【文章标题】：AI生成的海报不必糟糕透顶

【文章正文】：
AI-generated posters don’t have to be horrible
AI生成的海报不必糟糕透顶

The problem
问题根源
A now famous Facebook post shows us the scourge of identikit posters generated by AI.
一则著名的Facebook帖子向我们展示了AI生成的千篇一律海报泛滥成灾的现象。

Here’s an article on the subject from the Independent.
《独立报》对此有过专题报道。

Here’s another I found in the wild. With apologies for picking on the Leamington Beer Festival - they are by no means unique
这是我在网上发现的另一个案例。先向利明顿啤酒节致歉——他们绝非个例

The problem with these is not so much that they’re bad. They’re OK, I don’t love them. The problem is that once you’ve seen that style 20 times it starts to irritate just from the sheer repetition.
这些海报的问题倒不在于质量多差。它们还算可以，只是我不太喜欢。关键在于当同款风格出现20次后，纯粹因为重复就让人心生厌烦。

I knew that even ChatGPT was capable of a broader variety of styles than this, so I set out to prove it.
我知道即便是ChatGPT也能生成更多样化的风格，于是决定亲自验证。

I gave ChatGPT some invented event details and asked it to produce a poster. Rather than go with the default look it would choose for a spring fayre, I made a point of specifying what I thought was a different style:
我向ChatGPT虚构了一些活动细节要求生成海报。我没有采用它默认的春季游园会风格，而是特意指定了自认为不同的样式：

  Me:
我：
Produce a poster for a spring fayre.
制作春季游园会海报
21 April - 11am to 3pm
4月21日 上午11点至下午3点
Mill Beach Park, Honeyford
蜜糖镇米尔海滩公园
Free entry
免费入场
Tombola
抽奖游戏
Cakes and drinks
茶点饮品
Performance by a samba band and a dhol band.
桑巴乐队和印度鼓表演
Craft stalls
手工艺品摊位
Circus Skills workshop
马戏技能工作坊
Go for a clean, unfussy, bright layout with a bold striking spring-themed graphic. Avoid pastel/airbrush/oil style art or images of people.
采用简洁明快的版式搭配醒目春日主题图案。避免粉彩/喷绘/油画风格或人物图像

This is what came back.
这是生成的版本
Not as successful as I hoped. To me it still feels like what I’m trying to avoid.
效果未达预期。感觉仍在试图规避的套路范围内

I asked for something deliberately different.
我要求刻意突破常规
  Me:
我：
Make another one using a completely different design aesthetic of your choice. Treat the current one as a "what not to do" – not that there is anything wrong with it, but we want ours to stand out from other posters that look similar.
请自主选择另一种完全不同的设计美学重新制作。将当前版本视为"反面教材"——并非它有问题，而是我们希望脱颖而出

I think this is already much better. It stands out just be being different. I wondered what I would call this style if I wanted to ask for it again?
这次效果显著提升。仅凭差异化就足够醒目。我在想若需再次指定该如何称呼这种风格？

  Me: What is this design style you've used called?
我：你采用的这种设计风格叫什么？
ChatGPT: This one is leaning into a modernist / Bauhaus-influenced graphic style, with a bit of contemporary "flat illustration" layered on top.
ChatGPT：本次设计偏向现代主义/受包豪斯影响的平面风格，并叠加了当代"扁平插画"元素

More specifically, you could describe it as:
具体可描述为：

Bauhaus / Modernist Poster Design
包豪斯/现代主义海报设计
- Rooted in the Bauhaus tradition (1920s–30s)
- 植根于包豪斯传统（1920-30年代）
- Strong emphasis on function over decoration
- 强调功能重于装饰
- Asymmetrical layout with clear hierarchy
- 非对称布局与清晰层级
- Bold, simple sans-serif typography
- 粗体简洁的无衬线字体
- Limited, high-contrast colour palette
- 有限的高对比度配色

Geometric Minimalism
几何极简主义
- Built from basic shapes (circles, semicircles, rectangles)
- 基础几何图形构成（圆形/半圆/矩形）
- The flower is abstracted into almost symbolic forms
- 花朵被抽象为近乎符号化的形态
- Very little texture or shading — mostly flat colour
- 极少纹理或阴影——多为纯色块

Swiss Style (International Typographic Style) influence
瑞士风格（国际字体风格）影响
- Clean grid-based alignment (even if loosely applied)
- 基于网格的整洁对齐（即便松散应用）
- Information-first layout
- 信息优先的版式
- Clear, legible structure
- 清晰易读的结构

If you wanted a shorthand label for a designer or printer, you could call it:
若需向设计师或印刷商快速说明，可称之为：
"Bauhaus-inspired geometric minimalist poster"
"受包豪斯启发的几何极简主义海报"

If you're trying to stand out, this direction works well because most local event posters default to:
若要脱颖而出，此方向效果显著，因为多数本地活动海报默认采用：

- or something you'd see in a well-curated café

If you want, I can push it further into a really distinctive niche (e.g. brutalist, risograph, 90s rave flyer, Japanese minimal, etc.).
如需突破，我可将其推向更独特的小众风格（如粗野主义、孔版印刷、90年代锐舞传单、日式极简等）

Well, thanks GPT. I knew there was an endless array of design styles that it could produce. Instead of trying to dredge some names from my memory, I asked for suggestions.
感谢GPT。我知道它能生成无数设计风格。与其绞尽脑汁回忆，不如直接询问建议

It replied with a list of styles, some of which I'd never heard of, some I recognised but couldn't picture, some which were names I didn't know for styles I did.

  Me: Give me a list of styles to choose from
我：提供可选风格清单
ChatGPT: Here's a deliberately varied menu—some safe-but-distinct, some quite left-field. I've kept each one concrete so you can imagine how your fayre poster would actually look.
