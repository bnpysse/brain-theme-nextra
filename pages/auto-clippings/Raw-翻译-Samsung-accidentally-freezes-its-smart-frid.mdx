Samsung accidentally freezes its smart fridges with a software update  
三星软件更新意外导致智能冰箱"冻结"  

Sep 23, 2026 — 8:11 AM ET  
2026年9月23日 美国东部时间上午8:11  

- Samsung has halted a SmartThings update to smart fridges in Korea after some owners reported that their fridges stopped working.  
- 在部分韩国用户报告冰箱停止运行后，三星已暂停向智能冰箱推送SmartThings更新。  

- Samsung confirmed the issue and said it was taking "emergency measures."  
- 三星确认该问题并表示正在采取"紧急措施"。  

- This latest issue once again highlights the risks associated with using connected appliances.  
- 这起最新事件再次凸显了联网家电的使用风险。  

There are plenty of smart home appliances on the market, featuring internet connectivity, cross-device integration, and a host of other features. However, there’s no shortage of horror stories about these connected appliances failing. Now, we can add another Samsung smart refrigerator issue to the list.  
市场上充斥着具备互联网连接、跨设备集成等功能的智能家电，但关于这些联网设备故障的恐怖故事也不胜枚举。如今，三星智能冰箱又添一桩问题案例。  

Samsung has stopped rolling out a SmartThings update to its smart refrigerators in Korea after some owners reported major issues, ZDNET Korea reports. More specifically, many users on Samsung’s Korea Community forum report that their fridges completely stopped working, resulting in spoiled food. Other issues include the automatic door-opening function not working and the fridge’s internal lights not turning on.  
据ZDNET Korea报道，在部分用户报告重大问题后，三星已停止在韩国推送智能冰箱的SmartThings更新。具体而言，三星韩国社区论坛多名用户反映冰箱完全停止运行导致食物变质，其他问题还包括自动开门功能失效和冰箱内部照明无法开启。  

A Samsung representative confirmed the issue on the company’s forum:  
三星代表在公司论坛确认该问题：  

Yesterday (9/22), an error occurred during the testing process for a Samsung refrigerator software update.Abnormal symptoms have been observed in the refrigerator power and screen of some customers.We are currently taking emergency measures as a top priority through Samsung Electronics Service Centers.  
昨日（9/22）在三星冰箱软件更新测试过程中出现错误。部分客户的冰箱电源和屏幕出现异常症状。我们当前正通过三星电子服务中心优先采取紧急措施。  

The fact that this issue apparently occurred while Samsung was testing a software update for the fridges is very concerning. It raises the question of how a work-in-progress software update found its way onto users’ fridges in the first place. ZDNet Korea suggests that the update was pushed out to users due to a "system error." A Samsung representative also told the outlet that it had stopped distributing the update.  
问题显然发生在三星测试冰箱软件更新期间，这一事实令人担忧。这引发了"尚在测试阶段的更新如何被推送到用户冰箱"的疑问。ZDNet Korea指出此次更新因"系统错误"被推送给用户，三星代表也向媒体表示已停止更新推送。  

Samsung has advised affected customers to contact its service center. We’ve also seen user reports that company technicians have been replacing the motherboard inside these faulty fridges. However, many customers have also urged Samsung to compensate them for the spoiled food. This issue couldn’t have come at a worse time for Korean users, as the country celebrates the Chuseok holiday on Friday.  
三星建议受影响用户联系服务中心。另有用户报告称公司技术人员正在更换故障冰箱的主板。但许多消费者要求三星赔偿变质食物损失。对韩国用户而言这个时机再糟糕不过——本周五正值该国秋夕节假期。
