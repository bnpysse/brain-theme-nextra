【帖子标题】：I know I'm behind for my age.

【帖子标题】：我知道自己落后于同龄人

【帖子正文】：

Greetings everyone,

大家好，

I just turned 34. I admittedley goofed off in community college after highschool. It took me forever to earn my undergrad, and today, said degree isn't even earning me my living. For all intents and purposes, I'm only in the fourth year of my career. For the last two years, I've earned an annual salary of $70-$80k as a contractor. Having just got an apartment, my monthly expenses total about $4-$4.3k. While I ultimately have the money saved, quarterly tax payment months especially suck.

我刚满34岁。坦白说高中毕业后我在社区大学虚度了光阴，花了很长时间才拿到本科学位，而如今这个学位甚至没能让我谋生。严格来说，我的职业生涯才走到第四年。过去两年作为合同工，我的年收入在7-8万美元之间。刚租了公寓后，每月总开支约4000-4300美元。虽然最终能存下钱，但季度缴税月份尤其难熬。

I currently hold $41k in a HYSA, and I contribute $600/month. For the last 2yrs as a contractor, I've also been contributing to a SEP IRA. Although I'm choosing safe ETF's, my IRA will just barely hit $10k come the end of this year. While I know we all move at our own paces through life, I feel that statistically my net worth should be closer to $100k (if not greater). The good news is: I'm debt free.

目前我在高收益储蓄账户有4.1万美元，每月存入600美元。过去两年作为合同工，我还在向SEP IRA账户存钱。虽然投资的是安全型ETF，但到今年底我的IRA账户勉强能达到1万美元。我知道人生节奏各有不同，但按统计数据我的净资产应该接近10万美元（甚至更多）。好消息是：我没有负债。

I'm not asking for financial advice, but rather lifestyle anecdotes from others who played from behind or whom are also playing from behind. What changed the game for you?

我不是来寻求理财建议，而是想听听其他人生赛道落后者或正在追赶者的生活故事。是什么改变了你们的局面？
