Jen Abel

Jen Abel

is the co-founder of JJellyfish and GM of enterprise sales at State Affairs. She is widely regarded as one of the sharpest practitioners in enterprise sales, and for that reason, this is her third visit to the podcast. In our first conversation we went deep on founder-led sales; in our second we mapped the $1M–$10M playbook. This time we do something I’ve never seen on another podcast: walk step by step through the full enterprise sales cycle. Most people think it’s five steps. Jen shows it’s closer to 15.

Jen Abel 是 JJellyfish 的联合创始人，也是 State Affairs 的企业销售总经理。她被广泛认为是企业销售领域最敏锐的实践者之一，正因如此，这是她第三次做客本播客。在我们的第一次对话中，我们深入探讨了创始人主导的销售；第二次对话中，我们梳理了 100 万至 1000 万美元的销售打法。这一次，我们做了一件我在其他播客上从未见过的事：一步步走完整个企业销售周期。大多数人认为它只有五个步骤。Jen 则展示它其实接近 15 个步骤。
