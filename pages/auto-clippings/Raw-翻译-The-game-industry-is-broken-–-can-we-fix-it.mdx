The game industry is broken – can we fix it?

游戏产业已支离破碎——我们能修复它吗？

This article was originally published in Edge 428 - buy it here

本文原载于《Edge》第428期——点击此处购买

By Alex Spencer

作者：亚历克斯·斯宾塞

Almost 60,000 industry jobs lost over five years. Game budgets and schedules out of control. Hundreds of development studios closed. Console prices that have risen since launch. Despite analyst predictions of revenues growing for the videogame industry as a whole, the story behind the scenes is grim. In Edge 428's cover story, we meet with nine industry experts to ask how we might carve out a brighter future. Where now during the second videogame crash?

五年间近六万个行业岗位流失。游戏预算与开发周期失控。数百家开发工作室关闭。主机价格自首发以来不断上涨。尽管分析师预测整个电子游戏行业的收入将持续增长，但幕后的故事却令人沮丧。在《Edge》第428期的封面故事中，我们采访了九位行业专家，探讨如何开辟一个更光明的未来。在第二次电子游戏大崩盘中，我们该何去何从？

"This is the worst videogame crash, or disruption, that we've seen since the 1980s."

“这是自20世纪80年代以来，我们所见过的最严重的电子游戏崩盘或行业动荡。”

Tim Sweeney, Epic Games

蒂姆·斯威尼，Epic Games

Before we can begin prescribing potential cures, we need a solid diagnosis of the problem, and of its scale. Enter Amir Satvat, who has been running the ASGC Games Industry Layoffs Tracker since 2022. "I don't mean this immodestly," Satvat says. "I think we may have more data on the games labour market than anybody in the world."

在开出可能的药方之前，我们需要对问题及其规模做出扎实的诊断。让我们请出阿米尔·萨特瓦特，他自2022年以来一直运营着ASGC游戏行业裁员追踪器。“我这么说并非自夸，”萨特瓦特表示，“我认为我们掌握的关于游戏劳动力市场的数据可能比世界上任何人都多。”

According to Satvat's current projections, which add publicly disclosed numbers to data shared privately by affected individuals and organisations, more than 14,500 videogame workers will lose their jobs this year. That will put the five-year total, from 2022 to 2026, at over 58,000 people out of work.

根据萨特瓦特目前的预测——该预测将公开披露的数字与受影响个人和组织私下分享的数据相结合——今年将有超过14,500名电子游戏从业者失业。这将使2022年至2026年五年间的失业总人数超过58,000人。

But, Satvat says, it's not all bad news. "I also have hiring data, which suggests that something like 18,000 to 25,000 people are getting new jobs in games every year." The videogame industry has actually grown over this period, albeit "very modestly, by a small number of thousands of people," he explains. "The problem is comparing that to the five years before it – I believe the industry grew by 150,000 people in that period."

但萨特瓦特表示，也并非全是坏消息。“我还掌握了招聘数据，显示每年约有18,000至25,000人在游戏行业找到新工作。”他解释说，电子游戏行业在此期间实际上有所增长，尽管“增幅非常有限，只有区区数千人”。“问题在于与此前五年相比——我相信那段时间行业增长了150,000人。”

Even with this dramatic slowdown in growth, Satvat says, "it is hard for me to foresee a picture where the industry doesn't eventually contract from its current size. If I'm being optimistic, there is some number that is not a ghastly decrease from where we are now, where things can stabilise out. But for it to stay exactly the same as when you had these very large firms that were relatively stable? It's hard for me to visualise how that happens."

即便增长如此急剧放缓，萨特瓦特表示，“我很难预见到行业最终不会从目前的规模收缩的局面。如果乐观一点看，存在一个数字，不会让我们从现在的水平出现可怕的下降，届时行业可以趋于稳定。但要让它保持在这些大型企业相对稳定时的原状？我很难想象那会如何发生。”

Proposal 1: Be more like Japan. When it comes to the layoffs situation, Amir Satvat says, "Japan is a completely different ballgame." He acknowledges that the country isn't entirely "a utopia": there are still layoffs, and they are generally focused on contractors based outside the country, "to protect core staff in Japan", but these have generally numbered in the hundreds rather than thousands. "Everyone calls out Nintendo, but you can look at Konami or Capcom – these companies all have staff retention of 97 per cent plus." So, what can the west learn? "My understanding is that, generally, Japanese teams tend to be much smaller and leaner," Satvat says. "They didn't get swept up in the live-service trend, or into these mega-blockbusters with 500-person teams." And perhaps most important of all? "The executive salaries. They still make great money, but it's two or three million dollars, not 30 million." Image source: Official GDC.

方案一：向日本学习。在裁员问题上，阿米尔·萨特瓦特表示，“日本完全是另一番景象。”他承认日本并非完全的“乌托邦”：裁员仍然存在，而且通常集中在海外承包商身上，“以保护日本的核心员工”，但这些裁员规模通常只有数百人，而非数千人。“人人都拿任天堂说事，但你可以看看科乐美或卡普空——这些公司的员工留存率都在97%以上。”那么，西方能学到什么？“我的理解是，日本团队通常规模更小、更精干，”萨特瓦特说，“他们没有卷入服务型游戏的潮流，也没有陷入那些需要500人团队的超大型项目。”而也许最重要的是什么？“高管薪酬。他们仍然赚很多钱，但那是两三百万美元，而不是三千万美元。”图片来源：官方GDC。

So, what about the idea, as suggested by industry veteran Brenda Romero, that we are encountering a second videogame crash? Satvat caveats with the fact that his data shows the layoffs are very localised, to patches of the western world that have traditionally been considered hubs of game development.

那么，行业资深人士布伦达·罗梅罗提出的“我们正遭遇第二次电子游戏大崩盘”这一观点又如何呢？萨特瓦特对此提出告诫，指出他的数据显示裁员非常局部化，集中在传统上被视为游戏开发中心的西方地区。

Most affected are the UK, northern and western Europe, and North America – and one US state in particular. "There was a 12-to-18-month period where I estimated that over half of layoffs, globally, were in California," Satvat says. Other regions have been largely insulated from these effects. That includes countries just starting to build up a videogame industry – often those where wages are lower – but also more established areas such as eastern Europe, and one of the most storied development hubs of all: Japan.

受影响最严重的是英国、北欧和西欧以及北美——尤其是一个美国州。“在12到18个月的时间里，我估计全球超过一半的裁员发生在加利福尼亚州，”萨特瓦特说。其他地区在很大程度上未受这些影响。这包括刚刚开始建立电子游戏产业的国家——通常是那些工资较低的国家——也包括东欧等更成熟的地区，以及最具传奇色彩的游戏开发中心之一：日本。

"I think this is as bad as the '83 crash if you're a game developer based in North America or western Europe, in a traditional triple-A studio," Satvat says. "That is ground zero for the destruction."

“如果你是一名位于北美或西欧、在传统3A工作室工作的游戏开发者，我认为这次和83年大崩盘一样糟糕，”萨特瓦特说，“那里就是毁灭的震中。”

When we put this to Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, his answer is immediate. Are we living through a videogame crash? "We absolutely are," he says. "This is the worst videogame crash, or disruption, that we've seen since the 1980s, when Atari fell and was replaced by Nintendo. I was just a player back then – I only joined the industry as a programmer in the 1990s – but I've not seen anything this bad since then." Despite making one of the biggest videogames in existence – as we type these words, over two million people are playing Fortnite – Epic has been far from exempt from these problems. As CEO, Sweeney oversaw more than 1,000 layoffs in March this year, on top of 830 redundancies in September 2023.

当我们向Epic Games首席执行官蒂姆·斯威尼提出这个问题时，他立刻给出了回答。我们是否正经历一场电子游戏大崩盘？“绝对是的，”他说，“这是自20世纪80年代以来我们所见过的最严重的电子游戏崩盘或行业动荡，当时雅达利轰然倒塌，被任天堂取代。那时我还只是个玩家——直到90年代我才以程序员身份进入这个行业——但自那以后我从未见过这么糟糕的情况。”尽管制作了现存最大的电子游戏之一——就在我们写下这些文字时，有超过两百万人正在玩《堡垒之夜》——Epic也远未能幸免于这些问题。作为首席执行官，斯威尼在今年3月监督了超过1,000人的裁员，此前2023年9月已有830人被裁。
