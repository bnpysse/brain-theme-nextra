【文章正文】：
Hey HN! Ilya and Nikita here. We’re building HomeCat, a tool that lets you design a backyard office space -
大家好 HN！我们是Ilya和Nikita。我们正在开发HomeCat，一款能让你设计后院办公空间的工具 -

https://myhomecat.com
https://myhomecat.com

We started out by helping people design sheds, and quickly realized that many people actually want to build a backyard office. There wasn't really an easy way to do this, so we thought we'd take on the challenge.
我们最初是帮助人们设计储物棚，但很快发现很多人其实想建造后院办公室。当时并没有简单的方法来实现这个需求，所以我们决定接受这个挑战。

Right now, you can design a backyard shed and our tool will make it code compliant. It's maybe 90% of the way there, since you also have zoning/HOA and other edge cases. But it does figure out most of the details for you. Right now, it's pretty simple: enter your address, what you want to build (i.e. 8x8 backyard office), and it'll create a few concepts for you. If you like one, you can customize it with the chat.
目前，你可以设计后院储物棚，我们的工具会确保设计符合规范。虽然还有分区条例/业主协会等特殊情况需要处理（约完成90%），但工具能为你解决大部分细节问题。操作非常简单：输入地址和建造需求（比如8x8的后院办公室），系统就会生成几个设计方案。如果喜欢某个方案，可以通过聊天功能进行定制。

Here's an example of a design that's compliant in SF:
这是一个符合旧金山规范的案例设计：

https://myhomecat.com/scene/classic-cottage-137134ef

We would love for you to try our tool if you are planning your backyard office. As a bonus to the HN community, we can professionally design one for you for free if you comment what you're looking for.
如果你正计划建造后院办公室，我们非常期待你试用这个工具。作为给HN社区的福利，只要在评论区描述你的需求，我们就可以免费为你提供专业设计服务。

Happy labor day!
劳动节快乐！
