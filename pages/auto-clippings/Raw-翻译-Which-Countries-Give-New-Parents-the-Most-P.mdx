【文章标题】：Which Countries Give New Parents the Most Paid Leave?

哪些国家为新晋父母提供最长带薪休假？

【文章正文】：

How Much Paid Leave Do New Parents Get?

新晋父母能获得多少带薪休假？

Key Takeaways

核心发现

Slovakia leads the OECD with 192 weeks of combined paid leave entitlements per child across maternity, paternity, and shared leave.

斯洛伐克以每孩192周（含产假、陪产假和共享育儿假）的带薪休假时长领跑经合组织国家。

Several countries provide more than two years of combined paid leave, compared with an OECD average of 65 weeks.

多国提供超过两年的总带薪休假时长，而经合组织平均水平为65周。

The U.S. is the only OECD country with no national paid parental leave entitlement.

美国是经合组织中唯一没有全国性带薪育儿假政策的国家。

When a child is born, one question quickly becomes unavoidable for working parents: when do you go back to work?

当新生儿降临，职场父母很快会面临一个无法回避的问题：何时重返工作岗位？

Across OECD countries, the answer ranges from weeks to years, reflecting vastly different systems of parental support.

经合组织各国的答案从数周到数年不等，反映出差异巨大的育儿支持体系。

Using the latest available OECD data, this visualization compares paid birth-related leave entitlements across 38 countries. Figures are for 2024 and include time reserved for mothers and fathers, as well as leave that can be shared between parents.

基于经合组织最新数据，本图表比较了38个国家与生育相关的带薪休假权益。2024年数据包含母亲专属假、父亲专属假及父母可共享的假期。

The Countries Offering the Most Paid Leave

带薪休假最慷慨的国家

The OECD average stands at around 15 months of combined paid leave per child, but the total can mask significant differences in how that time is allocated.

经合组织国家平均为每孩提供约15个月总带薪假，但总时长背后隐藏着假期分配方式的重大差异。

Some countries concentrate benefits around mothers, while others reserve substantial time for fathers or give parents greater flexibility to divide leave between them.

部分国家以母亲福利为主，另一些则为父亲保留大量假期，或给予父母更灵活的假期分配自主权。

Slovakia tops the ranking with 192 combined weeks of paid leave entitlements per child, followed by Finland at 177 weeks and Hungary at 171. These totals combine maternity, paternity, and shared leave, so they do not represent the number of consecutive weeks a single parent can take.

斯洛伐克以每孩192周总带薪假位居榜首，芬兰（177周）和匈牙利（171周）紧随其后。这些总时长包含各类假期，因此不代表单亲可连续休假的时间。

For perspective, Canada provides 56 combined weeks, the UK 39, and Australia 22, compared with Slovakia’s 192.

作为参照，加拿大提供56周，英国39周，澳大利亚22周，而斯洛伐克高达192周。

How Parental Leave Has Evolved

育儿假政策演变

The length of parental leave is only part of the story. Over the past half-century, many countries have also redesigned these policies around how time is divided between parents.

休假时长仅是冰山一角。过去五十年来，多国还围绕假期分配方式重构了政策体系。

Sweden became the first country to introduce paid parental leave for both mothers and fathers in 1974, while Norway pioneered a non-transferable father quota in 1993.

瑞典1974年率先实施父母共享带薪育儿假，挪威则于1993年首创不可转让的父亲专属配额。

Since then, father-specific leave has spread widely. South Korea now stands out with 54 weeks reserved for fathers, compared with an OECD average of 13 weeks. These policies can give families more time at home while encouraging fathers to take on more childcare and helping both parents stay connected to their careers.

此后父亲专属假广泛普及。韩国目前以54周陪产假领先（经合组织平均13周）。这类政策既增加家庭团聚时间，又鼓励父亲参与育儿，同时助力父母维持职业联系。

Family policies are also evolving as fertility rates decline across wealthy economies. Today, 37 of 38 OECD countries have fertility rates below replacement level.

随着富裕经济体生育率普遍下降，家庭政策持续演进。目前38个经合组织国家中，37个生育率低于更替水平。

The U.S. Stands as an Outlier

美国的例外处境

At the opposite end of the ranking, the U.S. is the only OECD country without a national paid maternity leave entitlement and one of three without national paid leave for fathers.

榜单另一端，美国是经合组织唯一没有全国带薪产假的国家，也是三个没有全国带薪陪产假的国家之一。

American parents may still receive paid time through state programs or employers. Thirteen states and Washington, D.C., have enacted paid family and medical leave programs.

美国父母仍可通过州政府计划或雇主获得带薪假。目前13个州和华盛顿特区已实施带薪家事病假计划。

The stakes extend beyond time at home. First-time mothers who used paid leave were more likely to return to the same employer after childbirth (92.3% vs. 73.3%), highlighting how parental leave can shape career continuity as well as family life.

影响远超居家时间。使用带薪假的初产妇产后重返原雇主比例更高（92.3% vs 73.3%），凸显育儿假对职业连续性与家庭生活的双重塑造力。
