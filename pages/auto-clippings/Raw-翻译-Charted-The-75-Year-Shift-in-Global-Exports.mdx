How Global Exports Shifted Toward Asia Over 75 Years
全球出口格局如何在75年间向亚洲倾斜

Key Takeaways
核心发现

Asia’s share of global goods exports rose from 12.8% in 1950 to 35.6% in 2025.
亚洲占全球货物出口份额从1950年的12.8%升至2025年的35.6%

The 10 leading Western economies shown fell from 49.1% of world goods exports to 34.4% over the same period.
同期十大西方经济体占比从49.1%降至34.4%

China alone now accounts for 14.4% of global goods exports, the largest share within Asia.
中国独占全球货物出口14.4%份额，居亚洲之首

A major milestone in global trade has been reached.
全球贸易迎来重大转折点

By 2020, Asia had edged ahead of 10 leading Western economies in their combined share of global goods exports. In 2025, Asia remained ahead at 35.6%, compared with 34.4% for the Western group.
2020年亚洲首次超越十大西方经济体总和；至2025年仍以35.6%领先西方集团的34.4%

This graphic tracks Asia’s share of global goods exports from 1950 to 2025 against 10 leading Western economies, based on data from the World Trade Organization.
本图表基于世贸组织数据，对比1950-2025年间亚洲与十大西方经济体的出口占比

How Asia Closed the Export Gap
亚洲如何逆袭出口差距

In 1950, Asia accounted for 12.8% of world goods exports, compared with 49.1% for the 10 Western economies shown, including the U.S., Germany, the U.K., and France.
1950年亚洲占比12.8%，而美、德、英、法等十大西方经济体合计49.1%

The gap initially widened before beginning a long reversal. By 2000, it had narrowed to roughly 21 percentage points, and by 2020 Asia had moved slightly ahead.
差距先扩大后逆转：2000年缩至21个百分点，2020年实现反超

Much of the shift has taken place since 2000. Over the following 25 years, Asia’s share of global goods exports climbed from 27.1% to 35.6%, while the Western group’s share fell from 47.7% to 34.4%.
主要转变发生在2000年后：亚洲份额从27.1%升至35.6%，西方集团从47.7%降至34.4%

China is the largest single contributor to Asia’s export share, accounting for 14.4% of world goods exports in 2025, compared with roughly 8.3% for the United States. In 2000, China’s share was still below 4%.
中国是最大推手：2025年占全球14.4%（美国约8.3%），而2000年尚不足4%

Asia’s rise also extends beyond export volume. The region now sits at the center of several industries that underpin the digital and energy economies.
亚洲崛起不限于出口量，更成为数字与能源经济核心产业枢纽

Why Asia’s Rise Matters Today
亚洲崛起为何重要

Asia’s growing weight in global trade also reflects what the region produces.
贸易占比提升反映产业实力

Roughly 80% of the world’s electronics are made in Asia, including computers, chips, and electronic components. For key critical minerals such as lithium, cobalt, and rare earths, more than 70% of global processing takes place in China.
全球80%电子产品产自亚洲；锂、钴、稀土等关键矿物70%以上在中国加工

More broadly, China accounted for 32% of global manufacturing in 2024, more than the U.S., Japan, Germany, and South Korea combined. From AI infrastructure to clean energy, many of today’s most strategic supply chains rely heavily on Asian production networks.
2024年中国制造业占全球32%（超过美日德韩总和），从AI基建到清洁能源的战略供应链均依赖亚洲
