【文章标题】：Character.ai Files for Bankruptcy

【文章正文】：

Character.AI, one of the first breakout startups of the generative AI boom, has filed for bankruptcy and is selling some of its assets to Disney. The company, which builds various online bots for users to chat or roleplay with, has faced mounting scrutiny and lawsuits over the safety of its chatbots, but has continued releasing updates and products in recent months.

Character.AI，作为生成式人工智能热潮中首批脱颖而出的初创公司之一，已申请破产，并正在将其部分资产出售给迪士尼。这家公司为用户打造各种在线机器人，供其聊天或角色扮演，近年来因其聊天机器人的安全性而面临越来越多的审查和诉讼，但近几个月仍持续发布更新和新产品。

Character.AI files for bankruptcy

Character.AI申请破产

The chatbot startup is looking to sell its assets to Disney after years of legal challenges and safety concerns.

这家聊天机器人初创公司在经历多年的法律挑战和安全问题后，正寻求将其资产出售给迪士尼。

In a press release and blog post published Monday, Character said it had filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in the US to facilitate the sale of the company and its assets. “Over the past year, we’ve taken meaningful steps to sustain and improve our business amid significant legal, regulatory, and operational headwinds,” the company said.

在周一发布的新闻稿和博客文章中，Character表示已在美国申请第11章破产保护，以促进公司及其资产的出售。该公司表示：“过去一年，在面临重大法律、监管和运营阻力的情况下，我们已采取切实措施来维持和改善业务。”

In its bankruptcy filing, Character listed estimated liabilities between $50 million and $100 million. As part of the proposed sale, Disney would serve as the lead bidder for Character.AI’s assets, setting a baseline for competing offers. Disney would pay $20 million for certain undisclosed assets, and provide transition support “using former Company employees it has hired.” It would also receive a nonexclusive license to unspecified intellectual property.

在破产申请中，Character列出的预计负债在5000万至1亿美元之间。作为拟议出售的一部分，迪士尼将担任Character.AI资产的主要竞标者，为竞争性报价设定基准。迪士尼将支付2000万美元收购某些未公开的资产，并提供“利用其已聘用的前公司员工”的过渡支持。此外，迪士尼还将获得未指明知识产权的非独家许可。

Disney’s involvement comes shortly after the entertainment behemoth hired Character.AI’s former CEO Karandeep Anand as chief technology officer, part of its growing push into AI. Anand’s tenure as Character CEO lasted just over a year and during that time he oversaw the company’s move into microdramas. Disney has previously complained that Character was damaging its brand and exploiting its intellectual property by allowing unauthorized chatbots of its characters, including sending cease and desist letters.

迪士尼的介入紧随这家娱乐巨头聘请Character.AI前首席执行官Karandeep Anand担任首席技术官之后，这是其日益加大AI投入的一部分。Anand担任Character首席执行官仅一年多，期间他主导了公司向微短剧领域的进军。迪士尼此前曾抱怨Character允许未经授权的角色聊天机器人，损害了其品牌并利用其知识产权，包括发出停止侵权函。

Along with Anand, Disney also said it expected “a number of Character.AI’s technical team” to join the company. The move resembles a kind of “acquihire” strategy that has become increasingly common in Silicon Valley as a means of hoovering up startups’ talent that sometimes leaves the original company struggling to survive. Character.AI has survived it once already — Google spent over $2 billion to hire away much of the company’s talent, including co-founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas, in 2024.

除了Anand之外，迪士尼还表示预计“Character.AI技术团队中的多名成员”将加入公司。此举类似于一种“人才收购”策略，这种策略在硅谷日益普遍，旨在吸纳初创公司的人才，有时会让原公司难以生存。Character.AI已经经历过一次——2024年，谷歌斥资超过20亿美元挖走了该公司大部分人才，包括联合创始人Noam Shazeer和Daniel De Freitas。

Character said it expects the sale process to be completed by late November or early December and plans to continue operating its services, including paid subscriptions, in the meantime. However, the company warned that “the platform will be wound down” if a suitable bid does not emerge. “As we navigate this process, maintaining a safe and engaging experience for our community remains a top priority,” it said.

Character表示，预计出售过程将于11月底或12月初完成，并计划在此期间继续运营其服务，包括付费订阅。然而，该公司警告称，如果没有合适的竞标出现，“平台将被逐步关闭”。公司表示：“在我们推进这一过程的同时，为社区维护安全且引人入胜的体验仍是我们的首要任务。”
