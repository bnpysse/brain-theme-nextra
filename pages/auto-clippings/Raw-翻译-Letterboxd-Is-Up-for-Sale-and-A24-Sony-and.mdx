【文章标题】：Letterboxd Is Up for Sale, and A24, Sony and the New York Times Are Bidding  
【文章标题】：影迷社交平台Letterboxd挂牌出售 A24、索尼和纽约时报参与竞购  

【文章正文】：  
Letterboxd, the app where film Twitter went to live, could soon have a new owner.  
这款被影迷视为"电影版推特"的应用可能即将易主。  

According to a new report, The New York Times, A24, and Sony Pictures are among the suitors who've expressed interest in buying the site.  
最新报道显示，纽约时报、A24和索尼影业均已表达收购意向。  

The asking price? More than $300M, per the Times' sources.  
据纽约时报消息源透露，要价超过3亿美元。  

That's roughly 20 times the approximately $15M Letterboxd is projected to earn this year.  
这相当于该平台今年预计1500万美元收入的20倍。  

Not bad for a site started in 2011 by two New Zealand designers, Matthew Buchanan and Karl von Randow, who were annoyed that movie lovers had nowhere decent to gather online.  
对于2011年由新西兰设计师马修·布坎南和卡尔·冯·兰多创建的网站来说，这个价格相当可观——他们当年因影迷缺乏优质线上聚集地而萌生创业想法。  

The growth is honestly staggering.  
其增长势头确实令人惊叹。  

The site now has 30 million users, up from 1.8 million in 2020.  
平台用户数从2020年的180万激增至如今的3000万。  

Most people use it for free, but there are paid Pro ($19/year) and Patron ($49/year) tiers, plus ad revenue.  
虽然多数用户免费使用，但平台通过专业版（19美元/年）、赞助版（49美元/年）订阅及广告获得收入。  

Media analyst Ken Doctor summed up the appeal to the Times: an engaged audience that pays and that advertisers want to reach is "the gold standard of the internet."  
媒体分析师肯·多克特向纽约时报解释其吸引力：同时具备付费意愿和广告价值的活跃用户群堪称"互联网黄金标准"。  

My two cents: A24 buying Letterboxd would be the most on-brand acquisition imaginable, but also the messiest.  
笔者的看法：A24收购Letterboxd将是最符合品牌调性的交易，却也最可能引发争议。  

Its movies get rated on the platform every single day, and the conflict-of-interest questions write themselves.  
毕竟该平台每日都在对其电影进行评分，利益冲突问题不言而喻。  

Same goes for Sony. The Times makes the most sense on paper, but it has its own critics.  
索尼同样面临这个问题。纽约时报理论上最合适，但也存在自身争议。
