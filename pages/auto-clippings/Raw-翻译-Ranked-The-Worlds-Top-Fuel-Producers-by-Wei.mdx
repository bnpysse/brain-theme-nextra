Ranked: The World’s Top Fuel Producers by Weight
全球燃料生产重量排行榜

Key Takeaways
核心要点

China ranks first in fuel production by weight, driven by 4.4 billion metric tons of coal output.
中国以44亿吨煤炭产量推动，成为全球燃料重量产量第一大国。

The U.S. ranks second overall and is the world’s largest producer of both oil and natural gas.
美国综合排名第二，同时是全球最大的石油和天然气生产国。

Coal-heavy countries rank prominently because this comparison measures physical output by weight rather than energy content.
煤炭大国排名靠前，因为本次比较采用物理重量而非能量值作为衡量标准。

Fossil fuels remain the backbone of global energy supply, powering electricity grids, heavy industry, transportation systems, and petrochemical production.
化石燃料仍是全球能源供应的支柱，为电网、重工业、交通系统和石化生产提供动力。

This visualization ranks the world’s top 25 fuel producers in 2024 by the physical weight of their output, combining coal, oil, and natural gas production in metric tons.
本可视化榜单以煤炭、石油和天然气产量的公吨数为依据，展示了2024年全球燃料产量（物理重量）前25强。

Importantly, this is a comparison of production by mass, not energy content. Different fuels contain different amounts of energy per ton, so the ranking should not be interpreted as a measure of each country’s total energy production.
需特别注意：这是质量产量的比较而非能量值。不同燃料每吨所含能量不同，因此本排名不能等同于各国总能源产量指标。

The data for this visualization comes from the Austrian Ministry of Finance. Natural gas production is converted from cubic meters to metric tons.
数据来源奥地利财政部。天然气产量已从立方米换算为公吨。

China’s Coal Leads Fuel Output by a Wide Margin
中国煤炭产量遥遥领先

With 4.8 billion metric tons of fuel output in 2024, China ranks first in this comparison by a wide margin. Its output by weight is more than double that of the United States, which ranks second.
中国2024年燃料总产量达48亿公吨，以绝对优势位居榜首。其重量产量是第二名美国的两倍有余。

The table below ranks the 25 leading countries by the combined weight of their coal, oil, and natural gas production, measured in million metric tons. Because these fuels have different energy densities, the totals compare physical output rather than energy content. Uranium production is included in the totals but is not shown separately because of its much smaller mass.
下表按煤炭、石油和天然气总产量（单位：百万公吨）列出25个领先国家。由于这些燃料能量密度不同，总量比较的是物理产量而非能量值。铀产量计入总量但因质量过小未单独列出。

China’s lead is overwhelmingly driven by coal. The country produced 4.4 billion metric tons of coal in 2024, accounting for nearly all of its fuel output by weight and more than half of global coal production.
中国的领先地位主要来自煤炭。2024年其煤炭产量达44亿吨，占该国燃料总产量的绝大部分，并超过全球煤炭产量的50%。

In addition to being the largest coal producer, China is also the largest consumer of coal, using nearly all of its production domestically to generate electricity and fuel its manufacturing and industrial sectors.
中国不仅是最大产煤国，也是最大煤炭消费国，其国内产量几乎全部用于发电及制造业和工业领域。

The U.S. Dominates Oil and Natural Gas
美国主导石油和天然气领域

The United States ranks second overall, producing 2.3 billion metric tons of fuel by weight in 2024. Unlike China, its production mix is more balanced across oil, natural gas, and coal.
美国以23亿公吨燃料总产量位居第二。与中国不同，其石油、天然气和煤炭产量分布更为均衡。

U.S. oil production reached 948.8 million metric tons, while natural gas production totaled 854.6 million metric tons and coal production amounted to 473.8 million metric tons.
美国石油产量达9.488亿公吨，天然气8.546亿公吨，煤炭4.738亿公吨。

Globally, the U.S. is the leading producer of both oil and natural gas, accounting for about 25% of global natural gas production and 20.5% of global oil production in 2024.
2024年，美国贡献了全球约25%的天然气产量和20.5%的石油产量，在这两个领域均为世界第一。

A Concentrated Global Fuel Market
高度集中的全球燃料市场

The top five countries—China, the United States, Russia, India, and Indonesia—produced a combined 10.6 billion metric tons of fuel in 2024. That represents about 63% of the global total by weight.
前五名国家（中、美、俄、印、印尼）2024年合计产量106亿公吨，约占全球总产量的63%。

Russia ranks third with 1.5 billion metric tons, supported by large volumes of oil, natural gas, and coal. India ranks fourth at 1.1 billion metric tons, with coal making up the vast majority of its output.
俄罗斯以15亿公吨产量位居第三，其石油、天然气和煤炭产量均居前列。印度以11亿公吨产量排名第四，其中煤炭占绝对主导。

Indonesia rounds out the top five with 921.4 million metric tons, also led by coal. Across the full ranking, the top 25 countries account for 91% of global mineral fuel production by weight, illustrating how concentrated fossil fuel extraction is among a relatively small group of countries.
印尼以9.214亿公吨产量跻身前五，同样以煤炭为主。前25个国家占据全球矿物燃料总产量的91%，显示化石燃料开采高度集中在少数国家。
