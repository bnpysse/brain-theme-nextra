Which U.S. States Are the Happiest in 2026?

Key Takeaways:

Hawaii ranks as America’s happiest state in 2026, scoring 69.5 out of 100, followed by New Jersey and Utah.

West Virginia ranks last at 31.9, with Louisiana, Arkansas, Alaska, and Kentucky rounding out the bottom five.

Utah ranks first nationally in both work environment and community and environment, helping it secure third place overall.

Where Americans live can shape their quality of life, from physical and mental health to job satisfaction and community connections.

This visualization ranks all 50 states using

WalletHub’s 2026 happiness index

One of the more surprising results is New Jersey’s second-place finish. The state ranks first nationally for emotional and physical well-being, despite placing 24th for work environment.

Utah offers a different path to the top, ranking first in both work environment and community and environment. California also makes the top 10, despite ranking just 36th for work environment.

Why Hawaii Ranks First and West Virginia Last

Hawaii combines the nation’s longest life expectancy, at 81.9 years, with the second-lowest depression rate and an unemployment rate of just 2.5%. Its natural surroundings may offer additional lifestyle benefits, although the state’s high cost of living remains a challenge.

West Virginia presents a stark contrast, ranking last for both emotional and physical well-being and work environment. Its comparatively high depression rate and weaker employment conditions help explain its position.

How Workplace Culture Shapes Happiness

Work environment plays an important role in the rankings, but states with strong workplace conditions don’t always lead in overall happiness.

Utah ranks first nationally for work environment, while Idaho places third and North Dakota fourth. All three rank highly overall, suggesting that working conditions can contribute to broader well-being.

However, New Jersey ranks second overall despite placing 24th for work environment. California shows an even larger gap, ranking ninth overall but 36th for work environment.

Does Geography Influence Happiness?

Geography reveals some notable patterns in the rankings. The Northeast performs particularly well, with New Jersey, Connecticut, and New Hampshire all placing in the top 10.

Meanwhile, several Southern states rank near the bottom, including Louisiana, Arkansas, and Kentucky.

But neighboring states can have very different outcomes. Utah ranks third and Idaho fourth, while nearby Wyoming sits considerably lower with a score of 43.3.
