美国钾肥进口来源国分布图  

【文章正文】：  
So, Where Does America Get Its Potash?  
那么，美国的钾肥从何而来？  

Key Takeaways  
核心要点  

Potash supplies potassium, an essential nutrient for growing crops.  
钾肥提供钾元素，这是作物生长不可或缺的营养物质。  

Nearly three-quarters (73%) of U.S. potash consumption comes from Canada.  
美国近四分之三（73%）的钾肥消费量来自加拿大。  

Domestic supply, primarily from New Mexico, covers just 8% of U.S. consumption.  
美国本土供应（主要来自新墨西哥州）仅能满足8%的需求。  

The United States is one of the world’s largest agricultural economies. Growing those crops requires large amounts of fertilizer, including potassium-rich potash.  
作为全球最大农业经济体之一，美国农作物生长需要大量含钾肥料。  

This visualization shows the largest sources of U.S. potash consumption using data from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) Mineral Commodities Summaries 2026.  
本图表根据美国地质调查局《2026年矿产品摘要》数据，展示美国钾肥主要来源。  

Volumes are expressed in tonnes of potassium oxide equivalent (K₂O), a standard measure that allows different potash products to be compared by their potassium content.  
数据以氧化钾当量（K₂O）吨数为单位，该标准计量法可通过钾含量横向比较各类钾肥产品。  

Canada’s Natural Advantage  
加拿大的天然优势  

Canada accounts for 73% of U.S. potash consumption, supported by abundant mineral deposits and its proximity to American farms.  
加拿大凭借丰富矿藏和毗邻美国农场的优势，占据美国钾肥消费量的73%。  

Canada is the world’s largest potash-producing country, with much of its mining industry concentrated in Saskatchewan. Beneath the province lie extensive salt deposits that support a major fertilizer export industry.  
作为全球最大钾肥生产国，加拿大矿业集中在萨斯喀彻温省——该省广袤盐矿层支撑着庞大的肥料出口产业。

Established rail connections help move Canadian production south into the United States. Nutrien, a major Saskatchewan-based producer, ships potash across the U.S. through its North American transportation network.  
成熟的铁路系统将加拿大钾肥南运至美国。总部位于萨斯喀彻温的巨头Nutrien公司，通过其北美运输网络向全美输送钾肥。  

That overland connection gives Canadian supply a logistical advantage over alternatives that require ocean transport.  
相比需海运的其他供应方，陆路运输赋予加拿大物流优势。  

Washington Looks to Belarus  
华府转向白俄罗斯  

In September 2026, U.S. President Donald Trump said Washington was working on a major agreement to purchase potash from Belarus at prices below those paid for Canadian supply. The following day, he said the U.S. would continue buying Canadian potash while also pursuing the possibility of lower-priced Belarusian supply.  
2026年9月，美国总统特朗普表示正推动以低于加拿大报价的价格从白俄罗斯采购钾肥的协议。次日他补充称美国将继续购买加拿大钾肥，同时寻求低价白俄罗斯货源。  

Potash has been excluded from the additional Section 338 tariffs imposed on certain Canadian goods, underscoring its importance to U.S. agriculture.  
钾肥未被列入针对部分加拿大商品的338条款附加关税清单，凸显其对美国农业的关键性。  

However, adding Belarusian supply would involve more than finding another producer. Landlocked Belarus faces restrictions on European export routes and relies heavily on Russian transportation infrastructure. It also has existing customers, including China, Brazil, and India, limiting how much production could readily be redirected.  
但引入白俄罗斯货源不仅涉及寻找新供应商。这个内陆国面临欧洲出口路线限制，严重依赖俄罗斯运输体系，且现有客户（含中国、巴西、印度）制约其快速转移产能的能力。  

Transportation costs are another consideration. Unlike Canadian potash, which can move overland into the U.S., Belarusian supply would face a longer and more complex journey. That means a lower purchase price would not necessarily translate into lower delivered costs for American buyers.  
运输成本是另一考量因素。与可陆路输美的加拿大钾肥不同，白俄罗斯货源需经历更漫长复杂的运输链，意味着低价采购未必能转化为到岸成本优势。  

What It Means for American Farmers  
对美国农民的影响  

For farmers, the practical question is whether alternative suppliers can deliver affordable fertilizer when it is needed. Potassium helps plants regulate water use, develop roots, and withstand drought. When available potassium is insufficient, plant growth and yields can suffer.  
对农民而言，核心在于替代供应商能否及时提供平价肥料。钾元素帮助作物调节水分、发育根系、抵御干旱，缺钾会导致生长受阻和减产。  

Farmers can use soil testing to determine how much potash to apply. Nitrogen and phosphorus cannot replace potassium’s role in crop development, so cheaper supplies of other fertilizers do not remove the need for it.  
农民可通过土壤检测确定施肥量。氮磷肥料无法替代钾的作物生长功能，因此其他肥料降价也不能消除对钾肥的需求。  

Other suppliers, including Russia at 11% and Israel at 3%, already provide some diversification. Whether additional Belarusian supply ultimately lowers costs will depend on the final terms, available volumes, and transportation expenses.  
俄罗斯（11%）和以色列（3%）等供应方已提供一定多元化。白俄罗斯货源能否最终降低成本，将取决于最终条款、可供应量及运输费用。
