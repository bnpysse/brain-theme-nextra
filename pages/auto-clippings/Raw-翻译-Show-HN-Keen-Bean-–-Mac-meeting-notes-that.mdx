Not just a transcript. Tasks, decisions, a draft spec, a diagram, a rough mock-up of the screen you were describing — started while you talked, ready for you to finish. On your Mac, in files you own.  
不仅是文字记录。任务、决策、方案草案、图表、你描述的界面草图——在你讨论时即时生成，待你最终完善。所有内容存于你的Mac，专属文件由你掌控。  

See it work  
You ran the meeting, so you couldn't take notes. The bot that took them made your client uncomfortable and gave you a transcript nobody will read. And the write-up is still yours to do tonight — because there's nobody to hand it to.  
眼见为实  
你主导会议时无暇记录。传统记录机器人让客户不适，生成的文字记录无人阅读。而会议纪要仍要熬夜完成——因为根本无人可交接。  

Three things that make it different  
三大独特之处  
It hears both sides from your Mac's own audio. Nothing joins, nothing announces itself, nothing appears in the participant list — so the meeting stays a meeting.  
通过Mac原生音频捕捉双方对话。不接入会议、不自我宣告、不显示在参会名单——会议纯粹如常。  

Works where recording bots are banned.  
在禁用录音机器人的场景中依然可用。  

Tasks, decisions, risks and blockers appear as cards while people speak. When the conversation gets substantive it drafts the spec, sketches the diagram, roughs out the screen you're describing.  
实时将任务/决策/风险/阻碍转为卡片。深入讨论时自动起草方案、绘制图表、勾勒所述界面。  

Drafts, not deliverables — you finish them.  
生成的是草案而非终稿——由你完成最终打磨。  

Audio goes from your Mac to a transcription service to be transcribed, and the transcript to the model — that's the whole list. We run no proxy and no server in that path, so your meeting content never reaches us. Everything else is Markdown and JSON on your Mac.  
音频仅从Mac转至转录服务再至模型——流程至此终结。全程无代理服务器，会议内容永不触达我们。其余皆为存储于你Mac的Markdown和JSON文件。  

Open the files in any editor. Ever.  
所有文件始终可用任意编辑器打开。  

A day of meetings  
今日会议看板  
Today's calendar is on the launch pad. One click opens the meeting with attendees and project already filled in.  
当日日程一览无余。一键开启会议，参会者与项目信息自动填充。  

Cards appear as things are said. Type a question silently, or just say “hey Bean, mock that up” out loud. Pause for the off-record bit.  
发言即生卡片。可静默输入问题，或直接说"嘿Bean，画个草图"。非正式讨论时可暂停记录。  

Below about half your screen it folds into a single newest-first feed, so it sits next to the call window instead of fighting it.  
界面下半部折叠为最新优先的流式视图，与通话窗口和平共处。  

Duplicates merge, what's left is ranked with blockers first. Nothing is deleted — “show all” is one click away.  
自动合并重复项，按阻碍优先级排序。内容永不删除——"显示全部"一键可达。  

Export the summary, transcript, cards and every draft straight into your Obsidian vault or project folder.  
将摘要、转录稿、卡片及所有草案直输Obsidian知识库或项目文件夹。  

Ask questions across every meeting you've ever had — who owned that, when did we decide it, what did they object to.
