Alex Cornell

Alex Cornell

---

is a design director at Meta, where he leads design for Muse. In this episode, he explains how progressive disclosure makes Muse approachable for casual users without limiting technical ones, why its main chat is designed as a single persistent relationship, and how generative UI can feel personal without constantly changing the interface. He also shares how he uses Muse to turn years of Instagram saves into a shopping catalog, analyze his golf and chess games, and transform a 20-year-old blog into a daily podcast.

是Meta的设计总监，负责Muse的设计工作。在本期节目中，他解释了渐进式披露（progressive disclosure）如何让Muse对普通用户友好易用，同时又不限制技术型用户；为什么其主聊天被设计为一种单一、持续的对话关系；以及生成式UI（generative UI）如何在不过度改变界面的情况下带来个性化体验。他还分享了自己如何利用Muse将多年的Instagram收藏转化为购物目录、分析自己的高尔夫和国际象棋对局，以及将一个拥有20年历史的博客转变为每日播客。

---

Biggest takeaways:

最重要的收获：

---

AI agents should be designed for conversations with people, not interactions with software.

AI智能体应当为与人的对话而设计，而非为与软件的交互而设计。

---

Alex’s guiding question for Muse was “How would this work if you were talking to another human?” That principle shaped everything from removing traditional message bubbles to placing the agent at the top of the screen instead of inside a standard chat window.

Alex设计Muse时的核心指导问题是：“如果你是在和另一个人交谈，这件事会如何运作？”这一原则贯穿了所有设计决策——从移除传统的消息气泡，到将智能体置于屏幕顶部而非标准聊天窗口内。

---

Progressive disclosure is one of the most useful principles in AI product design.

渐进式披露是AI产品设计中最有用的原则之一。

---

Muse represents the same activity at three levels: a simple emoji for casual users, a task list for anyone who wants more detail, and raw logs for engineers. Each person gets the transparency they need without adding clutter for everyone else.

Muse在三个层面上呈现同一活动：对普通用户显示一个简单的表情符号，对想要更多细节的用户显示任务列表，对工程师显示原始日志。每个人都能获得所需的透明度，而不会给其他人增加信息干扰。

---

Visible chain-of-thought is often a workaround for latency, not a valuable feature.

可见的思维链（chain-of-thought）往往只是应对延迟的权宜之计，而非真正有价值的功能。

---

Many AI products display reasoning steps to reassure users while a slow model works. Muse is fast enough to operate quietly, showing that agents do not need to perform their thinking when they can simply deliver the result.

许多AI产品在慢速模型运行时会展示推理步骤，以安抚用户。而Muse足够快速，可以安静地完成任务，这表明当智能体能够直接给出结果时，并不需要“表演”思考过程。

---

Generative UI works best when the structure stays fixed and the content changes.

生成式UI在结构固定、内容变化时效果最佳。

---

Muse’s avatar, feed items, and idea cards are deliberately designed surfaces. The dynamic elements are the agent’s status emoji, personalized suggestions, and small animations, allowing the experience to feel alive without rebuilding the interface every session.

Muse的头像、信息流条目和创意卡片都是经过精心设计的固定界面元素。动态部分则包括智能体的状态表情符号、个性化推荐和小型动画，让体验保持鲜活，而无需在每次会话中重新构建界面。

---

Instagram saves may be one of the most overlooked data sources for consumer AI.

Instagram的收藏可能是消费级AI最被忽视的数据来源之一。

---

Alex connected years of saved posts to Muse, asked it to organize them, and turned the results into shopping recommendations styled like a SkyMall catalog. It gave forgotten personal data a practical new use without requiring technical setup.

Alex将多年的收藏帖子接入Muse，让它进行整理，并将结果转化为类似SkyMall商品目录风格的购物推荐。这让被遗忘的个人数据获得了实用的新用途，且无需任何技术配置。

---

Designing non-deterministic systems requires a different relationship with craft.

设计非确定性系统需要以不同的方式看待“工艺”这件事。

---

Alex has spent his career controlling exactly how interfaces appeared, but Muse looks different for every person and cannot guarantee pixel-perfect outputs. Learning to judge the results by usefulness rather than visual precision has been one of the most liberating shifts in his career.

Alex的职业生涯一直在精确控制界面的呈现方式，但Muse对每个人呈现的效果都不同，无法保证像素级的完美输出。学会以实用性而非视觉精确度来评判结果，是他职业生涯中最具解放意义的转变之一。

---

Muse’s single persistent chat is a meaningful product bet.

Muse的单一持续聊天模式是一个意义深远的产晶赌注。

---

Instead of dividing every interaction into separate threads, it treats the main conversation as an ongoing relationship with the agent. Side chats handle scoped tasks, but the central thread preserves continuous context in a way Alex believes the industry has not fully explored.

它没有将每次交互拆分为独立的对话线程，而是将主对话视为与智能体之间持续发展的关系。侧边聊天用于处理范围明确的任务，而中央对话线程则保留了持续的上下文——Alex认为，这一方向尚未被行业充分探索。

---

Polite prompting may be an effective strategy, not merely good manners.

礼貌的提示可能是一种有效策略，而不仅仅是良好的修养。

---

Alex responds to frustrating results with calm, specific feedback rather than anger. He’s also seen agents change their responses when users express frustration more sharply. His conclusion is that clear, respectful direction may produce better results.

面对令人沮丧的结果，Alex会以冷静、具体的反馈而非愤怒来回应。他还观察到，当用户更强烈地表达不满时，智能体会改变其回应方式。他的结论是：清晰、尊重的引导可能会带来更好的结果。
