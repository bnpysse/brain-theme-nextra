【文章标题】：Robin Williams' Daughter to Fans Creating AI Videos: 'Have Some Shame'  
罗宾·威廉姆斯之女怒斥AI换脸视频制作者："要点脸"  

【文章正文】：  
Zelda Williams is calling for fans to stop circulating AI-generated videos of her late father, Robin Williams, nearly a year after she last took to social media to ask them not to send her augmented content featuring the actor.  
距离泽尔达·威廉姆斯上次在社交媒体恳请粉丝不要发送AI合成视频近一年后，这位已故演员罗宾·威廉姆斯的女儿再次发声，呼吁停止传播其父亲的AI生成影像。  

In a statement posted to X on Monday, the "Lisa Frankenstein" director wrote that a "supposedly 'private video'" circulating of her father "is clearly AI, and not even particularly convincing AI," she wrote.  
这位《丽莎·弗兰肯斯坦》导演周一在X平台发文指出，当前流传的所谓"私人视频"明显是AI合成产物，且"连像样的AI都算不上"。  

"Anyone who claims to be a fan who believes it clearly listened to his movies on mute because that voice is robotic and terrible. That said, a human made a robot create it, and to you I say: leave him out of your delusional bullshit and let him rest. If you cannot make your case without making a dead man make it more convincing for you, then tell me: who's the one manipulating the public thru media now?"  
"任何自称粉丝却相信这段视频的人，肯定都是静音看他的电影——因为这声音既机械又难听。但归根结底是有人用AI制造了它，我要对这些人说：别用你们的妄想症玷污他，让他安息吧。如果非要靠死人来佐证观点，那现在到底是谁在通过媒体操纵公众？"  

Zelda continued: "I hate this cesspool of an app. It's mostly bots and people willingly being duped by bots at this point so not sure why I feel the need to come back to clarify, but I love him, so I will. Just because he's gone does not mean he's now your puppet. Have some shame."  
她继续写道："我讨厌这个粪坑般的应用。现在这里充斥着机器人账号和甘愿受骗的人，本不必再费口舌——但我爱他，所以必须说清楚。他的离世不意味着变成你们的提线木偶。做个人吧。"

The iconic comedian, who starred in films including "Jumanji," "Good Will Hunting," "Dead Poets Society," "Hook," "Mrs. Doubtfire," and more, died in 2014 at the age of 63. In recent years, his likeness has become a popular target for AI recreation as users continue to create and share augmented videos of the actor.  
这位曾主演《勇敢者游戏》《心灵捕手》《死亡诗社》《铁钩船长》《窈窕奶爸》等经典作品的喜剧大师于2014年离世，享年63岁。近年来随着AI技术发展，他的形象成为数字重制热门目标。  

This isn't the first time Zelda has voiced her feelings about AI; Back in Oct. 2025, she wrote "Please, just stop sending me AI videos of Dad" on Instagram.  
这并非泽尔达首次就AI问题发声——早在2025年10月，她就在Instagram写道："求你们别再发AI制作的父亲视频了"。  

"Stop believing I wanna see it or that I'll understand, I don't and I won't. If you're just trying to troll me, I've seen way worse, I'll restrict and move on. But please, if you've got any decency, just stop doing this to him and to me, to everyone even, full stop. It's dumb, it's a waste of time and energy, and believe me, it's NOT what he'd want."  
"别以为我想看或能理解，我根本不愿看也不会理解。若是存心挑衅，更恶毒的攻击我都见过，拉黑便是。但若你们还有半点良知，请立即停止这种行为——对他、对我、对所有人。这既愚蠢又浪费精力，而且相信我，这绝非他的本意。"  

Just last month, she revived her father's Instagram account with siblings Zak and Cody in an attempt to "fight back against rampant AI abuse of his voice and likeness on here."  
上月她与兄弟扎克、科迪重启父亲Instagram账号，旨在"反击平台上对其声音肖像的AI滥用乱象"。
