Key Takeaways

核心发现

Seven of America’s 10 youngest Forbes 400 billionaires built their fortunes in AI.

全美十大最年轻福布斯富豪中，有七位通过人工智能积累财富

Anthropic accounts for four of the 10, with their combined fortunes totaling an estimated $62 billion.

其中四位来自Anthropic公司，合计资产约620亿美元

Nine of the 10 are self-made, but the lone heir, Lukas Walton, is the richest at $44.4 billion.

十人中九位白手起家，唯一继承者卢卡斯·沃尔顿以444亿美元身家居首

Artificial intelligence isn’t just creating some of America’s most valuable startups. It’s also creating a new generation of billionaires.

人工智能不仅催生了美国最具价值的初创企业，更造就了新一代亿万富豪

The 2026 Forbes 400 includes founders and executives behind Anthropic, OpenAI, Surge AI, and Cognition among its youngest members, highlighting how quickly AI wealth has risen into the upper ranks of America’s richest people.

2026年福布斯400强最年轻成员中包含Anthropic、OpenAI、Surge AI和Cognition的创始人及高管，彰显AI财富如何快速跻身美国顶级富豪行列

This visualization ranks the 10 youngest members of the Forbes 400 by age and shows the companies behind their fortunes.

本可视化图表按年龄排序展示福布斯400强最年轻十位成员及其财富来源企业

Net worth figures are as of Sept. 4, 2026, and a fortune of at least $4.4 billion was required to make this year’s Forbes 400.

净资产数据截至2026年9月4日，入选本年度福布斯400强需至少拥有44亿美元财富

Four Anthropic Cofounders Are Worth $15.5 Billion Each

四位Anthropic创始人各拥155亿美元身家

Anthropic accounts for four of the 10 youngest billionaires: Sam McCandlish, Jack Clark, Daniela Amodei, and Tom Brown.

Anthropic贡献了四位最年轻富豪：山姆·麦坎德利什、杰克·克拉克、丹妮拉·阿莫代伊与汤姆·布朗

Forbes estimates that each holds a stake worth $15.5 billion, putting their combined fortunes at roughly $62 billion. The four range in age from 36 to 39.

据福布斯估算，四人各持155亿美元股权，总财富约620亿美元，年龄介于36至39岁

Other AI fortunes include OpenAI cofounder Greg Brockman, whose estimated net worth stands at $25.5 billion, Surge AI founder Edwin Chen at $18 billion, and Cognition cofounder Steven Hao at $5.8 billion.

其他AI富豪包括OpenAI联合创始人格雷格·布罗克曼（255亿美元）、Surge AI创始人陈德伟（180亿美元）及Cognition联合创始人史蒂文·郝（58亿美元）

Most of These Fortunes Are Tied to Private Companies

多数财富与私营企业挂钩

Another defining feature of the ranking is how much of the wealth comes from private companies. Eight of the 10 fortunes are based on private-company stakes valued using recent funding rounds, according to the source note accompanying the Forbes data.

另一显著特征是财富多源自私营企业。根据福布斯数据注释，十人中八位的财富基于最近融资轮次估值的私营企业股权

Unlike public stocks, private companies don’t have continuously traded market prices. New funding rounds can reset company valuations and significantly change the estimated paper wealth of major shareholders.

与上市公司不同，私营企业无持续交易市价。新融资轮次会重设公司估值，大幅改变大股东账面财富预估

That dynamic is especially important for privately held AI companies, where rising valuations have created multibillion-dollar fortunes well before many of these businesses have reached the public markets.

这种动态对私营AI企业尤为重要——在许多公司未上市前，估值上涨就已创造数十亿美元财富

The Only Heir Is Also the Richest

唯一继承者同时是最富者

At 39, Lukas Walton has an estimated net worth of $44.4 billion stemming from retail giant Walmart. He is the only person among the 10 whose fortune is identified as inherited.

39岁的卢卡斯·沃尔顿凭借零售巨头沃尔玛积累约444亿美元净资产，是十人中唯一被标注为继承财富者

His fortune also stands above every individual AI billionaire shown.

其财富也超过所有AI亿万富翁个体

The ranking captures two very different paths to extreme wealth within the same generation: a decades-old retail fortune at the top and a wave of newer fortunes built around some of the world’s fastest-growing technology companies.

该排行呈现同代人积累巨额财富的两条迥异路径：顶端是积淀数十年的零售业财富，下方则是围绕全球增长最快科技公司崛起的新贵浪潮
