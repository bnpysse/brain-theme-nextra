【文章标题】：Four eras of San Francisco tech culture  
旧金山科技文化的四个时代  

【文章正文】：  
Photo by Jack Dorsey via Wikimedia.org  
杰克·多尔西拍摄，图片来源Wikimedia.org  

The other day I went on the Monitoring the Situation livestream, and we talked about the history of San Francisco tech culture:  
前几日我参加了"Monitoring the Situation"直播节目，我们探讨了旧金山科技文化的历史：  

I’ve never actually worked in the tech industry, but I’ve lived on and off in the Bay Area for a quarter of a century now — including the last 10 years in San Francisco itself — and I’ve spent a lot of that time hanging out with people who work in or around the tech industry.  
虽然我从未真正在科技行业工作过，但过去二十五年来我断断续续生活在湾区——包括最近十年就住在旧金山——期间大量时间都在与科技行业从业者或相关人士打交道。  

Throughout much of that time, I was also reading some the same source materials that inspired lots of tech people — science fiction, futurist websites and books, nerdy forums and blogs, and so on.  
在此期间，我也阅读了许多启发科技人士的相同素材——科幻小说、未来主义网站和书籍、极客论坛和博客等等。  

Over that time, I’ve watched more and more people start to care about all this. Back in the early 2000s, as far as I could tell, pretty much nobody in the world cared about what some dorky hoodie-wearing computer programmers were up to on a Saturday night in SF.  
这些年来，我目睹越来越多人开始关注这一切。早在2000年代初，据我所知，世界上几乎没人在乎一群穿着连帽衫的呆萌程序员周六晚上在旧金山做什么。  

Fast forward 25 years, and the President of the United States is gathering opposition research on SF techie subcultures:  
转眼25年过去，如今美国总统正在收集旧金山技术亚文化的反对派研究资料：  

President Trump's allies are targeting Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei as the face of AI "doomerism" and a founding father of the effective altruism movement that's come under increasing political fire…  
特朗普总统的盟友将Anthropic CEO达里奥·阿莫代伊标榜为AI"末日论"的代表人物，以及近期备受政治抨击的有效利他主义运动的奠基人...  

A memo began circulating within the White House this week that seeks to paint effective altruism as a fringe, cultish collective out of touch with mainstream America.  
本周白宫内部流传一份备忘录，试图将有效利他主义描绘成与美国主流社会脱节的边缘邪教组织。  

The document lists a veritable parade of horribles for the average conservative mind: abortion, veganism, devaluing human life by overvaluing animals or granting "digital minds" civil rights-like protections.  
这份文件列出了一系列令保守派反感的清单：堕胎、素食主义、通过过度重视动物或赋予"数字意识"公民权利般的保护来贬低人类生命价值。  

If you could go back in time and tell the 2001 version of me that the President’s allies are talking on national news about shrimp consciousness, I would have laughed out loud.  
若能回到2001年告诉那时的我，总统盟友正在全国新闻上讨论虾类意识，我绝对会笑出声来。（当然，除非你告诉我总统是谁。）  

Ladies and gentlemen, it really happened. The nerds took over the world. We have arrived.  
女士们先生们，这确实发生了。书呆子们接管了世界。我们已抵达新时代。  

I’ve never been an Effective Altruist, of course, and I’ve only hung out a little bit in those circles.  
当然，我从未成为有效利他主义者，只是偶尔在那个圈子活动。  

But anyway, that whole intellectual history is a story for another day (or you can just go ask AI).  
不过这段思想史改日再谈（或者你可以直接问AI）。  

Today what I wanted to do is to give a brief anthropological history of the tech culture in San Francisco since the turn of the century, from my own quasi-outsider’s perspective.  
今天我想做的是，以我这个准局外人的视角，简述本世纪以来旧金山科技文化的人类学历史。  

There have been four distinct eras over that time period:  
这段时期可划分为四个鲜明时代：  

The Rebel Era (2001-2012)  
反叛时代（2001-2012）  

The Corporate Era (2012-2019)  
企业时代（2012-2019）  

The Interregnum (2020-2022)  
空位期（2020-2022）  

The AI Era (2022-present)  
AI时代（2022至今）  

But let’s start with some background.  
但让我们先从背景说起。  

The 20th century: SF tech pre-history  
20世纪：旧金山科技史前史  

SF tech culture is the modern descendant of a very long lineage.  
旧金山科技文化有着悠久的传承谱系。  

The modern tech industry began in the suburban towns around Stanford University — Menlo Park, Palo Alto, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, and so on.  
现代科技产业始于斯坦福大学周边的郊区城镇——门洛帕克、帕罗奥图、库比蒂诺、森尼韦尔等地。  

This had been a hub of the old electric equipment industry from World War 2 onward, and so William Shockley chose Mountain View to start the first semiconductor company in 1955.  
这里自二战以来就是传统电子设备产业中心，因此威廉·肖克利1955年选择在山景城创立第一家半导体公司。  

Fairchild broke off from Shockley, and Intel broke off from Fairchild.  
仙童半导体从肖克利公司分离，英特尔又从仙童独立而出。  

The whole area became a hub of semiconductor manufacturing in the 60s and 70s, then computer companies (Apple was founded in 1976 in Cupertino), then software companies like Oracle...  
整个地区在60-70年代成为半导体制造中心，继而涌现电脑公司（苹果1976年创立于库比蒂诺），再到甲骨文等软件公司...
