What is the criteria for being on this list?

- Weird, public politics, as judged by yours truly  
- 怪异的、公开的政治立场（由本人判断）  
- Leader in their community or otherwise powerful/influential  
- 社区领袖或具有权力/影响力  
- Involved in free and open source software  
- 参与自由及开源软件  

But that guy does good work!  
但那个人做了好事啊！  

A lot of people on this list have done good work. A lot of people not on this list have done good work, too.

See also: Violet Blue’s “But he does good work.”

Will you write a “cool people” list, too?  
你们会写一份“酷人物”榜单吗？  

No. Who’s on the list or off the list will introduce weird social dynamics, and risks creating a kind of popularity contest. Also, if I published a list like that, it would paint a target on the backs of everyone on the list for harassment or worse.  
不会。上榜与否会引发怪异的社会动态，可能演变成人气竞赛。此外，发布这种榜单等于为每个上榜者贴上被骚扰甚至更糟的靶子。

Known for: nostr stuff

Known for: SerenityOS, Ladybird

Alias: AwesomeKling

Andreas is the founder of SerenityOS and the Ladybird web browser. He posted in support of Charlie Kirk after his assassination,[source] gets triggered by the phrase “punch nazis”,[source] and the idea of using gender-neutral pronouns in documentation.[source] Andreas boosted David Heinemeier Hansson’s tweets on DEI and the white supremacist “white replacement” theory,[source] and expressed solidarity with David’s persecution for his “banal, mainstream” positions, positions which include, for example, the forced expulsion of non-white people from Europe.[source]

Ladybird and Andreas have received sponsorships from FUTO, Cloudflare, Shopify and 37signals (i.e. David Heinemeier Hansson), Proton, Jetbrains, Sourcegraph, and others.[source]

Brendan Eich  
Brendan Eich  

Known for: Mozilla co-founder, Brave browser, creator of JavaScript  
知名于：Mozilla联合创始人、Brave浏览器、JavaScript之父  

Brendan got a lot of flack several years ago, resigning as Mozilla CEO, after he donated a few thousand dollars to support California’s Proposition 8, an initiative that would have banned same-sex marriage in California.[source]  
多年前因捐赠数千美元支持加州8号提案（旨在禁止同性婚姻）而辞去Mozilla CEO职务。  

After Mozilla, he moved on to co-found and lead as CEO the Brave Browser, which he used to push his own cryptocurrency micro-payments system.[source] He used Brave to support the hate website Kiwi Farms, which has been alleged to have instigated several suicides as a result of their harassment, working with their site admins to pose Brave as a safe and secure means for their members to access the site and conduct their harassment.[source] Kiwi Farms also took advantage of Brave’s crypto scheme for funding, which was brought to Eich’s attention and never corrected.[source]  
后联合创立Brave浏览器并任CEO，借此推广其加密货币微支付系统。他利用Brave支持仇恨网站Kiwi Farms（该网站因骚扰行为被指控导致多起自杀），与网站管理员合作将Brave包装成用户安全访问及实施骚扰的工具，且纵容Kiwi Farms利用Brave加密方案募资。
