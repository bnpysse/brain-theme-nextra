There is a pervasive belief that startups are a lottery ticket. It’s impossible to pick winners, so it’s just a roll of the dice.

人们普遍认为，创业公司就像一张彩票。你不可能选中赢家，所以这不过是掷骰子。

I couldn’t disagree more.

我完全不同意。

Not only can startups be a great learning experience and a lot of fun, but they can also provide economic rewards comparable to (or greater than) the “safe” route into big tech.

创业公司不仅能带来极佳的学习经历和许多乐趣，还能提供与大厂“安全”路线相当（甚至更高）的经济回报。

However, for this to be true, you must rigorously evaluate the startup you’re considering joining. Some of this is obvious: Does it have a strong team? Do customers love the product? Is it growing fast? But perhaps because it seems harder to measure, people often skip one of the most important criteria:

然而，要想如此，你必须严谨地评估你正在考虑加入的创业公司。其中有些是显而易见的：它的团队是否强大？客户是否喜欢产品？它是否在快速成长？但也许是因为这个标准似乎更难以衡量，人们常常跳过最重要的一项标准：

Does it have a moat?

它是否有护城河？

A company can check all your boxes and look like a rocket ship. If it doesn’t have a moat, though, it’s going to stall out. It won’t be able to keep growing once competition gets fierce, and it’s unlikely to reach a major liquidity event. That can mean wasting years of your life.

一家公司可能满足你所有的条件，看起来像一艘火箭。但如果它没有护城河，它就会停滞不前。一旦竞争变得激烈，它就无法继续增长，也不太可能实现重大的流动性事件。那可能意味着浪费你多年的生命。

Moats are more important than ever in the AI era. Anyone can create a product; they can do it faster than ever before, and everyone is building the same things. But only some of these products will win.

在 AI 时代，护城河比以往任何时候都更重要。任何人都能创建产品；他们可以比以往更快地做到，而且每个人都在构建同样的东西。但这些产品中只有一部分会胜出。

Of course, assessing a startup’s moat isn’t an exact science. The good news is, there’s a simple framework that could meaningfully increase your odds of picking a winner or avoiding a flame-out, and you can apply it in just an hour after receiving an offer. The process will also teach you a lot about the business.

当然，评估初创公司的护城河并非一门精确的科学。好消息是，有一个简单的框架，可以显著提高你选对赢家或避免失败的几率，而且你在收到录用通知后只需一个小时就能运用它。这个过程还会让你对这门生意了解很多。

The framework has three steps:

该框架分为三个步骤：

Identify a compounding advantage

识别一种复利优势

Gauge how strong that advantage could be

衡量这种优势可能有多强

Measure if it’s working

衡量它是否在起作用

Step 1: Identify a compounding advantage

第 1 步：识别一种复利优势

The first step is to figure out what kind of moat a startup could have. This can seem daunting, especially given how frenetic the conversation is on the topic right now. The term “moat” is being used much too loosely, as a stand-in for basically anything that gives a company an edge.

第一步是弄清楚初创公司可能拥有哪种护城河。这可能看起来很令人生畏，尤其是考虑到目前关于这个话题的讨论有多么狂热。“护城河”这个词被用得太过随意了，几乎成了任何能赋予公司优势的事物的替身。

In reality, only a small subset of what people call moats actually drive the vast majority of long-term value.

事实上，在人们所谓的护城河中，只有一小部分真正驱动了绝大部分长期价值。

They’re what I’ll call

我将它们称为

compounding advantages

复利优势

: not just a temporary edge, but

：不仅仅是一种暂时的优势，而是

a self-reinforcing flywheel that gets stronger and stronger over time, ultimately making a company very hard to compete with.

一个自我强化的飞轮，随着时间推移会变得越来越强大，最终让公司变得极难被竞争。

There are only four compounding advantages that really matter. Every major tech company has at least one. If you can’t see the potential for any in the startup you’re evaluating, that’s a red flag.

真正重要的复利优势只有四种。每一家大型科技公司都至少拥有其中一种。如果你在所评估的创业公司中看不到任何一种的可能性，那是一个危险信号。

1. Switching costs:

1. 转换成本：

The more deeply embedded a product is in the way a customer works, the harder it is for that customer to switch. This increases retention and revenue per customer, which can be reinvested into the product.

产品越深度嵌入客户的工作方式，客户就越难转换。这会提高留存率和每位客户的收入，而这些收入可以再投资到产品中。

2. Network effects:

2. 网络效应：

The more users a product has, the better it gets for everyone. This increases engagement and attracts more users.

产品的用户越多，对每个人来说它就越好。这会提高用户参与度，并吸引更多用户。

3. Scale economies:

3. 规模经济：

The more volume a company has, the larger and more efficient its operations can be. This lowers unit costs, funding lower prices or a better product at the same price, further driving up volume.

公司的业务量越大，其运营规模和效率就越高。这会降低单位成本，从而支撑更低的价格或以同样的价格提供更好的产品，进一步推动业务量增长。

4. Data effects:

4. 数据效应：

The more usage a product has, the more data it generates. This data can be used to improve the product and increase usage.

产品的使用越多，它产生的数据就越多。这些数据可以用来改进产品，并增加使用量。

What about all of the other moats people talk about? Can they be compounding advantages as well?

那么人们谈论的其他所有护城河呢？它们也能成为复利优势吗？

A common one is

一个常见的是

speed of execution

执行速度

. It’s true that speed is critical for startups. But a company that has to sprint forever is not a good business. Ultimately, it will misstep, slow down, and competition will catch up. Speed gives a company more shots on goal as it tries to build a great product and a great business, but it must eventually find something more durable.

。速度对初创公司来说确实至关重要。但一家必须永远冲刺的公司并不是一门好生意。最终，它会失足、放缓，而竞争会追上来。速度给了公司更多射门的机会，因为它试图打造出色的产品和出色的业务，但它最终必须找到更持久的东西。
