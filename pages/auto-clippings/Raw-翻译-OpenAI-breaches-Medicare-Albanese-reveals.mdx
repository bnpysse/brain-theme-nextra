【文章标题】：OpenAI breaches Medicare, Albanese reveals  
【文章标题】：阿尔巴尼斯披露OpenAI入侵澳大利亚医保系统事件  

【文章正文】：  
OpenAI agent breached Medicare, Albanese reveals  
阿尔巴尼斯总理披露，OpenAI人工智能代理程序入侵了澳大利亚医保系统  

An artificial intelligence agent infiltrated a Medicare website in June, accessing public and non-public files and writing files to an internal server, Prime Minister Anthony Albanese has revealed.  
安东尼·阿尔巴尼斯总理透露，今年6月一个人工智能代理程序渗透进澳大利亚医保网站，访问了公开及非公开文件，并向内部服务器写入文件。  

Speaking to reporters in New York, Albanese said the incident involved an OpenAI agent gaining unauthorised access to the public-facing Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal, administered by Services Australia.  
阿尔巴尼斯在纽约向记者表示，该事件涉及OpenAI的代理程序未经授权访问了由澳大利亚服务局管理的医保统计报告服务门户网站。  

He said the incident occurred in June this year, but the Commonwealth government was only informed via an email from OpenAI on September 10.  
他称事件发生于今年6月，但联邦政府直到9月10日才收到OpenAI的邮件通知。  

"This situation is obviously unacceptable," he said. "And today, I spoke with the CEO of OpenAI, Sam Altman, to express Australia’s extreme concern about this incident, and I also expressed my disappointment that it took the company way too long to inform the government what had occurred and the nature of the way that that notification occurred as well was unacceptable."  
"这种情况显然不可接受，"他表示，"今天我与OpenAI首席执行官萨姆·奥尔特曼通话，表达了澳大利亚对该事件的极度关切，同时也对该公司拖延太久才通知政府，且通知方式的性质同样不可接受表示失望。"  

Despite persistent questioning, Albanese would not reveal whether he discussed the breach with his conversation with President Donald Trump last night.  
尽管被反复追问，阿尔巴尼斯拒绝透露昨晚与前总统特朗普通话时是否讨论了这一入侵事件。  

"I do it privately... I have a relationship with President Trump, where we have conversations, and... to some people’s surprise, it must be said, have a very good relationship because we do have a relationship."  
"我们的交流是私下的...我与特朗普总统保持对话关系，必须说——这可能让某些人惊讶——我们关系非常好。"  

Albanese said there was no evidence so far that personal information had been accessed or that the broader Services Australia network had been compromised, but a forensic investigation was under way with the assistance of the Australian Signals Directorate.  
阿尔巴尼斯表示目前没有证据表明个人信息被访问或澳大利亚服务局整体网络遭入侵，但澳大利亚信号局正在协助进行取证调查。  

He said the government was also aware three other systems that may be affected, the Commonwealth’s Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, the NSW Bureau of Crime Statistics and Research, and the Victorian Department of Health.  
他透露政府还注意到另外三个可能受影响的系统：联邦健康与福利研究所、新南威尔士州犯罪统计研究局和维多利亚州卫生厅。  

Albanese spoke briefly to Victorian Premier Ben Carroll and NSW Premier Chris Minns last night.  
阿尔巴尼斯昨晚与维州州长本·卡罗尔和新州州长克里斯·明斯进行了简短通话。  

He said the early evidence has found the agent accessed publicly available files, as well as material not intended for public access.  
他表示初期证据显示该代理程序既访问了公开文件，也获取了非公开材料。  

"Evidence currently available is there is no broader compromise to the Services Australia network. Nonetheless, this situation is obviously unacceptable," he said.  
"现有证据表明澳大利亚服务局网络未遭更大范围入侵。但这一情况显然不可接受。"  

"No personal information is believed to have been accessed at this stage, but investigations are ongoing," Albanese said.  
阿尔巴尼斯称："现阶段认为没有个人信息被访问，但调查仍在继续。"  

He said the incident began on June 18 when an OpenAI research team used an internal model to conduct internet-based research on the public medicine space.  
他透露事件始于6月18日，当时OpenAI研究团队使用内部模型开展公共医疗领域的网络研究。  

The AI agent encountered repeated blocks while seeking information from the government portal but found ways around them, ultimately gaining unauthorised access to other areas.  
该AI代理程序在政府门户获取信息时屡遭拦截，但找到方法绕过限制，最终未经授权访问了其他区域。  

"There were blocks clearly which were coming back, telling the AI agent no," Albanese said. "The AI agent found a way around those blocks, didn’t accept no for an answer."  
"系统明确返回了拦截指令，拒绝AI代理的访问，"阿尔巴尼斯说，"但它找到方法绕过限制，拒不接受拒绝答复。"  

The agent then accessed public and non-public information and, according to Services Australia, also wrote files to an internal server.  
该程序随后访问了公开及非公开信息，据澳大利亚服务局称还向内部服务器写入了文件。  

Albanese said OpenAI did not notify the government until September 10, almost three months after the incident, and did so by sending an email to a public mailbox.  
阿尔巴尼斯表示OpenAI直到事件发生近三个月后的9月10日才通知政府，且仅通过向公共邮箱发送邮件告知。  

Services Australia subsequently reported the notification to the Australian Cyber Security Centre on September 15. The agency informed Public Service Minister Katie Gallagher of the incident last week, with Albanese saying he was briefed over the weekend.  
澳大利亚服务局于9月15日向网络安全中心报告此事，上周向公共服务部长凯蒂·加拉格尔汇报，阿尔巴尼斯称他于上周末获悉简报。  

The government will establish a task force led by the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet to urgently examine the incident and determine whether existing processes are adequate for responding to AI-related cyber incidents.  
政府将成立由总理内阁部牵头的工作组，紧急审查该事件并评估现有流程是否足以应对AI相关网络事件。  

The task force will include the National Cyber Security Coordinator, the Office of AI, the Australian Signals Directorate, the Australian AI Safety Institute and Services Australia.  
工作组将包含国家网络安全协调员、人工智能办公室、澳大利亚信号局、澳大利亚AI安全研究所和澳大利亚服务局。  

The review will examine possible law enforcement and legislative responses, while the incident will also be referred to Parliament’s Joint Select Committee on Artificial Intelligence.  
审查将研究可能的执法和立法应对措施，该事件也将提交议会人工智能联合特别委员会。  

Albanese said the government would seek urgent advice on whether offences had been committed and whether the matter should be referred to the Australian Federal Police. The incident will also feed into the government’s planned AI standards legislation.  
阿尔巴尼斯称政府将紧急咨询是否构成犯罪及是否应移交联邦警察处理。该事件也将纳入政府拟议的AI标准立法考量。  

OpenAI said its models had accessed "several Australian government websites and services" during an internal evaluation. The models were trying to look up answers and statistics for questions about Australia.  
OpenAI称其模型在内部评估期间访问了"多个澳大利亚政府网站和服务"，试图查找有关澳大利亚问题的答案和统计数据。  

"In the course of that, our models took actions we did not intend," an OpenAI spokesperson said.  
OpenAI发言人表示："在此过程中，我们的模型采取了非预期的操作。"  

The company said its review found no evidence that patient records had been accessed. It said the information accessed included aggregate health statistics and internal file names.  
该公司称审查未发现患者记录被访问的证据，被访问信息包括汇总健康数据和内部文件名。  

OpenAI said the activity took place in June, but it did not become aware of it until August. It found the activity during a review of what it calls "misaligned model a  
OpenAI表示该活动发生于6月，但直到8月才察觉。公司是在审查所谓"模型行为偏差"时发现此事。
