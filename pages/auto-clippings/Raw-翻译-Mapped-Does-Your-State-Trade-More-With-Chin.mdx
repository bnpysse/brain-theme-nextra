Key Takeaways

核心发现

All but five U.S. states traded more with the European Union than China in 2025.

2025年，除五个州外，美国所有州与欧盟的贸易额都超过中国

China accounted for 13% of California’s goods trade, the highest share among the five China-oriented states.

中国占加利福尼亚州货物贸易的13%，在五个"亲华州"中占比最高

Alaska, Illinois, Oregon, and Wyoming shifted to trading more with the EU than China in 2025.

阿拉斯加、伊利诺伊、俄勒冈和怀俄明四州在2025年转向与欧盟开展更多贸易

China and the European Union are two of America’s biggest trading partners, but their influence looks very different from state to state.

中国和欧盟是美国最大的两个贸易伙伴，但其对各州的影响力差异显著

A Trade War on Two Fronts

双线贸易战

Within months of the start of President Donald Trump’s second term, the U.S. announced sweeping tariffs on dozens of economies, including major trading partners such as China and the EU.

特朗普总统第二任期开始数月内，美国就对包括中国、欧盟等主要贸易伙伴在内的数十个经济体全面加征关税

The U.S. initially announced a 34% reciprocal tariff on Chinese goods in April 2025, which was later raised above 100% amid retaliatory measures before being reduced. Against that backdrop,

美国最初在2025年4月宣布对中国商品征收34%的对等关税，后在报复性措施中升至100%以上，最终又有所降低。在此背景下，

Four states shifted to trading more with the EU than China in 2025: Alaska, Illinois, Oregon, and Wyoming. They joined 41 other states and Washington, D.C., in the EU-oriented group.

2025年有四个州转向与欧盟开展更多贸易：阿拉斯加、伊利诺伊、俄勒冈和怀俄明。它们与其他41个州和华盛顿特区共同组成"亲欧阵营"

With Illinois switching sides, every state east of the Mississippi River now trades more with the EU than with China.

随着伊利诺伊州转向，密西西比河以东所有州现在与欧盟的贸易额都超过中国

How EU Trade Gained Ground

欧盟贸易如何占据优势

The EU was also subject to the so-called "Liberation Day" tariffs, initially facing a 20% reciprocal tariff. Following negotiations between the U.S. and European Commission, the two sides announced a

欧盟同样受到所谓"解放日关税"影响，最初面临20%的对等关税。经过美欧委员会谈判，双方于2025年7月宣布达成

trade deal

贸易协议

in July 2025 that capped most U.S. tariffs on EU goods at 15%.

将美国对欧盟商品的大部分关税上限定为15%

Over the full year, U.S.-China goods trade declined by more than a quarter, while total EU-U.S. trade increased from 2024.

全年来看，美中货物贸易下降超25%，而欧美贸易总额较2024年有所增长

The states trading the most with the EU were Texas ($114 billion), Indiana ($110 billion), and California ($67 billion). Indiana stands out in particular, with the EU accounting for about half of its goods trade, supported by the state’s large pharmaceutical industry and companies such as Eli Lilly.

与欧盟贸易额最大的州是得克萨斯（1140亿美元）、印第安纳（1100亿美元）和加利福尼亚（670亿美元）。印第安纳州尤为突出，欧盟占其货物贸易约半数，这得益于该州庞大的制药产业和礼来等公司

The Five States Still Oriented Toward China

五个仍以中国为主要贸易对象的州

Only California, Nevada, New Mexico, South Dakota, and Washington traded more with China than with the European Union in 2025.

2025年，仅有加利福尼亚、内华达、新墨西哥、南达科他和华盛顿五州与中国的贸易额超过欧盟

California led by a wide margin, recording more than $86 billion in two-way goods trade with China.

加利福尼亚州以860亿美元的双边货物贸易额遥遥领先

The five states have distinct industries that help explain their trade ties with China. These include California’s port-driven electronics imports, aerospace exports from Washington, and semiconductor production in New Mexico.

这五个州拥有特色产业来解释其与中国的贸易联系，包括加州港口驱动的电子产品进口、华盛顿州的航空航天出口以及新墨西哥州的半导体生产
