【文章标题】：Ranked: Defense R&D Spending by Country  
各国国防研发支出排名  

【文章正文】：  
How Much Do Countries Spend on Defense R&D?  
各国国防研发投入几何？  

Key Takeaways  
核心发现  

The U.S. led defense R&D spending per $1,000 of GDP among OECD countries, at $3.64 in 2024.  
美国以每千美元GDP投入3.64美元（2024年数据）领跑经合组织国家国防研发支出。  

South Korea ranked second, allocating $2.06 per $1,000 of GDP to defense R&D.  
韩国以每千美元GDP投入2.06美元位列第二。  

Spending falls sharply after the top two, with 20th-ranked Greece allocating just $0.09 per $1,000 of GDP.  
前两名之后支出急剧下降，排名第20的希腊每千美元GDP仅投入0.09美元。  

Defense research and development is one way countries invest in future military capabilities, from advanced weapons systems to emerging technologies.  
国防研发是各国对未来军事能力的投资方式，涵盖从先进武器系统到新兴技术的各个领域。  

This visualization ranks selected OECD countries by defense research and development (R&D) budget allocations per $1,000 of GDP. The data comes from the OECD Economic Outlook, Volume 2026 Issue 1, with figures covering 2024 or the latest available year as of August 2026.  
本可视化图表以每千美元GDP国防研发预算为基准对经合组织国家进行排名，数据源自《2026年经合组织经济展望（第1卷）》，统计年份为2024年或截至2026年8月的最新可用数据。  

Defense R&D has also historically played an important role in shaping civilian technologies. Research tied to national security has helped support breakthroughs in fields such as computing, aerospace, satellites, medicine, and advanced materials. However, the link between military spending and innovation is not automatic, as secrecy can impede knowledge diffusion, among other factors.  
历史上国防研发对民用技术发展也至关重要。国家安全相关研究推动了计算、航空航天、卫星、医药和先进材料等领域的突破。但军费与创新并非自然关联，保密制度等因素可能阻碍知识传播。  

The U.S. Leads by a Wide Margin  
美国遥遥领先  

The United States ranked first, with defense R&D budget allocations equal to $3.64 per $1,000 of GDP. In absolute terms, the U.S. is also the world’s highest military-spending country by a wide margin, with 2025 spending totaling $954 billion and accounting for one-third of global military spending that year.  
美国以每千美元GDP投入3.64美元位居榜首。绝对数值上，美国2025年军费达9540亿美元，占全球总额三分之一，同样大幅领先各国。  

The U.S. allocation was nearly 1.8 times South Korea’s and more than 3.7 times the United Kingdom’s, which ranked second and third, respectively.  
美国投入是第二名韩国的1.8倍，第三名英国（0.97美元）的3.7倍以上。

Although U.S. military spending as a share of GDP decreased in 2025 compared with the annual average between 2015 and 2022, the Trump administration’s U.S. Department of War announced in April 2026 that its Fiscal Year 2027 (FY27) Defense Budget would request an unprecedented $1.5 trillion, a 42% increase compared with 2026 funding.  
尽管美国2025年军费占GDP比重较2015-2022年均值有所下降，但特朗普政府的美国战争部于2026年4月宣布，2027财年国防预算将申请创纪录的1.5万亿美元，较2026年增长42%。  

Included in that amount are plans to increase defense R&D spending from $16.6 billion in 2026 to $18.7 billion in 2027, with a stated focus on a general science and technology fund.  
该预算包含将国防研发支出从2026年的166亿美元增至2027年的187亿美元的计划，明确重点支持通用科技基金。  

South Korea and Europe Round Out the Top Five  
韩国与欧洲国家占据前五  

Allocating $2.06 per $1,000 of GDP to defense R&D, South Korea ranked second, making it the only other OECD country above $2.  
韩国以每千美元GDP投入2.06美元排名第二，成为经合组织中唯二超过2美元的国家。  

South Korea has emerged as one of the world’s fastest-growing arms producers and exporters, supported by sustained government investment in its defense industry.  
在政府持续投资国防工业的支持下，韩国已成为全球增长最快的武器生产国和出口国之一。  

Reflecting this growth, four South Korean companies appear in the SIPRI Top 100 list of global arms producers with the largest arms sales revenues in 2024. Their combined revenues increased by 30% from 2023 to 2024.  
这一增长体现在：2024年斯德哥尔摩国际和平研究所全球军火销售额百强榜中，四家韩国企业上榜，其总营收较2023年增长30%。  

Following South Korea, the ranking drops quickly. The United Kingdom placed third at $0.97 per $1,000 of GDP. Between 2019 and 2024, the UK’s Ministry of Defense tripled its net expenditure on R&D from £1.02 billion to £3.07 billion.  
韩国之后排名迅速下滑。英国以每千美元GDP投入0.97美元位列第三，其国防部2019-2024年研发净支出从10.2亿英镑增至30.7亿英镑，增长两倍。  

Germany followed in fourth place at $0.79 per $1,000 of GDP, while France ranked fifth at $0.50 per $1,000 of GDP. France and Germany are the European Union’s largest and second-largest defense R&D spenders, respectively.  
德国（0.79美元）和法国（0.50美元）分列第四、五位，分别是欧盟国防研发支出的第一、二大国。  

Most Countries Spend Far Less on Defense R&D  
多数国家国防研发投入较低  

Across the 20 OECD countries shown, the average defense R&D allocation was $0.58 per $1,000 of GDP.  
所示20个经合组织国家平均国防研发投入为每千美元GDP 0.58美元。  

The median was much lower, at $0.27 per $1,000 of GDP, showing how much the top spenders skew the average.  
中位数仅为0.27美元，凸显头部支出国对平均值的拉高效应。  

Within the top 20, the drop-off is steep. The U.S. allocation of $3.64 per $1,000 of GDP was more than seven times fifth-ranked France’s $0.50 and more than 40 times 20th-ranked Greece’s $0.09.  
前20名内差距悬殊：美国投入是第五名法国的7倍多，是第20名希腊的40余倍。  

Defense R&D intensity varies considerably among wealthy economies because countries may prioritize procurement, active personnel, operations, or research differently.  
富裕经济体间国防研发强度差异显著，因各国对装备采购、现役人员、军事行动或研究的优先排序不同。
