【文章标题】：How Trump and Xi Can Do AI Safety  
【文章标题】：特朗普与习近平如何推进人工智能安全  

Jay Kimmel is a seasonal fellow with GovAI, where he conducted this research, as well as a fellow with the Penn Project on the Future of U.S.-China Relations and a former U.S. diplomat.  
杰伊·金梅尔是GovAI的客座研究员（他在此完成了本研究），同时兼任宾夕法尼亚大学美中关系未来项目研究员，曾任美国外交官。  

As the next Trump-Xi summit approaches, details on the AI safety dialogue the two sides announced in May are scant.  
随着下一场"特朗普-习近平峰会"临近，双方五月宣布的人工智能安全对话仍缺乏细节。  

Past efforts have devolved into energetic airings of grievances, or have been derailed by unrelated issues.  
过去的尝试往往沦为激烈的相互指责，或被无关议题带偏方向。  

Trust remains low on both sides, with Washington unsure of China’s aims and Chinese state media claiming in a recent op-ed that the United States has conflated AI safety with a geopolitical blockade.  
双方信任度持续低迷——华盛顿质疑中国意图，而中国官媒近期评论文章指控美国将AI安全与地缘政治封锁混为一谈。  

There’s no guarantee that serious talks will proceed, let alone tackle the technical issues and political dynamics that underpin risk management.  
无法保证对话能深入进行，更遑论解决支撑风险管理的技术问题与政治博弈。  

But as a veteran U.S. diplomat now working on AI safety, I know that talking is the first step.  
但作为专注AI安全的前美国外交官，我深知对话是第一步。  

Both countries know the stakes are high. And there’s much that the two sides can do to prepare for the meeting between President Trump and Xi this month. Here’s how they should start.  
两国都明白利害攸关。为筹备本月特朗普与习近平的会晤，双方可做诸多准备。以下是破局之道。  

ChinaTalk is a reader-supported publication. To receive new posts and support our work, consider becoming a free or paid subscriber.  
《中国对话》为读者支持平台。订阅免费/付费内容可获取新文章并支持我们。  

First, know the risks  
首先，认清风险  

The U.S. and China have seen more than their share of failed talks — risking failure for diverse reasons.  
美中两国经历过无数失败对话，败因各异却教训深刻。  

Political crises have felled many such dialogues. Following House Speaker Pelosi’s 2022 visit to Taiwan, China suspended a series of military and law enforcement dialogues as well as climate change talks.  
政治危机屡次中断对话：佩洛西2022年访台后，中国暂停了军事、执法及气候变化等多领域对话机制。  

Prior incidents like the EP-3 collision with a Chinese jet and the NATO bombing of China’s Embassy in Belgrade triggered major pullback.  
更早的EP-3侦察机撞机事件、北约轰炸中国驻南联盟使馆等均导致关系大幅倒退。  

Mechanisms established on paper can go unused. During the 2023 spy balloon incident, Secretary of Defense Austin attempted to reach his counterpart via the Defense Telephone Link, which was established for crisis communication. The PLA did not connect the call.  
纸上机制可能形同虚设。2023年间谍气球事件中，防长奥斯汀试图通过国防热线联系中方——该热线专为危机沟通设立，但解放军未予接听。  

This could be a prologue for disuse of a hotline in a politically sensitive AI-related incident.  
这或预示在政治敏感的AI事件中，热线同样可能遭弃用。  

Deliverables that sound good can be unfeasible to enforce. Xi’s 2015 state visit to the United States was marked by a short-lived and unenforceable agreement that “neither country’s government will conduct or knowingly support cyber-enabled theft of intellectual property..."  
光鲜的承诺可能难以落实。2015年习近平访美期间达成的"两国政府均不得从事或纵容网络窃取知识产权"协议既短暂又缺乏约束力。  

Neither side trusts the other as arbiter of “intent.” As accusations fly about each side’s plans for frontier AI, that failure is also prescient.  
双方都不认可对方对"意图"的裁定。如今在尖端AI计划上相互指控时，当年失败早有预兆。  

What stands a chance?  
破局契机何在？  

Choices made for the first dialogue will have an outsized impact. The two sides should focus on the most tractable areas first, potentially launching a working group on one or two discrete areas, as they did in 2024 with nuclear command and control issues.  
首次对话选择将产生超常影响。双方应先聚焦最易突破领域，或如2024年核指挥控制议题般，就特定领域成立工作组。  

In the two years since the governments’ last AI safety dialogue, a bevy of unofficial dialogues between experts in the two countries have surfaced critical AI safety risks (Concordia AI counts 12 such “Track IIs” between China and the West).  
自上轮政府级AI安全对话后两年间，两国专家通过非正式对话（康科迪亚AI统计显示中西方存在12条"二轨"渠道）揭示了关键AI风险。  

But expectations diverge widely between the AI safety community and former negotiators with the (mis)fortune of eking out agreements and statements with China over the years.  
但AI安全界与曾（艰难）促成对华协议的前谈判人员间预期差异巨大。  

Wide-eyed scenarios like Plan A pitch a deal with China as the path to containing frontier AI risks. It envisions agreements on compute declaration and an AI training pause by 2029, and offers arms control analogues.  
"方案A"等理想化场景主张通过对华协议遏制尖端AI风险，设想2029年前达成算力申报协议、AI训练暂停，效仿军控模式。  

But China is forging its own path to compete at the AI frontier. It may see deal proposals like this as a covert way to contain its rise, and is hesitant to offer the transparency and restraint that underpin arms control in traditional areas.  
但中国正自主开拓AI竞争道路，或视此类提案为遏制其崛起的阴谋，且不愿提供传统军控所需的透明度与克制。  

Today, China appears to be racing for nuclear parity, rather than negotiate controls from ‘behind’ – and that is the stronger parallel to the AI race.  
当前中国似乎在追逐核均势而非"劣势谈判"，这种态势与AI竞赛更具可比性。  

To understand this divide, I polled a group of experts who participated in official U.S.-China dialogues under Obama, Trump, and Biden, and another group involved in ongoing Track II work on AI Safety.  
为理解分歧，我调研了两组专家：参与过奥巴马/特朗普/拜登时期中美官方对话者，以及从事AI安全"二轨"对话者。  

They were asked to rate twelve potential policy topics for a U.S.-China AI safety dialogue on two dimensions, on a scale of 1 to 5. First, feasibility: the chances that a governmental dialogue can lead to meaningful progress on this topic, given the numerous failure modes of U.S.-China dialogue. Second, value: the importance of the topic to mitigate serious risks from advanced AI.  
他们以1-5分评估12个潜在议题的可行性（考虑中美对话高失败率后取得实质进展的可能性）与价值（该议题对降低先进AI风险的重要性）。  

Experts from both camps broadly agreed that cooperation on the issues was valuable. But for the U.S.-China experts, only two topics eked out higher-than-50% feasibility for a productive dialogue: nuclear risk (the subject of an earlier agreement under the Biden administration) and utilizing new models to close vulnerabilities in open source software (which might benefit from a statement of principles, but requires close cooperation).  
两组专家均认同合作价值，但美中事务专家认为仅两议题具备超50%可行性：核风险（拜登政府时期达成过协议）与利用新模型修补开源软件漏洞（或需原则声明与紧密合作）。
