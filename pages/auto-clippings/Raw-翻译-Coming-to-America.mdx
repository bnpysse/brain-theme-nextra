【文章标题】：Coming to America  
【文章标题】：美国风云  

【文章正文】：  
“War should never be entered upon until every agency of peace has failed.”  
"除非所有和平手段均告失败，否则绝不轻启战端。"  

– William McKinley  
——威廉·麦金莱  

There’s no sugarcoating the ominous turn of events that occurred in the Middle East over the past two weeks.  
过去两周中东局势的凶险转变已无法粉饰。  

The rapid advance of the Houthis across the Red Sea coast, the serious damage sustained by the newly all-important East-West Pipeline in Saudi Arabia, and continuing signs of an imminent global diesel crunch all come on the heels of months of disruption to shipments through the Strait of Hormuz.  
胡塞武装在红海沿岸的迅猛推进、沙特阿拉伯至关重要的东西输油管道遭受严重破坏，以及全球柴油短缺迫在眉睫的持续迹象——这些接踵而至的危机，都发生在霍尔木兹海峡航运中断数月之后。  

The economic calamity that nearly everybody assumed would follow shortly after Hormuz closed—including us, it must be said—looks soon to be upon us.  
几乎所有人（必须承认包括我们）都预判霍尔木兹海峡关闭后将立即爆发的经济灾难，如今似乎即将降临。  

Bleeding the strategic reserves, stretching alternative supply routes, deferring massive amounts of crude demand, and talking up nonexistent peace negotiations certainly postponed the reckoning longer than most could have anticipated, but those tactics were always meant to buy time for things to get better.  
消耗战略储备、拓展替代供应路线、延缓巨额原油需求、鼓吹子虚乌有的和谈——这些手段确实将清算日推迟得比多数人预期更久，但它们本意只是为形势好转争取时间。  

That they have unarguably gotten materially worse is the issue confronting the global economy now.  
而无可争议的事实是：局势已实质性恶化。这正是全球经济当前面临的困局。  

And that’s not to say they can’t get even worse still.  
这还不算最糟——情况完全可能进一步恶化。  

The House of Saud has never looked more vulnerable, dependent as it always has been on the unwavering backing of an omnipotent US military.  
依赖美国全能军事力量坚定支持的沙特王室，此刻显得前所未有的脆弱。  

With both the backing and the potency in question, one wonders if Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman’s (MBS) numerous enemies, foreign and domestic alike, might be inclined to call his bluff.  
当这种支持与实力本身都遭质疑时，人们不禁怀疑国内外众多敌手是否会趁机揭穿穆罕默德·本·萨勒曼王储的虚张声势。  

If his toppling didn’t trigger a “full-tilt” moment for the global economy, one would be left wondering what ever could.  
若他的倒台都未能引发全球经济"全盘倾覆"，那真不知还有什么能撼动大局。  

At risk? | Getty  
危机四伏？| Getty  

Concurrent with these developments, Chinese buyers, after having apparently postponed a global economic reckoning by substantially cutting purchases of crude oil in the spring, were back with a vengeance as the Houthis made their lightning-quick advance:  
与此同时，中国买家在春季大幅削减原油采购量（看似推迟了全球经济清算）后，随着胡塞武装的闪电攻势卷土重来：  

“The yuan-denominated crude oil futures trading in China hit this week their highest level since the contract was launched in 2018 as escalating threats to Middle East’s oil supply and increased purchases by Chinese refiners push prices higher.  
"随着中东石油供应威胁升级与中国炼油商采购量增加推高价格，人民币计价原油期货本周创下2018年合约推出以来的最高交易记录。"  

Launched in March 2018, the INE crude oil futures contract (SC) is a physical-settlement contract priced and traded in Chinese yuan on the Shanghai International Energy Exchange.  
2018年3月推出的INE原油期货合约(SC)是上海国际能源交易所以人民币计价交易的实物交割合约。  

The crude futures soared to as much as 929.4 yuan, or $138.50 per barrel, on the exchange, according to data compiled by Bloomberg.  
彭博汇编数据显示，该原油期货在交易所最高飙升至929.4元/桶（合138.50美元）。"

While most analysts viewed China’s pivot as one based on necessity, our minds returned to a mental model we first published in early April, just five weeks after the war in Iran kicked off.  
虽然多数分析师将中国的转向视为必要之举，但我们想起了伊朗战争爆发五周后（4月初）首次提出的思维模型。  

In that missive, we posited that, far from being a neutral bystander and potential victim of a global crude shortage, China stood a good chance of emerging from the conflict a decisive winner.  
该文指出：中国绝非全球原油短缺的中立旁观者或潜在受害者，反而极可能成为这场冲突的决定性赢家。  

With President Xi Jinping set to visit Washington next week, the cascading series of events befalling the Middle East looks far less likely to be a coincidence of timing.  
随着习近平主席下周即将访美，中东接连爆发的事件链绝非时间巧合。  

Let’s return to that model and explore why.  
让我们重拾这个模型，探究其深层逻辑。
