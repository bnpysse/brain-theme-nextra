Here we go again…  
A few hours ago, US President Trump signed an Executive Order ordering GNIS to be updated to rename Lake Ontario to “Lake America”, and it seems the update has already taken effect (although to note, the GNIS entry cites “Executive Order 14420”, but that is in fact the executive order on “Delivering Gold Standard Childhood Vaccine Recommendations for Americans”; this new executive order has not been officially published in the Federal Register yet and thus does not yet have a number).  
又来了……几小时前，美国总统特朗普签署行政令，要求GNIS将安大略湖更名为"美洲湖"，且更新似乎已生效（需注意的是，GNIS条目引用了"第14420号行政令"，但该令实际是关于"为美国人提供黄金标准儿童疫苗接种建议"的；新行政令尚未在《联邦公报》正式发布，故暂无编号）。  

Precedent from the Gulf of Mexico/“Gulf of America” discussion as well as accepted OSM convention would suggest that once GNIS is updated (which it appears it is already), official_name:en-US=* should be added/changed to Lake America, but name=* left as is unless and until common usage on the ground on both sides of the border shifts to prefer the “Lake America” name (which seems very unlikely).  
参照墨西哥湾/"美洲湾"的讨论先例及OSM惯例，GNIS更新后（目前看来已完成），应添加/修改official_name:en-US=*为"Lake America"，但保留name=*原值，除非边境两侧实际使用中普遍转向"美洲湖"名称（这似乎极不可能）。  

So far, the Lake Ontario relation (1206310) as well as at least one other nearby feature has already had its name=* changed once, though it was swiftly reverted and changed to official_name:en-US=* instead, which is the current status quo as of this writing. I expect to see that happen a lot more in the near future, in line with what happened to the Gulf of Mexico, so good to get on top of this now.  
目前安大略湖关系（1206310）及至少一处邻近地物已发生过一次name=*变更，但被迅速回退并改为official_name:en-US=*——这也是本文撰写时的现状。预计近期会频繁出现类似墨西哥湾的更名情况，最好现在就未雨绸缪。  

Therefore, following a discussion with other local mappers on the OSM-US Slack and a request from @iandees, I created this thread for discussion with the relevant communities to confirm this consensus still applies in this case, and a thread that can be cited and mappers can be directed to documenting the resulting consensus. I’ve also pinged and briefed the DWG on the issue so they are aware. Once decided, it seems worth updating the Lake Ontario OSM wiki page (which was previously proposed for deletion) to document this consensus, as done on the Gulf of Mexico’s page.  
基于与OSM-US Slack本地制图员的讨论及@iandees的请求，我创建此讨论串以确认该共识仍适用本案，并供后续引用和记录共识结果。我已就此事通报DWG。若达成共识，建议参照墨西哥湾页面更新安大略湖OSM维基页面（此前该页面曾被提议删除）。
