The Gemini app is now available for Windows  
Gemini 应用现已上线 Windows

Today, we're launching the Gemini app for Windows. Designed to work seamlessly alongside your favorite tools and daily applications, the new desktop app gives you instant assistance without breaking your flow.  
今天，我们正式推出 Windows 版 Gemini 应用。这款全新桌面应用旨在与你常用的工具和日常应用无缝配合，让你无需打断工作流即可随时获得即时帮助。

Here are three ways you can use the Gemini app on your PC:  
以下是在电脑上使用 Gemini 应用的三种方式：

1. Access Gemini instantly with a keyboard shortcut.  
1. 使用键盘快捷键即可即时访问 Gemini。

Press Alt + Space on your PC at any time to open Gemini over your active work. Whether you need a quick fact-check on a document or a few catchy title ideas for a presentation, you can get the help you need and jump right back into your workflow.  
在电脑上随时按下 Alt + 空格键，即可在当前工作界面上打开 Gemini。无论你是需要快速核对文档中的事实，还是为演示文稿想几个吸引人的标题，都能获得所需帮助，并迅速回到工作流中。

2. Power through deep work in a dedicated workspace.  
2. 在专属工作区中高效完成深度工作。

Inside the new app, you can access everything you use Gemini for already. Hand off multi-step tasks to Gemini Spark 1 , your 24/7 personal AI agent, or ask Gemini to draft a project summary by pulling information directly from your Google apps like Gmail and Google Drive.  
在这款新应用中，你可以使用 Gemini 已有的所有功能。将多步骤任务交给你的 24/7 全天候个人 AI 代理 Gemini Spark 1，或让 Gemini 直接从 Gmail 和 Google Drive 等 Google 应用中提取信息，起草项目摘要。

3. Create images and videos.  
3. 创作图片和视频。

Bring creative concepts to life directly from your desktop. You can generate custom images with Nano Banana for a presentation, direct a high-quality video with Gemini Omni 1 , and best of all you can do it all in one convenient place.  
直接在桌面上将创意概念变为现实。你可以使用 Nano Banana 为演示文稿生成自定义图片，使用 Gemini Omni 1 制作高质量视频，最棒的是，这一切都可以在一个便捷的地方完成。

Lightweight and quiet, the app runs without slowing down your PC. This is just the beginning for the Gemini app on Windows, with more native desktop capabilities rolling out over time. The app is available today globally for Windows 10 and 11. Download the app at gemini.google/desktop.  
这款应用轻量安静，运行时不会拖慢你的电脑。这只是 Windows 版 Gemini 应用的开始，未来还将陆续推出更多原生桌面功能。该应用今日起面向全球 Windows 10 和 Windows 11 用户开放。请访问 gemini.google/desktop 下载应用。
