【文章标题】：Leak of Internal Valve Data  
【文章标题】：Valve内部数据泄露  

【文章正文】：  
A massive cache of internal builds from Valve has reportedly leaked online, including beta builds of several classic Valve titles and possible weapons from Half-Life 2's cancelled third episode.  
据报道，Valve公司大量内部构建版本已在网上泄露，包括多款经典Valve游戏的测试版本，以及《半条命2》被取消的第三章中可能出现的武器。  

The leak, which began circulating on Saturday and comes from an unknown source, includes 12 terabytes of data currently being sifted through by data miners. The files are dated from a ten-year period between 2003 and 2013, and include builds and assets of many Valve projects across that timespan.  
此次泄露始于上周六，来源不明，包含12TB的数据，目前正被数据挖掘者筛选。文件时间跨度为2003年至2013年，涵盖Valve在此期间多个项目的构建版本和资源。  

Thus far, data from Counter-Strike: Global Offensive, Left 4 Dead, and Portal 2 have all been claimed to feature in the leak. This reportedly includes the complete Portal 2 beta from 2009, a much sought-after artifact for Portal 2 fans.  
截至目前，泄露数据中已确认包含《反恐精英：全球攻势》《求生之路》和《传送门2》的内容。据称其中还包括2009年《传送门2》的完整测试版，这是该游戏粉丝梦寐以求的珍品。  

The 2009 beta has a somewhat different story from the final game. It still takes place in the ruins of Aperture Science, but sees you guided through the initial test-chambers by pre-recordings of GLaDOS rather than Wheatley, and features other personality cores much more prominently.  
2009年测试版的剧情与正式版有所不同。故事仍发生在光圈科技废墟中，但玩家最初由GLaDOS的录音（而非Wheatley）引导通过测试室，其他性格核心的戏份也更为突出。  

Curiously, a team of beta researchers and enthusiasts recently released a Portal 2 mod that attempts to recreate the beta build as accurately as possible, a project that took four years to realise.  
耐人寻味的是，一个由测试版研究者和爱好者组成的团队近期发布了《传送门2》模组，试图尽可能精确地复现测试版内容，这个项目耗时四年才完成。  

In addition, Valve-focussed YouTuber and enthusiast Gabe Follower reports that the files include the Weaponizer, an unfinished weapon that has been previously linked to Half-Life 2: Episode 3.  
此外，专注Valve的YouTuber兼爱好者Gabe Follower透露，文件中包含"Weaponizer"——这件未完成的武器此前被认为与《半条命2：第三章》有关。  

Information about the Weaponizer appeared in a separate leak that occurred in 2011, inside a directory labelled "ep3", while a gun that resembles the Weaponizer was shown in footage of Episode 3 that appeared in Valve's 20th anniversary documentary on Half-Life 2 (via Gamerant).  
2011年另一次泄露的"ep3"文件夹中曾出现过该武器的信息，而类似Weaponizer的枪械也出现在Valve《半条命2》二十周年纪录片里的第三章画面中（消息来源Gamerant）。  

Also apparently included are files (but apparently not a working build) of Valve's cancelled puzzle game F-STOP, a precursor to Portal 2 that was in-development for around a year before Valve canned the project.  
泄露内容似乎还包含Valve已取消的解谜游戏《F-STOP》的文件（但非可运行版本）。该项目开发约一年后中止，后来成为《传送门2》的前身。  

Also known as 'Aperture Camera' F-STOP featured a mechanic similar to the one seen in 2023 puzzler Viewfinder, in which the player takes photographs of the environment which can then be applied as 3D objects in the game world.  
这款又名"Aperture Camera"的游戏机制与2023年解谜游戏《Viewfinder》类似：玩家拍摄环境照片后，可将其作为3D物体应用于游戏世界。  

While it's not clear where the leak originated from, it has been linked to Valve's Steam2 content-server infrastructure, which was replaced by SteamPipe in March 2013, which would explain why the latest files from the archive are from that year.  
虽然泄露源头尚不明确，但据信与Valve的Steam2内容服务器架构有关。该架构于2013年3月被SteamPipe取代，这也解释了为何档案中最新的文件截止于该年份。
