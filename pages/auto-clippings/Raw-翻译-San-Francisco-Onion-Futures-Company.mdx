旧金山洋葱期货公司提供黄洋葱未来实物交割的私人可转让合约。

FAQ  
常见问题  

Is this legal?  
是否合法？  

Yes, to the best of our knowledge. 7 U.S. Code § 13-1 prohibits "contract[s] for the sale of [...] onions for future delivery [...] on or subject to the rules of any board of trade in the United States." The San Francisco Onion Futures Company is not a board of trade, defined as an "organized exchange or other trading facility". We sell contracts privately to individual buyers, and do not operate any exchange or secondary market.  
据我们所知完全合法。《美国法典》第7编第13-1条禁止"在美国任何交易所或受其规则管辖的洋葱期货交割合约"。旧金山洋葱期货公司并非定义为"有组织交易所或其他交易设施"的交易所，我们仅向个人买家私下出售合约，不运营任何交易所或二级市场。  

How does the delivery process work?  
交割流程如何运作？  

In the first week of each contract's delivery month, a representative of the San Francisco Onion Futures Company will contact the registered owner to arrange delivery. Usually, contract owners will be given a list of possible times and locations at which they can collect their onions. If a contract owner is not available for any of these, or if they are outside of our primary delivery areas, delivery may be handled by a third party at no extra charge.  
在合约交割月的第一周，公司代表将联系注册持有人安排交割。通常持有人将获得可选提货时间地点列表。若持有人无法配合或位于主要配送区外，可免费委托第三方完成交割。  

Our primary delivery areas are San Francisco, Toronto, Seattle, and Chicago (via For Our Futures). If you would like to become a delivery partner for another area, please email onionfutures@protonmail.com.  
主要配送区域为旧金山、多伦多、西雅图和芝加哥（通过For Our Futures平台）。若您希望成为其他地区的配送合作伙伴，请发送邮件至onionfutures@protonmail.com。  

How are prices determined?  
如何定价？  

Contract prices for each month are recalculated daily at midnight San Francisco time using a proprietary algorithm.  
每月合约价格采用专属算法，于旧金山时间每日午夜重新计算。  

How do I update my contact or delivery information?  
如何更新联系/交割信息？  

Visit the contract management page and look up your contract key(s). After entering your new information, you will be issued new keys registered to your updated information, and the old ones will become void.  
访问合约管理页面查询合约密钥。提交新信息后，系统将发放绑定更新信息的新密钥，原密钥即刻失效。  

Can I resell or transfer my contracts?  
可否转售或转让合约？  

Yes. You will be given a unique key for each contract you purchase. If you give this key to someone, they can use it to update the registered contact and delivery information for the contract. This will void the old key and reissue the contract under a new one, ensuring that you or any previous owners can't change the registered information back. There is no way to view the registered delivery information for a given key, so you're not exposing your information by sharing it.  
可以。每份合约均配有唯一密钥。将密钥转交他人后，对方可更新合约注册信息。原密钥将失效并生成新密钥，确保您或前任持有者无法还原信息。密钥无法查询绑定信息，因此共享时不会泄露隐私。  

Do you offer refunds or cash settlement?  
是否提供退款或现金结算？  

No. You are free to resell your contract keys up until the start of their delivery months. Please note that although we will do our best to contact you and deliver your onions, no refunds are offered even in the case of a failed delivery.  
不提供。在交割月开始前您可自由转售合约密钥。请注意即使交割失败，我们也不会退款（尽管将尽力联系您完成交割）。
