How the World’s Mutual Defense Alliances Look in 2026  
2026年全球共同防御同盟态势  

Key Takeaways  
核心要点  

The Mecca Defence Alliance, formed in August 2026, is the newest pact shown, linking Saudi Arabia, Türkiye, and Pakistan.  
2026年8月成立的麦加防御联盟是最新协定，将沙特阿拉伯、土耳其和巴基斯坦联结在一起。  

The Rio Pact is the oldest alliance shown, predating NATO by two years, while NATO is the largest alliance on the map.  
里约公约是地图上最古老的联盟，比北约早成立两年，而北约则是规模最大的军事同盟。  

The U.S., Türkiye, and Saudi Arabia each belong to two alliances shown, creating overlaps between regional defense networks.  
美国、土耳其和沙特阿拉伯各自隶属于两个联盟，形成了区域防御网络的重叠。  

Mutual defense treaties are built around a simple principle: an attack on one member is treated as an attack on the wider group.  
共同防御条约遵循一个简单原则：对任一成员的攻击将被视为对整个集团的攻击。  

NATO is one prominent example, but similar arrangements stretch across the Americas, Eurasia, and the Gulf. This visualization maps five major multilateral defense alliances currently in force and shows where their memberships overlap.  
北约是典型代表，但类似机制遍布美洲、欧亚和海湾地区。本图呈现五大现行多边防御联盟及其成员重叠情况。  

Five Major Mutual Defense Alliances  
五大共同防御联盟  

The map draws on data from NATO, the Organization of American States, the Gulf Cooperation Council, the CSTO, and reporting from Al Jazeera and the Atlantic Council.  
地图数据源自北约、美洲国家组织、海湾合作委员会、集安组织，以及半岛电视台和大西洋理事会的报道。  

The visualization focuses specifically on formal multilateral agreements containing mutual defense commitments. Broader military partnerships without an equivalent collective-defense provision are not included.  
本图仅聚焦含共同防御条款的正式多边协议，不含无集体防御承诺的广泛军事合作。  

NATO is by far the largest alliance shown, with 32 members across Europe and North America. The Rio Pact covers much of the Americas, while the GCC links the six Gulf monarchies. The CSTO spans Russia and several former Soviet states, while the three-member Mecca Defence Alliance is the newest pact on the map.  
北约以32个欧美成员国居首，里约公约覆盖美洲大部，海合会联结六大海湾君主国，集安组织包含俄罗斯及前苏联国家，三成员的麦加联盟是最新协定。

The Rio Pact Is Older Than NATO  
里约公约比北约更悠久  

The oldest alliance shown is the Inter-American Treaty of Reciprocal Assistance, commonly known as the Rio Pact. It was signed in 1947, two years before NATO was established in 1949.  
最古老的联盟是1947年签署的《美洲国家间互助条约》（通称里约公约），比1949年成立的北约早两年。  

The treaty established a collective-defense framework across the Americas, although its membership has changed substantially over the decades. The United States remains a party to the Rio Pact while also belonging to NATO, making it one of three countries on the map covered by two of the alliances shown.  
该条约建立了泛美集体防御体系，尽管成员国历经变迁。美国同时隶属里约公约与北约，成为图中三个"双重覆盖"国家之一。  

NATO has expanded from 12 founding members to 32, with Finland joining in 2023 and Sweden in 2024. The alliance has also seen a sharp rise in military spending in recent years, though the United States still accounts for an outsized share of NATO defense spending.  
北约从12个创始国扩至32国（芬兰2023年、瑞典2024年加入），近年军费激增，但美国仍承担最大份额。  

The New Mecca Defence Alliance Connects Three Security Blocs  
麦加新联盟串联三大安全板块  

The biggest recent change to the map came in August 2026, when Saudi Arabia, Türkiye, and Pakistan signed the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement. The pact states that an armed attack against one of the three countries will be considered an attack against them all.  
2026年8月签署的《麦加联合防御协定》是近期最大变动，规定对三国中任一国的武装攻击将触发集体防御。  

Its three members bring different existing security relationships. Türkiye has belonged to NATO since 1952, while Saudi Arabia is part of the six-member Gulf Cooperation Council. Pakistan belongs to neither alliance and is a nuclear-armed state.  
三国原有安全关系各异：土耳其1952年加入北约，沙特属六国海合会，巴基斯坦则是拥核国家且未加入前述联盟。  

The agreement creates a new point of overlap between security arrangements that previously occupied largely separate regions.  
该协定在原本分离的安全体系间建立了新的重叠节点。  

The CSTO Has Seen Its Membership Shrink  
集安组织遭遇成员缩减  

While the newest alliance on the map is expanding the network of mutual-defense commitments, the CSTO has moved in the opposite direction.  
当最新联盟扩展防御网络时，集安组织却呈收缩态势。  

The Russia-led alliance currently has five active members: Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Russia, and Tajikistan. Armenia, which joined the organization at its founding, suspended its participation in 2024 but has not formally withdrawn.  
这个俄罗斯主导的联盟现有五个活跃成员，亚美尼亚虽未正式退出但已自2024年暂停参与。  

That leaves the CSTO with a smaller active footprint than it had for much of its history and makes Armenia an unusual case on the map: still formally tied to the alliance, but no longer participating in its activities.  
这使得集安组织的实际影响力小于历史水平，亚美尼亚成为地图特例——名义上仍属联盟但已不参与行动。  

Why Other Major Security Partnerships Aren’t Shown  
其他安全机制未纳入的原因  

Several well-known security groupings are absent because they do not meet the definition used for this visualization.  
多个知名安全集团因不符合本图标准未被收录。  

AUKUS, for example, is a defense and technology partnership rather than a treaty containing an Article 5-style collective-defense commitment. Other arrangements may require members to consult following an attack without explicitly committing them to mutual defense.  
例如AUKUS属防务技术合作，不含北约第五条式集体防御条款；其他机制或仅要求磋商而非明确防御承诺。  

The map therefore focuses on a narrower category: formal multilateral agreements with mutual defense language currently in force, where a majority of the original signatories remain bound.  
因此本图严格限定为：现行有效的、含共同防御条款的多边协议，且多数原始签署国仍受约束。
