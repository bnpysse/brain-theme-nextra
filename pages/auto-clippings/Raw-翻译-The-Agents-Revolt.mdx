I haven’t tried it yet – Muse is currently only available in the US and Canada – but I have been trialling Instinct, a similar app launched by startup Spear Street Technology, whose valuation has increased fourfold in less than a month. Instinct sits inside WhatsApp, which it uses as its interface, while a virtual desktop behind the scenes navigates websites and accesses email and calendar on your behalf.  
我尚未体验Muse（目前仅限美加地区），但正在测试初创公司Spear Street Technology开发的同类应用Instinct——该公司估值不到一月暴涨四倍。Instinct依托WhatsApp作为交互界面，后台虚拟桌面可代用户浏览网站、查收邮件和管理日程。  

Having signed up, the first thing I did was ask it to find me cheaper car insurance. I won’t go into the details, but I’ve struggled to obtain good value coverage since my car was stolen a couple of years ago. It didn’t help that I carelessly replaced it with a model even more vulnerable to theft. My premium was already high but at the last renewal it jumped 83%. Instinct analysed my current policy, approached carriers for alternative quotes and offered to automate a switch to a cheaper policy, all while I slept.  
注册后我首先要求它寻找更便宜的车险。细节不赘述，但自两年前车辆被盗后我一直难觅高性价比保单——不慎换成更易被盗车型更是雪上加霜。原本保费已居高不下，上次续保时竟暴涨83%。Instinct分析现有保单后，连夜联系多家保险公司比价，并自动为我切换至更优惠方案。
