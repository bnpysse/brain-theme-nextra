Don’t be fooled by this summer of AI hype
别被这个"AI炒作之夏"蒙蔽双眼
—Timnit Gebru, executive director of the Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR), and Emily M. Bender, professor of linguistics at the University of Washington
——分布式AI研究所(DAIR)执行主任蒂姆尼特·格布鲁与华盛顿大学语言学教授艾米丽·本德联署

It’s been a busy few months for AI hype, with companies making breathless claims about hacking, mathematical breakthroughs, and the prospect of self-improving superintelligence.
过去几个月AI炒作甚嚣尘上，企业纷纷鼓吹黑客攻击、数学突破乃至自我进化超级智能的前景。

In all these cases, massive fanfare from the companies, presented as mea culpas in the hacking incidents, has been accompanied by intense press coverage. But once experts have had time to examine what happened, very different stories emerge.
这些事件中，企业声势浩大的宣传（在黑客事件中表现为公开致歉）总伴随媒体密集报道。但当专家深入调查后，真相往往大相径庭。

There’s a strong commercial incentive to overstate AI capabilities. The authors argue that the illusion of speed and urgency promulgated by the tech companies is also a ploy to misdirect policymakers and the public.
夸大AI能力存在强烈商业动机。作者指出，科技公司营造的速度与紧迫感假象，实则是误导政策制定者与公众的策略。

Here’s why they say we must remain skeptical about AI hype.
