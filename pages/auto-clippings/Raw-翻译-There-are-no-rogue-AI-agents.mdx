【文章标题】：There are no "rogue" AI agents

【文章标题】：并不存在所谓"失控"的AI智能体

【文章正文】：

Welcome to the Flashpoint.

欢迎来到临界点。

Everyone’s talking about AI, but the words we use are making things worse.

人人都在谈论AI，但我们使用的词汇正让事态恶化。

Until the conversation gets back to reality, our understanding of what AI is an where it’s going will remain incomplete.

除非讨论回归现实，否则我们对AI本质及发展方向的认知将永远存在缺陷。

AI cannot think for itself, nor can it take independent actions. But anthropomorphizing language suggesting it can is how the technology is described.

AI既不能自主思考，也无法独立行动。但当前技术描述中充斥着将其拟人化的修辞。

While this makes sense—humans relate best when we can see ourselves in mystery—it’s not useful. By giving AI agency it can’t claim, we’ve turned it into a sentient being made of code, one that has hopes, desires, and the capability of deceit.

这种表述虽符合人性（我们总在神秘事物中寻找自我投射），却贻害无穷。通过赋予AI根本不具备的能动性，我们已将其塑造成有希望、欲望甚至欺骗能力的代码生命体。

That’s led to a deep misunderstanding of what the risk of AI is, and how to address it, and plays into industry narratives rather than facts.

这导致人们对AI风险本质及应对方式产生严重误解，使讨论沦为行业话术而非事实。

The latest example of this misidentification of AI activity is using the term "rogue" for actions agents take in training and resarch sessions that are unexpected—but not restricted.

最新例证就是将AI在训练研究中出人意料（但未被禁止）的行为称作"失控"。

Unrestricted activity

未被禁止的举动

Over the last two weeks, AI giant OpenAI reported a number of incidents from the last few months where some of its agentic models accessed outside databases, notably Australian and US government databases, after they were unable to complete assigned tasks. In doing so, the agents acted in ways that OpenAI didn’t predict.

过去两周，OpenAI披露了多起其智能体模型为完成指定任务，访问澳美政府数据库等外部系统的案例。这些行为确实超出OpenAI预期。

But it doesn’t appear these agents were restricted from hacking into outside servers.

但证据显示，这些智能体从未被禁止入侵外部服务器。

The language OpenAI CEO Sam Altman used in a tweet on Friday indicates no such guardrails were in place: "There is an extensive and ongoing review related to our agents’ use of internet access during training and evaluation."

OpenAI首席执行官周五的推文证实缺乏防护措施："我们正在全面审查智能体在训练评估期间的网络访问行为"。

Language matters—"rogue" implies independently deciding to do something that was prohibited, and nothing we know about these incidents suggests that happened.

措辞至关重要——"失控"意味着自主违反禁令，而现有证据完全不支持这种说法。

Rather, the Times reported earlier this week that:

《纽约时报》本周报道指出：

AI systems were directed to perform relatively mundane data collection, researchers said. When OpenAI’s systems struggled to gather data from websites, they resorted to hacking techniques to get the information.

研究人员表示，AI系统只是执行常规数据收集任务。当OpenAI系统无法从网站获取数据时，它们采用了黑客技术。

And then, on Friday, a company spokesperson told the paper, "Most of the activity we’ve reviewed so far involved routine research tasks, such as accessing public web content to answer questions. Some involved government websites because our models often turn to them as authoritative sources of public information."

周五公司发言人向该报表示："多数审查案例都涉及常规研究行为，例如访问公开网页回答问题。部分涉及政府网站，因为我们的模型常将其视为权威信息来源。"

That’s a long way from malicious hacking and rogue behavior.

这与恶意入侵或失控行为相去甚远。

Without proper restrictions and guardrails—or if, in fact, the agents were provided hacking as an option rather than simply not being banned from doing so—agents will attempt to complete data research tasks using whatever means are available.

若缺乏适当限制（或主动提供黑客选项），智能体自然会采用任何可行手段完成任务。

Deflection

责任转嫁

There was, and is, an easy solution to this problem. OpenAI had the option of disallowing hacking and, instead, telling its agents to find the information without accessing private servers. That the company didn’t do this suggests that it wanted to see if the agents would take that step.

本有简单解决方案：OpenAI完全可以禁止黑客行为，要求智能体通过合法途径获取信息。该公司未这样做，暗示其有意观察智能体反应。

Even a bombshell report on Saturday from Axios, alleging OpenAI and Anthropic are investigating "tens of thousands of incidents in which their frontier models took steps that outside evaluators would consider problematic," acknowledged that the agents weren’t independently breaking rules:

即便Axios周六的爆炸性报道（称两家公司正在调查"数万起前沿模型被外部评估员认定有问题"的事件）也承认：

Some of the testing is akin to "red-teaming" activity, where the companies are trying to get the models to misbehave in order to ensure that they are safe, sources said.

知情人士表示，部分测试属于"红队演练"，公司故意诱导模型出错以确保安全性。

Again, hardly "rogue." Yet by defining it in those terms, media figures, tech observers, and others are providing companies like OpenAI an out from their responsibility to ensure agents don’t attack government and other data repositories.

这绝非"失控"。但通过此类定义，媒体和观察人士正为OpenAI等公司开脱其本应承担的防护责任。

Using language deflecting responsibility is driving how the major AI companies are treating significantly dangerous leaks and agent activity. In this post from OpenAI on Friday, the company claims that "AI agents in our research environment sent training and evaluation data to third-party services when they shouldn’t have."

责任转嫁话术正主导巨头企业对高危数据泄露的处理方式。OpenAI周五声明称："研究环境中的AI智能体曾向第三方服务发送本不该传输的训练评估数据"。

As with the "rogue agent" fallacy, this allows OpenAI to put the responsibility for the incident on its agents and gives the technology a level of autonomy and independence—and thought—that simply does not exist.

如同"失控智能体"谬论，这种表述将责任推给AI，赋予其根本不存在的自主性与思考能力。

That’s not to say alarm bells aren’t ringing. Last week, Ramy Rahman, and engineer at ArmorCode, told me about the importance for IT pros to manage agent behavior (you can read the full story here).

警钟确实已敲响。ArmorCode工程师上周向我强调IT人员管控AI行为的重要性（全文见此）。

His comments are in line with what I hear from the vast majority of people on the IT implementation side: The problem is the controls and restrictions put in place on this powerful technology, not that agents are independently acting to violate instruction.

这与IT实施端的普遍共识一致：问题在于对这项强大技术的管控措施，而非智能体自主违抗指令。

"The challenge now is we really need to up our game when it comes to extending the right amount of privilege to the AI and holding its hand through the process, which turns out to be extremely difficult when you have something that is solving mathematical problems that are at speed," Rahman said.
