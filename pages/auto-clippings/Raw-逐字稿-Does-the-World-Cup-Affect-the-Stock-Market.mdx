How can a football game's outcome affect

the nation's stock market?

&gt;&gt; Well, this is a great question because

it should not. So, so what you learn in

any finance class is that the market is

affected by fundamentals. So, what

should affect a stock price is dividends

and profits and sales growth. But what

research actually finds is that

sentiment and emotions make a

difference. And so, this is why I chose

to look at the effect of football or

soccer results is that this is something

which affects investor mood. And so what

my co-authors and I found is that if you

study over1,000 soccer games after a

country is knocked out of the World Cup,

the market falls by about a half a

percent the next day, which is 15

billion pounds when applied to the UK

stock market. And this is because of the

effect that it has on your emotions.

Now, some listeners might think, yeah,

my mood is affected by my emotions, but

I'm a professional. So when I go and

trade, I will put my game face on. I

will block out my emotions and I will

get back to rationality. But what this

research suggests is that's not

possible. You are human and if you're a

human then you are susceptible to your

emotions. And something like this does

affect how you trade or maybe how you

assess a company.

一场足球比赛的结果如何能影响

一个国家的股票市场？

&gt;&gt; 嗯，这是一个很好的问题，因为

按理说它不应该有影响。你在任何金融课上

学到的都是，市场受基本面因素影响。

因此，应该影响股价的是股息、

利润和销售增长。但研究实际发现的是，

情绪和情感确实会产生影响。

所以，这就是为什么我选择

研究足球比赛结果的影响，因为这是

一种会影响投资者情绪的因素。

我和我的合著者发现，如果你研究

超过1000场足球比赛，当一个国家

从世界杯中被淘汰出局后，

第二天市场会下跌约0.5个百分点，

放到英国股市上，这相当于150亿英镑。

而这正是因为比赛结果对

你的情绪产生的影响。

有些听众可能会想，没错，

我的情绪确实会影响我的心情，但我是专业人士。

所以当我去交易时，我会摆出严肃认真的表情。

我会屏蔽掉自己的情绪，回归理性。

但这项研究表明，这是不可能的。

你是人，而只要你是人，

你就容易受到情绪的影响。

像这样的事情确实会影响你如何交易，

或者可能影响你如何评估一家公司。
