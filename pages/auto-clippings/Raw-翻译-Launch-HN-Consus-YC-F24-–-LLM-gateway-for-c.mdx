【文章标题】：Launch HN: Consus (YC F24) – 面向处理敏感数据公司的LLM网关

【文章正文】：

Hey Hacker News! I'm Eric, the founder of Consus. We route all AI requests to ITAR restricted and controlled unclassified information (CUI) compliant infrastructure.

【文章正文】：

嘿，Hacker News！我是Eric，Consus的创始人。我们将所有AI请求路由到符合ITAR限制及受控非机密信息（CUI）合规要求的基础设施上。

Consus itself satisfies ITAR restrictions, and we recently completed our FedRAMP equivalency audit against the 410 FedRAMP High controls. FedRAMP High is the U.S. government’s strictest security baseline for cloud services handling sensitive unclassified data. A breach of this data could cause severe or catastrophic harm. Achieving FedRAMP equivalency, or authorization, is required before processing CUI information.

Consus自身满足ITAR限制要求，我们最近完成了针对410项FedRAMP高等级控制的FedRAMP等效性审计。FedRAMP高等级是美国政府对处理敏感非机密数据的云服务所设定的最严格安全基线，此类数据泄露可能导致严重或灾难性危害。在处理CUI信息之前，必须获得FedRAMP等效性认证或授权。

Aerospace and defense is experiencing rapid growth. Unfortunately, most companies are struggling to use AI because of necessary data protections & compliance risk. These companies can move even faster if they could use the AI tools that most other industries take for granted.

航空航天与国防领域正在经历快速增长。遗憾的是，由于必要的数据保护与合规风险，大多数公司难以使用AI。如果这些公司能够使用其他行业视为理所当然的AI工具，它们的发展速度本可以更快。

I spent most of my career prior to Consus in aerospace & defense. I launched national security payloads at SpaceX, and was an early employee at Castelion. I’ve watched first hand how compliance red-tape makes it difficult for companies to adopt new technologies and trust vendors that touch their sensitive data. For example, I worked with a satellite manufacturer that doesn’t allow any AI tools, even for non-sensitive work.

在创立Consus之前，我的职业生涯大部分时间都投入在航空航天与国防领域。我在SpaceX负责发射国家安全级有效载荷，也是Castelion的早期员工。我亲眼目睹了合规官僚主义如何让公司难以采用新技术、难以信任接触其敏感数据的供应商。例如，我曾与一家卫星制造商合作，他们不允许使用任何AI工具，即使是处理非敏感工作也不行。

Even as AI has become extremely useful over the past 9-12 months, I was surprised by how many aerospace & defense companies refused to touch it. They could see how it could be helpful, but they were scared about model providers training on their data, violating an ITAR rule, or risk losing new business because of a failed cyber assessment. These are all rational fears.

尽管在过去9到12个月里AI变得极为实用，但仍有大量航空航天与国防公司拒绝触碰它，这令我惊讶。他们能看到AI的潜在价值，却担心模型提供商利用他们的数据进行训练、违反ITAR规定，或因网络安全评估失败而失去新业务。这些都是合理的担忧。

There were a lot of ways to solve some/all of these problems, but we focused on building a compliant AI inference layer that can "plug" into any AI tool or workflow. Just as OpenRouter integrates with most tools, Consus can connect to tools like ChatGPT or Claude, open source tools including the Pi coding agent or Open WebUI, or integrate with applications built and maintained by individual organizations.

解决部分或全部这些问题的方法有很多，但我们专注于构建一个可“即插即用”的合规AI推理层，能够接入任何AI工具或工作流。正如OpenRouter与大多数工具集成一样，Consus可以连接到ChatGPT或Claude等工具、包括Pi编程代理或Open WebUI在内的开源工具，或与各组织自行构建和维护的应用程序集成。

Almost no aerospace & defense company lives in all three major GovClouds (AWS, Azure, Google). When I mapped where frontier models can legally run for ITAR and CUI work, each provider had some subset of models, and each model achieved varying levels of compliance. Our customers don’t think in terms of cloud providers. They think about the data controls they require, and the model they want to use.

几乎没有哪家航空航天与国防公司同时使用所有三大GovCloud（AWS、Azure、Google）。当我梳理前沿模型在何处能合法运行于ITAR和CUI工作场景时，发现每家云提供商都提供部分模型，且每个模型达到的合规等级各不相同。我们的客户不会以云提供商的角度思考问题。他们关注的是所需的数据控制措施以及想要使用的模型。

That is why we built the compliance suffix. Under the hood, every request hits a single OpenAI-compatible endpoint, where keys are verified and policy is enforced before anything touches inference. Every model name must carry a compliance suffix, such as gpt-5.6-sol:fedramp-high or claude-opus-5-5:itar, and any bare model name is rejected. We never proxy to the standard commercial endpoints.

这就是我们构建合规后缀的原因。在底层，每个请求都会命中一个兼容OpenAI的单一端点，在触及推理之前，会对密钥进行验证并强制执行策略。每个模型名称都必须携带合规后缀，例如gpt-5.6-sol:fedramp-high或claude-opus-5-5:itar，任何裸模型名称都会被拒绝。我们从不代理到标准的商业端点。

Our customers are also able to build an allowlist of models, and assign spend & model restrictions to individual users in the organization. This is all handled through our Consus Portal where teams can manage spend, keys, and policies.

我们的客户还可以建立模型白名单，并为组织内的单个用户分配消费额度与模型限制。这一切都通过Consus Portal管理，团队可在其中管理消费、密钥和策略。

A typical question we hear is why not just use Bedrock in AWS GovCloud or Azure Government directly. For small teams, just getting access to these platforms is difficult. For larger organizations, GovCloud lets you inherit some but not all of the controls needed to handle sensitive data. Also, models are split across providers: GPT-5.6 Sol is only available in Azure Government, while Claude Opus 5.5 is served through Bedrock in AWS GovCloud. If you want both, you are standing up and securing two separate government cloud environments. We run all of them behind one endpoint so customers don’t have to pick a cloud, and we handle the compliance work for each one.

我们经常听到的一个问题是：为什么不直接使用AWS GovCloud中的Bedrock或Azure Government？对于小型团队来说，仅仅获取这些平台的访问权限就很困难。对于大型组织，GovCloud让你继承处理敏感数据所需的部分控制措施，但并非全部。此外，模型分散在不同云提供商：GPT-5.6 Sol仅在Azure Government中可用，而Claude Opus 5.5则通过AWS GovCloud中的Bedrock提供。如果你两者都需要，就必须搭建并保护两个独立的政府云环境。我们将其全部跑在一个端点之后，这样客户就不必选择云平台，而我们则负责处理每个平台的合规工作。

Given the data sensitivities, Consus does not store any customer data at rest. All prompts and completions stay in memory only for the length of the request and are never logged or written to disk. Zero data retention is strictly enforced both on ourselves and the infrastructure/model providers. We do keep metadata on each request and response for billing and audit purposes.

鉴于数据的敏感性，Consus不会静态存储任何客户数据。所有提示和补全结果仅在请求期间驻留内存，绝不会被记录或写入磁盘。我们自身以及基础设施/模型提供商都严格执行零数据留存策略。我们会保留每个请求和响应的元数据，用于计费和审计。

Consus is not self-serve by design. ITAR requires us to know exactly who is on the other end of every key, so we verify every organization and put a contract in place before issuing API keys. We charge our customers a compliance fee starting at $40/mo/seat plus AI tokens at cost.

Consus在设计上并非自助服务。ITAR要求我们必须确切知道每个密钥背后的使用者身份，因此在发放API密钥之前，我们会验证每个组织并签订合同。我们向客户收取合规费用，起价为每月每个席位40美元，外加AI代币的成本价。

We are actively serving customers, and would love any feedback from security experts, or developers/engineers working in highly regulated industries!

我们正在积极服务客户，并欢迎安全专家或高度监管行业的开发者/工程师提供任何反馈！
