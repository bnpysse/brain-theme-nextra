Ranked: Countries With the Most Lakes  
排名：全球湖泊最多的国家  

Key Takeaways  
核心要点  

Canada has nearly 880,000 lakes, accounting for 62% of the 1.42 million lakes in this dataset.  
加拿大拥有近88万个湖泊，占该数据集142万湖泊总数的62%。  

Canada alone has more lakes than the rest of the world combined, followed by Russia with 201,240 and the U.S. with 102,519.  
加拿大一国的湖泊数量就超过世界其他国家的总和，俄罗斯（201,240个）和美国（102,519个）分列二三位。  

Canada has 4.4 times as many lakes as Russia, but only about 1.3 times as much total lake area.  
加拿大的湖泊数量是俄罗斯的4.4倍，但总湖泊面积仅为俄罗斯的1.3倍。  

Which country has the most lakes in the world? The answer isn’t particularly close.  
"世界上哪个国家湖泊最多？这个答案毫无悬念。"  

Past ice sheets helped shape some of the world’s most lake-rich landscapes, particularly across the Northern Hemisphere. That glacial history is still visible in today’s rankings.  
远古冰盖塑造了全球湖泊最密集的地貌，尤其在北半球。这种冰川历史在当今排名中仍清晰可见。  

This visualization ranks countries by their number of lakes, based on HydroLAKES data. It includes lakes covering at least 0.04 square miles (0.1 sq. km), while excluding reservoirs and regulated lakes. Cross-border lakes are assigned to the country where their water drains out.  
本可视化排名基于HydroLAKES数据，统计标准为面积≥0.04平方英里（0.1平方公里）的自然湖泊，不含水库及人工调控湖泊。跨境湖泊归属以出水口所在国为准。  

Where the World’s 1.4 Million Lakes Are Located  
全球140万湖泊分布图  

Beyond Canada’s commanding lead, the ranking drops quickly. Russia ranks second with 201,240 lakes, followed by the U.S. with 102,519. China is a distant fourth with 23,751.  
加拿大遥遥领先，其后排名断崖式下降：俄罗斯（201,240个）第二，美国（102,519个）第三，中国（23,751个）以较大差距位列第四。  

Together, Canada, Russia, and the U.S. hold over 83% of the 1.42 million lakes in the dataset.  
加、俄、美三国合计占据数据集142万湖泊总量的83%以上。
