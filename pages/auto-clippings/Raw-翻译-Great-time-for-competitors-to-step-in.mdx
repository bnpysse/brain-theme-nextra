【帖子标题】：Great time for competitors to step in

【帖子正文】：

Cursor had two things going for them which made it the best coding tool

First movers advantage into a niche market where many users came in and stayed

Built a really good model that works well event today (composer, 3rd party claude, etc)

It became difficult then for other Agent+IDE providers to step in and cover a market which was almost already captured.

Now the current state of Cursor is not great, the pricing change with restriction of 3rd party models along with Auto mode forcing a model etc has created situations where users are dropping their subscriptions and some while still on Cursor are looking to switch. Its safe to say users are not happy and feel wildly different (in a bad way) compared to 2-3 months back.

For competitors wanting to make a fresh move into this niche market like Cursor first did and capture it now is a great time, the time-window maybe small but its wide open.
