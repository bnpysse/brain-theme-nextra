Where AI Exposure Is Highest Across the U.S.

全美AI职业风险分布图

Key Takeaways

核心发现

Washington has the highest share of AI-exposed jobs, at 5.7% of its workforce.

华盛顿州受AI影响的岗位比例最高，占劳动力总数的5.7%

California has the largest number of AI-exposed positions overall, at roughly 724,000 jobs.

加利福尼亚州受AI影响的岗位总数最多，约72.4万个

Mississippi has the lowest share of AI-exposed jobs in the study, at 1.9%.

密西西比州受影响比例最低，仅1.9%

Artificial intelligence is becoming capable of performing tasks across a growing range of white-collar and technical occupations.

人工智能正逐步具备执行各类白领和技术岗位工作的能力

This visualization maps all 50 states and Washington, D.C., by the share of workers employed in occupations considered highly exposed to AI-related disruption. It also highlights the states with the largest and smallest numbers of workers in these roles.

本可视化图表根据各州受AI冲击风险较高职业的从业者比例，绘制了全美50州及华盛顿特区的分布图，并标注了受影响人数最多和最少的地区

The data for this visualization comes from SmartAsset, using U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data and research from the Virginia Economic Information and Analytics Division. The analysis covers 26 occupations identified as having particularly high exposure to potential AI-related disruption.

数据源自SmartAsset平台，整合了美国劳工统计局和弗吉尼亚经济信息分析部门的研究成果，涵盖26个易受AI冲击的高风险职业

AI exposure does not necessarily mean these jobs will disappear. Instead, it reflects how susceptible their tasks may be to changes such as automation, weaker hiring demand, wage pressure, or restructuring as AI tools become more capable.

AI风险暴露度并不直接等同于岗位消失，而是反映这些工作可能面临自动化、招聘需求下降、薪资压力或组织重构等变革的敏感程度

Why Washington Tops the Map

华盛顿州领跑原因

Washington ranks first, with 5.7% of its workforce employed in highly AI-exposed occupations.

该州5.7%的劳动力从事高风险职业，居全美之首

The state’s large technology sector helps explain its position, with companies such as Microsoft and Amazon supporting a significant concentration of software developers, programmers, database specialists, and other digital roles.

庞大的科技产业是主因，微软和亚马逊等企业聚集了大量软件开发师、程序员、数据库专家等数字岗位

At the other end of the ranking, Mississippi has the lowest share in the study, at 1.9%.

California Has the Most AI-Exposed Jobs

加州风险岗位总数最多

California has the largest absolute number of workers in highly exposed occupations, with approximately 724,000 positions.

加州高风险岗位总数约72.4万

Texas follows with about 500,000, while New York and Florida each have roughly 301,000.

德克萨斯州（50万）、纽约州和佛罗里达州（各约30.1万）紧随其后

What Types of Jobs Are Most Exposed to AI?

哪些职业风险最高？

The 26 occupations span technology, communications, administration, finance, and other knowledge-based fields.

26个高风险职业涵盖科技、通信、行政、金融等知识密集型领域

Mathematicians rank as the most exposed occupation, followed by proofreaders, correspondence clerks, court reporters, and media and communication workers. Computer programmers, database administrators, web developers, software developers, writers, translators, payroll clerks, and bookkeeping workers also appear on the list.

数学家风险最高，其次是校对员、文书文员、法庭记录员、媒体传播工作者。程序员、数据库管理员、网页开发师、作家、翻译、薪资专员和簿记员同样上榜

Many of these roles involve processing information, generating or reviewing text, working with structured data, or performing routine digital tasks, all areas where generative AI and other automation tools have advanced quickly.

这些岗位多涉及信息处理、文本生成/审核、结构化数据操作或常规数字任务——正是生成式AI和自动化工具快速发展的领域

Still, exposure should be interpreted as the potential for jobs to change rather than a direct estimate of how many positions will ultimately be eliminated.

需注意：风险指标反映的是岗位变革可能性，而非具体裁员数量预测
