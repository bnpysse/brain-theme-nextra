Zach Lloyd
Zach Lloyd

is the co-founder and CEO of Warp, an AI-powered terminal and software factory platform used by tens of thousands of engineers. Before Warp, he spent nearly a decade at Google, including time as a principal engineer on Google Sheets. He built Warp from the ground up as a modern, AI-native alternative to legacy terminals, and the team has since expanded into software factories: a full cloud-based system that takes an idea in Slack all the way through to a merged PR.
是Warp的联合创始人兼CEO。Warp是一个由AI驱动的终端和软件工厂平台，被数万名工程师使用。在创立Warp之前，他在谷歌工作了近十年，期间曾担任Google Sheets的首席工程师。他将Warp从零开始打造为传统终端的现代化AI原生替代品，团队随后扩展至软件工厂领域：这是一个完整的云端系统，能将Slack中的创意一路推进至合并PR。
