Frequently Asked Questions
常见问题

Gateside by TSA PreCheck is a special authorization that allows non-travelers who are eligible for TSA PreCheck screening to access parts of the airport beyond the security checkpoint. They can be used to meet family members at their gate, join a friend on a long layover for lunch or dinner, visit airport dining and shopping locations, or greet service members returning from deployment. Gateside by TSA PreCheck is the only way non-travelers can access the airport beyond security while using the TSA PreCheck lanes. If you get a gate pass from an airline or airport visitor program, you will need to proceed through security via the standard screening lanes.
本计划是允许符合TSA PreCheck资格的非乘机旅客进入安检后区域的特别授权，可用于登机口接机、陪同转机亲友用餐、体验候机楼商业设施或迎接归国军人。此为非乘机旅客使用PreCheck通道进入禁区的唯一方式，航空公司发放的登机口通行证仍需走常规安检通道。
