【文章标题】：How Pew Research Center is – and is not – using AI in our work  
皮尤研究中心如何运用——及如何避免运用——人工智能开展工作  

【文章正文】：  
At Pew Research Center, we’re approaching artificial intelligence from many perspectives.  
在皮尤研究中心，我们正从多维度审视人工智能。  

As public-facing social scientists committed to innovation, we’re exploring what AI might add to our toolkit.  
作为致力于创新的公共社会科学家，我们正在探索人工智能能为研究工具带来的可能性。  

As public opinion researchers, we’re studying and explaining how people are reacting to the advance of AI technologies.  
作为民意研究人员，我们持续研究并阐释公众对AI技术发展的反应。  

And as information providers whose fundamental values include accuracy and methodological rigor, we’re moving with great deliberation to ensure the quality of our work remains high.  
而作为以准确性与方法论严谨性为核心价值的信息提供者，我们审慎推进工作以确保质量始终卓越。  

In all of these spaces, our primary commitment is that our work remains people-centered:  
在所有领域，我们最根本的承诺是保持"以人为本"：  

- Real people, not machines, answer our surveys. We don’t use AI to create or model synthetic public opinion. Our survey results are based on the views that real people report to us. For U.S. polls, for example, we rely on a multimode, probability-based survey panel made up of roughly 10,000 adults who are selected at random from across the entire country.  
- 真实人类（而非机器）参与我们的调查。我们不用AI生成或模拟合成民意。调查结果完全基于真实人群反馈。例如在美国民调中，我们采用多模式概率抽样小组，涵盖全国随机抽取的约1万名成年人。  

- Humans decide what we study. Choosing research topics and deciding what to ask the public are among the most consequential things we do. Our researchers make those decisions.  
- 人类决定研究方向。选题和问卷设计是我们最具影响力的工作环节，这些决策均由研究人员完成。  

- Humans, not AI, write and review our research reports. Humans determine what’s analytically important and interesting.  
- 人类（而非AI）撰写并审校研究报告。分析重点与趣味性由人类判断。  

- Accuracy and rigor remain paramount. Humans oversee every aspect of our work, and humans hold the ultimate responsibility for its quality and accuracy.  
- 准确与严谨至高无上。人类监督工作全流程，并对质量与准确性负最终责任。  

- We protect respondents and their data. We never expose our survey respondents’ personal identifying information to any open, public AI tool.  
- 严格保护受访者数据。我们绝不将受访者个人信息暴露于任何公开AI工具。  

- The photographs that accompany our work are of, and by, real people. They’re selected by human editors. We don’t use AI to generate or modify photos. The same goes for our illustrations.  
- 研究报告配图均为真人实拍/绘制，由人类编辑遴选。我们不用AI生成或修改图片，插画同理。  

How we currently use AI  
当前AI应用场景  

- In the production of our website. Our engineers use AI code assistants to write the code underlying pewresearch.org.  
- 网站开发：工程师使用AI编程助手编写pewresearch.org底层代码  

- In parts of our research process. Researchers may use AI assistants to write the code needed to prepare, organize or run analysis on survey datasets. AI tools also assist with tasks involving textual data, such as coding open-ended survey responses into categories or scraping websites for key data. But even when we use AI, researchers design the analysis plans and interpret the data for our audiences.  
- 研究流程：AI助手协助编写数据整理/分析代码，处理文本数据（如开放题答案分类、网页关键数据抓取），但分析方案设计与数据解读始终由研究人员完成  

- In the final stages of our editorial process. Many widely available tools already use AI to help clean up grammar and punctuation. We’re experimenting with its use in initial phases of copy editing, though humans will continue to review and approve our final copy.  
- 编辑终审：尝试在初校阶段使用AI语法检查工具，但终稿仍需人工核验  

- In creating derivative products. We’re also experimenting with using AI to assist in creating derivative products based on human-authored research, such as social media posts. AI helps adapt existing findings for different formats and audiences, but it does not generate the Center’s original analysis or introduce new interpretations. Humans review all AI-assisted content prior to publication.  
- 衍生品制作：AI协助将人类研究成果转化为社交媒体等内容，但仅作形式适配不产生新观点，所有内容发布前需经人工审核  

We’ll be transparent  
透明度承诺  

- If we make meaningful use of AI in research production – for example, in analyzing textual data or coding open-ended responses – we’ll describe its use in the report’s methodology section.  
- 若在研究中实质性使用AI（如文本分析/开放题编码），将在方法论部分明确说明  

- If AI use expands in ways that materially affect how our external products are produced, we’ll revisit how we disclose that use publicly and update this article accordingly.  
- 若AI应用扩展至影响产品生产流程，将重新评估披露方式并更新本文  

We’d love to hear your thoughts, hopes and concerns on this topic.  
诚邀您分享对此议题的看法、期待与担忧。
