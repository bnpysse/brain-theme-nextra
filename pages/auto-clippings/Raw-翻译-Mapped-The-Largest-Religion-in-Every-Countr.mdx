Mapped: The Largest Religion in Every Country
地图解析：各国最大宗教信仰分布

Key Takeaways
核心发现

Christianity is the largest religion in 123 of 201 countries and territories, while Islam leads in another 54.
在201个国家和地区中，基督教是123个国家的主流宗教，伊斯兰教则在另外54个国家占据主导地位。

Just two countries changed their largest religious group between 2010 and 2020: New Zealand and Uruguay, both shifting from Christianity to the religiously unaffiliated.
2010至2020年间仅有两个国家改变了主要宗教群体：新西兰和乌拉圭，两国都从基督教主导转为无宗教信仰者占多数。

In seven countries, no religious group holds a majority. Singapore has the smallest leading share, with Buddhists accounting for just 30.8% of the population.
七个国家不存在占多数的宗教群体。新加坡的主导宗教比例最低，佛教徒仅占人口的30.8%。

Pew Research Center has completed its first comprehensive update of the world’s religious composition in more than a decade, revealing which religious group is largest across 201 countries and territories.
皮尤研究中心完成了十余年来首次全球宗教构成的全面更新，揭示了201个国家和地区中最大的宗教群体。

《各国宗教构成》数据集估算结果，展示各国最大宗教群体。

Christianity is the World’s Leading Religion in 123 Countries
基督教在123个国家占据主导地位

Christianity dominates the map across the Americas, Europe, much of sub-Saharan Africa, and Oceania. Islam forms another striking geographic belt, stretching from West Africa through the Middle East and into Central and South Asia.
基督教在美洲、欧洲、撒哈拉以南非洲大部及大洋洲占据主导。伊斯兰教则形成另一条显著地理带，从西非经中东延伸至中亚和南亚。
