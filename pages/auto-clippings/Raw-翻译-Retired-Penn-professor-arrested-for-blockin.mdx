【文章标题】：Retired Penn professor arrested for blocking Uber Eats robot

【文章标题】：退休宾大教授因阻挡Uber Eats机器人被捕

【文章正文】：

October 16, 2026

On Wednesday afternoon, Carolyn Marvin was leaving her flu shot appointment when she spotted an Uber Eats robot rolling down the Center City sidewalk. So she did what she has done for months whenever she's had the time: stand in front of it.

Marvin, 78, said she's not sure how many times she's blocked the path of these robots, which hit the streets of Philadelphia in March. The University of Pennsylvania professor emeritus has made a practice of it to protest "corporations that displace human delivery workers in order to enrich their own bottom line." Her past encounters have circulated online. The Wednesday incident was the first time, however, that the police got involved.

Officers approached Marvin after she'd been there "quite a long time," she said. They asked her to step aside and let the robot complete its delivery. When she repeatedly refused, they arrested her for disorderly conduct.

Police confirmed they had responded to a report of "criminal mischief involving a robot" on the 1200 block of Filbert Street around 1:16 p.m. on Wednesday. A woman on the scene ignored "multiple commands" to move, they said, resulting in a criminal violation notice for disorderly conduct.

Uber declined to comment.

Marvin, who plans to contest the charge on the grounds of her First Amendment right to protest, has been undeterred by the arrest. She plans to continue blocking Uber Eats robots and chatting with curious passersby who ask what she's doing.

"It allows me to talk to people about what's going on," she said. "It's always very interesting conversations that we have, and I think it's good to have those kinds of conversations in public space."

Marvin characterizes public opinion as "divided." Some people are very supportive, she said, while others view her protest as unfair to the customers who paid for delivery. Marvin usually tells them if they're "concerned about money," the robots' impact on delivery workers should worry them, too. She is careful never to block the sidewalk to human pedestrians, she added.

Before Marvin retired, she taught a course on freedom of expression at Penn. She doesn't see her actions as an extension of her academic work, however, as much as civic engagement.

"I would say it's my citizenship," she said. "I'm exercising the rights of my citizenship, so I think of it in those terms."

【文章正文】：

2026年10月16日

周三下午，卡罗琳·马文打完流感疫苗离开时，发现一台Uber Eats机器人正沿着市中心的人行道行驶。于是她做了几个月来只要有时间就会做的事：站在它面前。

现年78岁的马文表示，她不确定自己阻挡过这些机器人多少次了——这些机器人于今年3月出现在费城街头。这位宾夕法尼亚大学名誉退休教授一直以此举来抗议“那些为了充实自身利润而取代人类配送工人的企业”。她此前的遭遇曾在网上流传。然而，周三的事件是警方首次介入。

马文说，她在那里站了“相当长一段时间”后，警察向她走来。他们要求她让开，让机器人完成配送。当她多次拒绝后，警方以扰乱治安行为为由逮捕了她。

警方证实，他们于周三下午1点16分左右接到报告，称菲尔伯特街1200号街区发生“涉及机器人的刑事破坏行为”。警方表示，现场一名女子无视“多次命令”拒绝让开，最终因扰乱治安行为收到刑事违规通知。

Uber拒绝置评。

马文计划以宪法第一修正案赋予的抗议权利为由对指控提出异议，此次被捕并未吓退她。她计划继续阻挡Uber Eats机器人，并与好奇询问她在做什么的路人交谈。

“这让我有机会和人们聊聊正在发生的事情，”她说。“我们进行的对话总是非常有趣，我认为在公共空间进行这类对话是件好事。”

马文形容公众舆论“意见不一”。她说，有些人非常支持她，而另一些人则认为她的抗议对付费订购配送的顾客不公平。马文通常会告诉他们，如果他们“关心钱的问题”，那么机器人对配送工人的影响也应该让他们担忧。她补充说，她特别注意从不阻挡行人通行的人行道。

在马文退休前，她曾在宾大教授一门关于言论自由的课程。然而，她并不认为自己的行为是学术工作的延伸，而更像是公民参与。

“我会说这是我的公民身份，”她说。“我在行使作为公民的权利，所以我就是这样看待这件事的。”
