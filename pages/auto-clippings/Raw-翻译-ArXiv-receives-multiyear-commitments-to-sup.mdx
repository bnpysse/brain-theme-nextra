【文章标题】：ArXiv receives multiyear commitments to support it as an independent nonprofit
【文章标题】：arXiv获多年期资助承诺 将转型为独立非营利机构

【文章正文】：
arXiv is pleased to announce that three leading philanthropic organizations have provided multimillion-dollar support for arXiv. 
arXiv欣喜宣布，三家领先的慈善组织已为其提供数百万美元支持。

These new multiyear philanthropic commitments from Simons Foundation International, XTX Markets, and Siegel Family Endowment will support platform development, organizational capacity, and overall provide foundational support to arXiv’s establishment as an independent nonprofit. 
来自西蒙斯国际基金会、XTX Markets和西格尔家族基金会的多年期资助承诺，将为平台发展、组织建设提供支持，并为arXiv转型为独立非营利机构奠定基础。

The $17.2 million investment, spanning three to five years, will support arXiv as it transitions to independence by strengthening its technical infrastructure, supporting ongoing operations, and improving services for researchers around the world.
这项为期3-5年、总额1720万美元的投资，将通过加强技术基础设施、维持日常运营和完善全球科研服务，助力arXiv实现独立转型。

For three and a half decades, arXiv has been a trusted venue where researchers can share and access scientific findings. 
三十五年来，arXiv始终是科研人员分享与获取科学发现的可靠平台。

Today, we serve millions of users annually and play an essential role in scholarly communication across physics, mathematics, computer science, quantitative biology, quantitative finance, statistics, electrical engineering and systems science, and economics.
如今我们每年服务数百万用户，在物理学、数学、计算机科学、定量生物学、定量金融学、统计学、电气工程与系统科学及经济学领域的学术交流中发挥着关键作用。

Helping Fund a New Organization
助力新组织建设

I am grateful for the commitments from Simons Foundation International, XTX Markets, and Siegel Family Endowment, which put arXiv on solid footing as we move into this next chapter. 
arXiv首席执行官佩内洛普·刘易斯表示："感谢西蒙斯国际基金会、XTX Markets和西格尔家族基金会的承诺，为arXiv开启新篇章奠定坚实基础。

Their generous support provides a strong foundation and momentum for arXiv to continue to grow and serve the research community for the long term.” 
这些慷慨资助将为arXiv持续发展并长期服务科研界提供强大基础与动力。"

The new commitments build on previous philanthropic support for arXiv. 
新资助承诺建立在过往慈善支持基础上。

arXiv gratefully acknowledges their community-based donors, including member organizations, foundations, affiliates, sponsors, and longstanding supporters.
arXiv衷心感谢包括成员机构、基金会、附属机构、赞助商及长期支持者在内的社群捐助方。

These new commitments will support three core areas of work:
新资助将支持三大核心领域：
- General operation and ongoing improvement of arXiv’s services for the research community;
- 维持日常运营并持续改进科研服务
- Continued technical development of the platform, including work related to the management of AI-generated content and other emerging challenges in scholarly communication; and
- 持续推进平台技术开发（包括AI生成内容管理等学术传播新挑战）
- Strengthening of arXiv as an independent nonprofit, including investments in governance and organizational operations.
- 强化arXiv作为独立非营利机构的能力（包括治理结构与组织运营投资）

Simons Foundation International
西蒙斯国际基金会

Building on the Simons Foundation’s long-standing support of arXiv, Simons Foundation International has made a new multi-year commitment to support the organization’s next phase as an independent nonprofit.
基于西蒙斯基金会长期支持传统，西蒙斯国际基金会作出新的多年期承诺，助力arXiv进入独立非营利新阶段。

For 35 years, arXiv has made math and science more open, transparent, accessible and collaborative. 
西蒙斯基金会主席戴维·斯珀格尔指出："35年来，arXiv持续推动数学与科学领域更开放、透明、可及且协作。

By growing into an independent nonprofit, arXiv will be positioned to evolve and modernize its systems to better serve the scientific community and to secure its future."
通过转型独立非营利机构，arXiv将实现系统进化与现代化，从而更好服务科学界并保障未来。"

XTX Markets
XTX Markets

XTX Markets and its founder, Alex Gerko, have supported arXiv’s mission for the past two years. 
XTX Markets及其创始人亚历克斯·格尔科过去两年持续支持arXiv使命。

The firm is now making a new multi-year commitment to strengthen arXiv’s role as a trusted platform for open scientific research.
该机构现作出新的多年期承诺，以巩固arXiv作为开放科研可信平台的地位。

For decades, arXiv has been an important bedrock for open science. 
XTX Markets慈善事务主管西蒙·科伊尔表示："arXiv数十年来始终是开放科学的重要基石。

Now, with rapid advances in AI driving changes across academia, it is even more valuable to have robust, open-source infrastructure for science. 
当前AI技术飞速发展推动学界变革，此时拥有强大的开源科研基础设施更具价值。

XTX Markets is delighted to continue our support for arXiv in this important endeavor."
XTX Markets很高兴能继续支持arXiv这一重要事业。"

Siegel Family Endowment
西格尔家族基金会

Siegel Family Endowment has made a multi-year commitment to arXiv, advancing a shared vision for equitable digital infrastructure and open knowledge.
西格尔家族基金会作出多年期资助承诺，推动实现公平数字基础设施与开放知识的共同愿景。

Open access to knowledge is a cornerstone of a healthy research ecosystem, but it is also essential to a thriving digital ecosystem and to ensuring people can equitably access and participate in the knowledge that shapes our world. 
该基金会赠款事务高级副总裁约书亚·埃尔德指出："开放获取知识既是健康科研生态的基石，也对繁荣数字生态、确保人们公平获取并参与塑造世界的知识至关重要。

We’re looking forward to supporting arXiv in its next chapter as essential infrastructure, enabling communities to share ideas, build on one another’s work, and sustain a living body of knowledge."
我们期待支持arXiv作为关键基础设施开启新篇章，助力社群共享思想、相互借鉴，维系活态知识体系。"

We are especially grateful to our major funders, who trust in arXiv’s important work, and are committed to our vision. 
我们特别感谢主要资助方对arXiv重要工作的信任及愿景的认同。

Our major funders create a solid foundation of support and are an essential part of arXiv’s sustainable funding model, which is supplemented by our membership program, as well as our sponsors, our affiliates, and individual donors. 
主要资助方构建了坚实支持基础，是arXiv会员计划、赞助商、附属机构及个人捐赠者共同构成的可持续资金模式的核心部分。

Thank you for your unwavering support of arXiv!
感谢您对arXiv的坚定支持！"
