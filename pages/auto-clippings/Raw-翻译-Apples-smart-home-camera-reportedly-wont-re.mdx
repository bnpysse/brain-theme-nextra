【文章标题】：Apple's smart home camera reportedly won't record video

【文章标题】：据报道苹果智能家居摄像头将不录制视频

【文章正文】：

Apple’s upcoming smart home camera is reportedly taking a very different approach to products from Ring and Google Nest, with the device designed to understand what is happening around the home without recording video for users to watch back.

据报道，苹果即将推出的智能家居摄像头将采取与Ring和谷歌Nest完全不同的产品策略。该设备旨在理解家中发生的动态，而不会录制可供用户回看的视频。

Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman, speaking on the new Power On podcast, says Apple’s camera will have “no video recording.” Instead, the device will use AI to analyze its surroundings and report what it detects.

彭博社记者马克·古尔曼在最新一期Power On播客中透露，苹果摄像头将"不进行视频录制"，转而通过人工智能分析周围环境并报告检测结果。

The unusual approach could make privacy one of the defining features of Apple’s first dedicated home camera, while also making the product fundamentally different from a conventional security camera.

这种独特的设计可能使隐私保护成为苹果首款专用家居摄像头的标志性特征，同时也使其与传统安防摄像头存在本质区别。

The device, known internally as J450, is expected to use a low-frame-rate camera alongside facial recognition and infrared sensors to identify people and pets around the home.

这款内部代号J450的设备预计将采用低帧率摄像头，配合面部识别和红外传感器来识别家中的成员和宠物。

Rather than presenting a continuous or recorded video feed, it could provide information about activity in the house and use what it sees to power automations.

不同于提供持续或录制的视频流，该设备将提供房屋活动信息，并利用视觉数据驱动自动化操作。

That could allow Apple’s Home system to recognize when someone enters or leaves a room, switch lights off when a room becomes empty, or adjust music and other settings depending on which household member is present.

这意味着苹果智能家居系统可以识别人员进出房间，在房间无人时关闭灯光，或根据家庭成员身份自动调节音乐等设置。

Bloomberg has reported that Apple is planning a much broader push into smart home hardware, beginning with its new HomePad hub, HomePod mini 2, and Apple TV 4K.

彭博社报道称，苹果正计划大举进军智能家居硬件领域，首批产品包括新款HomePad中枢、HomePod mini 2和Apple TV 4K。

The Apple Post previously reported on the J450 camera as part of Apple’s wider smart home roadmap, including its expected use of facial recognition and infrared sensing.

此前《苹果邮报》曾报道J450摄像头作为苹果智能家居路线图的一部分，提及该设备将采用面部识别和红外传感技术。

Gurman’s latest comments clarify an important part of how the camera could work: despite having a camera sensor, the product isn’t being designed around giving customers a library of recorded footage.

古尔曼的最新说明澄清了该摄像头的重要工作逻辑：尽管配备摄像头传感器，但产品设计初衷并非为用户建立视频资料库。

That would separate it from Apple’s existing HomeKit Secure Video system, which lets compatible third-party cameras securely stream and store encrypted recordings in iCloud.

这将使其区别于苹果现有的HomeKit安防视频系统——后者允许兼容的第三方摄像头将加密录像安全传输并存储至iCloud。

It could also create limitations for customers who expect a security camera to provide footage after a break-in, package theft, or other incident. How Apple’s wider security system would handle those situations remains unclear.

对于期待摄像头能在入室盗窃、包裹丢失等事件后提供录像的用户，这种设计可能存在局限。目前尚不清楚苹果更广泛的安全系统将如何处理这类情况。

Apple is reportedly developing a home security and monitoring service alongside the hardware, suggesting the J450 may be intended as one component of a larger system rather than a direct replacement for every traditional security camera.

据报道苹果正在同步开发家庭安防监控服务，这表明J450可能是作为大型系统的组件，而非直接替代所有传统安防摄像头。

The camera is expected to follow Apple’s initial HomePad launch rather than arrive alongside it this month, with previous Bloomberg reporting pointing to 2027 for Apple’s broader home security push.

该摄像头预计不会随本月首批HomePad同时推出，此前彭博社报道指出苹果大规模进军家庭安防领域的时间点可能在2027年。
