【文章标题】：A Brief History of the Bloomberg Terminal

彭博终端的简史

【文章正文】：

Financial markets have always relied on timely information, and the drive for timeliness has always adapted to the latest technology. From clipper ships transiting the oceans to telegraph wires connecting cities to fiber-optic cables conducting trades in microseconds, traders have embraced any advantage to get the most up-to-date information. Indeed, the history of finance is really a story about how fast you can move information and who controls the interface.

金融市场始终依赖及时信息，而对时效性的追求总是与最新技术同步。从横渡大洋的快船、连接城市的电报线，到以微秒级速度执行交易的光纤电缆，交易者们拥抱一切能获取最新信息的优势。事实上，金融史本质上是一部关于信息传递速度与接口控制权的演进史。

It’s only natural that people also figured out a way to profit by supplying that market intel. In 1841, for example, the Mercantile Exchange (predecessor to Dun & Bradstreet) began selling proprietary business information to its U.S. clients. The following decade, Paul Julius Reuter began selling news services and stock price information. To supplement the company’s telegraph dispatches, he sent pigeons between Aachen, Germany, and Brussels; each bird carried a cylinder containing slips of paper with that day’s stock prices. In 1867, an inventor named Edward Calahan introduced the first telegraphic ticker-tape machine, which spooled out stock price information in near real time; Thomas Edison improved upon the design with his patented version in 1871.

人们自然也会找到通过提供市场情报盈利的方法。例如1841年，商业交易所（邓白氏前身）开始向美国客户出售独家商业信息。十年后，保罗·朱利叶斯·路透开始提供新闻服务和股价信息。为弥补电报传输的不足，他在德国亚琛和布鲁塞尔之间用信鸽传递信息——每只鸽子携带的圆筒内装有当日股价的纸条。1867年，发明家爱德华·卡拉汉推出首台电报行情收录机，近乎实时输出股价信息；1871年托马斯·爱迪生改进了该设计并获得专利。

The Dow Jones Industrial Average debuted in 1896 as an index of 12 key businesses listed on U.S. stock exchanges. It included gas, oil, coal, and electric companies, as well as enterprises dealing in leather, rubber, and tobacco. Messengers delivered quotes from the trading floor to brokerage offices, while stock tickers kept investors informed of prices. By the time New York City held its first official ticker-tape parade, in 1919, telegraphy in Western Europe and the United States had become the chief means for quick transmission of vital stock information.

道琼斯工业平均指数于1896年首次亮相，涵盖美国证券交易所12家重点企业，包括燃气、石油、煤炭、电力公司以及皮革、橡胶和烟草企业。信使将报价从交易大厅传递至经纪公司办公室，股票行情机则持续向投资者更新价格。到1919年纽约举行首次官方彩带游行时，电报已成为西欧和美国快速传递关键股票信息的主要方式。

In 1960, the first paperless financial service debuted, when Quotron introduced its electronic screens for displaying market quotes. Over the next two decades, other companies rolled out similar innovations for distributing financial news and data.

1960年，首项无纸化金融服务问世——Quotron推出显示市场报价的电子屏幕。此后二十年，其他公司相继推出分发金融新闻和数据的类似创新产品。

So when Michael Bloomberg decided to enter this well-established industry in 1981, the big question was: How would his new company stand out?

因此当迈克尔·布隆伯格1981年决定进入这个成熟行业时，核心问题是：他的新公司如何脱颖而出？

The Birth of the Bloomberg Terminal

彭博终端的诞生

Bloomberg had cofounded Innovative Market Systems (IMS) after being fired from the investment bank Salomon Brothers. Landing on his feet with his US $10 million equity payout and joined by former Salomon colleagues Thomas Secunda, Duncan MacMillan, and Charles Zegar, Bloomberg pursued his belief that Wall Street would pay a premium for specialized financial data. He’d earned an electrical engineering degree from Johns Hopkins University and an MBA from Harvard, and he’d built computerized financial systems for Salomon. IMS focused on developing a computer terminal that not only provided up-to-date information but could also do instant quantitative analysis based on historical data.

被投行所罗门兄弟解雇后，布隆伯格联合创立了创新市场系统公司（IMS）。凭借1000万美元股权补偿金重新起航，并携手前所罗门同事托马斯·塞昆达、邓肯·麦克米伦和查尔斯·泽加尔，他坚信华尔街愿为专业金融数据支付溢价。拥有约翰霍普金斯大学电子工程学位和哈佛MBA学位的布隆伯格，曾为所罗门搭建计算机化金融系统。IMS专注于开发既能提供实时信息、又能基于历史数据进行即时量化分析的计算机终端。

Michael Bloomberg believed Wall Street would pay a premium for access to specialized financial data. Karjean Levine/Getty Images

迈克尔·布隆伯格相信华尔街会为专业金融数据支付高价。Karjean Levine/Getty Images

At the time, most financial data still circulated through telephone calls, printed price sheets, and specialist publications, and analysis involved a fair amount of gut instinct guided by human expertise. Companies such as Reuters and Dow Jones provided subscription-based services for access to business news. But traders still had to assemble information from multiple sources and perform their own calculations and analysis.

当时多数金融数据仍通过电话、印刷报价单和专业出版物流通，分析过程大量依赖专业人士的直觉判断。路透和道琼斯等公司提供商业新闻订阅服务，但交易员仍需从多源整合信息并自行计算分析。

IMS proposed an integrated system with a single interface. Its Market Master terminal consisted of a monochrome CRT monitor, a custom keyboard, and a communications/controller unit that connected to the company’s private network. At launch, it provided only U.S. government bond prices and bond-calculation tools, but the dream was much bigger: a dedicated terminal that would sit on a trader’s desk and run different market scenarios, produce yield curves, and support investment calculations.

IMS提出了单一接口的集成系统方案。其Market Master终端包含单色CRT显示器、定制键盘及连接公司专网的通信/控制单元。初代产品仅提供美国政府债券价格和债券计算工具，但愿景更为宏大：打造置于交易员桌面的专属终端，可运行不同市场情景模拟、生成收益率曲线并支持投资计算。

IMS initially had just one client, Merrill Lynch, which invested $30 million (about $110 million today) in exchange for a 30 percent stake in the company and exclusive rights to the terminals for five years; Merrill waived that right in 1984. The first 22 Market Master terminals were delivered to Merrill in 1982, in the middle of a global recession. The timing was fortuitous. Worldwide, stock markets were transitioning to electronic trading, and the U.S. Federal Reserve was allowing more freely floating interest rates. Bond prices were more volatile, and investors were eager to figure out how to value them accurately. Bloomberg’s specialized financial terminals provided the data and the analytical tools to process and comprehend those sweeping changes.

IMS最初仅有美林证券一个客户——其投资3000万美元（现值约1.1亿美元）换取公司30%股份及五年终端独家使用权（美林于1984年放弃该权利）。首批22台Market Master终端于1982年全球衰退期间交付美林。这个时机恰逢其时：全球股市正向电子交易转型，美联储允许利率更自由浮动。债券价格波动加剧，投资者亟需准确估值方法。彭博的专业金融终端提供了处理和理解这些巨变的数据与分析工具。

Five years after its launch, IMS rebranded as Bloomberg LP and expanded its clientele, and the Market Master became known as the Bloomberg Terminal.

问世五年后，IMS更名为彭博有限合伙企业并扩展客户群，Market Master也由此被称为彭博终端。
