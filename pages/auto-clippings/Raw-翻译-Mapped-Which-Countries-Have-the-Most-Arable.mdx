Key Takeaways:
核心数据：

Bangladesh has the world’s highest share of arable land at 60.6%, followed by Denmark at 59.1%.
孟加拉国以60.6%的可耕地占比位居全球第一，丹麦以59.1%紧随其后。

South Asia has several countries with high arable shares, including India (51.8%) and Pakistan (39.3%).
南亚多国可耕地比例较高，包括印度（51.8%）和巴基斯坦（39.3%）。

Arable land is scarce across much of the Middle East, where aridity, water scarcity, and land degradation constrain crop cultivation.
中东大部分地区可耕地稀缺，干旱、缺水和土地退化制约了作物种植。

This visualization by Iswardi Ishak maps the percentage of each country’s land area classified as arable, using data from the World Bank Group.
本可视化图表由Iswardi Ishak制作，采用世界银行集团数据绘制各国可耕地面积占比。

Arable land includes areas under temporary crops, temporary meadows for mowing or pasture, market or kitchen gardens, and temporarily fallow land. The measure is a share of total land area rather than the absolute amount of farmland a country possesses.
可耕地包括种植短期作物的土地、临时割草或放牧的草地、商品菜园/家庭菜园以及临时休耕地。该指标是国土总面积占比，而非国家拥有的绝对农田数量。
