【文章标题】：Scientists Create a New Form of Ice at More Than 2000°C
【文章标题】：科学家在超过2000°C的条件下创造出一种新型冰

【文章正文】：
Water is one of the most commonplace, essential substances in the human world.
水是人类世界中最常见、最不可或缺的物质之一。

We literally can't function without its properties as a near-universal solvent. It falls from the sky. We bathe in it, drink it, and immerse ourselves in it for fun.
没有水作为近乎万能溶剂的特性，我们几乎无法生存。它从天而降；我们在其中沐浴、饮用，并为了乐趣而沉浸其中。

But if just considered as a liquid, water is extremely weird, behaving in ways completely at odds with other liquids. It becomes less dense when it freezes. Its surface tension is bizarrely high. So is its boiling point. And, based on its molecular weight, it should be a gas at room temperature.
但如果仅仅将水视为一种液体，它极其怪异，其行为方式与其他液体完全不一致。它结冰后密度反而变小；表面张力高得离奇；沸点也是如此；而且按照其分子量来看，它在室温下本应是气体。

And that's all at normal, ambient Earth conditions.
而这还只是在地球正常的环境条件下。

Tweak the pressure and the temperature a few notches, and water's outlandish behavior gets even more out of hand.
稍微调整压力和温度几个档位，水那超乎寻常的行为就会变得更加不可收拾。

Scientists have now demonstrated one of the weirdest forms of ice yet – under preposterous pressures up to 2.3 million atmospheres, and tremendous temperatures up to 2,630 kelvins (2,357 degrees Celsius, or 4,274 degrees Fahrenheit).
科学家如今展示了迄今最奇异的冰形态之一——在高达230万个大气压的荒诞压力，以及高达2630开尔文（2357摄氏度，或4274华氏度）的惊人温度下。

At those temperatures, you'd normally expect water to emphatically be a gas – even partially sundered into its constituent oxygen and hydrogen atoms. But something interesting happens at the astronomical pressures found deep inside planets.
在如此高温下，通常你会预期水明确地以气体形式存在——甚至部分分解为其组成的氧原子和氢原子。但在行星深处那种天文数字般的压力下，会发生一些有趣的事情。

When water transitions from a liquid to a gas, or vapor, it expands. Under crushing pressures of millions of atmospheres, this expansion is stymied. Instead, water can remain extraordinarily dense, taking on exotic forms unlike any ice we encounter at Earth's surface.
当水从液体转变为气体或蒸汽时，它会膨胀。在数百万个大气压的压碎性压力下，这种膨胀会被遏制。相反，水可以保持异常致密的状态，呈现出我们在地球表面所遇见的任何冰都无法比拟的奇异形态。

One of these is superionic ice – a deeply odd state of matter that's neither entirely solid nor entirely liquid. Its oxygen atoms remain fixed in a rigid crystal lattice, as they would in a solid. But the hydrogen nuclei are mobile, diffusing through that lattice more like particles in a liquid.
其中之一就是超离子冰——一种极其奇特、既不完全是固态也不完全是液态的物质状态。它的氧原子像在固体中一样，固定在刚性的晶格之中。但氢核却是可动的，更像液体中的粒子一样穿过晶格扩散。

At slightly different sets of conditions, the arrangement of the oxygen atoms shifts into different configurations known as phases. There are some twenty-something known phases of water ice, a few of which become superionic under extreme conditions. Scientists are always looking for more.
在略有不同的条件组合下，氧原子的排列会转变为被称为“相”的不同构型。已知的水冰相大约有二十几种，其中一些在极端条件下会变成超离子态。科学家们一直在寻找更多。

And it's not just weirdness for weirdness's sake. Superionic ice is thought to exist deep inside Uranus and Neptune, where its unusual properties may play a role in generating the planets' equally unusual magnetic fields.
而且这并非仅为猎奇。人们认为超离子冰存在于天王星和海王星深处，其异常特性可能在产生这两颗行星同样异常的磁场中发挥作用。

In their new experiments, a team led by physicist Alexis Forestier of the French Alternative Energies and Atomic Energy Commission subjected tiny samples of water to the sorts of extreme conditions expected in the interiors of ice giant planets.
在新的实验中，法国原子能和替代能源委员会的物理学家亚历克西·福雷斯蒂耶领导的团队，将微量水样置于冰巨行星内部预期的那种极端条件下。

They squeezed the samples between the tips of diamonds to pressures as high as 230 gigapascals, while using lasers to heat them to thousands of degrees. That's 2.3 million times Earth's atmospheric pressure at sea level – the pressure at the center of Earth, for context, is around 360 gigapascals.
他们用金刚石尖端将样品挤压至高达230吉帕斯卡的压力，同时用激光将其加热到数千度。这相当于地球海平面大气压的230万倍——作为参照，地球中心的压力约为360吉帕斯卡。

Then, using an extremely narrow beam of synchrotron X-rays, they probed for changes in the crystal structure of the ice.
然后，他们利用极细的同步加速器X射线束，探测冰晶体结构的变化。

What emerged was a configuration predicted theoretically but never unambiguously observed in experiments: hexagonal close-packed, or hcp, ice. As the hcp crystal was heated, its expansion also showed a signature of superionic behavior, suggesting it entered the superionic state at around 1,700 kelvins.
结果显现出一种理论上早有预测，但实验中从未被明确观察到的构型：六方密堆积，即hcp冰。当hcp晶体被加热时，其膨胀也显示出超离子行为的特征，这表明它在大约1700开尔文时进入超离子态。

The name refers to the arrangement of the oxygen atoms. Imagine you're packing identical balls in layers; there are a number of different ways those layers can be stacked while packing the balls as tightly as possible.
这个名称指的是氧原子的排列方式。想象你将相同的球体分层堆积；在尽可能紧密地堆球时，这些层有多种不同的堆叠方式。

One previously identified form of superionic ice has a face-centered cubic, or fcc, structure.
此前已确认的一种超离子冰具有面心立方，即fcc结构。

In the newly identified hcp ice, the layers are stacked in a different sequence. The researchers found evidence that one can transform into the other as the layers shift position.
在新确认的hcp冰中，各层以不同的顺序堆叠。研究人员发现证据表明，随着层的位置移动，一种结构可以转变为另一种。

This transformation seems to occur as conditions grow more extreme.
这种转变似乎随着条件变得更加极端而发生。

At 155 gigapascals and 2,000 kelvins, the signal observed from the X-ray probe was a mix of fcc and hcp.
在155吉帕斯卡和2000开尔文时，X射线探针观测到的信号是fcc和hcp的混合。

Dialing up to 197 gigapascals and 2,250 kelvins, the hcp signature became stronger relative to fcc.
提升到197吉帕斯卡和2250开尔文时，相对于fcc，hcp的特征信号变得更强。

By the final set of conditions – 219 gigapascals and 2,630 kelvins – the fcc signature had almost vanished, and hcp clearly dominated.
到了最后一组条件——219吉帕斯卡和2630开尔文——fcc的特征信号几乎消失，hcp明显占据主导。

Intriguingly, this may not have been the first time the researchers had produced hcp ice.
耐人寻味的是，这可能并非研究人员第一次制造出hcp冰。

Looking back at data from an earlier experiment, they realized that a previously unidentified X-ray diffraction peak observed above 130 gigapascals was likely the signature of hcp ice – they just hadn't recognized it at the time.
回顾此前实验的数据时，他们意识到，在130吉帕斯卡以上观测到的一个先前未被识别的X射线衍射峰，很可能就是hcp冰的特征信号——只是当时他们没有辨认出来。

Their results suggest that, at pressures above around 200 gigapascals, hcp may become the more stable arrangement of superionic ice.
他们的结果表明，在约200吉帕斯卡以上的压力下，hcp可能成为超离子冰更稳定的排列方式。

It seems like a relatively small change – literally on the atomic scale – but the difference could mean big things for the Solar System.
这看起来似乎是一个相对微小的变化——确切地说是在原子尺度上——但这一差异可能对太阳系意义重大。
