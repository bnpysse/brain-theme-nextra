【文章标题】：The insider trading you're allowed to do

【文章标题】：你被允许做的内幕交易

【文章正文】：

Insider trading is illegal in the stock market for a simple reason: it works too well. If you know the drug trial failed before the announcement does, you can't lose. So it's banned, and people go to prison for it.

【文章正文】：

内幕交易在股市中是非法的，原因很简单：它太有效了。如果你在公告发布之前就知道药物试验失败，你就不可能输。所以它被禁止，人们因此入狱。

But there's a version of it that's legal.

但有一种版本是合法的。

I'll use an example to illustrate first. Take Nokia, the biggest mobile phone company in the '90s. When the iPhone came out in 2007, Nokia's engineers internally knew their phones couldn't compete with the iPhone - their software and hardware were years behind.

我先用一个例子来说明。以诺基亚为例，它是90年代最大的手机公司。当2007年iPhone问世时，诺基亚的工程师们内部知道他们的手机无法与iPhone竞争——他们的软件和硬件落后了好几年。

The outside world had no idea. To analysts and headlines, Nokia was still the giant of the industry and the iPhone was just a new entrant. But the people working at Nokia really knew how bad the situation was. In other words, they were holding exactly what an insider trader holds: information the market doesn't have yet (that "Nokia is screwed!").

外界对此一无所知。在分析师和头条新闻看来，诺基亚仍是行业巨头，而iPhone只是一个新进入者。但在诺基亚工作的人真正知道情况有多糟。换句话说，他们掌握着与内幕交易者完全相同的东西：市场尚未掌握的信息（即“诺基亚完蛋了！”）。

A few of them "traded" on it. They retrained, and moved to Apple and Google while Nokia on a resume still opened doors. Instead of trading money, they "traded" their careers. But the mechanics are the same - they knew something early because they were internal employees, and used it to do something about their careers.

他们中的少数人利用这一点进行了“交易”。他们重新培训，跳槽到苹果和谷歌，而当时诺基亚在简历上仍然能打开大门。他们交易的不是金钱，而是自己的职业生涯。但机制是相同的——他们因为是内部员工而提前知道了一些事情，并利用它对自己的职业生涯采取了行动。

Most did nothing. They waited for the information to become public and when Nokia's phone business was gone by 2013, everyone hit the job market at once - which was way harder.

大多数人什么都没做。他们等待信息公开，当诺基亚的手机业务在2013年消失时，所有人都同时涌入就业市场——那要困难得多。

My point is: If you're working at a company, you probably know more about the state of your company, your industry, and yourself than other people. But are you using that information?

我的观点是：如果你在一家公司工作，你可能比其他人更了解你公司的状况、行业状况以及你自己的状况。但你在利用这些信息吗？

🤔 What "using it" actually looks like

🤔 “利用它”实际上是什么样子

Lemme give you three situations where people hold an edge and sit on it:

让我给你三个人们握有优势却坐视不理的情况：

Things are bad, but you don't act because of comfort: This is the Nokia situation. The product hasn't really improved, or it's being eaten by AI, the best people are leaving, and there are constant reshuffles. This is useful info: you're seeing issues before management decides layoffs are necessary. In software, it often looks like the roadmap being rewritten every quarter, the engineers who built the core system quietly leaving, or a migration that's been "nearly done" for two years. "Using it" is getting closer to the faster-growing competitors, or building new skills - like the people who left Nokia early.

情况不好，但你因为安逸而不行动：这就是诺基亚的情况。产品没有真正改进，或者正在被AI吞噬，最优秀的人正在离开，而且不断有重组。这是有用的信息：你在管理层决定裁员之前就看到了问题。在软件行业，这通常表现为路线图每季度重写一次，构建核心系统的工程师悄悄离开，或者一项迁移“差不多完成”却拖了两年。“利用它”就是接近增长更快的竞争对手，或者培养新技能——就像那些早早离开诺基亚的人。

Move closer to the "good". Say there's an initiative at your company that's doing very well. That's inside information too. "Using it" means getting closer to that good, before it becomes the official center of the company and gets super competitive. In tech, that might be the internal platform other teams keep asking to use, or the small AI project whose usage keeps climbing before anyone's announced it.

向“好”靠近。假设你公司有一个项目做得非常好。那也是内部信息。“利用它”意味着在它成为公司正式核心并变得竞争激烈之前，接近那个好的东西。在科技领域，那可能是其他团队不断要求使用的内部平台，或者那个在任何人宣布之前使用量持续攀升的小型AI项目。

If you're job searching: Most people apply to jobs based on big brand names, even though they aren't necessarily the best companies. Use your own experience to build your search around where the opportunities are.

如果你在找工作：大多数人根据大品牌名称申请工作，即使它们不一定是最好的公司。用你自己的经验，围绕机会所在之处构建你的求职搜索。

Almost everyone has that gap between what they know and what they're doing about it - it's normal! I want to dig into why.

几乎每个人在所知和所行之间都存在这种差距——这很正常！我想深入探讨原因。

Why nobody "trades" it

为什么没有人“交易”它

We don't act on stuff like this because we're often waiting for one of three confirmations: the numbers to show it, someone official to say it, or the move to finally feel justified.

我们不对此类事情采取行动，是因为我们常常在等待三种确认之一：数字显示出来，官方人士说出来，或者行动最终感觉合理。

The numbers arrive late. This is why most of Nokia's engineers didn't leave even though the writing was on the wall. Nokia had a record year in 2007, the year the iPhone came out. It just took time for the numbers to catch up. The cracks you see now show up in 3-4 years.

数字来得晚。这就是为什么尽管迹象已经很明显，大多数诺基亚工程师仍然没有离开。2007年，也就是iPhone问世的那一年，诺基亚创下了创纪录的业绩。数字只是需要时间才能赶上。你现在看到的裂缝会在3-4年后显现。

People wait for confirmation, and confirmation never comes. Researchers who studied Nokia's collapse found that middle managers were afraid to pass bad news upward. Execs had a reputation for shooting messengers, so at every layer of the company, reports got a little more optimistic. "We're in serious trouble" left the engineering floor and arrived at the top as "we're on track."

人们等待确认，而确认永远不会到来。研究诺基亚崩溃的研究人员发现，中层管理者害怕向上传递坏消息。高管们有“杀信使”的名声，所以在公司的每一层，报告都变得稍微乐观一些。“我们遇到了严重麻烦”从工程部门传出，到达高层时变成了“我们正按计划进行”。

And the feeling of "now it's justified" comes last. Think about what "justified" actually means — it means the situation has become obvious enough that anyone would agree with you. But at that point it's not "inside information" anymore because everyone knows it. The edge was when the move was still a bit early.

而“现在合理了”的感觉最后才到来。想想“合理”到底意味着什么——它意味着情况已经变得足够明显，任何人都同意你的看法。但在那时，它不再是“内部信息”，因为每个人都知道。优势在于行动还稍微早一点的时候。

If you're unsure what you know, here are a few things you can run that no outsider can.

如果你不确定自己知道什么，这里有几件只有你能做而外人无法做的事。

Customer energy: Do customers still love the product the same way, or have things changed?

客户能量：客户是否仍然以同样的方式喜爱产品，还是情况已经改变？

Pivot speed: When something clearly isn't working, does it get killed in a quarter, or does it limp along for two years? This tells you how fast your company admits mistakes — and companies that admit mistakes fast handle big shifts (like AI) much better.
