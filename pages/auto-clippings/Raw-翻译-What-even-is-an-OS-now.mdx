【文章正文】：
What Even Is An OS Now?
现在的操作系统到底是什么？

Crazy as it sounds to say this, since it’s all anybody’s been able to talk about for over a year, but the impact of AI on computing hasn’t yet sunk in.
尽管听起来很疯狂——毕竟这个话题已经被讨论了一年多——但AI对计算领域的影响仍未真正显现。

Today I’m parting company with Fly.io to work with Kurt on a new project. I’m getting that out of the way so you all know up front I’m talking my book.
今天我离开Fly.io，将与Kurt合作一个新项目。先说明这点，好让大家知道我是带着个人立场在讨论。

When I was a little kid, a family friend sold us our first "computer" — scare quotes because I’m pretty sure it was a VTech Laser 200 clone, a chiclet keyboard Z80 that ran off cassette tapes and plugged into our TV. 
小时候，一位家庭朋友卖给我们第一台"电脑"（打引号是因为那其实是VTech Laser 200的山寨机，带巧克力键盘的Z80处理器，用磁带运行，连接电视）。

I was old enough to know what a computer was, and conceptually what it meant to program one, because I remember dreaming of all the dumb games I could make. 
当时的我已经明白电脑是什么，以及编程的概念，因为我总幻想着自己能做出各种蠢游戏。

Finally! We had a device that would allow me to make things like the "Hitchhiker’s Guide" text adventure I’d play at my dad’s office.
终于！有了这个设备，我就能做出像在父亲办公室玩的《银河系漫游指南》文字冒险游戏了。

But it booted straight into BASIC. That’s all it did. I was, like, 8 years old. I wasn’t about to learn BASIC. What I learned instead: computers were not as awesome as I’d imagined.
但它直接启动到BASIC界面。八岁的我根本不想学BASIC。我学到的教训是：电脑没想象中那么酷。

By my mid-teens, computer security finally dragged me into programming. Coding only reinforced that crummy lesson. 
青少年时期，计算机安全领域最终把我拽进了编程世界。写代码只是强化了那个糟糕的认知。

The more I understood how programs were constructed, the more forbidding it sounded to build an entire complicated application. 
越是理解程序构造原理，开发完整复杂应用就越显得可怕。

I could belt out fast network servers no problem, but a word processor? You might as well have asked me to build a diesel locomotive.
我能轻松写出快速网络服务器，但文字处理器？那简直像要求我造柴油机车。

Suddenly, everything has changed. Computers now work the way I thought they did when I was 8.
突然间，一切改变了。现在的电脑终于像我8岁时想象的那样运作。

I’ve been writing these past several months about all the native macOS apps I’ve been churning out, and how weird it is to watch AI dissolve the boundaries between backend programming and frontend, between web and native, systems and applications. 
过去几个月我一直在写自己开发的macOS原生应用，看着AI消融后端与前端、网页与原生、系统与应用之间的界限，感觉非常奇妙。

All huge stories; any one of them would land in my career top 10 big shifts.
这些都是重大变革，任何一个都能排进我职业生涯的十大转折。

But AI is knocking down a much more important boundary: the one between programmers and users. 
但AI正在推倒更重要的界限：程序员与用户之间的藩篱。

My Mac menu bar and task switcher are cluttered with icons for programs I conjured for myself, with English as my programming language.
我的Mac菜单栏和任务切换器挤满了用英语作为编程语言为自己打造的应用程序图标。

What I did on my computer, any power user can do on theirs.
我在电脑上做的事，任何高级用户都能做到。

Will do on theirs.
而且必将做到。

It’s fucking inevitable.
这他妈是必然趋势。

❦

That’s a statement about the reality; a "prediction", if you want a concession to your skepticism about AI, but only in the sense that I can predict that the sun will rise in the east tomorrow. 
这是对现实的陈述；若要对AI怀疑论让步，也可称为"预测"——就像预测太阳明天会从东方升起那样确定。

So what do you do with that?
那么你该如何应对？

I think we’re all so bumfuzzled by the first-order impacts of AI that we haven’t considered the second-order effects.
我想我们都因AI的一阶影响而晕头转向，尚未考虑二阶效应。

Consider: what does computing look like in a world where many (maybe most) applications have an audience of just 1-2 people? 
试想：当多数（甚至绝大多数）应用只有1-2个用户时，计算世界会变成什么样？

How is software distributed? What does it run on?
软件如何分发？运行在什么平台上？

When I was younger, I learned that programs were normally something you bought off the shelves at Egghead Software. 
年轻时我知道程序通常是在Egghead软件店货架上购买的。

Then I watched the shelves at Egghead become obsolete. Then boxed software altogether. 
后来看着Egghead的货架过时，盒装软件彻底消失。

Then software shipped on CD-ROMs — you know that’s why we call it "shipping", right? we used to have to arrange shipping! — wiped away by the Internet. 
接着是CD-ROM分发——明白为什么叫"发版"(shipping)了吧？我们以前真要安排物流！——最终被互联网淘汰。

Now I think I’m about to see something else start to wash away: the entire concept of prefabricated fixed-function applications.
现在我将见证另一场变革：预制固定功能应用的整体概念将被冲刷殆尽。

Here’s what’s about to happen: users will increasingly interact with stuff they themselves conjured into being. 
未来趋势是：用户将越来越多地使用自己创造的工具。

Them, or people they’re on a first-name basis with. Programs will solve their problems specifically:
这些工具要么自用，要么供亲密伙伴使用。程序将精准解决他们的问题：

- 
How can I get an accurate weather forecast for Roscoe Village in Chicago when the nearest weather stations are attached to O’Hare and miss the lake effect?
"如何在最近气象站设在奥黑尔机场（无法反映湖效应）的情况下，获取芝加哥罗斯科社区的精准天气预报？"

- 
Forget the map, just tell me if I should get off the Eisenhower Expressway and take Roosevelt, Madison, or Lake back home?
"别给我地图，直接告诉我是该从艾森豪威尔高速下来走罗斯福路、麦迪逊路还是湖滨路回家？"

- 
Is there a meeting happening anywhere right this minute that I’m supposed to be in?
"此刻是否有我该参加但忘记的会议正在某处进行？"

You can go on and on like this. These all sound trivial. That’s the point. 
这类需求可以无限列举。它们听起来都很琐碎——这正是关键所在。

They’re normal-life problems nobody would have built serious programs around, because the audience sounds too small.
这些日常生活问题原本不值得开发正式程序，因为受众看似太小。

Today, almost all software comes from expert strangers. 
如今几乎所有软件都来自陌生的专家。

But soon, strangers will stop supplying our apps, and instead ship just their building blocks. 
但很快，陌生人将不再提供完整应用，转而只交付基础构件。

Sure, there will still be megaproject browsers and word processors. 
当然，大型浏览器和文字处理器仍会存在。

But there’ll be thousands of times more applications that pull in 1/7th of the guts of a word processor to solve some idiosyncratic work or home life problem for somebody who doesn’t know what a for-loop is.
但会有成千上万的应用，它们调用文字处理器1/7的核心功能，为不懂for循环的人解决特定工作或生活问题。

You can disagree with me about all of this, but if you understand where I’m coming from, it’s all just straightforwardly obvious how weird this is going to make everything.
你可以反对我的所有观点，但若明白我的出发点，就会明显看出这一切将让世界变得多么不同。

Why do we have modern operating systems? It’s not simply to "provide access to hardware resources"; plenty of embedded systems do that with runtimes that have no business calling themselves an "OS". 
现代操作系统的存在意义？不仅是"提供硬件资源访问"——许多嵌入式系统的运行时也能做到这点，却不敢自称"操作系统"。

No, the core purpose of a modern operating system is to partition different applications off from each other, and carefully control how they can communicate.
不，现代操作系统的核心目的是隔离不同应用，并严格控制它们的通信方式。

That makes a lot of sense in a world where we’re importing all our software
在我们导入所有软件的世界里，这种设计非常合理
