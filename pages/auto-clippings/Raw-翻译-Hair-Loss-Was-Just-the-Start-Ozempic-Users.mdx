【文章标题】：Hair Loss Was Just the Start. Ozempic Users Are Also Reporting Nail Trouble

脱发只是开始。Ozempic使用者还报告了指甲问题

【文章正文】：

GLP-1 medications like semaglutide and tirzepatide may come with some unexpected hair-raising side effects, research out this week suggests—or rather, hair- and nail-losing ones.

本周研究显示，司美格鲁肽和替尔泊肽等GLP-1类药物可能带来一些令人毛骨悚然的副作用——确切地说是脱发和指甲脱落的副作用。

Scientists in Europe studied hundreds of people taking a GLP-1. Compared to non-users, people on GLP-1s were significantly more likely to develop hair loss and nail-related problems, particularly nail detachment, they found. Though these issues aren’t necessarily too serious, both patients and doctors should be aware of the potential risk, the researchers say.

欧洲科学家对数百名GLP-1使用者进行研究后发现，与非使用者相比，用药者出现脱发和指甲相关问题（尤其是甲剥离）的概率显著更高。研究人员表示，虽然这些问题未必很严重，但患者和医生都应意识到潜在风险。

“For people taking or considering GLP-1 receptor agonists, the main takeaway is: do not panic, and do not stop an effective treatment on your own because of your hair or nails—but do mention these symptoms to your doctor,” study author Charles Taïeb, a clinical dermatologist at Necker University Hospital in Paris, told Gizmodo.

"对于正在使用或考虑使用GLP-1受体激动剂的人，关键建议是：不要恐慌，不要因为头发或指甲问题擅自停用有效治疗——但务必向医生报告这些症状，"研究作者、巴黎内克尔大学医院临床皮肤科医生Charles Taïeb告诉Gizmodo。

Losing nails and hair

指甲与头发的脱落

Numerous studies in recent years have found a link between GLP-1 use and hair loss, also called alopecia. And the Food and Drug Administration, following its own investigation of post-market data, now requires GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic (semaglutide) and Zepbound (tirzepatide) to list alopecia as a potential adverse reaction on their product labeling. Some people have also anecdotally reported changes to their nails after starting a GLP-1, but this possible complication has never been formally studied until now, according to the authors.

近年多项研究发现GLP-1类药物与脱发（alopecia）存在关联。美国食品药品监督管理局根据上市后数据调查，现已要求Ozempic（司美格鲁肽）和Zepbound（替尔泊肽）等GLP-1药物在产品标签中将脱发列为潜在不良反应。作者指出，虽然也有个别使用者报告用药后出现指甲变化，但这一可能的并发症此前从未被正式研究过。

The researchers formed a team, called SCOP-GLP1, to investigate both of these topics. The team is composed of scientists in France and Italy, and the project was funded by the pharmaceutical company Pierre Fabre, one of the largest dermo-cosmetics firms in the world (in fact, Pierre Fabre markets hair loss products via brands like Ducray and René Furterer, so the company has a commercial interest in this line of research). They conducted two similar studies, one that focused on nail-related changes following GLP-1 use and the other on hair-related changes.

研究人员组建了名为SCOP-GLP1的团队来研究这两个问题。该团队由法国和意大利科学家组成，项目由制药公司Pierre Fabre资助（该公司是全球最大皮肤化妆品企业之一，旗下Ducray和René Furterer等品牌销售防脱发产品，因此对此类研究存在商业利益）。他们进行了两项相似研究，分别关注GLP-1使用后的指甲变化与头发变化。

The studies each compared the outcomes of 575 people taking a GLP-1 for their type 2 diabetes or obesity to 1,379 controls with these conditions who hadn’t started GLP-1 therapy. According to the authors, both the GLP-1 users and controls were selected from a representative sample of people in four countries: France, the United States, Brazil, and Mexico. People’s average length of treatment was 18.5 months, and the researchers tracked weight changes over a three-month period.

两项研究分别将575名因2型糖尿病或肥胖症使用GLP-1的患者，与1379名未接受GLP-1治疗的同类患者进行对照。作者表示，用药组和对照组均来自法国、美国、巴西和墨西哥四国的代表性人群样本。受试者平均治疗时长18.5个月，研究人员追踪了他们三个月的体重变化。

As other studies have shown, GLP-1 users were more likely to lose significant weight and to report hair issues than non-users, the researchers found. Roughly half of people on a GLP-1 reported “unusual hair loss” compared to a third of controls, for instance. The former were also more likely to report scalp irritation. Doctors have long known, even before GLP-1s were around, that weight loss can sometimes trigger (usually temporary) hair loss. Interestingly, though, the researchers found that people’s likelihood of scalp problems wasn’t correlated with the amount of weight they lost. That suggests these hair issues aren’t entirely caused by weight loss while taking a GLP-1.

与其他研究一致，GLP-1使用者比非使用者更可能出现显著减重并报告头发问题。例如约半数用药者报告"异常脱发"，而对照组仅三分之一。用药组也更常报告头皮刺激。医生早已知晓（甚至在GLP-1问世前），减重有时会引发（通常暂时的）脱发。但有趣的是，研究人员发现头皮问题发生率与减重量无关，这表明脱发问题不完全由GLP-1用药期间的体重下降导致。

About 66% of GLP-1 users reported nail problems in general compared to 53% of non-users. The difference was far more substantial when it came to nail detachment, however, with roughly 39% of GLP-1 users reporting it compared to 9% of controls; 46% of people on GLP-1s also reported nail discoloration compared to 17% of controls.

约66%的GLP-1使用者报告指甲问题（非使用者为53%）。但甲剥离差异尤为显著：用药组发生率约39%，而对照组仅9%；46%的用药者报告指甲变色（对照组17%）。

What should this mean for GLP-1 users?

这对GLP-1使用者意味着什么？

The studies were presented this week at the annual conference of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology. That means this research has yet to go through the formal peer-review process. The researchers also cautioned that their findings do not prove that GLP-1 drugs can cause nail or hair loss. But the research should warrant further follow-up, according to Taïeb.

该研究本周在欧洲皮肤病与性病学会年会上发表，意味着尚未经过正式同行评审。研究人员也警告称，这些发现不能证明GLP-1药物会导致指甲或头发脱落。但Taïeb认为该研究值得进一步跟进。

“Both studies are cross-sectional and based on self-reported symptoms without dermatological examination, so they describe associations, not causes, and call for confirmation in prospective studies with clinical assessment,” he said. The team is now planning to conduct such a study that will proactively follow people at the start of GLP-1 therapy and objectively track their skin and hair health over time. That will allow the researchers to not only better study the causes of these problems but also whether they’re reversible.

"两项研究均为横断面调查，基于未经皮肤科检查的自述症状，因此只能说明相关性而非因果关系，需要通过临床评估的前瞻性研究来验证。"他表示。团队正计划开展此类研究，在GLP-1治疗初期就对受试者进行主动随访，并客观追踪其皮肤和头发健康变化。这将帮助研究人员不仅更好地探究问题成因，还能判断这些症状是否可逆。

In the meantime, there is reason to hope that these added risks, if genuine, can be managed effectively. The researchers found evidence that people’s nail issues were often better explained by their preexisting health problems, skin conditions, and nutritional deficiencies rather than the direct bodily effects of the drug itself, for instance. That should suggest it’s possible to treat these conditions without needing to discontinue GLP-1 use. Either way, it

与此同时，有理由希望这些额外风险（如果确实存在）可以得到有效控制。例如研究人员发现，受试者的指甲问题往往更能用既存健康问题、皮肤状况和营养缺乏来解释，而非药物本身的直接影响。这表明有可能在不停止GLP-1治疗的情况下处理这些问题。无论如何...
