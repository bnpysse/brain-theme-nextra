【文章标题】：Which Countries Pay the Most Interest on Government Debt?
哪些国家的政府债务利息支出最高？

【文章正文】：
How Government Interest Costs Compare
政府利息成本比较

Key Takeaways
核心要点

Mexico has the OECD’s highest interest burden, with 10.9% of government spending going toward net interest, narrowly ahead of the U.S. at 10.6%.
墨西哥是经合组织中利息负担最重的国家，净利息支出占政府支出的10.9%，略高于美国的10.6%。

Italy’s 7.1% interest burden is roughly five times Germany’s 1.4%, despite both being major euro-area economies.
尽管同属欧元区主要经济体，意大利7.1%的利息负担约为德国1.4%的五倍。

Four countries devote less than 1% of government spending to net interest: the Netherlands, Lithuania, Finland, and Estonia.
有四个国家的净利息支出占政府支出比例不足1%：荷兰、立陶宛、芬兰和爱沙尼亚。

The U.S. government’s annual net interest bill now exceeds $1.2 trillion, larger than the annual economic output of most countries around the world.
美国政府年度净利息支出现已突破1.2万亿美元，超过全球大多数国家的年度经济产出。

But the U.S. isn’t alone in facing a sizable interest burden. Across the OECD, the share of government spending absorbed by interest varies dramatically, showing how differently debt and borrowing costs are affecting national budgets.
但美国并非唯一面临巨额利息负担的国家。经合组织各国政府支出中利息占比差异巨大，显示出债务和借贷成本对各国预算的不同影响。

The rankings shift considerably depending on how interest costs are measured. The U.S. has by far the largest interest bill in dollar terms, while Mexico ranks first when interest is measured as a share of government spending.
排名会因利息成本计算方式发生显著变化。以美元绝对值计算，美国利息支出遥遥领先；而以占政府支出比例计算，墨西哥位居榜首。

Unlike spending on infrastructure or public services, interest largely reflects the cost of financing existing obligations. Across OECD countries, government interest payments reached 3.3% of GDP in 2024, exceeding spending on defense.
与基础设施或公共服务支出不同，利息支出主要反映现有债务的融资成本。2024年经合组织国家政府利息支付达GDP的3.3%，已超过国防支出。

Why the Interest Bill Could Keep Growing
利息支出为何可能持续增长

Today’s interest costs do not yet fully reflect current borrowing rates. Much of the outstanding debt was issued when rates were exceptionally low, but that debt gradually needs to be refinanced.
当前利息成本尚未完全反映现行借贷利率。大部分未偿债务发行时利率极低，但这些债务将逐步面临再融资。

Nearly 45% of OECD sovereign debt is set to mature by 2027. Debt maturing between 2025 and 2027 carries an average yield below 2%, compared with a projected average 10-year rate of around 3.6% in 2025.
经合组织国家近45%的主权债务将在2027年前到期。2025-2027年到期的债务平均收益率低于2%，而2025年10年期国债预期平均利率约为3.6%。

That large wave of cheap government debt could put further upward pressure on interest bills as it is refinanced at higher rates.
这批大规模的低成本政府债务若以更高利率再融资，可能进一步推高利息支出。

Why Some Countries Have Negative Interest Costs
为何部分国家出现负利息成本

Surprisingly, seven countries in the OECD data have negative net interest spending: Switzerland, Japan, Sweden, Luxembourg, Denmark, South Korea, and Norway.
令人惊讶的是，经合组织数据中有七个国家净利息支出为负值：瑞士、日本、瑞典、卢森堡、丹麦、韩国和挪威。

In these countries, governments hold financial assets that generate interest income. When that income exceeds the interest paid on government debt, net interest spending turns negative. These countries are therefore excluded from the ranking.
这些国家政府持有的金融资产产生利息收入。当该收入超过政府债务支付的利息时，净利息支出转为负值。因此这些国家未纳入排名。

This shows why debt alone doesn’t tell the full story. What governments own, what they owe, and the rates they pay all shape the true interest burden.
这说明单看债务无法反映全貌。政府资产、负债及支付利率共同决定了真实利息负担。
