【文章标题】：Why you're not getting a response to your podcast pitch from me (or others)  
为什么你（或其他人）的播客邀约邮件得不到我的回复  

【文章正文】：  
This email is a response to the 50+ podcast pitches that arrived in my inbox just last month, and keep arriving in growing numbers, from PR agencies and marketing teams at various companies.  
这封邮件是对上个月涌入我收件箱的50多封播客邀约的回应——这些来自各公司公关机构和营销团队的邮件数量还在持续增长。  

It's also a message to tech founders building stuff for developers: focus on building something that devs love, or find epic, and not hiring marketing/PR folks who then mass-email podcast hosts with AI-generated podcast pitches.  
同时也是对为开发者打造产品的科技创始人的忠告：专注于构建开发者喜爱或认为惊艳的产品，而非雇佣营销/公关人员用AI生成的邀约邮件轰炸播客主。  

---  

I host *The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast*. Thanks to so many of you tuning in, the podcast is widely listened to – it's one of the *top-ranked* software engineering podcasts, and episodes *typically reach* 500K+ people.  
我主持的《务实工程师播客》因大家的收听而广受欢迎——它是*顶级*软件工程播客之一，单期听众*通常超过*50万。  

If you're listening to the podcast, or reading the newsletter: thank you!  
如果你正在收听播客或阅读通讯：感谢你！  

---  

The podcast only has guests who, as as a software engineer, I'm excited to talk to and learn from. These folks typically fall into one of these categories:  
作为软件工程师，我只邀请那些让我兴奋并想与之交流学习的嘉宾。他们通常具备以下特质之一：  

1. **Built/builds epic pieces of technology.**  
   **创造过革命性技术**  
   Examples include *Chris Lattner* (built LLVM, Swift, Mojo), *Anders Heljsberg* (created C#, TypeScript, TurboPascal), *Andrey Breslav* (created Kotlin).  
   例如LLVM/Swift/Mojo之父*Chris Lattner*，C#/TypeScript/TurboPascal创造者*Anders Heljsberg*，Kotlin之父*Andrey Breslav*。  

2. **Works at a company I find very interesting and relevant.**  
   **就职于令我深感兴趣的相关公司**  
   Examples include *Boris Cherny* at Anthropic (created Claude Code), *Elizabeth Stone* (CTO at Netflix).  
   例如Anthropic公司Claude Code创造者*Boris Cherny*，Netflix CTO *Elizabeth Stone*。  

3. **Is a deep domain expert** - typically with a decade+ of experience.  
   **深耕某一领域十年以上的专家**  
   For example, *Nicole Forsgren* (developer productivity research), *Alice Ryhl* (Rust语言专家).  
   例如开发者效能研究权威*Nicole Forsgren*，Rust语言专家*Alice Ryhl*。  

4. **Deep thinkers with a decade+ of experience.**  
   **拥有十年以上经验的深度思考者**  
   For example, *Kelsey Hightower*, *Grady Booch*, or *Charity Majors*.  
   例如*Kelsey Hightower*，*Grady Booch*，或*Charity Majors*。  

---  

As the podcast has become more popular, I am getting overloaded with pitches from PR people, Comms people, and Marketing people, though. The typical pitch is usually AI-written, and goes something like this:  
但随着播客热度上升，我被公关/传播/营销人员的邀约淹没。典型AI生成邮件如下：  

&gt; *From &lt;CommsPerson@Startup.com&gt;*  
&gt; *Hi Gergely,*  
&gt; *X is the co-founder and CEO of Startup, which now serves ## million developers...*  
&gt; *...Would this be worth exploring for the podcast?*  
&gt;  
&gt; **模板邮件示例**  
&gt; *来自&lt;某初创公司通讯人员&gt;*  
&gt; *"X作为初创公司CEO，平台服务数百万开发者...探讨AI时代工程团队面临的挑战..."*  

I get 10-20 of these pitches, per week, in my inbox. Until now, I've been ignoring them, but then MarketingPerson sends two more follow-ups.  
每周我会收到10-20封此类邮件。原本选择忽略，但营销人员还会追加两封跟进邮件。  

---  

**To spell it out to the marketing folks:**  
**给营销人员的明确答复：**  

A) **Send out AI emails: expect nothing back.**  
**群发AI邮件=石沉大海**  
Don't expect a response to an AI-written email. You *think* it's a numbers game, but you're just adding to the noise.  
别指望AI模板邮件能得到回复。你以为这是概率游戏，实则只是在制造噪音。  

B) **My podcast doesn't take self-pitches.**  
**不接受自我推销式邀约**  
The only pitch I listen to is when a software engineer says *"this person built something amazing"*. But a CEO pitching themselves? *Ignore*.  
我只接受工程师的第三方推荐（如"某人创造了惊艳技术"）。CEO自荐？*直接忽略*。  

C) **Founders: stop outsourcing your hustle.**  
**创始人：别把推广外包了事**  
If you want to be on a podcast, do the work yourself. The best guests got invited because they *earned* it through their work.  
想上播客就亲力亲为。优秀嘉宾是靠*实打实的成就*获得邀请的。  

---  

**Final note to founders:**  
**给创始人的最后建议：**  
Build something developers genuinely love. That's how you'll get invited – not through some marketing person's cold email.  
打造开发者真正热爱的产品。这才是获得邀约的正道——而非靠营销人员的群发邮件。
