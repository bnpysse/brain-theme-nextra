A world where every interaction is worth having  
一个值得拥有每次互动的世界  

Join the team of driven individuals that push the limits of what is possible with AI  
加入这支充满激情的团队，共同突破AI的极限  

There’s a certain type of service you never forget. You call, and they remember who you are. You talk, and they know what happened last time. You need support, and they help you on the spot. You are busy, and they know when to leave you alone. The type of conversation that makes you feel understood. The type of service that shows that you’re cared for. The type of interaction that's actually worth having.  
有一种服务令人难忘：来电时对方记得你是谁；交谈时知晓过往情况；需要支持时立刻提供帮助；忙碌时懂得适时保持距离。这种对话让你感到被理解，这种服务让你感到被重视，这种互动才真正值得拥有。  

But over the past decade, this kind of service has become increasingly rare. Instead we got: Chatbots that forget what you said five minutes ago. Forms that ask what you’ve already answered a 100 times. Tickets that reduce you to just another number in the system. Call queues that make you want to throw your phone out the window.  
但过去十年间，这种服务日益罕见。取而代之的是：五分钟就忘记对话的聊天机器人、重复提问100次的表单、将你视为系统编号的工单、让人想摔手机的呼叫队列。  

Businesses had to make these tradeoffs in order to scale their service. But this is changing.  
企业曾为扩大服务规模不得不妥协，但改变正在发生。  

At telli, we are building AI to run customer-facing operations. With agents that don’t just answer questions. But act, understand, learn, and improve. At any hour. With any volume. In any language. On any channel.  
在telli，我们正构建用于客户服务的AI。这些智能代理不仅能回答问题，更能行动、理解、学习与进化。全天候响应、海量处理、多语言支持、全渠道覆盖。  

The future won't be a world where businesses simply automate their customer interactions with AI. But a world where every interaction has context, attention, and intent. A world where every interaction is worth having.  
未来不应是企业用AI简单自动化客户交互，而是每次互动都具备上下文、专注力与意图的世界。一个值得拥有每次互动的世界。
