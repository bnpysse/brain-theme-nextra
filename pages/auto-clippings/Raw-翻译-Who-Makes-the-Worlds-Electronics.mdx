Where Are the World’s Electronics Made?

全球电子产品产自何方？

Key Takeaways

核心要点

China accounts for 58.8% of global digital hardware capacity, more than all other countries combined.

中国占据全球数字硬件产能的58.8%，超过其他所有国家总和。

The top four countries are all in East Asia and together account for 80.5% of global capacity.

前四大生产国均位于东亚地区，合计占全球产能的80.5%。

India and Vietnam rank ahead of the U.S. in physical hardware capacity.

印度和越南在实体硬件产能方面领先于美国。

From semiconductors and displays to smartphones and computers, much of the hardware powering the global economy is produced in a relatively small group of countries.

从半导体、显示器到智能手机和计算机，推动全球经济发展的硬件设备大多产自少数几个国家。

This visualization ranks countries by their share of global digital hardware capacity in 2024–25. The data for this graphic comes from Ember analysis, drawing on data from SEMI, DSCC, Prismark, Counterpoint Research, TrendForce, IEA, BNEF, and GWEC.

Digital hardware includes semiconductor fabrication, displays, printed circuit boards, smartphones, computers, and related electronics manufacturing capacity.

数字硬件涵盖半导体制造、显示器、印刷电路板、智能手机、计算机及相关电子产品制造产能。

China Accounts for Nearly Three-Fifths of Capacity

中国占据近五分之三产能

China’s lead is enormous: its share of global digital hardware capacity is nearly six times that of second-ranked Taiwan.

中国的领先优势巨大：其全球数字硬件产能份额是第二名台湾地区的近六倍。

In fact, China has more capacity than every other country in the dataset combined.

实际上，中国产能超过榜单中其他所有国家的总和。

China’s position reflects its broad manufacturing base across finished electronics, displays, printed circuit boards, and other components.

中国的地位源于其在成品电子产品、显示器、印刷电路板等领域的广泛制造基础。

Large-scale production networks also connect factories with dense clusters of suppliers, helping make the country a central hub for global hardware production.

大规模生产网络将工厂与密集的供应商集群相连，使其成为全球硬件生产的核心枢纽。

China is also seeking to reinforce its electronics leadership through a new five-year plan focused on semiconductors, artificial intelligence, and other strategic technologies. The plan prioritizes advances across the integrated-circuit supply chain, including chips, as Beijing seeks to reduce reliance on Western technology.

中国正通过聚焦半导体、人工智能等战略技术的新五年计划巩固电子产业领导地位。该计划优先推进包括芯片在内的集成电路供应链发展，旨在减少对西方技术的依赖。

The country targets more than 30 trillion yuan (about $4.5 trillion) in operating revenue from related industries by 2030.

中国计划到2030年实现相关产业营业收入超过30万亿元（约合4.5万亿美元）。

East Asia Forms the Core of the Electronics Supply Chain

东亚构成电子供应链核心

Taiwan, South Korea, and Japan add another 21.7% of global capacity beyond China’s share.

台湾地区、韩国和日本在中国之外贡献了全球21.7%的产能。

Each market plays a distinct role in the electronics ecosystem, with major companies including TSMC, Foxconn, Samsung, BOE, and Sony operating across the region.

各市场在电子生态系统中扮演独特角色，台积电、富士康、三星、京东方和索尼等巨头企业在此区域运营。

Taiwan is particularly important in semiconductor fabrication and electronics assembly, while South Korea has substantial chip and display capacity.

台湾地区在半导体制造和电子组装领域尤为重要，韩国则拥有强大的芯片和显示器产能。

Japan remains an important producer of electronics, components, and technologies that support the wider hardware supply chain.

日本仍是支持更广泛硬件供应链的电子产品、零部件及技术的重要生产者。

India and Vietnam Lead the Next Tier

印度越南领跑第二梯队

Beyond the top four countries, electronics manufacturing capacity drops sharply.

前四名之外，电子制造产能急剧下降。

India ranks fifth with a 3.8% share, narrowly ahead of Vietnam at 3.6%.

印度以3.8%的份额排名第五，略高于越南的3.6%。

The U.S. follows at 2.9%. While the country remains a major force in semiconductor design and innovation, its share of physical digital hardware capacity is comparatively small.

美国以2.9%紧随其后。虽然该国仍是半导体设计和创新的主要力量，但其实体数字硬件产能份额相对较小。

Thailand and Mexico are the only other countries above 1%, at 1.3% and 1.2%, respectively.

泰国和墨西哥是仅有的另外两个占比超1%的国家，分别为1.3%和1.2%。

The ranking highlights the gap between where the world’s biggest technology companies are headquartered and where their physical hardware is actually produced. While the U.S. is home to many leading technology and chip-design firms, the manufacturing capacity behind the global electronics supply chain remains heavily concentrated in Asia.

该排名凸显了全球顶尖科技公司总部所在地与实际硬件生产地之间的差距。尽管美国拥有众多领先的科技和芯片设计公司，但支撑全球电子供应链的制造产能仍高度集中在亚洲。
