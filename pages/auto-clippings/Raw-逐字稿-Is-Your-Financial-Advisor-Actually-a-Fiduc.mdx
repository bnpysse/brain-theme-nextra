Important should obtaining a producducial relationship be from the client's perspective? 
从客户的角度来看，建立信托关系是否重要？

I think from the client perspective, there's no drawback. 
我认为从客户角度来看，这没有任何弊端。

I believe that a lot of people, when they're seeking out advice, they probably have an expectation that those kinds of things would be disclosed to them or there would be a discussion about it. 
我相信很多人在寻求建议时，都期望这类事项会被披露或进行相关讨论。

My sense when I speak to most people is that they believe that that's something that must showed or is happening. 
我与大多数人交谈时的感受是，他们认为这是必须展示或正在发生的事情。

When the reality can be a little bit different and a lot of other professional advisors are automatically fiduciaries, doctors are fiduciaries, lawyers or fiduciaries. 
而现实可能略有不同，许多其他专业顾问（如医生、律师等）都自动具备受托人身份。

There's a whole bunch of other categories where that does automatically attach. 
在很多其他专业领域，这种身份都是自动附带的。

But when we're you're in the world of financial and investment advice, it's not something that's as automatic is that whether people believe it or not. 
但在金融投资咨询领域，这种身份并非自动获得，无论人们是否相信这点。

So I think. From the client perspective, there aren't really any drawbacks. 
因此我认为，从客户角度来看确实没有任何弊端。

It can make proving legal claims easier and it might also provide some level of assurance that they know that the person they're dealing with holds themselves to a heightened standard. 
这既能使法律主张更易举证，也能提供某种程度的保证——让客户知道自己正在与恪守更高标准的人打交道。

So I know it can be difficult to identify. 
我明白这可能难以辨别。

And maybe depending on the geographic area you're in, if you want na work with somebody in person, the pool might be more limited. 
根据所在地区，若想进行线下合作，可选范围可能更有限。

So I don't know that it should be the only deciding factor, but all else being equal, there's absolutely no drawback from the client perspective. 
虽然这不应该是唯一决定因素，但在其他条件相同时，从客户角度绝对有利无弊。

And I would think that you would always want a fiduciary over an non fiduciary.
我认为人们永远会更倾向选择具备受托人资格的专业人士。
