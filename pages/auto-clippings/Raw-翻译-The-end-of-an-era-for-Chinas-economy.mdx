【文章标题】：中国经济一个时代的终结

Zhu Rongji has passed away at the age of 97. He was not the only architect of the economic reforms that have made China a modern powerhouse — Deng Xiaoping, Zhou Enlai, Chen Yun, and others were all important — but Zhu was perhaps the most skilled, versatile, and far-sighted. He presided over China’s greatest era of reform and opening up — 1991 to 2003 — and handled both macroeconomic and microeconomic policy with a skill that perhaps no other Chinese leader has ever matched.
朱镕基逝世，享年97岁。他并非推动中国成为现代强国的经济改革的唯一设计者——邓小平、周恩来、陈云等人都很重要——但朱镕基或许是最娴熟、最多才多艺、最具远见的一位。他主持了中国最伟大的改革开放时代——1991年至2003年——并以或许任何其他中国领导人都未曾企及的技巧处理了宏观经济和微观经济政策。

In many ways, Zhu was the man who really
made China a capitalist country
. The privatization of state-owned enterprises had begun earlier, but Zhu
continued and accelerated it
. He was the main champion of
China’s successful drive to join the WTO
, which supercharged China’s export industries and led to a massive wave of foreign investment; he also engaged in a deregulation program to lure foreign businesses to manufacture things in China.
在很多方面，朱镕基确实是那个真正
让中国成为资本主义国家
的人。国有企业私有化早已开始，但朱镕基
继续并加速了这一进程
。他是
中国成功加入世贸组织
的主要推动者，这一壮举极大地推动了中国出口产业，并引发了大规模的外国投资浪潮；他还推行了一项放松管制的计划，以吸引外国企业在中国设厂制造。

When the Asian financial crisis of 1997 led to a wave of bad loans, Zhu bailed out the banks, and growth quickly resumed. And Zhu also devised
the most important tax reform
in China’s modern history, transferring large amounts of local government revenue to the central government, while simultaneously letting local governments do things like borrow more money and sell more leases on land to fund their own activities.
1997年亚洲金融危机引发一波不良贷款潮时，朱镕基出手拯救银行，经济增长迅速恢复。此外，朱镕基还设计了
中国现代史上最重要的税收改革
，将大量地方政府收入转移到中央政府，同时允许地方政府通过增加借款和出售更多土地使用权等方式为其自身活动筹集资金。

There is no question in my mind that Zhu ought to be remembered as one of history’s great heroes. When he entered office, East Asia had 1.22 billion people living on less than $3 a day (in 2021 dollars) — more than half the world’s total. By the time Xi Jinping became China’s top leader, that number was one-sixth of what it had been:

在我看来，毫无疑问，朱镕基应被铭记为历史上最伟大的英雄之一。他上任时，东亚有12.2亿人每天生活费不足3美元（按2021年美元计算）——超过全球总数的一半。到习近平成为中国最高领导人时，这一数字已降至原来的六分之一：

OWID

This is the biggest and fastest decrease in absolute poverty that the human race has ever seen. Zhu didn’t do it alone, of course, but the economic model he devised was so effective at generating rapid growth that he deserves more credit than perhaps any individual other than Deng Xiaoping. The list of people who have done more to decrease aggregate human suffering is short indeed.
这是人类历史上绝对贫困最大幅、最快速的下降。当然，这不是朱镕基一人之功，但他设计的经济模式在推动快速增长方面如此有效，以至于除了邓小平之外，他比任何个人都更值得赞誉。实际上，在减少人类总体苦难方面贡献更大的人屈指可数。

China is no longer the growth powerhouse it was in those earlier years. According to official statistics, over the last 15 years, China’s growth rate has fallen to 5% — less than half of what it was in Zhu’s time:

World Bank

中国已不再是早年间那样的增长引擎。根据官方统计数据，在过去15年里，中国的增长率已降至5%——不到朱镕基时期的一半：

世界银行

Some independent estimates
put the growth rate much lower
— maybe 2-3%. China is going through a difficult economic time right now, and its leaders
have a tradition
of “smoothing” official numbers by understating growth during booms and overstating it during busts.
一些独立估计
将增长率定得更低
——可能在2-3%之间。中国目前正经历艰难的经济时期，其领导人
有一个传统
，即通过在经济繁荣时低估增长、在萧条时高估增长来“平滑”官方数据。

Some amount of slowdown is to be expected. China is a much richer country than it was when Zhu left office — maybe
five times richer
. When Zhu ended his historic tenure in 2003, China was about as rich as Tanzania or Zambia is now. Today, it’s closer to Argentina or the Dominican Republic. A country can’t keep growing at supercharged rates forever; eventually, technological catch-up and capital deepening run their course, and a country is forced to grow based more on innovation and improved efficiency.
一定程度的放缓是可以预料的。中国比朱镕基离任时富裕得多——也许
富裕五倍
。2003年朱镕基结束其历史性任期时，中国的富裕程度大约相当于如今的坦桑尼亚或赞比亚。今天，它更接近阿根廷或多米尼加共和国。一个国家不可能永远以超高速增长；最终，技术追赶和资本深化会走到尽头，一个国家被迫更多依靠创新和效率提升来实现增长。

To be fair, Xi Jinping has taken some serious steps toward making that transition. His government has designed a “new national system” of research and development that tries to
coordinate the entire supply chain of innovation
, all the way from government-funded research labs to private companies putting products on the shelves. He has unleashed the most expensive and extensive
system of industrial subsidies
in world history, promoting essentially every advanced manufacturing industry in existence, as well as AI. These policies may be unorthodox, but their goal is the right one — they’re aimed at pushing China past middle-income status by replacing catch-up growth with frontier innovation.
平心而论，习近平已经为推进这一转型采取了一些严肃举措。他的政府设计了一种“新型举国体制”的研发体系，试图
协调整个创新供应链
，从政府资助的研究实验室一直到将产品上架的私营企业。他推出了世界历史上最昂贵、最广泛的
产业补贴体系
，实质上推动了现存所有先进制造业以及人工智能的发展。这些政策可能不合常规，但其目标是正确的——它们旨在通过以前沿创新取代追赶式增长，推动中国跨越中等收入阶段。

Whether Xi’s policies have been
effective
at this goal is less clear. There’s actually a big debate over whether Chinese total factor productivity — a measure of overall economic efficiency — grew in the 2010s. The Penn World Tables, which had until recently shown a severe slowdown in TFP growth, recently
changed their assumptions
and now report very healthy annualized growth of over 2% throughout the 2010s. Other sources, such as
the Conference Board
and
Brandt et al. (2022)
, still think there was a slowdown, and peg the rate at around 1%. But everyone agrees that China’s TFP growth
has been slowing over time
, and that Xi’s “industrial policy for everything” hasn’t managed to re-accelerate it so far.
习近平的政策在这一目标上是否
有效
，则不那么明确。实际上，对于中国全要素生产率（衡量整体经济效率的指标）在2010年代是否增长，存在很大争议。宾大世界表（Penn World Tables）直到最近还显示全要素生产率增长严重放缓，但最近
改变了假设
，现在报告2010年代的年化增长率超过2%，非常健康。其他来源，如
世界大企业联合会
和
布兰特等人（2022）
，仍然认为存在放缓，并将增长率定在1%左右。但所有人都同意，中国的全要素生产率增长
一直在放缓
，而且习近平的“万物产业政策”迄今未能重新加速这一增长。

In fact, a lot of people are noting that China now appears to have a “two-speed” or “k-shaped” economy — split between a high-tech manufacturing sector that’s increasingly the envy of the world, and a broader economy that’s still mired in the aftermath of the country’s epic real estate bust.
事实上，许多人注意到，中国现在似乎呈现出“双速”或“K型”经济——一方面是日益令世界羡慕的高科技制造业，另一方面是仍深陷于该国史诗级房地产崩盘余波之中的更广泛经济。
