【帖子标题】：Cursor operates like a scam

【帖子正文】：

The way this company operates feels like a scam.

I use Grok Bot, which shows a weekly usage limit that resets every week — so you'd think whatever the bot does is covered. On Oct 3 my bot spun up three Cloud Agents in my repos and created branches I never asked for, and my Cursor included usage went up while the Grok Bot meter sat there looking perfectly fine. No popup, no "this will use your Cursor tokens, confirm?", nothing — and I don't even have on-demand billing on, so imagine the real money that could burn for someone who does.

What is the weekly reset even for if the real cost quietly comes out of my Cursor allowance? It's theater.

Then support: their AI first told me Grok Bot work comes from the weekly allowance and that I could switch annual to monthly with credit for unused time, then a human from billing told me the exact opposite on both — yes, Cloud Agents use your Cursor usage, no refund, and you're locked into yearly until Sept 2027. Two opposite answers depending on who picks up the ticket. I even offered to pay the full monthly price for the month I used, just let me switch — they said no, stay until 2027. Do not buy the yearly plan, go monthly so you can walk away.
