阅读笔记（6）

- Country entries marked 'no published restriction' reflect absence from Mercury's prohibited-countries list only; Mercury states eligibility is determined following a full review of each application, and no page affirms acceptance for any specific residence country.
- 标记为“无已发布限制”的国家条目仅反映其未出现在Mercury的禁止国家名单中；Mercury声明资格需在全面审查每份申请后确定，且没有任何页面确认接受任何特定居住国的申请。

- The list runs to 48 countries plus 4 sanctioned regions and reaches well past the eight columned in this index; it is scoped to where a founder LIVES, not to citizenship. A reader whose country has no column here learns nothing from that absence — read the linked list. Named on it and not columned: Indonesia, the Philippines, Vietnam, Bangladesh, Nepal, Venezuela and Angola, and — against the assumption that Europe is safe — Albania, Croatia and Latvia, the last two EU member states (verified against the list 2026-07-15).
- 该名单多达48个国家加4个受制裁地区，远超本指数所列的8个国家；其范围针对的是创始人居住地，而非国籍。如果读者的国家未在此列出专栏，并不能从这种缺失中得出任何结论——请阅读链接名单。名单上列出但未设专栏的国家包括：印度尼西亚、菲律宾、越南、孟加拉国、尼泊尔、委内瑞拉和安哥拉，以及——打破欧洲绝对安全这一假设——阿尔巴尼亚、克罗地亚和拉脱维亚，后两者为欧盟成员国（已于2026年7月15日根据名单核实）。
