【文章标题】：Ubisoft's FOR HONOR will block SteamOS / Linux players starting September 10
【文章标题】：育碧《荣耀战魂》将于9月10日起封禁SteamOS/Linux玩家

【文章正文】：
Ubisoft have announced that their third-person hero-based melee fighting game FOR HONOR will be blocking all SteamOS / Linux players on September 10th.
育碧宣布其第三人称英雄格斗游戏《荣耀战魂》将于9月10日起封禁所有SteamOS/Linux玩家。

The game uses Easy Anti-Cheat which is currently enabled for Linux, but they plan to disable it - making it another game that will no longer be playable across Linux Desktop, Steam Deck and Steam Machine.
该游戏目前采用支持Linux系统的Easy Anti-Cheat反作弊系统，但育碧计划停用该功能——这意味着《荣耀战魂》将加入那些无法在Linux桌面系统、Steam Deck掌机和Steam Machine主机上运行的游戏名单。

In an announcement on the official site they said:
育碧在官网公告中表示：

"To reinforce a fair competitive environment, the team is introducing additional player safety measures. We are removing For Honor from the Linux platform to ensure a safer environment for our players as we cannot meaningfully deploy protection on this platform. This means that For Honor will no longer be playable on Steam Deck starting September 10th. We will continue to investigate different ways to improve our anti-cheat with the possibility of reopening the platform in the future."
"为强化公平竞技环境，开发团队正在实施额外的玩家安全措施。由于我们无法在Linux平台有效部署防护系统，为确保玩家享有更安全的环境，将下架该平台的《荣耀战魂》。自9月10日起，Steam Deck将无法运行本游戏。我们将持续探索改进反作弊系统的方案，未来可能重新开放该平台。"

A real shame - any game that intentionally blocks Linux is a loss for the platform. We've seen the same in the past with the likes of the Battlefield series, Apex Legends, GTA V and more listed on our anti-cheat page.
这实在令人遗憾——任何刻意封禁Linux的游戏都是对该平台的伤害。过去我们在反作弊页面上已见证过《战地》系列、《Apex英雄》、《GTA V》等游戏的类似行为。

Hopefully Linux will continue pulling in more gamers and grow, so that these bigger publishers will one day be unable to just block Linux. Or perhaps Valve will be able to do something about it so publishers have some kind of system that they feel is good enough to secure their games from cheaters.
希望Linux能持续吸引更多玩家并发展壮大，终有一日让这些大型发行商无法轻易封禁该平台。又或者Valve能采取行动，为发行商提供足以防范作弊者的系统方案。
