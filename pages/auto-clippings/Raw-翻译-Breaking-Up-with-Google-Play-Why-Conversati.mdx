Breaking Up with Google Play: Why Conversations Is Now Free
与Google Play分道扬镳：为何Conversations现在免费了

Conversations, my federated instant messaging client for Android, started out as many traditional open-source projects do: as an attempt to scratch my own itch. 
Conversations——我为Android开发的联邦式即时通讯客户端——和许多传统开源项目一样，最初只是为了解决自己的需求。

Development started in January 2014 in my student dormitory, and within weeks I started dogfooding and using the client as the primary means of communicating with my friends. 
2014年1月，我在学生宿舍开始开发，几周内就开始"吃自己的狗粮"，将其作为与朋友交流的主要工具。

However, when it came to releasing the app to the public on March 24, 2014âexactly twelve and a half years ago todayâit was immediately clear to me that I would at least try to turn my open-source project into a business. 
但在2014年3月24日（距今正好十二年半）公开发布时，我就明确至少要尝试将这个开源项目商业化。

While I didnât invent the business model of making the source code publicly available but charging for the convenience of a compiled binary, it was certainly unusual in 2014.
虽然公开源代码但对编译版本收费的模式并非我首创，但在2014年确实罕见。

Fast forward a decade, and I did manage to turn Conversations into a sustainable business. 
十年过去，我确实将Conversations做成了可持续的事业。

The exact sources of income have shifted over the years. 
这些年收入来源具体有所变化。

In the beginning, it was a lot of paid development for companies that wanted to use Conversations. 
最初主要来自企业为使用Conversations支付的开发费用。

Some paid for features that made it into mainline Conversations; others wanted custom features so specific to their workflow that they never made sense to merge upstream. 
有些是为进入主线的功能付费，有些则定制了过于特定而无法合并到上游的工作流功能。

This was occasionally supplemented with providing server setup or even some consulting on instant messaging and security-related topics. 
偶尔还通过提供服务器设置或即时通讯/安全相关咨询来补充收入。

Later on, grants and funding opportunities played a more and more important role.
后来，资助和资金支持变得越来越重要。

One surprisingly steady source of income, however, has always been the Play Store revenue. 
但出人意料稳定的一直是Play商店收入。

I used to say that it pays my rent.

Every freelancer knows the feeling of uncertainty that comes with only being able to send out invoices every few months or receiving payment for funded projects only at the end of the funding period. 
每个自由职业者都懂这种不确定性——几个月才能开发票一次，或项目结束后才能收款。

Any form of regular incomeâespecially in the early stages, when you have not yet built up any savingsâis a blessing. 
任何定期收入——尤其在没有积蓄的早期阶段——都是恩赐。

My relationship with Google was never good. 
我与谷歌的关系素来不佳。

App updates have been rejected more times than I can count for incomprehensible reasons. 
应用更新因莫名理由被拒的次数多到数不清。

无数次我渴望能和一个真人对话五分钟。

但谷歌有责任优先审核历史悠久、非AI生成且更新不频繁的应用。

Waiting a little longer for new features might not sound like a big deal to some, but Google makes no distinction between feature updates and security updates. 
功能更新多等几天或许无妨，但谷歌对功能更新和安全更新一视同仁。
