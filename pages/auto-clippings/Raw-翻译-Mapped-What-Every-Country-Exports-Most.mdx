【文章标题】：Mapped: What Every Country Exports Most  
【地图解析】各国最大出口商品一览  

Key Takeaways  
核心发现  

Agricultural goods remain the largest export category for nearly a third of countries worldwide.  
农产品仍是全球近三分之一国家最大出口类别。  

The world’s four largest economies rely most on machinery and electronics exports.  
全球四大经济体主要依赖机械与电子产品出口。  

Canada, Russia, and Saudi Arabia are among the world’s largest exporters of fuels.  
加拿大、俄罗斯和沙特阿拉伯位列全球最大燃料出口国。  

Practically every country relies on international trade and global demand for its products. Yet nations differ immensely in what goods they ship to the rest of the world.  
几乎所有国家都依赖国际贸易和全球产品需求，但各国对外输出的商品差异显著。  

This world map highlights the top export category for countries around the world, based on the latest available data (2024) from the World Trade Organization. Service exports are excluded.  
本世界地图基于世界贸易组织最新数据（2024年）标注各国最大出口类别，服务贸易除外。  

Blessings in the Soil  
土地馈赠  

When it comes to natural resource endowments, some countries have been blessed more than others. For many countries, these resources provide an early foundation for exports: extracting primary goods from the ground and selling them to foreign buyers.  
就自然资源禀赋而言，某些国家得天独厚。对许多国家来说，这些资源构成了出口基础：从土地中开采初级产品并销往海外。  

For example, countries like Canada, Nigeria, and Saudi Arabia have all become major players in the global petroleum industry. Others, like Algeria, Russia, and Qatar, rely more on their massive natural gas deposits.  
例如加拿大、尼日利亚和沙特阿拉伯已成为全球石油产业重要参与者，而阿尔及利亚、俄罗斯和卡塔尔则更依赖其庞大的天然气储量。
