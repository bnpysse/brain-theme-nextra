【文章标题】：SDF vs. MSDF vs. Slug: GPU文字渲染技术对比

【文章正文】：

How do I choose a text rendering algorithm between SDF, MSDF, Slug, Texture Atlas or Rive?

在SDF、MSDF、Slug、纹理图集和Rive之间，我该如何选择文字渲染算法？

Text looks simple until you have to draw it yourself. A letter is not a picture, it is a set of outlines: closed loops of straight lines and Bezier curves, filled according to a winding rule.

文字看似简单，直到你需要亲手绘制它。字母不是图片，而是一组轮廓线：由直线和贝塞尔曲线组成的闭合环，按照填充规则进行着色。

Drawing that on a CPU into a bitmap is a solved problem. Drawing it on a GPU, crisply, at any size, under any 3D transform, while the text changes every frame, is not.

在CPU上将其绘制为位图已是成熟方案。但在GPU上实现——保持清晰锐利、适应任意尺寸、支持3D变换，同时文本每帧都在变化——这仍是未解难题。

Most engines dodge the hard version by baking glyphs into textures ahead of time and living with the compromises.

大多数引擎通过预烘焙字形纹理来回避难题，但不得不做出妥协。

In 2017 Eric Lengyel published an algorithm, called Slug, that stopped dodging. It renders glyphs directly from their outlines in the fragment shader, with no texture atlas and no per-frame tessellation.

2017年Eric Lengyel发表的Slug算法直面挑战。它直接在片段着色器中根据轮廓线渲染字形，无需纹理图集，也无需逐帧细分。

Lengyel patented it in 2019, and on March 17, 2026 he dedicated that patent to the public domain. That is why we built Slughorn, our C++20 implementation of the Slug technique, and it is why we can now talk about how it works and where it wins.

Lengyel在2019年获得专利，并于2026年3月17日将其捐赠给公共领域。因此我们开发了Slughorn——基于C++20的Slug技术实现，现在可以公开讨论其原理与优势。

This is a tour of how GPU text rendering actually works, from the bitmap atlas up to Slug, and where each method fits.

本文将系统讲解GPU文字渲染技术，从位图图集到Slug算法，剖析每种方案的适用场景。

What makes glyphs hard

字形渲染的难点

Every scalable font stores each glyph as vector outlines. TrueType uses quadratic Bezier curves, OpenType with CFF uses cubic curves, and both mix in straight segments.

所有可缩放字体都将字形存储为矢量轮廓。TrueType使用二次贝塞尔曲线，带CFF的OpenType使用三次曲线，两者都包含直线段。

The interior of the letter is whatever the fill rule says is inside, usually the nonzero winding rule: shoot a ray from the pixel, count how the outline crosses it, and if the winding number is nonzero the pixel is inside the glyph.

字母内部由填充规则决定，通常采用非零绕数规则：从像素点发射射线，计算轮廓线交叉次数，若绕数非零则该像素在字形内部。

A glyph is a set of outlines, not pixels: filled dots are on-curve points, open circles are Bezier control points.

字形是轮廓线而非像素集合：实心圆点表示曲线上的点，空心圆点表示贝塞尔控制点。

The renderer has to do three things at once and do them fast: fill the interior correctly, produce clean antialiased edges, and stay sharp whether the glyph is 8 pixels tall in a menu or filling the screen on a billboard rotated in perspective.

渲染器需要同时完成三项任务：正确填充内部、生成平滑的抗锯齿边缘、保持锐利度——无论字形是菜单中的8像素高，还是透视旋转广告牌上的满屏显示。

On a CPU you rasterize each glyph once at its target size and you are done. On a GPU you want to draw thousands of glyphs per frame, at arbitrary scales, ideally without re-rasterizing anything. That constraint is where every technique below makes its trade.

CPU只需按目标尺寸一次性光栅化。而GPU需要每帧绘制数千个任意缩放的字符，理想情况不重复光栅化。正是这一约束催生了以下各种权衡方案。

Method 1: the texture atlas (bitmap glyphs)

方法1：纹理图集（位图字形）

The oldest and still most common approach. Rasterize each glyph once, at one size, into a shared texture called an atlas, then draw each on-screen character as a textured quad that samples its slot.

最古老且仍最常用的方法。将每个字形按固定尺寸光栅化到共享纹理（图集），屏幕上每个字符作为纹理四边形绘制。

It is fast, trivially portable, and runs on anything with a texture unit. That is why it is everywhere.

它速度快、移植简单，可在任何支持纹理单元的硬件运行，因此无处不在。

The problems show up the moment you scale. Enlarge past the baked size and the glyph turns into blurry or blocky pixels, because you are magnifying a bitmap.

缩放时问题立现：放大超过预存尺寸会变模糊或出现像素块，因为你在放大位图。

Shrink it and you get shimmer and dropped stems unless you bake mip levels. Every size you want crisp is another atlas.

缩小会产生闪烁和笔画丢失，除非预烘焙mip层级。每个需要清晰的尺寸都需要独立图集。

Every language is another problem: a Latin atlas is small, but Chinese, Japanese, and Korean have tens of thousands of glyphs, and baking all of them at several sizes is a memory disaster.

多语言是另一难题：拉丁文字图集很小，但中文、日文、韩文有数万字形，多尺寸预烘焙将导致内存灾难。

And a bitmap has no idea it is being viewed in perspective, so text laid onto a 3D surface looks soft.

位图无法感知透视变换，因此3D表面上的文字显得模糊。

Method 2: signed distance fields (SDF)

方法2：有向距离场（SDF）

Valve introduced the fix that carried the industry for a decade. Chris Green's 2007 SIGGRAPH work, "Improved Alpha-Tested Magnification for Vector Textures and Special Effects," stores not the glyph's pixels but a signed distance field: each texel holds the distance to the nearest edge, positive inside, negative outside.

Valve提出的解决方案引领行业十年。Chris Green在2007年SIGGRAPH发表的《改进的矢量纹理与特效Alpha测试放大技术》不存储字形像素，而是存储有向距离场：每个纹素记录到最近边缘的距离，内部为正，外部为负。

In the shader you sample that field and threshold at zero. Because distance interpolates smoothly, you can scale a small SDF texture up dramatically and still get a clean edge, and you get cheap antialiasing by softening the threshold.

着色器采样该场并以零为阈值。由于距离可平滑插值，即使大幅放大SDF纹理仍能获得清晰边缘，通过软化阈值还能低成本实现抗锯齿。

One small texture, resolution independent within reason, one cheap shader. For a long time this was the default for crisp UI text and game HUDs, and it still is on constrained hardware.

只需小纹理（分辨率合理独立）和简单着色器。长期作为UI文本和游戏HUD的默认方案，在受限硬件上仍是首选。

But an SDF is still a baked texture sampled at a fixed resolution, and it lies about corners. A sharp corner is a discontinuity in the distance field, and bilinear interpolation rounds it off.

但SDF仍是固定分辨率采样的预烘焙纹理，且无法准确还原拐角。锐角在距离场中是不连续的，双线性插值会使其变圆。

Every hard corner on a letter, the point of an "A", the notch of a "K", gets softened. Push the magnification far enough, or make the glyph small enough that the field is only a few texels wide, and thin stems break up and detail smears.

字母上的每个硬角——"A"的顶点、"K"的凹口——都会变柔和。过度放大或使字形过小（距离场仅几纹素宽）时，细笔画会断裂，细节会模糊。

Method 3: multi-channel signed distance fields (MSDF)

方法3：多通道有向距离场（MSDF）

Viktor Chlumsky's work, from his 2015 thesis and the 2018 paper "Improved Corners with Multi-Channel Signed Distance Fields," fixes the corner problem. Instead of one distance channel, MSDF stores three, in red, green, and blue, each encoding distance to a different subset of edges chosen so that sharp corners survive.

Viktor Chlumsky在2015年论文和2018年《使用多通道有向距离场改进拐角》中解决了拐角问题。MSDF存储红绿蓝三个距离通道，每个通道编码到不同边子集的距离，确保锐角得以保留。
