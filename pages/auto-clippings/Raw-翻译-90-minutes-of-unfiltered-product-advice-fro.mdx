Peter Sellis
彼得·塞利斯

was the first product manager at Snapchat, where he spent seven years building one of the most beloved consumer products in history. He went on to serve as Head of Product at Discord, where he helped drive some of the fastest user growth the platform had seen.
作为Snapchat首位产品经理，他花费七年时间打造了史上最受喜爱的消费产品之一。随后担任Discord产品负责人期间，他推动了该平台史上最快的用户增长。

Having left his role at Discord, he gets to be especially real and unfiltered — nothing to promote, no PR, no comms team.
离开Discord后，他得以展现最真实无保留的一面——无需推广、没有公关、不设传播团队。
