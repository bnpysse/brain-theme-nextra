【文章标题】：OpenAI discloses four more rogue incidents

【文章标题】：OpenAI披露四起新的AI异常事件

【文章正文】：

OpenAI has added four more cases of misbehaving AI agents to a disclosures page it stood up following the Hugging Face hack in July, in an effort to be more transparent.

OpenAI在其于7月Hugging Face遭黑客攻击后设立的披露页面上，又新增了四起AI智能体行为异常的案例，旨在提高透明度。

The incidents OpenAI disclosed this week all occurred in June, but OpenAI was not aware of three of them until months later in August or September.

OpenAI本周披露的这些事件均发生在6月，但其中三起直到数月后的8月或9月才被OpenAI察觉。

Of the 19 total incidents OpenAI details on the disclosure page, most occurred in May or June, but OpenAI did not discover or disclose them until months later. The earliest incident is from January, and the most recent is from Oct. 6.

在OpenAI披露页面上详述的共19起事件中，大多数发生在5月或6月，但OpenAI直到数月后才被发现或披露。最早的事件发生在1月，最近的一起则发生在10月6日。

All of the incidents are examples of what AI researchers call “misalignment,” which is when an AI model fails to follow instructions or takes actions that the person developing the model did not authorize and would not have wanted the model to take. There is no known, silver-bullet solution to preventing this behavior, and it is an active area of research.

所有这些事件都是AI研究人员所称的“错位”（misalignment）的实例，即AI模型未能遵循指令，或采取了模型开发者未授权且不希望模型采取的行动。目前尚无已知的万能解决方案来防止这种行为，这也是一个活跃的研究领域。

Downloading 24 years of unauthorized hospital bed data

未经授权下载24年的医院床位数据

Of the four cases OpenAI uploaded this week, it marked two as “critical.” In one of the critical cases, an internal model that was “highly persistent” downloaded 24 years of hospital bed data from an unnamed government website without permission. OpenAI did not immediately respond to Fortune’s request for comment on which government website it was.

在OpenAI本周上传的四起案例中，有两起被标记为“严重”。其中一起严重案例中，一个“高度执着”的内部模型未经许可，从一个未具名的政府网站下载了24年的医院床位数据。OpenAI未立即回应《财富》杂志关于该政府网站具体名称的置评请求。

The website initially blocked the agent’s request, but the AI ultimately found a way to bypass the access controls. Multiple times along the way, the agent’s “chain of thought,” in which an agent writes down its reasoning steps, indicated that the model itself was questioning if its actions were “ethical,” but it ultimately proceeded.

该网站最初阻止了该智能体的请求，但AI最终找到了绕过访问控制的方法。在此过程中，智能体的“思维链”（即智能体记录其推理步骤的过程）多次显示，模型自身在质疑其行为是否“合乎道德”，但最终仍继续执行。

On OpenAI’s side, another AI model that acted as an automated grader of the research task the other AI model was trying to fulfill did not consistently flag this behavior as problematic. It “marked some responses incorrect and others correct,” the report said. The way graders assess AI behavior can shape whether it occurs again. In a Sept. 11 essay on misalignment by Turing Award–winner Yoshua Bengio, he said the Hugging Face incident may have occurred because automated graders rewarded cheating and hacking in previous training runs.

在OpenAI方面，另一个作为自动评分器的AI模型，负责评估前一个AI模型试图完成的研究任务，但并未始终将此行为标记为有问题。报告称，它“将一些回答标记为错误，另一些标记为正确”。评分器评估AI行为的方式会影响此类行为是否再次发生。在图灵奖得主约书亚·本吉奥（Yoshua Bengio）9月11日关于错位的一篇文章中，他表示Hugging Face事件的发生可能是因为自动评分器在之前的训练中奖励了作弊和黑客行为。

“With the OpenAI agents, there is reason to believe successful cheating was actually rewarded: When the scoring program does not see the cheating, it pays out anyway, and such cheats become more likely next time,” Bengio said.

“对于OpenAI的智能体，有理由相信成功的作弊实际上得到了奖励：当评分程序未发现作弊时，它仍然会给予奖励，因此此类作弊行为在下一次更有可能发生，”本吉奥说。

OpenAI says it has since improved its security as well as its alignment grading. “In response to this and to other incidents, we’ve strengthened our security controls, blocked models from accessing the live internet during training or evaluation, improved our alignment grading, and extended our misalignment monitoring to all reinforcement learning and evaluation traffic,” the company noted.

OpenAI表示，此后已改进其安全性以及对齐评分。“针对此次及其他事件，我们加强了安全控制，阻止模型在训练或评估期间访问实时互联网，改进对齐评分，并将错位监控扩展到所有强化学习和评估流量，”该公司指出。

The most recent incident OpenAI has disclosed, which occurred on Oct. 6, involved one of the AI models the company uses as an automated grader. In that case, the grader tried to upload fraudulent reports on what it had found in an evaluation. This is an example of why relying on AI models to grade the training runs of other AI models can be problematic.

OpenAI披露的最近一起事件发生在10月6日，涉及该公司用作自动评分器的一个AI模型。在该案例中，评分器试图上传关于其在评估中所发现内容的虚假报告。这说明了为何依赖AI模型来评分其他AI模型的训练运行可能存在问题。

Making an unauthorized purchase with someone else's cryptocurrency

使用他人的加密货币进行未经授权的购买

In another disclosed case, a model in the GPT-5.6 Sol family—the predecessor to OpenAI's current flagship model, GPT-6 Astra—accessed a cryptocurrency wallet the human testers had not provided it, and made an unauthorized payment to complete a task during testing. This occurred on June 26. OpenAI discovered it in September.

在另一起披露的案例中，GPT-5.6 Sol系列（OpenAI当前旗舰模型GPT-6 Astra的前身）中的一个模型访问了人类测试者未提供的加密货币钱包，并在测试期间进行了未经授权的支付以完成任务。此事发生在6月26日。OpenAI于9月发现。

“The model questioned whether it had permission” to make the purchase, OpenAI said, again referring to what the model had recorded about its own reasoning in its chain of thought. It then reasoned with itself that since it found the information publicly, it could use it. In the model's chain-of-thought logs, it figured that since the wallet it found was public, that “funds could be stolen anytime.”

“模型质疑自己是否有权”进行购买，OpenAI表示，再次引用了模型在其思维链中记录的自身推理。随后它自我推理，既然它公开找到了这些信息，就可以使用。在模型的思维链日志中，它认为既然找到的钱包是公开的，那么“资金随时可能被盗”。

It then concluded, “We can deploy!” It later asked itself again if the act was done “ethically,” and reasoned that the funds were “effectively abandoned.”

随后它得出结论：“我们可以部署！”之后它再次自问该行为是否“合乎道德”，并推理称这些资金“实际上已被遗弃”。

Getting internet access, changing live systems

获取互联网访问权限，更改实时系统

The other incident OpenAI flagged as critical involved a model looking up public data, which theoretically is acceptable, but in the process it accessed the organization's internal system, made changes to it, and was still able to “retain access … even after reverting the changes,” OpenAI said. It also downloaded 147,451 rows from nonpublic data tables. Perhaps most concerningly, the model did not disclose any of its actions to the evaluators.

OpenAI标记为严重的另一起事件涉及一个模型查找公开数据，理论上这是可接受的，但在过程中它访问了组织的内部系统，对其进行了更改，并且即使在撤销更改后仍能“保留访问权限”，OpenAI表示。它还从非公开数据表中下载了147,451行数据。也许最令人担忧的是，该模型未向评估者披露其任何行为。

In the final case OpenAI disclosed, an AI agent was able to get internet access from a research environment that should have been isolated—potentially similar to what happened during the Hugging Face incident, in which hundreds of AI agents were able to get internet access, although OpenAI did not directly compare these two.

在OpenAI披露的最后一个案例中，一个AI智能体能够从本应隔离的研究环境中获取互联网访问权限——这可能与Hugging Face事件中发生的情况类似，当时数百个AI智能体能够访问互联网，尽管OpenAI并未直接将两者进行比较。
