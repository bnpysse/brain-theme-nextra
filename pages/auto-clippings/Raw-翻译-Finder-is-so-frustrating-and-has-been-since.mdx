【文章标题】：Finder is so frustrating and has been since day one  
【文章标题】：Finder从第一天起就令人沮丧  

【文章正文】：  
Chad Maltby, September 2026  
查德·马尔特比，2026年9月  

Let me tell you why.  
让我告诉你原因。  

When you save something the Mac dumps you into a folder somewhere. You may or may not move away from that. Then you save. Later, both Finder and Spotlight require you to know something. Where the file is, what it is called, or some content to surface it for you. It requires some knowledge on your part. If I am saving many files every day, it is a thin knowledge. If I worked all day yesterday on ten different files, how am I supposed to remember what I saved on Thursday?  
当你保存文件时，Mac会把你丢进某个文件夹。你可能离开也可能不离开那个位置。然后你点击保存。之后，无论是Finder还是Spotlight都要求你知道某些信息——文件的位置、名称或部分内容才能帮你找到它。这需要你具备一定的知识储备。如果我每天保存大量文件，这种知识储备就很薄弱。如果我昨天一整天处理了十个不同文件，怎么可能记得周四保存了什么？  

I use Finder in gallery view, or list view with space bar, and scroll. Without a better option. This process is completely nuts. So I have to select a place in the hierarchy of folders and search in it.  
我被迫使用Finder的图库视图，或用空格键列表视图滚动浏览。因为没有更好的选择。这个过程简直荒谬。所以我必须在文件夹层级结构中选个位置进行搜索。  

I help lots of friends and family with IT stuff. You’d be surprised how many people don’t get the difference between the Desktop and Documents or Downloads. I’ve seen Desktops with thousands of files on them. I’ve seen Downloads folders that have never been emptied out, and so many people who never move anything out of them.  
我经常帮亲友解决电脑问题。你会惊讶有多少人分不清桌面、文档和下载文件夹的区别。我见过堆满上千个文件的桌面，见过从未清空的下载文件夹，还有无数人从不整理这些地方。  

Computer folders are still organized in structures set up some 50 years ago. Operating systems change. Computers change. Search changes. But this has never changed. I can't change that.  
电脑文件夹仍沿用50年前的结构体系。操作系统在变，硬件在变，搜索方式在变。但这个体系从未改变。我无力改变这点。  

In one instance, a friend asked me to help him locate something on his Desktop. I discovered that his Desktop contained more than 1,000 files. One year later a person knows almost nothing about a file, but they still have to find it. So I built a custom app for him: a drawer that eliminates folder hierarchy and location knowledge, and shows everything visually. It allowed him to scroll through everything quickly and find it.  
有次朋友让我帮他在桌面找文件，我发现他桌面竟有1000多个文件。一年后人们对文件的记忆几乎为零，却仍要找到它。于是我为他开发了定制应用：一个抽屉式界面，消除文件夹层级和位置记忆负担，用可视化方式展示所有内容。这让他能快速滚动浏览并定位文件。  

That app has grown over the last year to something I've used with 20+ people. I found more and more people with the same frustration, so I decided to turn it into something useful for them and useful for me. This is Kepter.  
过去一年这个应用已服务20多人。我发现越来越多人有相同困扰，于是决定将其打造成对大家和我都有用的产品。这就是Kepter。  

I would be considered a power user, have a good knowledge of the mac's whole structure. But when I started using Kepter, and started bringing all the features of Finder into it, I realized how much less my brain was thinking about any context of a file. It's saved me time and then some. It releases that capacity in my brain for something more important.  
我算得上高级用户，对Mac整体结构很了解。但当我开始使用Kepter并将Finder所有功能整合进来时，才发现大脑根本不需要思考文件上下文。它节省了我的时间——远不止如此。这释放了我大脑的容量去处理更重要的事。
