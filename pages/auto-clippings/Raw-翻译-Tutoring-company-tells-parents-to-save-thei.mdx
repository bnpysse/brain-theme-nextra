【文章标题】：Tutoring company tells parents to save their money and 'use AI instead'
【文章标题】：补习公司建议家长省钱"改用AI"

【文章正文】：
An Australian tutoring company has told parents to invest in Gemini or ChatGPT rather than pay overpriced human tutors to give their children an academic edge, as students increasingly turn to artificial intelligence for exam preparation and immediate study feedback.
一家澳大利亚补习公司建议家长投资Gemini或ChatGPT，而不是支付高价聘请人类家教来帮助孩子获得学业优势。随着学生越来越多地转向人工智能进行考试准备和即时学习反馈。

Dymocks Tutoring and Talent 100, which has five centres across Sydney, will close at the end of the week after telling customers that technology has rendered its service obsolete.
