【文章标题】：Flock Wants the Most Detailed Map of Its Surveillance Cameras Taken Offline  
【文章标题】：Flock公司要求将其最详细的监控摄像头地图下线  

【文章正文】：  
Amid mounting concerns about its sprawling surveillance camera network, Flock Security told the press this summer that it operates more than 120,000 cameras nationwide.  
随着对其庞大监控摄像头网络的担忧日益加剧，Flock Security今年夏天向媒体透露，其在全国范围内运营着超过12万个摄像头。  

But a new map published Wednesday by a cybersecurity researcher reveals Flock’s nationwide reach is even bigger. Based on location coordinates from Flock’s own database, the map shows more than 170,000 cameras, plus more than 130,000 accompanying gadgets that play a part in the company’s expansive American surveillance network.  
但周三一位网络安全研究人员发布的新地图显示，Flock的全国覆盖范围更为庞大。根据Flock自身数据库中的位置坐标，该地图显示了超过17万个摄像头，以及超过13万个辅助设备，这些设备共同构成了该公司在美国的庞大监控网络。  

Joshua Michael’s Flock Surveillance Map highlights the locations of what he says are 300,000 Flock surveillance devices spread across the country. His findings — which were cited in Wednesday’s Senate Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism hearing on Flock — differ from existing maps of Flock’s automated license plate readers in scope and methodology.  
Joshua Michael的Flock监控地图突出了他所说的分布在全国各地的30万个Flock监控设备的位置。他的发现在周三参议院犯罪和反恐小组委员会关于Flock的听证会上被引用，与现有的Flock自动车牌读取器地图在范围和方法上有所不同。  

Unlike crowd-sourced projects such as DeFlock, which are built on locations submitted by users, the Flock Surveillance Map relies on location data culled from a snapshot, archived by Michael in December 2025, of Flock’s own records. In addition to cameras, it maps supplemental devices — including 27,000 acoustic detection devices, as well as networking equipment that integrates third-party cameras — to illustrate the scale of Flock’s surveillance web.  
与DeFlock等基于用户提交位置的众包项目不同，Flock监控地图依赖于从Flock自身记录中提取的位置数据，这些数据由Michael在2025年12月存档。除了摄像头，该地图还标注了辅助设备——包括2.7万个声学检测设备，以及整合第三方摄像头的网络设备——以展示Flock监控网络的规模。  

“These cameras form a nationwide surveillance network that tracks where everyone drives,” Michael told The Intercept, “so foreign nations don’t need to send spies to harm our country. They can simply watch where our soldiers, federal agents, and politicians go.”  
“这些摄像头构成了一个全国性的监控网络，追踪每个人的行驶路线，”Michael告诉The Intercept，“因此外国无需派遣间谍来危害我国。他们只需观察我们的士兵、联邦特工和政治人物的去向。”  

The Intercept visited six random Arizona locations on Michael’s map; each location had a Flock camera present at the indicated coordinates.  
The Intercept随机访问了Michael地图上的六个亚利桑那州地点；每个地点在指定坐标处都有一个Flock摄像头。  

The Flock Surveillance Map also color-codes each Flock device according to its model — showing, for instance, whether the device is a Flock camera, known as a Falcon, or an accompanying processing unit known as a Picard. (Flock did not immediately respond to a request for comment.)  
Flock监控地图还根据型号对每个Flock设备进行了颜色编码——例如，显示该设备是Flock摄像头（称为Falcon）还是配套的处理单元（称为Picard）。（Flock未立即回应置评请求。）  

The accompanying searchable dataset table also lists each camera’s individual name, as outlined in Flock’s database, which typically includes a street address and sometimes other identifying characteristics. A camera with the name “FBI Pilot Camera,” for example, is shown to be located at the J. Edgar Hoover Building — the FBI headquarters in Washington.  
随附的可搜索数据集表格还列出了每个摄像头的单独名称，如Flock数据库中所述，通常包括街道地址，有时还包括其他识别特征。例如，一个名为“FBI Pilot Camera”的摄像头显示位于J. Edgar Hoover大楼——FBI在华盛顿的总部。  

The map illustrates Flock’s national spread, but also its clustering in certain areas. For example, 860 Flock devices appear to be concentrated just outside Chicago O’Hare International Airport, at the Rosemont Public Safety Department, which provides police, fire, and emergency medical services in the Chicago suburb.  
该地图展示了Flock在全国的分布，但也显示了其在某些区域的集中。例如，860个Flock设备似乎集中在芝加哥奥黑尔国际机场外的Rosemont公共安全部门，该部门为芝加哥郊区提供警察、消防和紧急医疗服务。  

Numerous Flock cameras appear to be installed inside detention centers. A camera titled “C-F-23 FOXTROT MALE HOLDING 2/SHOWERS” appears at the coordinates of the Silverdale Detention Center in Chattanooga, Tennessee.  
许多Flock摄像头似乎安装在拘留中心内。一个名为“C-F-23 FOXTROT MALE HOLDING 2/SHOWERS”的摄像头出现在田纳西州查塔努加Silverdale拘留中心的坐标处。  

In November 2025, Michael discovered a novel way to identify the location of Flock’s devices. Trawling the company’s website, he realized that Flock’s servers were publicly leaking data in the form of an access token that could be acquired without needing to log in. With that token, Michael said he could query ArcGIS, a third-party geographic information system platform used by Flock, to retrieve the locations of Flock devices.  
2025年11月，Michael发现了一种识别Flock设备位置的新方法。通过浏览该公司网站，他意识到Flock的服务器以访问令牌的形式公开泄露数据，无需登录即可获取。Michael表示，利用该令牌，他可以查询ArcGIS（Flock使用的第三方地理信息系统平台）以获取Flock设备的位置。  

Michael told The Intercept he promptly contacted Flock and described his findings. As Michael wrote in his initial email to Flock on November 13, 2025: “all testing was strictly non-intrusive, limited to open unauthenticated endpoints, and did not involve bypassing authentication, modifying data, or invoking any billable ArcGIS or Google operations.” Michael said he didn’t receive a reply, so he followed up with Flock the next day, and a third time several days later.  
Michael告诉The Intercept，他立即联系了Flock并描述了他的发现。正如Michael在2025年11月13日给Flock的初始电子邮件中所写：“所有测试严格遵循非侵入性原则，仅限于开放的未认证端点，未涉及绕过认证、修改数据或调用任何可计费的ArcGIS或Google操作。”Michael表示他未收到回复，因此第二天又跟进了一次，几天后又第三次联系。  

After his third attempt to inform Flock of the discovered vulnerability, Michael received a reply from a Flock that said, “Thank you for the findings. We are internally triaging them and will reach back out with next steps soon.”  
在第三次尝试告知Flock发现的漏洞后，Michael收到了Flock的回复：“感谢您的发现。我们正在内部评估，并将很快与您联系下一步行动。”  

Michael said he never heard back about it from Flock.  
Michael表示，他再未收到Flock的回复。  

In December 2025, Michael downloaded the Flock device location data, and in January wrote an in-depth technical blog post about what he had found. After Michael’s post, it appears that Flock fixed the vulnerability.  
2025年12月，Michael下载了Flock设备位置数据，并于1月撰写了一篇深入的技术博客文章，介绍他的发现。在Michael发布文章后，Flock似乎修复了该漏洞。  

Despite being alerted of Michael’s findings in November 2025, Flock published its own blog post the following January saying it hadn’t had any data breaches. “Flock has never been hacked, and there has not been a leak of Flock information,” the company claimed. “Flock Safety’s cloud platform has never experienced a data breach.”  
尽管在2025年11月已被告知Michael的发现，Flock在次年1月发布了自己的博客文章，称其未发生任何数据泄露。“Flock从未被黑客入侵，也未发生过信息泄露，”该公司声称。“Flock Safety的云平台从未经历过数据泄露。”
