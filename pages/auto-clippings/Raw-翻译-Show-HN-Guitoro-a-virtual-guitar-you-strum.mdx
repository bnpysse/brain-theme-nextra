【文章标题】：Show HN：Guitoro，一把用键盘弹奏的虚拟吉他

【文章正文】：

Loading guitar

加载吉他

The most realistic virtual guitar online

网上最逼真的虚拟吉他

Guitoro is the most realistic and the most complete virtual guitar you can play online, free in your browser. Play it with your computer keyboard, your left hand on the chords and your right hand strumming, or swipe the strings with your finger on a phone or tablet. Choose a steel-string acoustic, a classic guitar, an electric guitar or a four-string bass.

Guitoro 是你在网上能弹奏的最逼真、最完整的虚拟吉他，免费在浏览器中使用。你可以用电脑键盘弹奏，左手按和弦，右手扫弦，或者在手机或平板电脑上用手指滑过琴弦。可以选择钢弦原声吉他、古典吉他、电吉他或四弦贝斯。

It's also a way to learn chords and songs that a chord chart or a video lesson can't show you. Each song plays its best-known part close to the original recording, stroke for stroke. Slow it down to 20% and watch it being played: each string lights up the moment it's hit, the pick swings down and up with the strum, and the neck shows which finger goes where.

这也是一种学习和弦和歌曲的方式，这是和弦图表或视频课程无法展示的。每首歌都演奏其最著名的部分，接近原始录音，一拍不差。放慢到20%的速度观看演奏：每根弦在被拨动的瞬间亮起，拨片随着扫弦上下摆动，琴颈显示哪个手指按在哪里。

Then it's your turn. No guitar? Play the song on the keys or the screen, and it waits for you at every chord change. Have a guitar? Pick it up and play along at your own speed, with the strings, the strum and the fingering right in front of you.

然后轮到你了。没有吉他？在键盘或屏幕上弹奏歌曲，它会在每次和弦变化时等你。有吉他？拿起吉他，按照自己的速度跟着弹，琴弦、扫弦和指法就在你眼前。

Every guitar and bass note is a recording of a real instrument. There's nothing to download and no account to create. If you played the Google guitar doodle, Guitoro takes the idea further: six real strings, 15 chords in every key, and 51 songs to play along with.

每一个吉他和贝斯音符都是真实乐器的录音。无需下载任何东西，也无需创建账户。如果你玩过谷歌吉他涂鸦，Guitoro 将这个想法更进一步：六根真实的琴弦，每个调15个和弦，以及51首可以跟着弹的歌曲。

Play

弹奏

No guitar needed. Guitoro is built to be played, not just clicked, on whatever you have in front of you.

不需要吉他。Guitoro 是为了弹奏而设计的，而不仅仅是点击，无论你面前有什么设备。

Your left hand holds a chord on the letter keys and your right hand strums with H and J, the way you'd play a real guitar. 15 chords sit under your fingers, plus keys for arpeggios, single strings, chucks and palm mutes.

你的左手在字母键上按和弦，右手用 H 和 J 扫弦，就像弹真正的吉他一样。15个和弦就在你的手指下，还有用于琶音、单弦、闷音和手掌制音的按键。

On a phone or tablet, tap a chord pad and swipe the strings with your finger. Up to five fingers at once, and you can tap the frets on the neck to play single notes.

在手机或平板电脑上，点击和弦垫，用手指滑过琴弦。最多可以同时使用五个手指，你还可以点击琴颈上的品丝来弹奏单音。

Drag across the strings. A fast swipe plays louder, a slow drag rolls the notes one by one, and a click plucks a single string.

在琴弦上拖动。快速滑动会弹得更响，慢速拖动会逐个滚动音符，点击则拨动单根弦。

On a computer, switch to Solo and every keyboard row becomes a string and every column a fret, with bends and vibrato on the arrow keys.

在电脑上，切换到独奏模式，每一行键盘变成一根弦，每一列变成一个品，方向键用于弯音和颤音。

Learn by watching

通过观看学习

In a video lesson the hand moves fast and hides the strings, so you can't tell which ones were really hit. Guitar apps teach the basic chords and stop there. Guitoro plays the song's real part on the guitar in front of you, at the speed you choose, and shows exactly what the hands do. Then you play it, on the virtual guitar or on your own.

在视频课程中，手移动得很快，遮住了琴弦，所以你无法分辨哪些弦真的被拨动了。吉他应用程序只教基本和弦就止步了。Guitoro 在你面前的吉他上演奏歌曲的真实部分，以你选择的速度，并精确显示双手的动作。然后你可以在虚拟吉他上或用自己的吉他弹奏。

Each song plays its best-known part close to the original recording, stroke for stroke. Slow it anywhere from 20% to full speed and the chords, the strum and the band slow down together.

每首歌都演奏其最著名的部分，接近原始录音，一拍不差。你可以将速度从20%到全速任意调整，和弦、扫弦和乐队会一起放慢。

Each string lights up the moment it sounds, then vibrates as it rings. You can see whether a stroke hits all six strings, just the bass strings or only the top three.

每根弦在发声的瞬间亮起，然后随着余音振动。你可以看到一次扫弦是击中了所有六根弦、只有低音弦还是只有顶部三根弦。

Over a strummed part, the pick swings down and up like a real strumming hand, once per step of the pattern. Solid swings hit the strings and faint ones miss them, so you see the hand keep moving through the gaps.

在扫弦部分，拨片像真实的扫弦手一样上下摆动，每个节奏型步骤摆动一次。实心摆动击中琴弦，虚影摆动则错过，这样你可以看到手在间隙中持续移动。

While a chord rings, the neck shows it: finger numbers from 1 to 4, barres, muted strings and the capo. Copy the shape onto your own guitar.

当和弦响起时，琴颈会显示：手指编号1到4、横按、制音弦和变调夹。把这个形状复制到你的吉他上。

Play a song's chords on the keys or the screen and it follows you. The keys show its chords in order, the next one lights green, and the song holds until you play it. No score and no rush.

在键盘或屏幕上弹奏歌曲的和弦，它会跟随你。键盘按顺序显示和弦，下一个和弦亮起绿色，歌曲会一直等待直到你弹奏。没有乐谱，也不用着急。

Watch a part slowly, copy it on your guitar, then play along as you raise the speed. On a computer, choose My guitar: the on-screen keys step aside and the song box shows the strum or picking pattern, a chord strip you can click to jump around, and the speed.

慢慢观看一个部分，在你的吉他上模仿，然后随着速度提高跟着弹。在电脑上，选择“我的吉他”：屏幕上的按键让开，歌曲框显示扫弦或拨弦模式、一个可以点击跳转的和弦条，以及速度。

Realism

真实感

Most online guitars only let you click single strings or frets, or play a synthesized tone when you click a chord. On Guitoro you strum: swipe across the strings or use the keys, and every string sounds as you cross it, from recordings of real instruments.

大多数在线吉他只让你点击单根弦或品，或者点击和弦时播放合成音。在 Guitoro 上你可以扫弦：滑过琴弦或使用按键，每根弦在你经过时都会发声，来自真实乐器的录音。

Every guitar and bass note is a recording of a real instrument: a steel-string acoustic, a classic guitar with nylon strings, a solid-body electric on its bridge pickup, and a four-string bass.

每一个吉他和贝斯音符都是真实乐器的录音：钢弦原声吉他、尼龙弦古典吉他、使用琴桥拾音器的实心电吉他，以及四弦贝斯。

Each string sounds as your finger or mouse crosses it. Swipe down or up, fast or slow, and you hear the difference, down to a slow roll across the strings.

每根弦在你手指或鼠标经过时发声。向下或向上滑动，快或慢，你都能听到差异，甚至包括缓慢滚过琴弦的效果。

Chords use the shapes guitarists play, with open strings, barres and muted strings, so a G sounds like a G on a guitar, not on a keyboard.

和弦使用吉他手实际演奏的指型，包括空弦、横按和制音弦，所以 G 和弦听起来像吉他上的 G，而不是键盘上的。

Chucks, palm mutes, hammer-ons, pull-offs, bends and vibrato, while the strings vibrate on screen as they ring.

闷音、手掌制音、锤音、勾弦、弯音和颤音，同时琴弦在屏幕上随着余音振动。

Use cases

使用场景

Strum real chords and play whole songs with your computer keyboard or your fingers, with a backing band behind you. On a train, at the office or on the sofa, a laptop or a phone and headphones are enough.

用电脑键盘或手指扫出真实和弦，弹奏整首歌曲，身后有伴奏乐队。在火车上、办公室或沙发上，一台笔记本电脑或一部手机加耳机就足够了。

Songs like Wonderwall, Hallelujah and Let It Be come set up with the right key, capo, tempo and the strum from the record. Lead the chords from the keys, or pick up your own guitar and play along at any speed.

像《Wonderwall》、《Hallelujah》和《Let It Be》这样的歌曲已经设置好了正确的调、变调夹、速度和唱片中的扫弦方式。你可以从键盘上引导和弦，或者拿起自己的吉他以任何速度跟着弹。

Slow a song's strum down, watch which way the pick moves on every stroke, and copy it on your own guitar.

放慢歌曲的扫弦，观察每次扫弦时拨片移动的方向，并在你自己的吉他上模仿。
