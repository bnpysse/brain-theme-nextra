Is Carbon open source?
Carbon是开源的吗？

Yes. The Community edition â the core ERP, MRP, MES and QMS â is on GitHub under AGPL-3.0 and free to self-host. A private fork is fine under AGPL-3.0. You need a commercial license to use Enterprise features, or to keep your changes private from the people who use your modified version (AGPL-3.0 requires you to offer them the source). Either way, every line is in the public repository, so you can audit it before you deploy.
是的。社区版——包含核心ERP、MRP、MES和QMS功能——在GitHub上以AGPL-3.0协议开源，可免费自主托管。根据AGPL-3.0允许创建私有分支。如需使用企业功能，或对修改版本的用户保密您的改动（AGPL-3.0要求您向用户提供源码），则需要商业许可。无论哪种方式，所有代码都在公开仓库中，您可以在部署前进行审计。
