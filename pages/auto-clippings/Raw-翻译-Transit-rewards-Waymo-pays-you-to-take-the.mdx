【文章标题】：Transit rewards (Waymo pays you to take the train)
交通奖励计划（Waymo付费请你乘火车）

【文章正文】：
Introducing transit rewards
Waymo今日激动宣布推出交通奖励计划

Today, Waymo is excited to announce our transit rewards program, which we will initially offer in the San Francisco Bay Area, with future cities to follow. 
Waymo今日激动宣布推出交通奖励计划，首阶段将在旧金山湾区试行，后续将扩展至更多城市。

This first-of-its-kind initiative rewards riders with Waymo Cash when they connect their Waymo rides with public transit and use their Visa card. 
这项首创举措将奖励那些使用Visa卡将Waymo行程与公共交通接驳的乘客Waymo现金。

We’ll start with employee access before gradually rolling out to the public in the coming weeks.
我们将从员工内部测试开始，未来数周逐步向公众开放。

Public transit networks and shared autonomous vehicles are part of a complementary mobility ecosystem. 
公共交通网络与共享自动驾驶汽车共同构成了互补的出行生态系统。

As transit agencies modernize their payment systems to accept tap-to-pay debit and credit cards, multimodal trips are becoming more convenient than ever.
随着交通机构支付系统升级支持拍卡支付，多模式出行正变得前所未有的便捷。

"Governor Newsom has challenged all of us to build a transportation system that meets Californians where they are, and that means making the trip to the station as effortless as the train ride itself," said California Transportation Secretary Toks Omishakin. 
加州交通部长Toks Omishakin表示："纽森州长要求我们打造真正服务加州人的交通系统，这意味着要让前往车站的过程像乘坐列车本身一样轻松。"

"CalSTA welcomes innovations that reward people for choosing transit and make a multimodal trip feel like one seamless journey."
"加州交通局欢迎那些奖励民众选择公共交通、让多模式出行如同无缝旅程的创新方案。"

Recent surveys show that over 50% of Waymo riders in our most mature service areas (San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Phoenix) use public transit, so we’re proud to invest directly in this connectivity to make everyday commutes easier and more affordable. 
最新调查显示，在Waymo服务最成熟的旧金山、洛杉矶和凤凰城，超50%乘客会使用公共交通。我们很自豪能直接投资这种接驳服务，让日常通勤更轻松经济。

The transit rewards program works alongside the regional transfer折扣 programs and tap-to-pay model launched in the San Francisco Bay Area last December. 
该计划将与去年12月在旧金山湾区推出的区域换乘折扣计划和拍卡支付模式协同运作。

The program applies to all 27 San Francisco Bay Area transit agencies that accept contactless Visa tap-to-pay.
适用于湾区所有27家接受Visa非接触式支付的交通机构。

"San José has always believed innovation should make everyday life work better," said San José Mayor Matt Mahan. 
圣何塞市长Matt Mahan表示："我们始终相信创新应该改善日常生活。"

"This partnership does exactly that by using new technology to strengthen public transit, not replace it, making regional travel more seamless and giving people more ways to get where they need to go."
"这次合作正是通过新技术强化而非取代公共交通，使区域出行更无缝，为民众提供更多出行选择。"

To participate, riders will link their Visa card in the Waymo app. 
乘客只需在Waymo应用中绑定Visa卡即可参与。

When riders in the SF Bay Area use their Visa card to take a Waymo trip and public transit within 2 hours of each other, we will automatically issue $2.85 in Waymo Cash, which is the cost of a bus fare in San Francisco.
当湾区乘客在2小时内先后使用Visa卡支付Waymo行程和公共交通时，系统将自动发放2.85美元Waymo现金（相当于旧金山单程公交票价）。

Alongside the launch of our transit rewards program, we’re also announcing a partnership with Caltrain, which accepts tap-to-pay. 
同步宣布与支持拍卡支付的Caltrain建立合作。

Waymo will lease 40 dedicated Caltrain station parking spaces to better serve transit riders with staged vehicles. 
Waymo将租用40个Caltrain车站专属停车位，通过预备车辆更好服务换乘乘客。

This is a model we hope to replicate with additional transit agencies, ultimately enabling more seamless connections between Waymo’s service and public transit.
我们希望将该模式复制到更多交通机构，最终实现Waymo服务与公共交通的无缝衔接。

This launch is the next step toward making our vision of shared mobility a reality. 
这是实现共享出行愿景的重要一步。

By taking the learnings from our transit credit pilot programs in San Francisco and Los Angeles, adding Waymo’s service to the LA Metro Mobility Wallet program, and kicking off a strategic microtransit partnership with Via in Chandler, AZ, we’ve been steadily learning what it takes to offer a successful transit integration product and are pleased to formally launch transit rewards.
通过旧金山和洛杉矶的交通积分试点经验，参与洛杉矶地铁移动钱包计划，以及在亚利桑那州钱德勒市与Via展开战略微出行合作，我们持续积累成功交通整合产品的经验，现正式推出交通奖励计划。

"Shared mobility is a team effort, and our autonomous ride-hailing service helps expand clean transportation options for everyone," said Adam Lenz, Head of Sustainability & Environment at Waymo. 
Waymo可持续发展与环境负责人Adam Lenz表示："共享出行需要多方协作，我们的自动驾驶服务正为所有人拓展清洁出行选择。"

"We’re thrilled to partner with local transit agencies to fill multimodal gaps and make daily commutes easier through our new transit rewards program."
"我们很高兴与地方交通机构合作，通过新推出的交通奖励计划填补多模式出行空白，简化日常通勤。"

Our goal is to expand this transit rewards program and transit agency partnerships to more cities over time, ultimately making shared, all-electric transportation more accessible and affordable for everyone.
我们的目标是逐步将该计划和交通机构合作扩展至更多城市，最终让所有人都能更便捷、更经济地使用共享纯电交通服务。
