【文章正文】：
Alibaba open-sources medical AI model that can detect cancer and nearly 150 conditions
阿里巴巴开源医疗AI模型，可检测癌症及近150种病症

Tested on nearly 40,000 real-world exams, the model outperformed most radiologists, according to a new study published in Science
根据《科学》杂志发表的最新研究，该模型在近4万次真实检查中的表现优于大多数放射科医生

Alibaba Group Holding’s research arm, Damo Academy, has open-sourced an artificial intelligence model capable of identifying nearly 150 abdominal conditions – including cancers – by reading computed tomography (CT) scans, marking the latest step in the firm’s growing medical AI efforts.
阿里巴巴集团旗下达摩院开源了一款人工智能模型，该模型可通过读取计算机断层扫描（CT）图像识别近150种腹部病症（包括癌症），标志着该公司在医疗AI领域的最新进展。

The vision-language model, called Damo Radar, was designed to analyse contrast-enhanced CT scans covering 18 abdominal organs and identify a broad range of diseases and other abnormalities, such as malignant tumours, the institute said on Friday.
该机构周五表示，这款名为"达摩雷达"的视觉语言模型专为分析覆盖18个腹部器官的增强CT扫描而设计，可识别包括恶性肿瘤在内的多种疾病及其他异常情况。

The model was trained using CT scans paired with clinical reports. In nearly 40,000 real-world examinations, it achieved an average area under the curve (AUC) of 0.913 across 146 clinical findings. An AUC of 1.0 represents perfect diagnostic accuracy.
该模型通过CT扫描与临床报告配对数据进行训练。在近4万次实际检查中，针对146项临床指征的平均曲线下面积（AUC）达到0.913（AUC值为1.0表示诊断完全准确）。

The research team said the training method could eventually be extended to other types of medical imaging, calling the model "the world’s first expert-level generalist medical imaging model".
研究团队表示该训练方法最终可扩展至其他医学影像领域，并称该模型是"全球首个专家级通用医学影像模型"。
