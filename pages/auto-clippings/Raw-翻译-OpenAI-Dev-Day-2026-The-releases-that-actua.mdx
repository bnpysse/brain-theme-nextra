【文章标题】：OpenAI Dev Day 2026: The releases that actually matter

【文章标题】：OpenAI开发者日2026：真正重要的发布

【文章正文】：

I spent the day at OpenAI’s DevDay in San Francisco, and I have good news and bad news: OpenAI released a lot of stuff.

我在旧金山参加了OpenAI的开发者日活动，有个好消息和坏消息要分享：OpenAI这次发布了一大堆东西。

In this episode, I break down the announcements worth paying attention to - and show you what happened when I tested some of them early. We’ll meet my Dot, explore why Spaces and Sites could matter for how teams work, and get into the model and API updates I’m most excited about as a developer.

本期内容我将解析那些值得关注的发布——并展示我提前测试部分功能的实况。你会认识我的Dot助手，了解Spaces和Sites如何改变团队协作模式，以及我作为开发者最期待的模型与API更新。

I use the Decisions API to find podcast thumbnails where nobody looks awkward, build a collaborative sketchpad with Astra ultrafast, and let my kids redesign a 3D world in real time. That last experiment cost about $97. My wallet has thoughts.

我用Decisions API筛选出人物表情自然的播客缩略图，通过Astra ultrafast搭建协同画板，还让孩子们实时改造3D世界——最后这个实验花了97美元，我的钱包表示有话要说。

These are my early impressions: what’s promising, what still feels rough, and what I think you should try first.

以下是初期体验：哪些功能潜力十足，哪些尚待完善，以及我建议优先尝试的内容。
