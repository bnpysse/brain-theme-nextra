Which Countries Are Best at Math?
哪些国家数学最强？

Key Takeaways
核心发现

China ranks first in mathematics with an average PISA score of 612, nearly 150 points above the OECD average.
中国以612分的PISA平均分位居数学榜首，比OECD平均水平高出近150分。

Asian education systems occupy seven of the top 10 positions, with Singapore, Macao, and Taiwan all ranking in the top four.
亚洲教育体系包揽前十名中的七席，新加坡、澳门和台湾均位列前四。

Canada ranks 11th globally, while the United States ranks 27th and matches the OECD average.
加拿大全球排名第11位，美国排名第27位，与OECD平均水平持平。

PISA assesses students in mathematics, reading, and science. Its mathematics assessment goes beyond memorizing formulas or procedures, measuring whether students can apply mathematical knowledge and reasoning to real-world problems.
PISA评估学生的数学、阅读和科学能力。其数学评估不仅测试公式记忆，更注重考察学生运用数学知识解决实际问题的能力。

More than 760,000 students took part in PISA 2025 across 91 countries and economies, representing roughly 33 million 15-year-olds worldwide.
来自91个国家/地区的76万余名学生参与2025年PISA测试，代表全球约3300万15岁青少年。

China in this ranking represents the participating jurisdictions of Beijing, Shanghai, Jiangsu, and Zhejiang. Uzbekistan is excluded because a comparable mathematics score was not reported.
本排名中"中国"指参与测试的北京、上海、江苏和浙江地区。乌兹别克斯坦因未报告可比数学成绩被排除。

Japan ranks fifth with 525 points, while Korea and Hong Kong are tied at 522. Estonia breaks up the concentration of East Asian education systems at the top, ranking eighth globally with a score of 508 and placing highest among European countries.
日本以525分排名第五，韩国和香港同获522分。爱沙尼亚以508分打破东亚教育体系垄断局面，位列全球第八，在欧洲国家中排名最高。

Where the U.S. and Canada Rank
美国与加拿大排名情况

The United States ranks 27th with a score of 463, exactly matching the OECD average.
美国以463分排名第27位，与OECD平均水平完全一致。

Canada ranks 11th overall with an average mathematics score of 485, putting it 22 points above the OECD average. It also places ahead of several major European economies, including Germany, France, Spain, and Italy.
加拿大以485分的数学均分位列第11名，超出OECD平均22分，领先于德国、法国、西班牙和意大利等欧洲主要经济体。

The United Kingdom ranks 10th with a score of 488. Australia comes in 17th at 478, while New Zealand ties for 14th at 480.
英国以488分排名第10位。澳大利亚478分位列第17，新西兰480分并列第14名。

Math Scores Have Fallen Across the OECD
OECD国家数学成绩普遍下滑

Across OECD countries, average mathematics scores fell by 22 points between 2015 and 2025, equivalent to just over one year of learning. Declines were also recorded among non-OECD participants.
2015至2025年间OECD国家数学均分下降22分（相当于一年多学习量），非OECD参与者也出现下滑。

PISA also found an association between heavy digital leisure use and student outcomes. Students spending more than four hours a day on digital leisure activities outside school tended to have lower mathematics scores and a weaker sense of belonging.
PISA发现过度数字娱乐与学生表现存在关联：每日课外数字娱乐超4小时的学生数学成绩更低、归属感更弱。
