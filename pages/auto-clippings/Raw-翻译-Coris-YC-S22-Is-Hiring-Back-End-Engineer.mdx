🧠 About Coris

Coris is the AI platform for risk and financial operations. Platforms, marketplaces, payment providers, and banks - GoFundMe, Clio, Cherry, and a growing list - use Coris to onboard merchants faster, monitor them continuously, and act on fraud without adding headcount. One customer described us as "Cursor + Lovable for risk teams."

We're backed by Y Combinator, Lux Capital, and Pathlight, founded by payments-domain experts, and growing quickly on customer pull. We're a small team; this hire owns a large share of the backend.

🧠 关于 Coris

Coris 是面向风险与金融运营的 AI 平台。各类平台、市场、支付服务商和银行——包括 GoFundMe、Clio、Cherry 以及越来越多的客户——都在使用 Coris 来更快地接入商户、持续监控风险，并在不增加人手的情况下应对欺诈。有客户将我们形容为“风险团队的 Cursor + Lovable”。

我们获得了 Y Combinator、Lux Capital 和 Pathlight 的投资，由支付领域专家创立，并凭借客户口碑快速成长。我们是一个小团队；这个岗位将负责后端的大部分工作。

👥 Who you'll work with

Coris was started by Vinodh (CEO) and Shyam (CTO). We've each spent 20+ years in fraud and risk management, running risk engineering, operations and product teams at Google, eBay, PayPal and WePay. You'll work directly with both of us in Palo Alto.

🚀 Why this role matters

Every Coris decision runs on data we pull from payment processors and platforms: Stripe, Adyen, Fiserv, TSYS, Repay, and more, over APIs, webhooks, and SFTP file feeds - hundreds of millions of events a month, billions of rows in Postgres. On top of that sits a signals and rules engine, an account graph, and the partner-facing API our customers build on.

Here's the honest version of the work. Files arrive out of order over SFTP. One processor sends dates in UTC, another in the merchant's local time, and neither says which. A processor's decline code lives in a different column than the spec says. A webhook ships malformed JSON at 2am. A backfill worker runs out of memory on a 40B-row table. A customer's risk lead says "our numbers look off" and needs an answer today.

We're hiring the engineer who will own all of that end-to-end, and who thinks it's the interesting part - because every integration we make reliably is one a competitor hasn't, and that's the moat. Getting the boring work right every time is how enduring companies are built!

🚀 为什么这个岗位很重要

Coris 的每一个决策都基于我们从支付处理商和平台获取的数据：Stripe、Adyen、Fiserv、TSYS、Repay 等，通过 API、webhook 和 SFTP 文件推送——每月数亿个事件，Postgres 中数十亿行数据。在这些数据之上，运行着信号与规则引擎、账户关系图谱，以及客户所依赖的面向合作伙伴的 API。

以下是这份工作最真实的一面。文件通过 SFTP 到达时顺序错乱。一家处理商以 UTC 发送日期，另一家以商户本地时间发送，而且都没有标明。某家处理商的拒付代码所在的列与规范不符。凌晨 2 点，一个 webhook 推送了格式错误的 JSON。一个回填 worker 在 400 亿行的表上内存溢出。客户的風控负责人说“我们的数字看起来不对”，而且今天就要答案。

我们要招聘的工程师，将端到端地负责所有这些工作，并且认为这才是最有意思的部分——因为我们每可靠地完成一个集成，就意味着竞争对手还没有做到，而这就是护城河。把枯燥的事情每次都做对，正是伟大公司得以长久的原因！

🥷 What you'll do

- Own processor and platform integrations. Build and operate ingestion from Stripe, Adyen, Fiserv, TSYS, Repay, and new processors as we sign them: APIs, webhooks, and batch file feeds. Make them correct, idempotent, observable, and PCI-safe.

- Own the partner API and customer-specific work. Extend the v2 API, build per-customer configuration and signals, and work directly with customers' risk and ops teams to turn a vague report into a fix, fast.

- Build the risk engine. Add signals and rules to the DAG engine that scores merchants and transactions; design Postgres schemas, indexes, partitions, and aggregations that stay fast at billions of rows.

- Run it in prod. Celery workers, backfills, deadlocks, circuit breakers, latency, Sentry monitoring. You ship it, you own it.

- Use AI everywhere it earns its place. Coding agents for first drafts and refactors, AI review on every PR, and LLMs inside the product (dispute agents, document parsing, structured extraction). You'll also help set the team's standards for how we use these tools.

- ~10–20% product-facing TypeScript/Next.js when a feature needs it.

🥷 你将做什么

- 负责处理商和平台集成。构建并运营来自 Stripe、Adyen、Fiserv、TSYS、Repay 以及我们签约的新处理商的数据接入：API、webhook 和批量文件推送。确保其正确性、幂等性、可观测性，并符合 PCI 安全标准。

- 负责合作伙伴 API 和客户定制化工作。扩展 v2 API，构建针对每个客户的配置和信号，并直接与客户的風控和运营团队合作，将模糊的报告快速转化为修复方案。

- 构建风险引擎。为对商户和交易进行评分的 DAG 引擎添加信号和规则；设计在数十亿行数据下依然保持高性能的 Postgres 表结构、索引、分区和聚合。

- 负责生产环境运维。Celery worker、回填、死锁、熔断器、延迟、Sentry 监控。你发布的代码，由你负责到底。

- 在 AI 能发挥价值的地方全面使用 AI。使用编码代理完成初稿和重构，对每个 PR 进行 AI 审查，并在产品中集成 LLM（争议处理代理、文档解析、结构化提取）。你还将帮助团队制定使用这些工具的标准。

- 约 10–20% 的时间用于面向产品的 TypeScript/Next.js 开发，当某个功能需要时。
