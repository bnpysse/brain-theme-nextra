【文章标题】：Attimet (YC F24) Is Hiring Members of Technical Staff – Engineering and Research  
【文章标题】：Attimet（YC F24）正在招聘技术团队成员——工程与研究方向  

【文章正文】：  
Why you should join us  
为什么你应该加入我们  

- We’re building a research lab with a real-time feedback loop.  
- 我们正在建立一个具有实时反馈机制的研究实验室。  

- We’re building AI systems that expand how quickly a small research team can understand, build, and experiment.  
- 我们正在构建AI系统，以提升小型研究团队的理解、构建和实验速度。  

- Our edge is the speed at which we can form ideas, build systems, run experiments, and learn from reality.  
- 我们的优势在于快速形成想法、构建系统、运行实验并从现实中学习。  

- You’ll work directly with the founders and have real ownership over what we build and how we build it.  
- 你将直接与创始人合作，并对我们构建的内容和方式拥有真正的自主权。  

- We’re a small team with over a decade of experience at places like Optiver, DRW, and Argo AI.  
- 我们是一支小型团队，在Optiver、DRW和Argo AI等公司拥有超过十年的经验。  

What you’ll do  
你将负责的工作  

- Build LLM-powered systems and agent harnesses that help us research, engineer, and operate faster.  
- 构建基于LLM的系统和智能体工具，帮助我们更快地研究、开发和运营。  

- Design the infrastructure around them: tools, context, memory, evals, orchestration, observability, and execution environments.  
- 设计相关基础设施：工具、上下文、记忆、评估、编排、可观测性和执行环境。  

- Find new ways to turn frontier models into reliable systems that do useful work.  
- 探索新方法，将前沿模型转化为能够完成有用工作的可靠系统。  

- Work across the stack when needed. At our size, the problem matters more than the job boundary.  
- 必要时跨技术栈工作。在我们这样的规模下，问题本身比职责边界更重要。  

- Own projects end-to-end: identify opportunities, prototype quickly, ship, measure what works, and iterate.  
- 端到端负责项目：发现机会、快速原型开发、交付、评估效果并迭代优化。  

What we’re looking for  
我们期待的人选  

- You have unusually high initiative: you notice important problems, figure out what should exist, and make it happen without waiting to be told.  
- 你具备超强的主动性：能发现重要问题，明确解决方案，并主动推动实现。  

- You’re technically sharp and learn extremely quickly.  
- 你技术敏锐且学习能力极强。  

- You’ve built seriously with LLMs, agents, harnesses, or adjacent AI infrastructure.  
- 你有扎实的LLM、智能体、工具或相关AI基础设施的构建经验。  

- You move quickly, reason from first principles, and enjoy problems where the right architecture has not been figured out yet.  
- 你行动迅速，善于从第一性原理思考，并乐于解决尚未确定正确架构的问题。  

- You care more about what works in practice than following established patterns.  
- 你更关注实际效果而非遵循既定模式。  

- Curiosity, intensity, and technical judgment matter much more to us than credentials or years of experience.  
- 对我们而言，好奇心、专注力和技术判断力远比学历或经验年限重要。  

Your first 30 days  
入职前30天  

- First week: learn how we work, find something important that could be dramatically better, and start making it better.  
- 第一周：了解我们的工作方式，发现可以显著改进的重要事项，并着手优化。  

- By 15 days: take ownership of a meaningful technical problem end-to-end, including deciding what to build and how to measure whether it works.  
- 15天内：端到端负责一个有价值的技术问题，包括决定构建内容和评估标准。  

- By 30 days: we want you independently identifying what matters and driving projects that materially increase what the team can do.  
- 30天内：我们希望你能独立识别关键问题，并推动实质性提升团队能力的项目。  

You’ll have the autonomy and resources to do some of the best work of your life.  
你将获得自主权和资源，完成职业生涯中最出色的工作之一。
