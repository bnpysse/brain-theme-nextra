【文章标题】：China Responds to Starlink

【文章标题】：中国如何应对星链

【文章正文】：

Paddy Stephens

帕迪·斯蒂芬斯

writes about space, security, and energy for

为《The Wire China》、《Resilience Media》等媒体撰写太空、安全与能源领域的报道

The Wire China, Resilience Media and others. He’s based between London, Nice and Taipei. This is Part 1 of his two-part investigation of China’s Starlink challengers!

常驻伦敦、尼斯和台北三地。本文是其关于中国"星链挑战者"系列调查的上篇。

The test lacked the cool confidence of the Falcon 9, SpaceX’s dependable workhorse rocket that slows itself down before landing gently on four legs.

这次试验缺少猎鹰9号那种从容自信——SpaceX这款主力火箭能在着陆前自主减速，最终由四条支架稳稳接住。

Nor was it as elegant as SpaceX’s super-heavy Starship rocket booster, which the company has managed to catch on three occasions with two "chopstick" arms.

也不如SpaceX超重型星舰助推器优雅——该公司已三次用机械臂"筷子"成功接住它。

But as the first stage of the Long March 10B slowed itself into a netted metal box on a sea barge in July this year, the state-owned China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation (CASC) joined SpaceX and Bezos’ Blue Origin to become the third entity in the world to have successfully recovered a rocket booster.

但今年7月长征10B一级火箭自主减速落入海上驳船的网状金属箱时，国有航天巨头中国航天科技集团(CASC)就此成为继SpaceX、贝索斯蓝色起源之后，全球第三个实现火箭助推器回收的机构。

Long March 10B first stage is collected on a sea barge. Credit: Xinhua.

长征10B一级火箭在海上驳船完成回收。图片来源：新华社

Three soon became four. On its first attempt in December, the booster of the Zhuque-3, a reusable model developed by LandSpace, one of China’s leading commercial火箭公司, ended up as a fireball that smashed spectacularly into the Gobi Desert.

这个数字很快变成四。去年12月朱雀三号（中国头部商业火箭公司蓝箭航天研发的可复用型号）助推器首试时，化作火球壮观坠毁于戈壁滩。

Since then, its engineers have clearly been busy. On 18th August, it touched down softly on its extended legs, in size and landing style much like the Falcon 9.

此后工程师们显然没闲着。8月18日，该助推器伸展支架平稳着陆，尺寸与着陆姿态酷似猎鹰9号。

The two tests don’t mean China has cracked rocket reusability — it’ll take multiple successful launches of each of the models to prove their reliability.

两次试验不意味着中国已攻克火箭复用技术——各型号还需多次成功发射验证可靠性。

Then it will take more time still to actualize the falling costs and increased launch capacity that reusability has brought SpaceX.

而要像SpaceX那样实现复用技术带来的成本下降与发射能力提升，仍需更长时间。

Blaine Curcio, the founder of Orbital Gateway Consulting and an authority on China’s space industry, told me that the Long March 10B test is "a meaningful step forward, but the short term impact [on launch capacity] is not huge".

轨道门户咨询创始人、中国航天业权威布莱恩·柯西奥告诉我，长征10B试验是"重要进步，但（对发射能力的）短期影响有限"。

Nevertheless, with these two tests and other commercial Chinese rocket companies due to test reusable models later this year, the US monopoly on reusable rockets is coming to an end.

不过随着这两次试验及中国其他商业火箭公司计划年内测试可复用型号，美国对可复用火箭的垄断正走向终结。

These two tests are particularly important for China’s long-term ambitions to build its own equivalents to Starlink, which will require the kind of launch capacity that, as of right now, only SpaceX has.

这两次试验对中国建设对标星链的星座计划至关重要——该计划需要当前唯有SpaceX具备的发射能力。

Starlink’s more than 10,0000 satellites account for 70% of the total satellites in low Earth orbit (LEO). China launched 371 satellites in 2025; SpaceX managed 3,169.

星链逾1万颗卫星占低地球轨道卫星总数70%。中国2025年发射371颗卫星，SpaceX则达3169颗。

One of SpaceX’s Falcon 9 rockets landing in 2021. Image: SpaceX.

2021年SpaceX猎鹰9号火箭着陆瞬间。图片来源：SpaceX

How China views Starlink

中国如何看待星链

SpaceX’s Starlink, the world’s largest low Earth orbit satellite constellation, started operational launches in 2019 and conducted a beta launch from the US in 2020.

SpaceX星链作为全球最大近地轨道星座，2019年启动业务发射，2020年在美国进行测试发射。

By the end of 2022, the company had over 3,300 working satellites in LEO — half of the global total.

至2022年底，其在轨工作卫星超3300颗，占全球总数一半。

As Ukraine demonstrated the tactical utility of Starlink, SpaceX’s orbital dominance started to worry decisionmakers in Beijing.

随着乌克兰战场展现星链战术价值，SpaceX的轨道优势开始引发北京决策层忧虑。

The People’s Liberation Army (PLA) was concerned that Starlink gave the US and its partners an edge, with obvious benefits on the battlefield.

解放军认为星链赋予美国及其盟友战场优势。

In May 2022, PLA Daily wrote of the constellation:

2022年5月《解放军报》评价该星座：

"[Starlink] will provide high-bandwidth, high-speed military communications worldwide, establishing a robust command-and-control network linking U.S. combat platforms."

"将为全球美军作战平台提供高带宽、高速率通信，构建强韧指控网络"。

Even in the first year of Russia’s full-scale invasion, it was clear how useful this capability could be on the front line.

俄乌冲突首年便清晰展现了该能力的前线价值。

As researchers affiliated with the PLA and state space apparatus noted in a piece republished in Security Insider 安全内参, a cybersecurity think tank publication, in January 2023, Starlink had already enabled Ukraine to maintain communication despite disruption, support military coordination and send intelligence from civilian mobile phones via military intelligence to combat units.

2023年1月，解放军及国家航天机构研究人员在智库刊物《安全内参》转载文章中指出，星链已助乌军在通信中断时保持联络、支撑军事协同，并能通过军情系统将民用手机情报传至作战单元。

It had also supported drone operations.

还支撑了无人机作战。

Analysts from Beijing Institute of Tracking and Telecommunications Technology 北京跟踪与通信技术研究所 (BITTT), a PLA Aerospace Force research institute, concluded that with its thousands of satellites, "this mega-constellation … poses a major challenge to our existing situational awareness and traditional defense capabilities."

解放军航天系统部下属北京跟踪与通信技术研究所(BITTT)分析认为，这个"由数千卫星组成的巨型星座…对我国现有态势感知与传统防御能力构成重大挑战"。

China had no clear way to disable the rapidly growing megaconstellation either.

中国亦无明确手段瘫痪这个快速扩张的巨型星座。

As PLA Daily highlighted, the system offers low latency and is difficult to jam.

《解放军报》强调，该系统低延迟且抗干扰性强。

With thousands of satellites, it has plenty of redundancy to keep providing coverage even if China ever managed to take out tens or even hundreds of its satellites by kinetic means.

凭借数千卫星的冗余设计，即便中国动能打击摧毁其数十甚至上百颗卫星，系统仍可维持覆盖。

And it is able to rapidly respond to cyber or electronic warfare, as the Security Insider paper noted.

《安全内参》文章指出，该系统还能快速应对网络或电子战攻击。

Other worries were more far-fetched, such as that Starlink could be used to threaten and attack China’s own satellites, deepening US dominance in orbit.

其他担忧则更牵强，例如星链可能被用于威胁攻击中国卫星，强化美国轨道霸权。

At the end of February 2022, PLA Daily warned that Starlink could be deployed to disable or destroy other countries’ satellites.

2022年2月底《解放军报》警告，星链可能被部署用于瘫痪或摧毁他国卫星。

The BITTT analysts wrote that Starlink satellites are highly maneuverable and could be weaponized.

BITTT分析人员撰文称，星链卫星具备高机动性，可能被武器化。
