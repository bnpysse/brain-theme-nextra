How AI Companies Are Reshaping Markets in 2026

Despite wars, energy shocks, and geopolitical uncertainty, AI has become the biggest force shaping markets in 2026. But as some AI companies thrive while other parts of the market face disruption, AI’s impact has been far from uniform.

Stock returns are under pressure

Strong valuations of AI companies and relative high real rates have reduced investors’ reward for taking on stock market risk, a term known as the equity risk premium.

Data from March 31, 1984, to March 31, 2026. MSCI’s forward ERP reflects the expected return premium needed to reconcile market prices with macro fundamentals. A low premium corresponds to high market prices relative to future cash flows and suggests both lower long-term returns and a higher risk of a near-term market correction. Traditional valuation measures like the price-to-earnings ratio also indicate that valuations are high, but accounting for changing growth expectations and higher real rates significantly amplifies the signal. For more details, refer to The MSCI Macro Finance Model.

Importantly, the equity risk premium is at a low last seen during the dot-com era, making stocks more vulnerable to a correction.

A balancing act for markets

Strong earnings from AI companies have supported stock prices so far, but high valuations and real rates leave little room for disappointment. If earnings growth falls short of expectations, stock prices could come under pressure.
