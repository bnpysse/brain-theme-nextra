【文章标题】：'That's so AI ' What gen Alpha's biggest insult tells us
【文章标题】：“太AI了”——Z世代最狠侮辱语的启示

【文章正文】：
Name: “That’s AI!”
名称："太AI了！"
Age: Brand new.
年龄：新鲜出炉
Appearance: Primary schools everywhere.
出没地：各地小学

What’s AI? It’s just a thing the kids are saying now: “That’s AI!” or “That’s so AI!”
什么是"太AI了"？这只是现在孩子们的流行语："太AI了！"
But what is? Something inauthentic, unbelievable or rubbish.
具体指什么？指那些虚假、不可信或垃圾的事物
Which was made by AI? No. Artificial intelligence has very little to do with this.
是指AI生成的东西吗？不，人工智能与此几乎无关

But isn’t artificial intelligence poised to destroy humankind? Very possibly, but that doesn’t concern us right now.
但人工智能不是要毁灭人类吗？很有可能，但这不是我们现在讨论的重点
It doesn’t? No. The kids – gen Alpha – have noticed that certain qualities of AI-generated slop, such as the way it’s superficially convincing but ultimately cheap and of dubious value, can be found in lots of other things: knock-off merchandise, exaggerated claims, excuses your parents make. If something is considered somehow suspect, then it is also, by definition, AI.
不是重点？对。Z世代孩子们发现，AI产出的垃圾具有某些特质——表面看似可信实则廉价可疑——这些特质也存在于许多其他事物中：山寨商品、夸大宣传、父母编造的借口。任何可疑之物，按定义都算"太AI了"

Is “That’s AI” the new “six-seven”? No, this bit of slang means something: the young people have simply broadened the definition of AI to make it equivalent to “bullshit”.
这是新一代黑话吗？不，这个俚语有意义：年轻人扩展了AI的定义，使其等同于"胡扯"
So it’s always deployed as an insult? Always.
所以总是用作侮辱？总是

But aren’t things more complicated than that? Couldn’t AI also be our saviour, as long as good judgment is used and the right guardrails are in place? While the rest of us are having that debate, gen Alpha has already made up its mind: AI is so AI.
但现实不是更复杂吗？若有正确判断和防护措施，AI不能成为救星吗？当大人们还在争论时，Z世代早已认定：AI本身就很AI

What will happen when AI finds out it’s AI? Will it get trapped in a logical feedback loop until all the datacentres start exploding? You’re not a very scientific person, are you?
当AI发现自己很AI会怎样？会陷入逻辑死循环直到所有数据中心爆炸吗？你科学素养不太行啊

Anyway, didn’t Donald Trump just change the name of artificial intelligence to “super intelligence”, or SI? SI is so AI. And Donald Trump is AI.
话说特朗普不是刚把AI改名叫"超级智能"(SI)吗？SI太AI了。特朗普本人也很AI
Not for the first time this year, I feel like dumping all my tech stocks. Tech stocks are AI.
今年第N次想抛售所有科技股。科技股太AI了

What about driverless taxis? Driverless taxis are AI, especially the ones that have humans in the driver’s seat.
无人驾驶出租车呢？无人车很AI，特别是那些驾驶座有人的

My job in marketing was certainly AI, which is probably why AI took it. I’m sorry.
我之前的营销工作确实很AI，所以被AI取代也合理。抱歉

Do say: “That’s so AI!”
请说："太AI了！"
Don’t say: “No, that’s Al – Al Gordon. He’s in my year, and he’s actually OK. But his shoes are AI.”
别说："不，那是Al——Al Gordon。他是我同学，人还行。但他的鞋子很AI"
