关于本摘要

KFC is ending its decadeslong relationship with PepsiCo in the U.S. and will switch to Coca-Cola products starting in January 2027.

肯德基将终止其在美国与百事公司长达数十年的合作关系，并从2027年1月起改用可口可乐产品。

PepsiCo shares fell 0.7% and Coca-Cola gained 0.2% on Thursday, reflecting a subdued stock market response to the announcement.

周四，百事公司股价下跌0.7%，可口可乐上涨0.2%，反映出股市对这一公告反应平淡。

The loss of KFC’s U.S. business comes as PepsiCo struggles to revive North American beverage demand, where organic revenue growth recently stalled.

在失去肯德基美国业务之际，百事公司正努力重振北美饮料需求，而该地区的有机收入增长近期已陷入停滞。

KFC is ending its decadeslong relationship with PepsiCo in the U.S. and switching to Coca-Cola. The deal marks a notable victory in the long-running cola wars, but the stock market’s response was subdued.

肯德基将终止其在美国与百事公司长达数十年的合作关系，转而改用可口可乐。这笔交易标志着长期“可乐战争”中的一次显著胜利，但股市反应平淡。

KFC announced Thursday that it will begin replacing PepsiCo beverages with Coca-Cola products at its roughly 3,500 U.S. restaurants in January 2027, with the transition expected to be completed by spring. The companies didn’t disclose the financial terms of the agreement.

肯德基周四宣布，将于2027年1月开始在其约3500家美国餐厅用可口可乐产品替换百事饮料，预计过渡期将在春季前完成。双方未披露协议的财务条款。

The announcement marks a historic break between two companies with deep ties. PepsiCo acquired KFC in 1986 and continued supplying its beverages after spinning off its restaurant business in 1997.

这一公告标志着两家渊源深厚的公司之间的历史性决裂。百事公司于1986年收购了肯德基，并在1997年剥离其餐饮业务后继续向其供应饮料。

Yet investors appeared largely unfazed by the change. PepsiCo shares fell 0.7% Thursday, while Coca-Cola gained just 0.2%. Yum! Brands

然而，投资者似乎对这一变化基本无动于衷。周四百事公司股价下跌0.7%，而可口可乐仅上涨0.2%。百胜餐饮集团

, KFC’s parent company, was up about 2.8%, although other major restaurant stocks also rallied Thursday.

（肯德基的母公司）上涨约2.8%，尽管其他主要餐饮股周四也出现了上涨。

One reason for the limited reaction among the beverage giants is that KFC’s business represents only a small fraction of PepsiCo’s overall sales.

饮料巨头反应有限的一个原因是，肯德基的业务仅占百事公司总销售额的一小部分。

PepsiCo doesn’t disclose how much revenue comes from supplying individual restaurant chains or the entire food service segment. But public data could provide some ballpark estimates.

百事公司并未披露其向单个餐饮连锁或整个餐饮服务领域供应饮料所获得的收入。但公开数据可以提供一些粗略估算。

KFC generated $36.4 billion in global systemwide sales in 2025, with the U.S. accounting for just 12% of the total. That translates to roughly $4.4 billion in its U.S. restaurant sales. But only a portion of that comes from beverages, and PepsiCo receives the wholesale revenue from supplying those drinks—not the prices customers pay at restaurants.

肯德基2025年全球系统销售额为364亿美元，其中美国仅占总额的12%。这意味着其美国餐厅销售额约为44亿美元。但其中只有一部分来自饮料，而百事公司获得的是供应这些饮料的批发收入，而非顾客在餐厅支付的价格。

That means the loss of a household-name customer may not translate into a meaningful hit to PepsiCo’s $28.2 billion beverage sales in North America. PepsiCo’s total annual revenue, including its North American food business and international food and beverage operations, was nearly $94 billion in 2025.

这意味着失去一个家喻户晓的客户可能不会对百事公司282亿美元的北美饮料销售额造成实质性打击。百事公司2025年的年总收入（包括北美食品业务和国际食品饮料业务）接近940亿美元。

PepsiCo will continue supplying beverages to KFC’s international operations and to Taco Bell, which is also owned by Yum! Brands, according to The Wall Street Journal.

据《华尔街日报》报道，百事公司将继续向肯德基的国际业务以及同样隶属于百胜餐饮集团的塔可钟供应饮料。

Still, the loss carries symbolic weight for PepsiCo. The company has been struggling to revive demand for its beverages in North America, where organic revenue growth stalled in the latest quarter, slowing from 1% in the previous quarter and 2% at the beginning of the year.

尽管如此，这一损失对百事公司仍具有象征意义。该公司一直在努力重振北美地区的饮料需求，而该地区最近一个季度的有机收入增长陷入停滞，从此前一季度的1%和年初的2%放缓。

“We are not competing well in soft drinks,” CEO Ramon Laguarta said during PepsiCo’s third-quarter earnings call last week.

“我们在软饮料领域竞争不力，”首席执行官拉蒙·拉瓜尔塔上周在百事公司第三季度财报电话会议上表示。

For KFC, the new partnership is also far from a guaranteed turnaround. Its U.S. system sales declined 2% in the second quarter, underscoring the challenge of winning back customers in a fiercely competitive chicken market.

对肯德基而言，新的合作关系也远非扭转局面的保证。其美国系统销售额在第二季度下降了2%，凸显出在竞争激烈的鸡肉市场中赢回顾客的挑战。

KFC said it plans to use Coca-Cola’s extensive beverage portfolio and product innovation capabilities to develop new drink offerings, create more food-and-beverage combinations, and give customers more reasons to visit.

肯德基表示，计划利用可口可乐广泛的饮料组合和产品创新能力来开发新的饮品，创造更多餐饮组合，并给顾客更多光顾的理由。

The move is part of what KFC calls its “Kentucky Fried Comeback,” an effort to revive its struggling U.S. business. The chain has been experimenting with new beverages, including iced coffees, boba drinks, and shakes, at its Open House test restaurant in McKinney, Texas.

此举是肯德基所称“肯德基回归”计划的一部分，旨在重振其陷入困境的美国业务。该连锁品牌一直在其位于得克萨斯州麦金尼的“Open House”测试餐厅尝试新饮品，包括冰咖啡、波霸饮料和奶昔。

“This partnership is about more than what’s in the cup,” said Catherine Tan-Gillespie, president of KFC U.S., in a statement.

“这一合作关系不仅仅关乎杯中之物，”肯德基美国总裁凯瑟琳·谭-吉莱斯皮在一份声明中表示。
