How Much Are Homes Worth Across the U.S. in 2026?

2026年美国各地房屋价值几何？

Key Takeaways

关键要点

A typical home in Hawaii is worth $833,000, about 4.6 times one in West Virginia ($179,000).

夏威夷的典型房屋价值83.3万美元，约为西弗吉尼亚州（17.9万美元）的4.6倍。

Eight of the 10 most expensive states are on a coast, while all 10 of the least expensive are in the South and Midwest, each with typical values below $250,000.

最昂贵的10个州中有8个位于沿海地区，而最便宜的10个州全部位于南部和中西部，每个州的典型价值都低于25万美元。

Land makes up about 60% of a home’s value in Hawaii and California, compared with less than 20% in West Virginia and Mississippi.

在夏威夷和加利福尼亚，土地约占房屋价值的60%，而在西弗吉尼亚和密西西比，这一比例不到20%。

For most American families, a home is both their biggest purchase and their largest asset. But how much that home is worth depends heavily on where it sits.

对大多数美国家庭来说，房屋既是他们最大的购买项目，也是他们最大的资产。但房屋的价值在很大程度上取决于其地理位置。

Across the U.S., typical home values range from under $200,000 in the least expensive states to more than $750,000 in the priciest markets. Geography, housing supply, and demand all help explain the differences.

在美国各地，典型房屋价值从最便宜州的不到20万美元到最昂贵市场的超过75万美元不等。地理、住房供应和需求都有助于解释这些差异。

This graphic maps the typical home value in every U.S. state as of August 2026, using the Zillow Home Value Index, which estimates what a mid-priced home is worth.

这张图表使用Zillow房屋价值指数，绘制了截至2026年8月美国各州的典型房屋价值，该指数用于估算中等价位房屋的价值。

Why Hawaii and California Have the Most Expensive Homes

为什么夏威夷和加利福尼亚的房屋最昂贵

Ten states and D.C. have typical home values above $500,000. Eight of those states are on a coast, led by Hawaii ($833,000) and California ($764,000).

有10个州和哥伦比亚特区的典型房屋价值超过50万美元。其中8个州位于沿海地区，以夏威夷（83.3万美元）和加利福尼亚（76.4万美元）为首。

Land is a major reason. In Hawaii and California, land makes up about 60% of a home’s value, compared with 40% nationally, according to FHFA estimates for 2022. Oceans, mountains, and zoning rules constrain the supply of buildable land, while strong demand pushes up the price of each lot.

土地是一个主要原因。根据FHFA对2022年的估计，在夏威夷和加利福尼亚，土地约占房屋价值的60%，而全国平均水平为40%。海洋、山脉和分区规划限制了可建设土地的供应，而强劲的需求推高了每块土地的价格。

Among other populous states, a typical home is worth $527,000 in New York, compared with $375,000 in Florida and $299,000 in Texas.

在其他人口众多的州中，纽约的典型房屋价值为52.7万美元，而佛罗里达为37.5万美元，得克萨斯为29.9万美元。
