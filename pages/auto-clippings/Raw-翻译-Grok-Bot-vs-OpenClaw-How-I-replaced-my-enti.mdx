I’m running about 30 active agents at any given moment, and in this episode I break down my full Grok Bot setup: what it is, how it compares to OpenClaw, and the nine bots I’ve built for work and my personal life.  
我随时运行着约30个活跃智能体，本期将详解我的Grok Bot体系：它的特性、与OpenClaw的对比，以及我为工作和生活打造的九款机器人。
