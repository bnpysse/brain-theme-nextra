Ranked: The Most Popular AI Tools in 2026

排名：2026年最受欢迎的AI工具

Key Takeaways

核心要点

ChatGPT drew 5.3 billion web visits in June 2026, more than the next 14 most popular AI tools combined (4.7 billion).

2026年6月，ChatGPT吸引了53亿次网页访问，超过排名其后的14个最受欢迎AI工具的总和（47亿次）。

Google's Gemini ranks second at 1.1 billion monthly visits, followed by Anthropic's Claude at 968 million.

谷歌的Gemini以每月11亿次访问量排名第二，紧随其后的是Anthropic的Claude，访问量为9.68亿次。

12 of the 15 most-visited AI tools are American. Canva (Australia), DeepSeek (China), and Magnific (Spain) are the only exceptions.

访问量最高的15个AI工具中有12个来自美国。Canva（澳大利亚）、DeepSeek（中国）和Magnific（西班牙）是仅有的例外。

Less than four years after ChatGPT's launch, AI tools have become some of the most visited destinations on the internet.

距ChatGPT发布不到四年，AI工具已成为互联网上访问量最高的一批目的地。

But while new tools launch constantly in a crowded market, web traffic remains highly concentrated among the biggest players.

然而，尽管在这个拥挤的市场中新工具不断涌现，网页流量仍高度集中在最大的几家参与者手中。

ChatGPT Pulls More Traffic Than the Next 14 Tools Combined

ChatGPT的流量超过其后14个工具的总和

ChatGPT accounts for roughly 53% of all web traffic among the 15 tools in the ranking, highlighting how far ahead it remains of its closest competitors.

ChatGPT约占排名中15个工具全部网页流量的53%，凸显了它相较于最接近的竞争对手仍保持着多大的领先优势。

The ranking is overwhelmingly American, with 12 of the 15 tools based in the United States.

该排名以美国为主导，15个工具中有12个总部位于美国。

The exceptions are Canva (760 million visits), the Australian design platform that has rebuilt itself around AI features, China's DeepSeek (319 million), and Magnific (117 million), the AI image upscaler from Spain's Freepik.

例外情况是Canva（7.6亿次访问）——这家澳大利亚设计平台已围绕AI功能进行了全面重构；中国的DeepSeek（3.19亿次）；以及Magnific（1.17亿次）——来自西班牙Freepik的AI图像增强工具。

Character chat is another notable source of traffic. JanitorAI (173 million) and Character AI (165 million) draw roughly 339 million monthly visits together, more than Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot combined. That puts character-based AI among the largest consumer use cases outside the leading general-purpose assistants.

角色聊天是另一个值得关注的流量来源。JanitorAI（1.73亿）和Character AI（1.65亿）合计每月吸引约3.39亿次访问，超过Perplexity和微软Copilot的总和。这使得基于角色的AI跻身于领先通用助手之外最大的消费者用例之列。

Google's Distribution Machine Still Trails a Startup

谷歌的分发机器仍落后于一家初创公司

Google is the only company with two tools in the ranking, with Gemini drawing 1.1 billion visits and Google Translate another 343 million. Together, they still attract less than a third of ChatGPT's traffic.

谷歌是唯一一家有两个工具上榜的公司，Gemini吸引了11亿次访问，谷歌翻译则吸引了3.43亿次。两者合计仍不到ChatGPT流量的三分之一。

That's a striking gap for a company that owns the world's dominant search engine, browser, and mobile operating system. Microsoft faces a similar challenge, with Copilot drawing just 93 million web visits. However, that figure doesn't include usage built into Windows and Office.

对于一家拥有全球主导搜索引擎、浏览器和移动操作系统的公司来说，这是一个惊人的差距。微软也面临类似的挑战，Copilot仅获得9300万次网页访问。不过，这一数字不包括集成在Windows和Office中的使用量。
