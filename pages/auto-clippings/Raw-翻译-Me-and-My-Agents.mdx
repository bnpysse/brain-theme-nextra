In my former career as a research analyst, I would approach earnings season with a mix of trepidation and excitement. The period – which kicked off this week in the banks sector – meant early starts and late nights. Companies report as early as 6am and I would need to be on standby to read the release. With filings stretching to 75 pages, there was a lot to absorb. I would need to communicate my thoughts to an internal salesforce and to institutional clients, and then update my model, dial in to the earnings call, ask three questions, revise my model and publish a note – all against the backdrop of a cutthroat Wall Street environment where a dozen other analysts were doing the same thing. Sometimes we’d juggle several earnings releases on the same day, and unless it was Friday, it’d start all over again the next morning.

在我之前担任研究分析师的职业生涯中，我总是怀着忐忑与兴奋交织的心情迎接财报季。这个时期——本周从银行业开始——意味着早起晚睡。公司最早在早上6点发布财报，我需要随时待命阅读公告。文件长达75页，需要吸收大量信息。我需要向内部销售团队和机构客户传达我的观点，然后更新模型、拨入财报电话会议、提出三个问题、修订模型并发布报告——这一切都发生在华尔街残酷竞争的环境中，还有十几个其他分析师在做同样的事情。有时我们一天要同时处理多份财报，除非是周五，否则第二天早上一切又从头开始。

As an investor, the experience was less intense, though the stakes were higher. My time on the sell-side taught me what to look for in a release, so by the time I started managing money, I could process earnings much faster. It proved useful. Although they make up just 1.6% of trading days, earnings release dates

account

for 15% of annual share-price variance. Earnings revisions remain a key driver of stock performance and the market doesn’t always get them right. On more than a few occasions, I was able to exploit the heightened liquidity around announcements to act on my analysis before the market opened or while earnings calls were underway.

作为投资者，这种体验虽然没那么紧张，但风险更高。我在卖方的工作经历教会了我该在财报中关注什么，所以当我开始管理资金时，我能更快地处理财报信息。事实证明这很有用。尽管财报发布日只占交易日总数的1.6%，但它们却

贡献

了年度股价波动的15%。盈利修正仍然是股票表现的关键驱动因素，而市场并不总能正确解读。在很多情况下，我能够利用财报公告前后流动性增强的机会，在市场开盘前或财报电话会议进行中，根据我的分析采取行动。

This week, the six largest US banks reported third-quarter earnings. Collectively, they poured over 250 pages of financial information into the market and hosted over eight hours of calls to explain it. JPMorgan alone furnished investors with 523 new data points.

本周，美国六大银行公布了第三季度财报。它们合计向市场发布了超过250页的财务信息，并举办了超过八小时的电话会议来解释这些内容。仅摩根大通一家就为投资者提供了523个新数据点。

These days, I’m fortunate to have a team to help me distill it all. I’ve trained four AI agents to parse earnings materials, and this week they earned their keep.

如今，我很幸运有一个团队帮我提炼所有这些信息。我训练了四个AI代理来解析财报材料，本周它们证明了自己的价值。

**Ernie**

does the heavy lifting. I have a folder on my desktop for each of the banks I follow, containing historic earnings releases, investor presentations, call transcripts and the latest annual report. When a new release lands, Ernie studies the folder, builds a picture of what the market is expecting from analyst previews and tells me where the quarter confirms that view and where it departs. Once the call transcript is in, Ernie incorporates it, delivering a five-page report and a two-page brief in the time it takes me to brew an espresso.

**Ernie**

负责重活。我的桌面上为每只我跟踪的银行股都建了一个文件夹，里面包含历史财报、投资者演示文稿、电话会议记录和最新年报。当新财报发布时，Ernie会研究文件夹，根据分析师的预览构建市场预期的图景，并告诉我本季度在哪些方面证实了该观点、哪些方面有所偏离。一旦电话会议记录到手，Ernie会将其整合，在我煮一杯浓缩咖啡的时间内，交付一份五页的报告和一份两页的简报。

My agents were more impressed with earnings than the market seemed to be. Guidance was broadly up, in particular on higher net interest income. Equities trading and banking fees were mostly strong enough to offset a weaker period for fixed income trading. Consumers continued to spend and credit held up.

我的代理们对财报的印象比市场似乎更积极。指引普遍上调，尤其是在净利息收入方面。股票交易和银行手续费大多强劲，足以抵消固定收益交易的低迷期。消费者继续支出，信贷保持稳健。
