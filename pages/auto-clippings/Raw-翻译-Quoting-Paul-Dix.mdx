【文章标题】：Quoting Paul Dix

【文章正文】：
【文章正文】：
The fact that AI wrote 1M LOC and then refined it over the course of the next couple of months to produce a reliable piece of software that is currently running on millions of developer machines is absolutely mind blowing.
人工智能编写了100万行代码，并在随后的几个月里不断对其进行优化，最终打造出一款可靠的软件，目前正运行在数百万台开发者设备上，这一事实简直令人震撼。

And you can say, “well it’s not that impressive because they had an oracle to compare against, so it was simple to go from one language to another”, but I think that’s selling this entire thing short.
你或许会说，“这没什么了不起的，因为他们有标准答案可供比对，所以从一种语言转换到另一种语言很简单”，但我认为这完全低估了整件事的意义。

If you can build a verification system and give proper direction, AI can produce a highly complex, highly sophisticated piece of software and it can continue to refine it until it just works.
如果你能构建一个验证系统并给予正确的指引，人工智能就能开发出高度复杂、极其精密的软件，并且能够持续优化，直到其完美运行。

—
—

Paul Dix
保罗·迪克斯
