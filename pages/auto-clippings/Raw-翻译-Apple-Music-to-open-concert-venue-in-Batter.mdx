【文章标题】：Apple Music to open concert venue in Battersea Power Station
【文章标题】：苹果音乐将在巴特西发电站开设演出场馆

【文章正文】：
Apple Music to open concert venue in Battersea
苹果音乐将在巴特西开设演出场馆

The US tech giant Apple is to open a new, state-of-the-art concert hall in London, situated underneath its European headquarters at Battersea Power Station.
美国科技巨头苹果公司将在伦敦巴特西发电站其欧洲总部地下，开设一个全新的顶级音乐厅。

The 600-capacity venue will host one-off shows by major recording artists and new bands, all of which will be filmed and broadcast live online.
这个可容纳600人的场馆将举办知名歌手和新乐队的专场演出，所有演出都将被录制并在线直播。

Apple Music's vice president, Oliver Schusser, told BBC News the facility, which is expected to open in the coming weeks, contained "more cameras and speakers than any venue of its size, anywhere in the world".
苹果音乐副总裁奥利弗·舒瑟告诉BBC，这个预计数周内开放的场馆"拥有的摄像机和扬声器数量超过全球任何同等规模的场馆"。

It comes as London's live music scene faces huge pressure, with 45 venues closing in the capital last year alone.
此举正值伦敦现场音乐行业面临巨大压力之际——仅去年就有45家场馆在首都关闭。

It's a reflection of a national trend: Between 2020 and 2025, as the cost of living increased and people made post-Covid lifestyle changes, about 400 of Britain's clubs closed.
这反映了全国趋势：2020至2025年间，随着生活成本上升和疫情后生活方式改变，英国约有400家俱乐部关闭。

The rate in London is higher than the rest of the country, so the arrival of a deep-pocketed tech company could give independent venue owners cause for concern.
伦敦的关闭率高于全国其他地区，因此这家财力雄厚的科技公司的入场可能引发独立场馆经营者的担忧。

But Schusser said its new venture was not trying to steal customers.
但舒瑟表示新项目并非要抢夺客源。

"We're not trying to be in the live business. We're not trying to compete with anyone.
"我们并非要进入现场演出行业，也不想与任何人竞争。

"We're going to run the venue like a venue, with tickets and everything, but the sole purpose for us is to do great work with artists, to connect artists with fans, and to broadcast the content on Apple Music.
"我们会像普通场馆那样运营，售票等一应俱全，但我们的唯一目的是与艺术家合作创作优质内容，连接艺人与乐迷，并通过苹果音乐播放。

"We have really no interest in trying to be in the way of anyone else, and that's one of the main reasons it's a smaller space."
"我们绝无妨碍他人之意，这也是选择较小场地的主要原因之一。"

The venue, known as Apple Music Hall, marks the company's return to London, nine years after it scrapped the popular iTunes Festival, which saw acts like Lady Gaga, Elton John, Britney Spears and Adele play free concerts at venues such as Koko and The Roundhouse.
这个名为"苹果音乐厅"的场馆标志着该公司重返伦敦——九年前其取消了广受欢迎的iTunes音乐节，该音乐节曾邀请Lady Gaga、埃尔顿·约翰、布兰妮·斯皮尔斯和阿黛尔等艺人在Koko和圆屋剧场举办免费演出。

Culture secretary Lisa Nandy welcomed the development, calling it "a huge vote of confidence in the UK as a global creative powerhouse" and "a signal of just how strong our creative sectors really are".
文化大臣丽莎·南迪对此表示欢迎，称这是"对英国作为全球创意强国的巨大信任票"，也"彰显了我们创意产业的真正实力"。

Details of the opening act are expected to be announced soon.
开幕演出的详细信息预计将很快公布。

BBC News got an early look at the venue as the finishing touches were being made in early September.
BBC新闻在九月初场馆收尾阶段提前探访了现场。

It's a small, intimate space, with fluted vaults and brick-lined walls that echo the Art Deco architecture of the former power station. But echo itself won't be an issue -even the bricks have been specially selected to absorb reflected sounds.
这是一个私密的小型空间，带有凹槽拱顶和砖砌墙面，呼应了原发电站的装饰艺术风格。但回声不会成为问题——连砖块都是特选的吸音材料。

The stage is 38-foot long, and can be configured for both "in the round" and more traditional front-facing shows, while the dressing rooms are kitted out with en-suite showers and dedicated costume storage areas.
舞台长38英尺，可配置为圆形环绕式或传统正面式演出，化妆间配有独立淋浴间和专用服装储存区。

The facility also contains new studios for Apple Music's radio stations, which will move from their current home in King's Cross, and dedicated rooms for DJ mixes, writing camps and band rehearsals.
场馆还设有苹果音乐电台的新录音室（将从现址国王十字区迁入），以及DJ混音室、创作营和乐队排练室。

The whole building is wired for video and audio, meaning artists can walk in, record a podcast, appear on a radio show, and live-stream a gig in the same day.
整栋建筑配备音视频系统，艺人可在同一天内完成播客录制、电台节目和演出直播。

New music focus
聚焦新音乐

Apple's hope is that creating bespoke content will help to distinguish its service from competitors like Spotify and Amazon Music.
苹果希望通过定制内容使其服务区别于Spotify和亚马逊音乐等竞争对手。

The effort comes after legendary music mogul Jimmy Iovine said music streaming services were "minutes away from being obsolete", because they don't foster a connection between the listener and the music.
此前传奇音乐大亨吉米·艾欧文称音乐流媒体服务"距离过时只有几分钟"，因其未能建立听众与音乐的联系。

"It's one dimensional. It's an ATM machine," said Iovine, who has worked with everyone from Bruce Springsteen to Tupac Shakur, in an interview this February.
"这太单一了，就像ATM机。"曾与布鲁斯·斯普林斯汀、图派克·夏库尔等众多艺人合作的艾欧文在今年二月采访中表示。

"The artists want to communicate with their fans, period. That's what they want.
"艺术家想与粉丝交流，仅此而已。这就是他们的需求。

"Not for philanthropic reasons, but this is what TikTok does. That's what Instagram does."
"不是出于慈善，但这就是TikTok和Instagram正在做的。"

Schusser, who has been at Apple for 21 years (including the period from 2014-2018 when Iovine was a creative consultant) agreed.
在苹果工作21年的舒瑟（2014-2018年间艾欧文曾任创意顾问）对此表示认同。

"We want our music services not just to be an anonymous place on the internet where you press a button and music starts playing.
"我们希望音乐服务不仅是互联网上点击按钮就能播放音乐的匿名场所。

"For most artists, it's kind of sad in streaming. There's nothing for them to do. The music gets delivered by the labels, and then it's a bunch of playlists.
"对多数艺人来说，流媒体现状可悲。他们无事可做，音乐由厂牌上传后，就只剩播放列表。

"But [fans] want to have a chance to meet the artists, know more about the artists, understand why they made an album the way they made it."
"但乐迷希望有机会接触艺人，了解更多背景，理解专辑创作初衷。"

Schusser said Apple also hoped to promote new and upcoming bands who might otherwise struggle to make headway, as people's listening habits stagnate.
舒瑟表示，鉴于人们听歌习惯停滞不前，苹果也希望推广那些可能难以突围的新兴乐队。

"It's one of the biggest opportunities and challenges we have in the music business right now.
"这是当下音乐行业最大的机遇与挑战之一。

"There are too many artists who rely on [their back] catalogue. Catalogue music is great, but we need to make sure we have a new era of catalogue music in 10, 20, years from now."
"太多艺人依赖过往作品。经典音乐很棒，但我们需要确保未来10年、20年能诞生新时代的经典。"
