【文章标题】：What is the size of Yemen? (2024)  
也门的面积究竟有多大？（2024年）  

【文章正文】：  
Have you ever asked yourself, “What is the size of Yemen?” Yeah, me neither. At least, not until I received this peculiar comment under my video.  
你可曾自问过："也门的面积有多大？" 说实话，我也没想过——至少在我的视频下方出现那条古怪评论之前。  

I became intrigued, so I googled the size of Yemen, and voila, he wasn't lying. 555,000 sq km – as the first Google response with the same number used by Wikipedia.  
好奇心驱使我搜索了也门面积，结果发现那位网友所言非虚——谷歌首条结果显示555,000平方公里，与维基百科数据完全一致。  

I wanted to see what went wrong, if this person was right or not, so I tried replicating the borders by drawing them on Google Maps. And…. I arrived at 456,000 km2.  
为验证真伪，我尝试在谷歌地图上手动绘制国界测算面积。结果却显示...仅有456,000平方公里。  

I included all the islands, including Socotra, to ensure I didn't miss anything. There was no possibility of some small border error – the missing area was the size of a quarter of the country.  
我甚至纳入了索科特拉岛等所有岛屿，但近10万平方公里的误差（相当于国土四分之一）绝非边界误差所能解释。  

Where 555k km2 came from  
555,000平方公里说法的起源  

So how did the 555 claim originate? With the help of Google Books, I managed to find the oldest mention possible:  
通过谷歌图书，我溯源到最早的记载：  

This is a statistical yearbook published by the Central Statistical Office of Yemen in 1991, the first one after the unification of the country.  
1991年也门中央统计局发布的统一后首版统计年鉴。  

When I read the paragraph carefully, I noticed one slight problem. Yemen does not stretch into the 20th North latitude. In fact, the northernmost point of Yemen is exactly at the 19th altitude, as per the border agreement with Saudi Arabia.  
细读后发现关键问题：也门最北端实际位于北纬19度（根据与沙特边界协议），但年鉴声称领土延伸至北纬20度。  

So what happened here?  
究竟为何出现这种差异？  

Before 2000, the border of Yemen with Saudi Arabia was not clearly demarcated. So, it’s quite possible that some official drew the northern border up to the 20th altitude for propaganda purposes to make the country as big as possible.  
2000年前也沙边界未明确勘定，很可能有官员为夸大国土面积，将北部边界虚标至北纬20度。  

When I plugged the new borders into Google, my assumption seemed to have worked out.And yes, this area includes the Rub-Al-Khali desert despite what the text says.  
将修正后边界输入谷歌地图验证，假设成立——尽管年鉴否认，但该算法确实将鲁卜哈利沙漠部分区域划入也门。  

Where 527k km2 came from  
527,000平方公里数据的真相  

"Well, it's a case of Wikipedia using bad sources – a very common occurrence indeed. Surely, other sources must have gotten it right?"  
"这不过是维基百科引用劣质信源的又一例证，但其他权威机构总该正确吧？"  

So I looked into the most reliable source: the CIA World Factbook, which claimed that the size of Yemen is 527,968 km2.  
调查最权威的CIA《世界概况》，其记载也门面积为527,968平方公里。  

The same number can be found in the World Bank database, the United Nations website, as well as an old geography book that I found in my closet.  
世界银行数据库、联合国官网，甚至我衣柜里的旧地理书都沿用该数据。  

But it still diverges from the Google Maps number. Why? I delved into previous editions of the CIA World Factbook and found the earliest editions where the number appeared – 1991, exactly a year after the unification of Yemen happened.  
但与谷歌地图测算值仍有出入。追溯CIA档案发现：该数字最早出现于1991年版（也门统一次年）。  

I looked into previous editions and found separate entries for North and South Yemen – with the first one being 195,000 km2, and the second one being 332,968 km2.  
更早版本分别记载南北也门面积：北也门195,000平方公里，南也门332,968平方公里。  

Both numbers were summed up to form the total size of United Yemen. But were they correct? I plugged the borders used in the textbook into Google Earth, and to my surprise, I found out that none of them were correct – North Yemen only had 136,000 km2, and South Yemen was only 288,000 km2.  
二者相加即统一后总面积。但实测发现：北也门实际仅136,000平方公里，南也门288,000平方公里——教科书数据完全错误。  

There was no possibility of some minor border or rounding error. They were both wildly off. What was happening here?  
这绝非边界或四舍五入误差，而是系统性错误。  

North Yemen’s size  
北也门面积之谜  

I started with North Yemen. I was looking for the earliest mention of the number 195,000 sq km2 in relation to North Yemen. And… after a lot of digging, I found it – the 1964 edition of the Statesman Yearbook described it as 75,000 sq. miles (195,000 sq. km).  
溯源发现：1964版《政治家年鉴》记载北也门为7.5万平方英里（合19.5万平方公里）。  

This gave me a hint to search for the area in square miles. After a long search with the help of Google Books, I found the earliest mention.  
顺藤摸瓜找到最早记载：  

The 1904 edition of the New International Encyclopedia described the size of the Yemeni Vilayet, a province of the Ottoman Empire.  
1904版《新国际百科全书》记录的奥斯曼帝国也门行省面积。  

When I plugged its border into Google Earth, it fit perfectly into 195,000 km2. Despite the fact that independent North Yemen was half the size of the vilayet, every book until 1990 continued to show its size from the 1904 source, repeating it without any fact-checking!  
实测该行省面积确为19.5万平方公里。但独立后的北也门仅有其一半大小，1990年前所有文献却照搬1904年数据！  

South Yemen’s size  
南也门面积乌龙  

Every source up to 1976 showed South Yemen’s size as 287,000 km2 (here, here, here). CIA Factbook changed it to 332 in 1986.  
1976年前所有文献均记载南也门28.7万平方公里，但1986年CIA突然改为33.2万。  

What happened in 1976 to cause such a sudden expansion? Nothing about South Yemen’s size changed.  
1976年究竟发生了什么？其实国土毫无变化。  

After much searching, I found it: Buried among other documents was an obscure World Bank report about South Yemen’s economic situation from October 1976.  
最终在1976年10月世行一份冷门经济报告中找到答案：  

Buried on the 51st page was a table with South Yemen’s governorates and their areas summing to 338,100 km2.  
第51页表格将"萨穆德"地区（实属第五省辖区）重复计算，导致总面积虚增。  

Its area had been counted twice in the table and led to a massive overestimation of South Yemen’s size. And since then, every source has repeated this erroneous claim without double-checking.  
自此所有机构都未加核实，集体沿用了这个错误数据。
