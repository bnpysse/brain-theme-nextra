【文章标题】：Owner of Former UWS Barbecue Restaurant Indicted for Wage Theft: Manhattan DA

【文章标题】：前上西区烧烤餐厅老板因克扣工资被起诉：曼哈顿地区检察官

【文章正文】：

By Gus Saltonstall

作者：格斯·索尔顿斯托尔

The owner of a former barbecue restaurant on the Upper West Side has been indicted for wage theft, Manhattan District Attorney Alvin Bragg announced on Thursday.

曼哈顿地区检察官阿尔文·布拉格周四宣布，上西区一家前烧烤餐厅的老板因克扣工资而被起诉。

Patrick Griffin, 71, was indicted for allegedly stealing at least $8,000 in wages from five of his employees, according to the DA's office.

据地区检察官办公室称，71岁的帕特里克·格里芬被起诉，涉嫌从五名员工手中窃取至少8000美元的工资。

Au Jus, the Oklahoma-style barbecue chain, had three New York City locations, including at 2621 Broadway, on the corner of West 99th Street. All three are now closed.

俄克拉荷马风格烧烤连锁店Au Jus在纽约市曾有三家分店，其中包括位于西99街拐角处百老汇2621号的一家。目前三家分店均已关闭。

"As alleged, Patrick Griffin, owner of the popular Au Jus restaurant, took advantage of his workers and stole their hard-earned wages while they provided physically demanding labor," Bragg said in a news release. "Griffin was allegedly vacationing in Europe while his employees were begging him for their money that they earned and need to support their families."

布拉格在新闻稿中表示：“据指控，人气餐厅Au Jus的老板帕特里克·格里芬利用他的工人，在他们从事高强度体力劳动的同时窃取他们辛苦挣来的工资。据称，格里芬在欧洲度假时，他的员工正在恳求他支付他们应得的、用以养家糊口的工资。”

Au Jus's Upper West Side location closed in September of 2025, after the front door was boarded and a Marshal's Notice was posted on the front door. The neighborhood outpost opened in 2020.

Au Jus的上西区分店于2025年9月关闭，此前该店前门被木板封住，门上贴有法警通知。这家社区分店于2020年开业。

Griffin allegedly engaged in a "pattern of wage theft against his workers" beginning in January 2025, and ignored messages and blocked phone numbers when those employees followed up for their payment, according to the Manhattan DA's office.

据曼哈顿地区检察官办公室称，格里芬涉嫌从2025年1月开始“对员工实施一系列克扣工资行为”，并在员工跟进讨薪时无视信息、拉黑电话号码。

"Griffin told a former employee that he would not be paid unless he and his coworkers returned to work at the restaurant. When the worker returned to work, Griffin continued withholding his wages, and the worker was forced to leave again," the Manhattan DA's office wrote. "In another instance, Griffin demanded that a worker sign a document that falsely claimed he did not have government-issued identification. When the worker refused to sign the false document, Griffin withheld his wages."

曼哈顿地区检察官办公室写道：“格里芬告诉一名前员工，除非他和同事回到餐厅工作，否则不会得到工资。当该员工回去工作后，格里芬继续扣发他的工资，该员工被迫再次离开。另一次，格里芬要求一名员工签署一份虚假声明其没有政府签发身份证件的文件。当该员工拒绝签署这份虚假文件时，格里芬扣发了他的工资。”

After the unsuccessful attempts to recover the wages, employees reported Griffin's conduct to the Worker Protection Hotline, which led to the Manhattan District Attorney investigation.

在讨薪未果后，员工们向工人保护热线举报了格里芬的行为，这促使曼哈顿地区检察官展开调查。

Griffin is officially charged with grand larceny in the third degree by wage theft, scheme to defraud in the first degree, and failure to pay wages in accordance with the labor law.

格里芬被正式指控犯有三级重大盗窃罪（以克扣工资方式）、一级欺诈阴谋罪，以及违反劳动法未支付工资罪。
