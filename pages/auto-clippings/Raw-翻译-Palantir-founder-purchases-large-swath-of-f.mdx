Palantir founder purchases large swath of forest in Sweden  
Palantir创始人购入瑞典大片森林  

Alex Karp, CEO of Palantir Technologies, purchased 15,000 hectares (37,000 acres) of forest in Härjedalen, Sweden, for 235 million kronor ($22 million). For comparison, Liechtenstein covers an area of about 16,000 hectares.  
Palantir Technologies首席执行官亚历克斯·卡普以2.35亿瑞典克朗（2200万美元）购入了瑞典耶姆特兰省1.5万公顷（3.7万英亩）森林。作为对比，列支敦士登的国土面积约为1.6万公顷。  

The purchase from paper company SCA AB became public when local hunters received cancellation of their lease to hunt on the property, according to Tidningen Härjedalen. Local authorities were given no advance information about the transaction.  
据《耶姆特兰日报》报道，这笔从纸业公司SCA AB购入的交易之所以曝光，是因为当地猎人收到了取消该地块狩猎租约的通知。地方政府事先未获知交易信息。  

Karp made the purchase through his newly registered company Cladonia Skog AB. The chairman of Cladonia told the Swedish newspaper that the land provides valuable habitat for wildlife and space for recreation.  
卡普通过新注册的Cladonia Skog AB公司完成收购。该公司董事长向瑞典媒体表示，这片土地为野生动物提供了宝贵栖息地，也是休闲活动空间。  

Swedish defense and police use services from Palatir, as does NATO. The purchase comes at a time when European leaders are considering reducing their reliance on U.S. technology.  
瑞典国防部、警方及北约均使用Palantir的服务。此次收购正值欧洲领导人考虑减少对美国技术依赖之际。
