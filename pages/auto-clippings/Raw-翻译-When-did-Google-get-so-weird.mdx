【文章正文】：

When did Google get so f-ing weird?

谷歌他妈的什么时候变得这么诡异了？

I recently had an experience while doing a simple Google search that was so profoundly weird that it stopped me in my tracks.

最近我在进行简单谷歌搜索时遇到了一件诡异至极的事，让我当场愣住。

Understanding this Google search experience involves understanding a niche mid-2010s basketball meme, so bear with me for a minute. In 2014 the Philadelphia 76ers drafted Dario Saric, who was playing basketball professionally in Turkey at the time. He announced his intention to finish out his contract in Turkey, meaning he wouldn't come to the USA join the Sixers for a couple of years. There was a joke within the fan community that Dario was "never coming over" which became sort of a shibboleth for part of the fanbase.

要理解这次搜索经历，得先了解2010年代中期一个小众篮球梗。2014年费城76人队选中了当时在土耳其打职业联赛的达里奥·萨里奇，他宣布要履行完土耳其的合同，意味着未来几年都不会来美国加入76人。球迷圈里流传着"达里奥永远不会来"的梗，成了部分球迷的接头暗号。

I saw Dario's name mentioned in an NBA article recently and I wanted to find some of those old funny tweets about him from the 2010s, so I Googled simply "hes never coming over dario". I assumed I would be either find nothing (maybe I was remembering the phrasing wrong) or find old Tweets/Reddit posts from that time.

最近看到NBA文章提到达里奥的名字，我想找些2010年代关于他的搞笑推文，就简单搜索"hes never coming over dario"。本以为要么搜不到（可能记错说法），要么找到当年的推文/Reddit帖子。

结果2026年的谷歌给了我AI概览。这类功能曾经让我困扰，但现在基本能接受，有时还挺有用。以下是AI概览的内容：

Google, a search engine which does not have human emotions, assumed that I had been spurned by a man in my life named Dario and decided what I wanted was an empathetic digital friend. What I wanted was some links, but that's not what Google does in 2026. Expanding the AI overview to see the full answer gave me this:

这个没有人类情感的搜索引擎，竟假定我被一个叫达里奥的男人拒绝，判定我需要一位充满同理心的数字朋友。而我想要的只是几个链接——但这显然不是2026年谷歌该做的事。点开完整AI回复后我看到：

What in the fucking hell? I think this was the moment I, the frog, noticed the pot had been boiling for a while. In what universe is it Google's job to console me and be an empathetic listener rather than just find what I am looking for on the internet? In what way does this "organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful"? Maybe if I had loaded up a Gemini app with a chat interface this would be somewhat acceptable, but I'm using a search engine! Google has seriously lost the plot.

什么鬼东西？这一刻我就像温水里的青蛙突然意识到锅已经烧开很久了。在哪个平行宇宙里，谷歌的职责是安慰我当知心听众，而不是帮我在网上找东西？这算哪门子"整合全球信息，供大众使用"？如果我用的是带聊天界面的Gemini应用还情有可原，但这他妈是搜索引擎！谷歌彻底跑偏了。

If I scroll down a few hundred pixels below the AI slop, Google did have exactly what I was looking for:

在AI废话下方几百像素处，谷歌确实给出了我要的内容：

Regardless of what you think of AI or chatbots, I think it's pretty obvious this is just plain weird. Have we gotten to the point where we have to constantly be in a parasocial dialogue with our computers? Is it so hard to imagine that some parts of search were just fine before LLMs?

无论你对AI或聊天机器人怎么看，这显然太诡异了。难道我们已经沦落到必须和电脑维持准社交对话的地步？想象没有大语言模型时的搜索体验，真的这么难吗？

I'm not sure how to feel about all of this. Maybe I should go talk to my friend Google, it's always so nice to me.

我不知该作何感想。或许该去找我的好朋友谷歌聊聊，它对我总是这么"贴心"。
