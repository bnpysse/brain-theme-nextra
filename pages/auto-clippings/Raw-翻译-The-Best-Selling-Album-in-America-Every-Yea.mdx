Which Album Was America’s Biggest Each Year?

Key Takeaways

Only five artists have topped the annual list more than once since 2000: Taylor Swift, Adele, Eminem, Drake, and Morgan Wallen.

Taylor Swift’s four appearances span 16 years, from

Fearless

in 2009 to

The Life of a Showgirl

in 2025.

Adele’s

21

is the only album in the dataset to lead in two consecutive years, topping the list in both 2011 and 2012.

At the turn of the millennium, NSYNC’s

No Strings Attached

was America’s best-selling album, moving nearly 10 million copies in 2000. Twenty-five years later, Taylor Swift’s

The Life of a Showgirl

led the year with 5.6 million album-equivalent units.

Between those two albums, the music industry underwent a major transformation as digital downloads and then streaming changed how Americans consumed music. Using data from

Luminate via Business Insider

, this visualization tracks the best-selling album in the U.S. for every year from 2000 to 2025.

Blockbuster Albums Look Different in the Streaming Era

Some of the largest annual totals on the list are concentrated in the early 2000s. NSYNC’s

No Strings Attached

sold 9.9 million copies in 2000, while Eminem’s

The Eminem Show

reached 7.6 million in 2002, 50 Cent’s

Get Rich or Die Tryin’

reached 6.5 million in 2003, and Usher’s

Confessions

reached 8 million in 2004.

By the 2010s, many annual leaders posted totals between roughly 2 million and 4 million. However, figures across the full timeline are not directly comparable with traditional album sales because, as streaming grew, industry measurements increasingly incorporated album-equivalent units to capture consumption beyond purchases.

The shift is also visible in a different kind of album leaderboard. Visual Capitalist’s ranking of

Spotify’s most-streamed albums of all time

shows how today’s biggest releases can accumulate tens of billions of streams.

More recently, annual leaders have again posted larger totals. Morgan Wallen’s

One Thing at a Time

reached 5.4 million in 2023, while Taylor Swift’s

The Tortured Poets Department

approached 7 million in 2024.

Adele’s 2015 Album Stands Out

One album breaks sharply from the pattern of the mid-2010s. Adele’s

25

reached just over 8 million copies in 2015, more than double the total of Taylor Swift’s

1989

, which led the previous year with 3.7 million.

Adele had already topped the annual list twice with

21

. The album led with 5.8 million copies in 2011 and another 4.4 million in 2012, making it the only album in the dataset to lead in two consecutive years.

Together, those appearances give Adele three years at the top of the list.

Taylor Swift Has Led the Year Four Times

Taylor Swift appears more often than any other artist in the dataset.

Fearless

led in 2009 with 3.2 million copies, followed by

1989

in 2014 with 3.7 million.

A decade later, Swift returned to the top with

The Tortured Poets Department

, which recorded nearly 7 million copies in 2024.

The Life of a Showgirl

followed with 5.6 million in 2025.
