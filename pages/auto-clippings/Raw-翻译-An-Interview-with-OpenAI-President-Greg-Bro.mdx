如今历经风云变幻，布罗克曼作为OpenAI总裁，昨日亲自发布了公司最新模型Astra。

这段录制于Astra发布前的访谈中，我们探讨了布罗克曼的成长经历、Stripe岁月以及OpenAI初创时光。

我们聊到ChatGPT的发布与2023年风波，探讨十亿用户量是否反成掣肘。

亦谈及OpenAI在价值链中的定位，及其与微软等终端企业、英伟达等供应商的竞合关系。

计算机其实一直是我成长的背景音。

爱玩电脑游戏，但更痴迷数学与科学。

九年级前我一直以为自己会当演员——热衷表演艺术，还浅尝过哲学。

And I was thinking about, I wanted to double down on something, I felt like I could be best in the world and really try to move the needle in a field, and I felt like I either had to pick a more cerebral, hard sciences approach or more of the arts and acting direction and creative route.

最终选择理科，因觉其更能改变世界。

布罗克曼：  

I guess for me it felt like — one of the things I loved about acting was actually the group aspect of it.  
表演最吸引我的是团队协作——  

I loved improv, you’re just creatively thinking about things, you’re bouncing ideas back and forth, but it also really requires being part of a team that works together super well, and that’s something that’s not always guaranteed.  
热爱即兴创作中的思维碰撞，但这需要完美配合的团队，而这不常有。  

That’s a hard thing to accomplish and find. The thing that I really liked about the more cerebral route is it feels like you sharpen your own skills.  
相比之下，理科道路更能磨砺个人技能。  

One thing I did learn, actually, was that even if you’re great at writing code, that’s not enough.
