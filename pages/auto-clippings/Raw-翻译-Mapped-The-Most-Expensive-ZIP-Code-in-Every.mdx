Key Takeaways:
核心发现：

The priciest ZIP code in each state ranges from $500,000 to $10.8 million in median home list price.
各州最贵邮编区的房屋挂牌中位数价格从50万到1080万美元不等

Florida and California are the only states whose most expensive ZIP codes exceed $10 million.
佛罗里达和加州是唯二拥有超千万美元邮编区的州

Eight state-leading ZIP codes are located on islands or barrier islands, highlighting the prominence of scarce waterfront markets.
八个州的顶级邮编区位于岛屿或堰洲岛上，凸显稀缺滨水地产的价值

The gap between states is striking. Florida’s 33109 and California’s 94027 are the only state leaders above $10 million. New York’s Sagaponack ranks third at nearly $6 million, while Mississippi’s most expensive ZIP code, in Oxford, comes in at just $500,000.
州际差异令人震惊。佛罗里达33109和加州94027是仅有的两个超千万美元邮编区。纽约萨加波纳克以近600万美元位列第三，而密西西比最贵的牛津市邮编区仅50万美元。

Several resort and second-home markets also appear near the top, including Teton Village, Sea Island, Paradise Valley, Aspen, and Sullivan’s Island.
多个度假胜地和第二住宅市场名列前茅，包括提顿村、海岛、天堂谷、阿斯本和沙利文岛。

What America’s Priciest ZIP Codes Have in Common
美国顶级邮编区的共同特征

Many of the most expensive ZIP codes on the map share two characteristics: limited housing supply and strong demand from affluent buyers. In some cases, geography itself creates that scarcity.
这些昂贵邮编区普遍具备两大特征：有限房源和高净值买家强劲需求。地理因素本身也造就了稀缺性。

Eight of the locations highlighted on the map are situated on islands or barrier islands, including Fisher Island, Sea Island, Sullivan’s Island, and Block Island. Fisher Island is an extreme example: the 216-acre private island has no road or bridge connecting it to the mainland.
地图标注的八个地区位于岛屿，包括渔夫岛、海岛、沙利文岛和布洛克岛。渔夫岛是典型代表：这个216英亩的私人岛屿没有连接大陆的道路或桥梁。

But waterfront isn’t a requirement. Resort destinations such as Aspen, Teton Village, Park City, and Paradise Valley also lead their respective states, while affluent communities such as Greenwich, Medina, and Kenilworth benefit from proximity to major economic centers.
但滨水并非必要条件。阿斯本、提顿村、帕克城等度假胜地同样领跑各州，格林尼治、麦地那等富人社区则受益于毗邻经济中心。

Together, the results show that America’s priciest ZIP codes span several types of luxury markets, from exclusive waterfront enclaves to resort towns and high-end suburbs.
总体来看，美国最贵邮编区涵盖多种豪宅市场类型，从专属滨水飞地到度假小镇再到高端郊区。
