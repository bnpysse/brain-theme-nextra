【文章标题】：Roundup #88: Is it time to panic yet?
综述第88期：现在该恐慌了吗？

【文章正文】：
Photo by Solving Healthcare on Unsplash
Unsplash上Solving Healthcare拍摄的照片

This week’s roundup has a lot of AI in it. Fortunately or unfortunately, it seems like a lot of the news is going to revolve around AI for the rest of our lives. 
本周综述包含大量AI内容。无论幸与不幸，在我们余生中，似乎大量新闻都将围绕AI展开。

That was true of industrial technology in 1870-1970; people basically got used to the idea that railroads and factories and oil and industrialized warfare and such matters were central to the way the world was run and to the collective future of humanity. 
1870-1970年的工业技术就是如此；人们早已习惯铁路、工厂、石油、工业化战争等事物主导世界运行方式和人类共同未来的观念。

AI is going to be like that going forward, and we’re just going to have to get used to that. So let’s make it as fun and interesting as we can!
AI未来也将如此，我们必须适应这一点。那就让我们尽可能让它变得有趣吧！

1. Americans are getting scared about AI risk
1. 美国人对AI风险日益恐惧

AI risk has exploded onto the national scene. 
AI风险已在全国范围内爆发性显现。

AI companies and AI researchers generally believe that the technology they’re building has the capacity to do great harm — perhaps even to end the human race — if it’s not developed more slowly and deliberately. 
AI公司和研究者普遍认为，若不更审慎缓慢地发展，他们开发的技术可能造成巨大危害——甚至终结人类文明。

There are five main groups of people opposing the slowdown:
反对减速发展的主要有五类群体：

The Trump administration, which is worried that an AI slowdown might also slow down economic growth
特朗普政府——担心AI减速会拖累经济增长

Investors who think a slowdown might hurt their bottom line
投资者——认为减速会影响利润

Libertarians and techno-optimists who think regulating technological progress is bad on principle
自由主义者与技术乐观派——原则上反对管制技术进步

China hawks who worry that a slowdown would let China take the lead in the AI race
对华鹰派——担心减速会让中国赢得AI竞赛

Progressives who spent the last few years telling themselves that AI doesn’t work, that AI is a huge economic bubble, and so on, and who now can’t bring themselves to admit that yes, the techbros actually built something that works.
进步派——过去数年坚称AI无效、是经济泡沫，如今难以承认技术精英确实创造了有效成果

This is a strange alliance indeed. 
这确实是个奇特联盟。

The last of these — the progressives who simply couldn’t admit that billionaire-funded private industry could build something powerful enough to endanger humanity — were the strangest of all, since their refusal to acknowledge the effectiveness of AI basically put them in an alliance with libertarians, hawks, and Trump. 
最后这类——拒绝承认亿万富翁资助的私企能造出威胁人类的强大技术——最为怪异，因其否认AI有效性的立场使其与自由派、鹰派和特朗普形成同盟。

In fact, in recent days there has been a tremendous civil war within the progressive movement between the "AI is dangerous" and "AI is fake" camps, with Bernie Sanders supporting the former and his former comms director David Sirota supporting the latter.
实际上，进步派内部正爆发"AI危险派"与"AI虚假派"的内战，伯尼·桑德斯支持前者，其前通讯主任大卫·西罗塔支持后者。

But although it has the support of the president (for now), the anti-slowdown coalition is losing in the court of public opinion. 
尽管（暂时）获得总统支持，反减速联盟正在民意法庭败退。

2. A very cool debate about AI and growth
2. 关于AI与增长的精彩辩论

Quite apart from the question of whether AI will kill us is the question of whether AI will deliver explosive economic growth. 
与"AI是否会毁灭人类"并行的，是"AI会否带来经济爆发式增长"的问题。

There’s a pretty epic public bet on this:
对此有个史诗级公开赌约：

The people on the "fast growth" side are mostly AI researchers, while the people on the "slow growth" side are mostly (but not entirely) economists. 
"快速增长"方多为AI研究者，"缓慢增长"方则多为（非全部）经济学家。

This is an exaggerated version of a broader disconnect — AI researchers are generally more optimistic about AI’s impact on economic growth than economists are:

Source: Forecasting Research Institute

But the people publicly betting on fast growth are making an even more extreme forecast — they’re forecasting 15% growth, which is much higher than the 5.3% that AI experts forecasted as their most optimistic scenario. 
但公开押注快速增长者预测更为极端——他们预测15%增长率，远高于AI专家最乐观情景预测的5.3%。

That’s an absolutely stupendous growth rate — China has hit it only in one year (1984) since it began its rapid growth, and that was when it was a very poor country. 
这是惊人增速——中国自高速增长以来仅在1984年达到过，当时还是极度贫困国家。

And yet, a number of people in the AI industry think it’s going to happen to us very soon.
然而AI业界许多人认为我们很快将见证这种增长。

Economists — even those who work on the economics of superintelligent AI, like Alex Imas — are skeptical. 
经济学家——即便是研究超级智能AI经济学的亚历克斯·伊马斯——持怀疑态度。
