ZK-JPEG: Zero-knowledge Image Editing and Compression  
ZK-JPEG：零知识图像编辑与压缩  

Abstract  
摘要  

Tools for generating deep fake photographs are proliferating with greater ease of use and prominence in pop culture.  
生成深度伪造照片的工具正以更低的门槛和更高的流行度在文化领域扩散。  

Image authentication tools can defeat these deceitful developments by verifying that a digital image was actually produced by a physical camera.  
图像认证工具可通过验证数字图像确实由物理相机拍摄，从而遏制这类欺骗性技术发展。  

The challenge is that these tools must be robust to desirable image transformations.  
挑战在于这些工具必须对合理的图像变换保持鲁棒性。  

Camera attestation uses digital signatures to prove an image's provenance from a camera.  
相机认证通过数字签名来证明图像的相机来源。  

Lossy compression makes minute changes in order to reduce an image's size, and blurring or redacting regions of an image can protect its subjects.  
有损压缩通过细微修改来减小图像体积，而模糊或涂抹图像区域可保护主体隐私。  

These changes invalidate an image's signature.  
这些操作会使图像签名失效。  

Prior works use zero-knowledge (ZK) to prove a published image's edit history, but they do not survive lossy encoding such as the JPEG format.  
现有研究使用零知识证明（ZK）来验证公开图像的编辑历史，但无法兼容JPEG等有损编码格式。  

We present \zkjpeg, a cryptographic tool for JPEG compression that proves an image was correctly compressed from a secret, committed input.  
我们提出\zkjpeg——一种JPEG压缩密码学工具，可证明图像是从秘密承诺输入正确压缩而来。  

In addition, our tool can verify a large family of image transformations by integrating them into JPEG compression with minimal cost.  
此外，本工具能以极小代价将多种图像变换整合至JPEG压缩流程中进行验证。  

Our system is fast, flexible, and can be instantiated from off-the-shelf ZK tools.  
该系统快速灵活，可直接基于现有ZK工具实例化。  

We use PicoZK to convert Python image editing code into a ZK circuit for the line-point zero knowledge (LPZK) proof system.  
我们采用PicoZK将Python图像编辑代码转换为适用于线点零知识（LPZK）证明系统的ZK电路。
