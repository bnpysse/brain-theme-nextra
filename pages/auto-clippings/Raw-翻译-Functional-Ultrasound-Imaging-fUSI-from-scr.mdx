【文章标题】：Functional Ultrasound Imaging (fUSI) from scratch

【文章标题】：从零开始的功能性超声成像（fUSI）

【文章正文】：

Functional ultrasound imaging (fUSI) is a brain imaging modality that is just starting to be demonstrated in human studies. I’m sure many will be familiar with structural ultrasound imaging, which forms static images using ultrasound, as in a sonogram1. Functional ultrasound instead captures movies of brain tissue very rapidly, tracking subtle changes caused by the blood flow that follows neural activity.

功能性超声成像（fUSI）是一种刚开始在人体研究中得到验证的脑成像技术。相信许多人都熟悉结构性超声成像——它通过超声波生成静态图像（如超声扫描图）。而功能性超声则以极快速度捕捉脑组织动态影像，追踪神经活动引发的血流细微变化。

While this is the same source of contrast as functional MRI, it has the potential to be ten times higher linear resolution (1000X the voxels), smaller (no magnet!), cheaper (no magnet!), and higher signal-to-noise. The modality naturally pairs with low-intensity focused ultrasound (LIFU, or confusingly, fUS) which focuses energy on a target area of the brain to modulate it, a new treatment option for neuropsychiatric and neurological disorders. Extensions leveraging contrast agents or gene therapies could go beyond hemodynamic responses to measure vasculature at super-resolution or even directly measure neural activity. And while ultrasound cannot easily penetrate the skull, fUSI has been demonstrated intraoperatively and in a patient that received an acoustically transparent cranial implant following reconstructive skull surgery.

虽然其对比源与功能磁共振（fMRI）相同，但fUSI可能实现十倍更高的线性分辨率（体素量达1000倍）、更小体积（无需磁体！）、更低成本（无需磁体！）和更高信噪比。该技术天然适配低强度聚焦超声（LIFU，或易混淆的fUS），后者能将能量聚焦于脑部靶区进行调控，成为神经精神疾病的新疗法。通过造影剂或基因疗法的延伸应用，还能突破血流动力学响应限制，实现超分辨率血管成像甚至直接测量神经活动。尽管超声波不易穿透颅骨，但fUSI已在术中及接受声学透明颅骨植入物的患者身上得到验证。

What’s the source of contrast? Does it measure blood flow or volume? How does it deal with motion of the head? What is ultrasound, anyway?

At the end of this essay, you should understand what fUSI is, how it works, and why it is nowhere near its ceiling.

How fUSI works at a high level

fUSI高层级工作原理

Functional ultrasound imaging, like conventional ultrasound imaging, uses transducers—which act as both transmitters and receivers—to communicate ultrasound through tissue. That ultrasound travels mostly unimpeded through brain tissue2, backscattering where it encounters a change in impedance, like a red blood cell, an air bubble, or the skull. By measuring these backscattered waves, it is possible to computationally reconstruct local changes in impedance. The resolution of the reconstructed images is proportional to the wavelength of the communicated ultrasound; that can mean up to ~100 um linear resolution when sonifying at 18 MHz, at the high end of what’s used clinically.

与传统超声成像类似，功能性超声成像通过兼具发射接收功能的换能器使超声波穿透组织。超声波在脑组织中基本不受阻，仅遇阻抗变化处（如红细胞、气泡或颅骨）发生背向散射。通过测量这些散射波，可计算重建局部阻抗变化。重建图像分辨率与超声波波长成正比——临床最高频段18MHz成像时，线性分辨率可达约100微米。

That explains how we can measure high-resolution structural images, but what about functional imaging? When neurons fire, they consume energy, which triggers vasodilation and a delayed influx of blood to the active region: hemodynamics. That blood has different properties than baseline: there’s more of it, it moves more rapidly, it has a different oxygen content, and it has a different color. Multiple brain imaging modalities take advantage of this fact, measuring changes in some or all of these properties, including fMRI (BOLD) and fNIRS. fUSI uses the fact that moving red blood cells cause shifting patterns of constructive and destructive interference in backscattered wavefronts, the fine-grained texture in ultrasound images known as speckle. fUSI acquires images of the brain very rapidly—over a kHz—and tracks changes in speckle over time. The amount of change in these images relates to blood flow and volume. The percentage signal change measured with fUSI is remarkably high: up to 20% compared to fMRI’s few-percent BOLD signal.

这解释了高分辨率结构成像，但功能成像呢？神经元放电时耗能，引发血管扩张及延迟性血流涌入活跃区域——即血流动力学。此时血液特性异于基线：量更大、流速更快、含氧量变化且颜色改变。fMRI（BOLD）和fNIRS等多种脑成像技术都利用这些特性变化进行测量。fUSI则基于运动红细胞会改变背向散射波阵面的相长/相消干涉模式（即超声图像中的"散斑"纹理）。fUSI以超千赫兹速率快速采集脑部图像，追踪散斑随时间的变化，其变化程度与血流和血容量相关。fUSI测量的信号变化百分比显著高于fMRI——可达20%，而fMRI的BOLD信号仅几个百分点。

Here’s an intuitive analogy3 for how that works. Imagine a nice high-res image of Brooklyn Bridge at night, taken with a long shutter speed. The surface of the water looks smooth and unbroken. But where is the movement in this image? To track movement, we can take a high-speed movie of the scene and measure how the brightness of each pixel varies in a window of time using the standard deviation. Color coding the resulting image, the shimmering surface of the water appears bright red, even though there may be no bulk flow, ignoring the tide. One could use this to quantify the water’s choppiness, or measure how water reacts to a boat speeding across it. Video analysis can thus reveal flow4.

举个直观类比：想象用慢门拍摄的布鲁克林大桥夜景高清图，水面看起来平滑完整。但运动信息在哪？要追踪运动，可拍摄高速视频并通过标准差计算时间窗内各像素亮度变化。将结果图像色彩编码后，即使忽略潮汐无整体水流，闪烁的水面也会呈现亮红色。此法可量化水面波动，或测量船只掠过时的水纹反应。视频分析由此揭示流动特性。
