I don’t think Ne-Yo is coming back this year.  
我可没押注妮琪·米娜。但习近平将享受国宴礼遇——这非同小可。如今总统不会随意举办国宴，通常还伴有音乐表演。2015年习近平听到R&B歌手尼欧献唱，今年估计不会再现。

All right, let’s talk about substance. People could not have lower expectations for what is coming. It’s the first time this administration has managed expectations correctly in a long while.  
好吧，谈谈实质内容。外界对此次访问的期待值已降至冰点，这倒是本届政府许久以来首次正确管理了预期。  

Julian Gewirtz:  
朱利安·格维茨：  

When Trump went to China in May, the last time we spoke on the podcast, the administration was in a mode of building up expectations: this is going to be a historic visit. Beijing was playing into that too, because for Beijing it was a huge deal to have the President of the United States go there.  
上次播客讨论特朗普五月访华时，政府正极力营造预期：这将是一次历史性访问。北京也配合造势，因为美国总统到访对中方意义重大。  

This time we are not seeing that same amping-up, for a few reasons. One is that this state visit is happening forty days before the US midterm elections. President Trump clearly wants to do it. He’s excited to show off his White House renovations to Xi Jinping. He’s excited to have somebody he sees as a peer on the world stage come through town. But the rest of the apparatus around him is not in the mode of building this one up.  
此次未见类似炒作，原因有三：其一，国事访问距美国中期选举仅四十天。特朗普总统虽热衷于此——他急于向习近平炫耀白宫翻新成果，也乐见这位他眼中的国际舞台同等级人物到访——但其周边团队并未着力造势。  

Second, on the Chinese side, my own view is that there’s a lot of anxiety about how little planning has gone into this. When the Chinese side is hosting, Beijing decides what they’re going to do. In this case, they’re sending Xi Jinping into what a lot of them see as the lion’s den. Washington, DC is not a terribly friendly place. Seventeen percent of Americans have a favorable impression of Xi Jinping. It’s a pretty fraught moment, and both sides are in that mode.  
其二，在我看来中方对筹备不足深感焦虑。当中国做东时，北京掌控全局；而此次他们正将习近平送入许多人眼中的"龙潭虎穴"。华盛顿绝非友善之地——仅17%美国人对习近平有好感。此刻双方都处于高度紧张状态。
