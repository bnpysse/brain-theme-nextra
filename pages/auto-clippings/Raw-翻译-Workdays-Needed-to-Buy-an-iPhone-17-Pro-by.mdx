【文章正文】：  
Workdays Needed to Buy an iPhone 17 Pro, by Country  
各国购买iPhone 17 Pro所需工作日对比  

Key Takeaways  
核心发现  

Workers in Luxembourg and Switzerland need just three eight-hour workdays to afford an iPhone 17 Pro, compared with 160 days in India.  
卢森堡和瑞士的劳动者仅需工作3个八小时工作日即可购买iPhone 17 Pro，而印度则需要160天。  

An average U.S. worker needs four days, while a Canadian or Australian worker needs five.  
美国劳动者平均需要4天，加拿大和澳大利亚则需5天。  

Affordability falls sharply further down the ranking, with Türkiye requiring 89 workdays and Vietnam 99.  
购买力在排名靠后的国家急剧下降——土耳其需89个工作日，越南需99天。  

An iPhone 17 Pro can represent less than a week of average wages in some of the world’s wealthiest economies, but months of work elsewhere.  
在全球最富裕经济体，iPhone 17 Pro的价格还不到一周平均工资，而其他国家则需数月劳动。  

This visualization ranks 33 countries by the number of eight-hour workdays an average worker would need to afford an iPhone 17 Pro with 256GB of storage.  
本图表根据普通劳动者购买256GB版iPhone 17 Pro所需八小时工作日数，对33个国家进行排名。  

The data for this visualization comes from Tenscope. The ranking is based on local iPhone prices and average monthly wages and average weekly hours worked for each country based on the International Labour Organization’s public statistics database.  
数据来源Tenscope，排名依据各国iPhone本地售价、国际劳工组织公开统计数据库中的月均工资及周均工时计算得出。  

Europe Dominates the Most Affordable Markets  
欧洲主导最易购市场  

European countries make up most of the places where the iPhone 17 Pro is easiest to afford. Luxembourg and Switzerland lead at three days, while Belgium, Denmark, the Netherlands, and Norway follow at four days.  
欧洲国家占据iPhone 17 Pro最易购地区多数席位。卢森堡和瑞士以3天居首，比利时、丹麦、荷兰和挪威以4天紧随其后。

Austria, Finland, Ireland, and Germany each require five days of average wages.  
奥地利、芬兰、爱尔兰和德国均需5天平均工资。  

High salaries and relatively strong purchasing power help offset the premium price of Apple’s flagship device in these markets.  
高薪资和较强购买力帮助这些市场抵消苹果旗舰机型的高溢价。  

Europe also contains sizable affordability gaps. Workers in Portugal need 24 days to buy the phone, compared with five in Germany, while Poland requires 17 days and Hungary 27.  
欧洲内部也存在显著购买力差异：葡萄牙需24天，德国仅5天；波兰需17天，匈牙利需27天。  

The U.S. also ranks among the most affordable markets globally, requiring four days of average wages. Canada and Australia each require five.  
美国位列全球最易购市场第四（需4天），加拿大和澳大利亚各需5天。  

Affordability Drops Sharply Across Emerging Markets  
新兴市场购买力骤降  

The number of required workdays rises quickly outside the wealthiest economies.  
在非最富裕经济体，所需工作日数迅速攀升。  

Chile requires 32 days, Malaysia 45, and Thailand 61. Brazil reaches 77 days, while Türkiye requires 89 days and Vietnam 99.  
智利需32天，马来西亚45天，泰国61天。巴西达77天，土耳其89天，越南99天。  

The Philippines crosses the 100-day mark, at 101 days of work.  
菲律宾突破100天门槛，达到101天。  

India Stands Out at 160 Workdays  
印度以160个工作日垫底  

India sits at the bottom of the ranking, with an average worker needing 160 eight-hour workdays to afford the iPhone 17 Pro. That is more than five months of workdays and more than 50 times the requirement in Luxembourg or Switzerland.  
印度位居榜尾，普通劳动者需160个八小时工作日才能购买iPhone 17 Pro，相当于超过五个月工作量，是卢森堡或瑞士需求的50余倍。  

The gap is particularly notable given Apple’s expanding manufacturing footprint in India, including assembly of premium iPhone models.  
考虑到苹果正在印度扩大生产布局（包括高端iPhone组装），这一差距尤为显著。  

Local production alone does not determine affordability. Average wages and the final retail price remain crucial to how much work consumers need to purchase the device.  
本地生产并非决定购买力的唯一因素，平均工资与最终零售价仍影响消费者的购买成本。
