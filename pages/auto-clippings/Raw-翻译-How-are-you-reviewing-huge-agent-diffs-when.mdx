【帖子标题】：How are you reviewing huge agent diffs when multi-agent setups are writing most of the code?
在多智能体编写大部分代码时，你如何审查庞大的差异文件？

【帖子正文】：
Been thinking about this after some Amsterdam talks on agent workflows.
在阿姆斯特丹参加完关于智能体工作流的讨论后，我一直在思考这个问题。

Once you have agents that can plan, implement, and open a PR, writing gets cheaper. The hard part moves to the loop around them: what is done, who reviews, and how you stop babysitting every turn.
当你的智能体能够规划、实现并提交PR（拉取请求）时，编写代码变得更容易了。难点转移到围绕它们的工作循环：哪些任务已完成、由谁审查、以及如何避免每次都要人工干预。

Huge diffs are the sharp edge. A multi-thousand-line agent PR is not something you review the old way line by line. So the useful question becomes: what's the smallest unit of work, what's the definition of done for the agent, and what still needs a human before merge?
庞大的差异文件是最棘手的问题。一个数千行的智能体PR不是你能用传统方式逐行审查的。因此关键问题变成：最小工作单元是什么？如何定义智能体的完成标准？以及在合并前哪些环节仍需人工参与？

Curious how people here are actually shipping with multi-agent setups (plan / implement / review / fix), not just one long chat:
很好奇大家如何实际运用多智能体协作（规划/实现/审查/修复），而非仅通过冗长对话：

What's your loop today? One agent, or several with different jobs?
你们当前的工作循环是怎样的？单一智能体，还是多个分工不同的智能体？

How do you define done so the agent can keep going without you every 30 seconds?
你们如何定义"完成"标准，使智能体无需每30秒就等待你的指令？

When the diff is huge, what do you still review yourself vs hand to another agent (or to Cursor's review tools)?
面对庞大差异文件时，哪些内容你会亲自审查，哪些会交给其他智能体（或Cursor的审查工具）？

War stories from people shipping real PRs welcome.
欢迎分享实际提交PR过程中的实战经验。
