【文章标题】：postmarketOS Rebrand: Nura

【文章标题】：postmarketOS更名公告：新品牌Nura

【文章正文】：

After a one and a half year selection process, the new name of the operating system formerly known postmarketOS has been announced during the mystery talk of our very own conference. From now on this project is known as Nura!

经过一年半的筛选流程，这个曾用名postmarketOS的操作系统在我们大会的神秘演讲环节揭晓了新名称。即日起，本项目正式更名为Nura！

by Sailko, CC-BY-3.0

作者：Sailko，CC-BY-3.0协议

FAQ

常见问题

Why are you renaming the project?

为何要更名？

Through the years, most members of the team have experienced difficulties doing outreach for "postmarketOS" due to the complexity of the name. The name is long and can be very hard to pronounce in many languages. This also has the implication that it gets very hard to remember in those same languages.

多年来，由于名称复杂性，团队多数成员在推广"postmarketOS"时屡遇困境。该名称过长且在多种语言中发音困难，这也导致其在相同语言环境中难以被记住。

The capitalization is also grammatically awkward, with lower-case "p" and "m" but upper-case "OS". We have seen Postmarketos (PostMarketOS?, PostmarketOS?, post-market OS?) written in too many ways, which is confusing to both the reader and the writer.

大小写规则在语法上也显得别扭——"p"和"m"小写而"OS"大写。我们见过Postmarketos/PostMarketOS/post-market OS等过多变体写法，这对读者和作者都造成困扰。

Finally the name is descriptive. Although that can help people understand what the project is about, it also limits the scope of the project in ways that were out-of-touch with reality long ago, for example when PINE64 sold PinePhones with postmarketOS pre-installed. What was initially an asset, became a liability after all these years. The name being descriptive also means it cannot be trademarked. We believe trademarks can be important, as it allows the project to protect users against fraudulent uses of the name, or users clearly misaligned with the project goals. A trademark for the new name has already been filled.

最后，原名称具有描述性。虽然这有助于理解项目内涵，但也早已不切实际地限制了项目范畴——比如当PINE64预装postmarketOS销售PinePhone时。这个曾经的优点经年后已成负担。描述性名称也意味着无法注册商标。我们认为商标很重要，它能保护用户免受名称欺诈使用，或阻止与项目目标明显背离的行为。新名称商标注册已完成。

All in all, "postmarketOS" has served us very well, it brought us to where we are now, and will always be part of our history. At the same time, we believe that to make the project better known outside the hacker community, a rename to a name with less meaning and easier to write, pronounce, and remember would be of benefit of the project.

总之，"postmarketOS"曾完美服务于我们，将项目带到今日高度，并永载史册。同时我们相信，要让项目在黑客圈外获得更高认知度，更名为更易书写、发音、记忆且语义更简练的名称将大有裨益。

Is the logo going to remain?

会保留现有Logo吗？

Yes, we really like the logo and its connection with our mission, and we don't see a need for a change, regardless of the rename.

是的，我们非常喜爱这个Logo及其与使命的关联，认为即使更名也无修改必要。

You announced the initial change in March 2025, and it's now past mid 2026, why did it take so long?

你们2025年3月宣布更名计划，现在已到2026年中，为何耗时这么久？

Changing something that is core to the project identity, like its name, is not something done lightly. Our initial process did not produce the consensus that we expected, so we came up with new approach that resulted in this new name. We have also been waiting for trademark lawyers, and eventually it made sense to wait some weeks to announce it at our conference.

更改项目身份核心要素（如名称）绝非儿戏。初期流程未达成预期共识，因此我们采用新方法才得出这个新名称。同时我们也在等待商标律师流程，最终认为在大会上宣布更合适。

Why is the new domain Nura.eco instead of Nura.org?

为何新域名是Nura.eco而非Nura.org？

Although there is no public site in Nura.org, the domain is taken. We sent an offer to the owner through a broker, but the owner did not consider selling it for a reasonable price. We looked for alternatives and found the eco domain, which we like even more now! Eco is short for ecology and the intended use is: ".eco is open to organizations, businesses and people committed to positive change for the planet". The .eco domain has strong policies which prevent abuse of the domain for greenwashing, adding another layer of accountability. It just fits perfectly with our mission and goals.

尽管Nura.org没有建站，但该域名已被占用。我们通过经纪人报价，但所有者拒绝合理出售。寻找替代方案时发现.eco域名，现在反而更中意！Eco是生态学缩写，其宗旨是".eco向致力于地球积极变革的组织、企业和个人开放"。该域名有严格政策防止绿色洗白滥用，增加责任约束，与我们的使命目标完美契合。

How was the new name chosen?

新名称如何选定？

A selection team composed by Pablo, Pan, Ranny, and Sriram Ramkrishna (thank you all!) was tasked to select good names that would meet the criteria from the 300+ the community shared with us. The team did multiple rounds through the list, evaluated pros and cons of different names, and came up with a proposal of 4 names for the team to choose from. Those names went through further triaging to make sure it didn't have very problematic connotations in as many languages as we could find speakers, from all continents. All of them passed the criteria, and where put to a vote within the team using range voting. Nura was the name with the highest value, and received a 0.4 mean for a vote between -2 and 2. It was therefore chosen as our next name.

由Pablo、Pan、Ranny和Sriram Ramkrishna（感谢各位！）组成的遴选团队负责从社区提供的300+个名称中筛选符合标准的候选名。团队经过多轮评估，列举各名称优劣后提出4个最终候选名。这些名称经过进一步筛选，确保在我们能找到的各大洲语言使用者中不具负面含义。所有候选名达标后，团队采用范围投票法表决。Nura以最高分值（-2至2分区间均分0.4）当选。

Are you doing any other changes to the brand than the name?

除名称外还有其他品牌变更吗？

Yes, there are some small updates to the logo! Ranny identified and fixed some small design inconsistencies in the logo, and we used the name change as an opportunity to update it too. Due to slightly more spacing between the arrows, it is now a bit more legible from further distances. Furthermore it is also now mathematically constructed, so anyone can reproduce it!

有的，Logo有些微调！Ranny发现并修正了Logo中的设计不一致处，我们借更名契机同步更新。因箭头间距略增，远距离辨识度有所提升。此外新版采用数学化构建，任何人都能准确复现。

Why am I still seeing "postmarketOS" used in multiple places?

为何仍见多处使用"postmarketOS"？

The renaming of all user-facing strings might take a while. If you notice a place where we missed something, any contribution in the form of Merge Requests to update it to Nura would be super helpful. If you notice it is taking a while to update, and you do not have the skills to update it, opening issues to remind the team would also be welcomed.

所有面向用户的字符串更新可能需要时间。若发现遗漏之处，提交Merge Request更改为Nura将极有帮助。若发现更新延迟且您无修改能力，提交issue提醒团队也同样欢迎。
