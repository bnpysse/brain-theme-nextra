【文章标题】：Navy Sailor Dies by Suicide After Deployment Keeps Getting Extended

【海军水手在部署期一再延长后自杀身亡】

【文章正文】：

Jaimie Bethea spent 11 months aboard the USS Fort Lauderdale, photographing life at sea, staying in touch with friends and family, and trying to cope with the death of her mother.

21岁的海军通讯专家杰米·贝西亚在"劳德代尔堡号"军舰上度过了11个月，期间她用镜头记录海上生活，与亲友保持联系，同时努力应对母亲离世的悲痛。

The 21-year-old Navy communications specialist took her own life on June 20, weeks before the ship was scheduled to return to Norfolk, Virginia.

6月20日，在这艘军舰预定返回弗吉尼亚州诺福克基地的几周前，这位年轻水手结束了自己的生命。

Now, Bethea’s family is questioning whether the Navy did enough to help her after she began struggling with her mental health during the deployment.

如今，贝西亚的家人质疑海军是否在她驻外期间出现心理健康问题时提供了足够帮助。

"Nothing matters to me more than my sister," Bethea’s older sister, Arianna Calderon, told NPR. "I lost my world, genuinely."

"没有什么比我妹妹更重要，"贝西亚的姐姐阿里安娜·卡尔德隆告诉美国国家公共电台，"我真的失去了我的整个世界。"

Bethea’s deployment came shortly after her mother died of cancer. Calderon told NPR that their mother died about three months before Bethea left on the ship, prompting the Navy to give Bethea a month of shore duty so she could spend time with her mother in a South Carolina hospital.

贝西亚的派遣任务紧随其母因癌症去世之后。卡尔德隆透露，母亲在贝西亚登舰前三个月离世，海军为此批准了贝西亚一个月的岸勤任务，让她能在南卡罗来纳州医院陪伴母亲。

Bethea later sought mental health treatment and doctors prescribed antidepressants, according to her sister.

据其姐姐所述，贝西亚后来寻求了心理健康治疗，医生为其开具了抗抑郁药物。

But once she returned to the ship, Calderon said her sister began having nightmares about their mother.

但卡尔德隆表示，妹妹重返军舰后开始频繁梦见母亲。

"Definitely, I mean, I’m not a psychologist, but she was definitely experiencing symptoms of PTSD," Calderon told NPR.

"虽然我不是心理学家，但她显然出现了创伤后应激障碍的症状，"卡尔德隆说道。

The Navy deployed the USS Fort Lauderdale to the Caribbean as part of a military operation involving Venezuela. According to NPR, its mission included operations related to the capture of Nicolás Maduro, attacks on boats suspected of transporting drugs, and earthquake relief efforts.

海军派遣"劳德代尔堡号"前往加勒比海执行涉及委内瑞拉的军事行动，任务包括针对尼古拉斯·马杜罗的抓捕行动、打击涉嫌运毒船只以及地震救援工作。

The length of the deployment also became a source of stress for Bethea.

漫长的部署期也成为贝西亚的压力源。

Calderon said her sister repeatedly learned that the Navy had extended the ship’s deployment, sometimes with little warning.

卡尔德隆称妹妹多次接到军舰延期部署的通知，有时几乎毫无预警。

"It was constantly like getting texts like, ‘Guys, you’ll never f***ing believe it. We just got extended again,’" Calderon said. She said the extensions happened several times, adding to the uncertainty surrounding when Bethea would finally return home.

"她总发来这样的信息：'伙计们绝对不敢相信，我们又延期了'。"卡尔德隆表示多次延期加剧了归期的不确定性。

Bethea’s friends noticed changes as well.

贝西亚的朋友们也察觉到了异常。

Tiana Cook, a former high school marching band classmate who remained in frequent contact with Bethea, told NPR that the sailor complained about shortages aboard the ship and struggled to consistently obtain her medication.

与贝西亚保持密切联系的高中军乐队同学蒂安娜·库克透露，这位水手曾抱怨舰上物资短缺，且难以持续获得处方药物。

Cook said Bethea described waking up crying, sleeping frequently and feeling exhausted and depressed.

库克表示贝西亚描述过自己惊醒哭泣、嗜睡以及持续疲惫抑郁的状态。

Messages Bethea exchanged with family and friends also showed that she experienced a panic attack that lasted for hours. She sought medical assistance aboard the ship but later told people close to her that she was surprised by how easily she could leave the ship’s hospital.

贝西亚与亲友的通信记录显示，她曾经历持续数小时的惊恐发作。虽然她在舰上寻求过医疗帮助，但事后向亲友表示惊讶于自己能轻易离开舰上医院。

Officials found Bethea early on June 20.

6月20日凌晨，官兵发现了贝西亚的遗体。

Cook said Bethea’s final message to her came around 12:30 a.m.

库克透露收到贝西亚最后信息是在凌晨12:30。

"She said, ‘I love you guys. I need you to know that,’" Cook told NPR. "That was it. That was the last I heard from her."

"她说'我爱你们，必须让你们知道'，"库克回忆道，"这就是诀别。"

After Bethea’s death, Cook and another friend aboard the Fort Lauderdale told her family that Bethea had previously attempted suicide several months into the deployment.

贝西亚去世后，库克和舰上另一位朋友向家属透露，她在部署数月后曾有过自杀未遂经历。

Calderon believes that should have prompted the Navy to intervene.

卡尔德隆认为这理应促使海军采取干预措施。

"There just needed to be some sort of intervention. They should have removed her from the ship," she said.

"当时就需要介入干预，他们早该让她离舰，"她强调。

The Navy has not responded to NPR’s questions about Bethea’s death, according to the report.

报道称海军尚未就贝西亚死亡事件回应质询。

Her death also comes amid broader concerns about mental health resources available to sailors during lengthy deployments.

这起悲剧发生时，正值外界普遍关注长期部署中水兵心理健康资源不足问题。

The Fort Lauderdale carries a crew of about 350 sailors as part of the Iwo Jima Amphibious Ready Group, which deploys roughly 4,500 sailors and Marines. Unlike larger Navy vessels, the Fort Lauderdale lacks onboard mental health specialists. Instead, a medical officer and Navy corpsmen manage daily healthcare, while chaplains handle suicide-prevention support.

作为硫磺岛两栖战备群（约4500名官兵）组成部分，"劳德代尔堡号"载有350名水兵。与大型舰艇不同，该舰未配备专职心理健康专家，日常医疗由医务官和医护兵负责，自杀预防支持则由随舰牧师提供。

A 2023 Department of Defense Inspector General’s report identified a significant shortage of mental health professionals throughout the Navy, including aboard ships.

国防部总监察长2023年报告指出，海军整体（包括舰上）存在心理健康专业人员严重短缺问题。

The issue has received renewed attention as sailors spend longer periods away from home.

随着水兵海外部署时间不断延长，该问题再次引发关注。

The USS Abraham Lincoln recently began returning to San Diego after more than eight months at sea during the war with Iran, according to NPR. The Navy also announced additional mental health initiatives following a cluster of suicides aboard the USS George Washington in 2022.

美国国家公共电台报道，"亚伯拉罕·林肯号"在与伊朗对峙海域执勤八个多月后正返回圣迭戈。2022年"乔治·华盛顿号"连环自杀事件后，海军曾宣布加强心理健康措施。

Acting Secretary of the Navy Hung Cao later announced a Suicide Prevention Task Force to examine Navy practices following concerns about mental health during Lincoln’s extended deployment.

代理海军部长杭·曹随后宣布成立自杀预防特别工作组，以审查"林肯号"超期部署期间暴露的心理健康问题。

Navy officials have acknowledged that lengthy deployments are likely to continue.

海军官员承认长期部署可能持续。

Before the USS Stout departed Norfolk, Capt. Bryan Carmichael told sailors they should prepare for the possibility of spending as long as a year deployed.

"斯托特号"离港前，布莱恩·卡迈克尔舰长告诫水兵需做好驻外长达一年的准备。

"We’re telling them the same thing," Carmichael said, explaining that commanders were trying to keep sailors informed about the potential length of their assignments.

"我们都在传达相同信息，"卡迈克尔解释称指挥官们正努力让水兵清楚任务可能时长。

For Bethea’s family, however, the questions surrounding her death remain unanswered.

但对贝西亚的家人而言，围绕她死亡的诸多疑问仍未得到解答。
