Key Takeaways  
核心发现  

Five of the world’s 10 largest foreign investors in 2025 were tech companies.  
2025年全球十大海外投资者中有五家是科技企业。  

TSMC led the ranking with $100 billion in announced investment tied to its Arizona expansion.  
台积电以1000亿美元亚利桑那州扩建计划投资额位居榜首。  

The top 10 companies accounted for more than a quarter of the $1.3 trillion in new foreign investments announced globally.  
这十家企业的投资额占全球1.3万亿美元新增海外投资总额的四分之一以上。  

In 2025, multinational companies announced more than $1.3 trillion in new foreign investments, up 2.2% from the previous year. The largest commitments spanned semiconductor fabs, data centers, energy projects, and other major infrastructure.  
2025年跨国公司宣布的新增海外投资超1.3万亿美元，同比增长2.2%。最大投资涉及半导体晶圆厂、数据中心、能源项目等重大基础设施。

Why TSMC Invested $100B in Arizona  
台积电千亿投资亚利桑那的背后  

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), the world’s largest semiconductor fabricator, topped the ranking after announcing an additional $100 billion investment in its Arizona operations in 2025.  
全球最大半导体制造商台积电在2025年宣布追加1000亿美元亚利桑那业务投资后登顶榜单。

TSMC first pledged roughly $12 billion in 2020 to open an Arizona fabrication plant. These facilities, known as “fabs,” were designed to reduce semiconductor supply-chain risk by shifting some production away from Taiwan.  
台积电2020年首次承诺120亿美元建设亚利桑那晶圆厂，通过转移部分产能降低对台湾供应链的依赖。  

The company steadily expanded its investment over the following years as U.S.-China tensions increased, particularly around advanced chip technology. TSMC plans to produce some of its most advanced chips in Arizona as part of a 2024 deal with the U.S. government.  
随着中美在先进芯片技术领域摩擦加剧，台积电持续扩大投资，计划根据2024年与美政府协议在亚利桑那生产最尖端芯片。

Free-Flowing Tech Capital  
科技资本的全球流动  

TSMC stood well ahead of the field, but tech companies dominated the ranking overall, taking five of the top 10 spots.  
台积电遥遥领先，但科技企业整体占据榜单主导地位，十强中占五席。  

ByteDance, the Chinese parent company of TikTok, ranked second with $45.1 billion in announced investment. Nearly $40 billion of that total came from plans to build a major data center in Brazil, a project expected to create roughly 5,000 jobs.  
TikTok母公司字节跳动以451亿美元投资额位列第二，其中近400亿美元用于建设巴西大型数据中心，预计创造约5000个岗位。  

Big Tech firms including Alphabet ($25.1 billion) and Microsoft ($17.7 billion) also announced sizable foreign investments. Alphabet subsidiary Google, for example, pledged more than $5 billion for a large data center campus in Belgium to help meet growing demand for Google Cloud.  
Alphabet（251亿美元）、微软（177亿美元）等科技巨头也宣布大规模海外投资。如谷歌承诺超50亿美元建设比利时数据中心园区以满足云服务需求。  

The Non-Tech Firms Want In Too  
非科技企业的数字化布局  

Digital infrastructure also shaped the investment priorities of companies outside the tech sector, particularly in Europe.  
数字基础设施也重塑了非科技企业的投资重点，尤其在欧洲市场。  

Emirati state-owned investment firm MGX Fund Management, for example, focuses heavily on global AI technologies. The company announced about $43.4 billion in investment, including a major French data center project aimed at creating one of Europe’s largest campuses of its kind.  
阿联酋国有投资公司MGX重点布局全球AI技术，宣布434亿美元投资计划，包括建设法国大型数据中心——目标打造欧洲最大同类园区之一。  

Meanwhile, Canadian firm Brookfield Asset Management also targeted Europe’s AI and digital infrastructure market. Brookfield pledged about $28.2 billion in greenfield FDI in 2025, primarily for projects in France and Sweden. Its announced investments are expected to create roughly 4,800 jobs.  
加拿大布鲁克菲尔德资产管理公司同样瞄准欧洲AI与数字基建市场，2025年承诺282亿美元绿地投资，主要投向法瑞两国项目，预计创造4800个就业岗位。
