【文章标题】：9 Ads per Minute: FIFA Cup 26 – "the price of the beautiful game"
【文章标题】：每分钟9条广告：2026年世界杯——"美丽足球的代价"

【文章正文】：
The research, led by the University of Bristol in collaboration with the University of Oxford, is the first to examine harmful commodity branding across an entire FIFA World Cup.
这项由布里斯托大学与牛津大学合作开展的研究，首次对整届 FIFA 世界杯中的有害商品品牌推广进行了全面考察。

The team used the UK’s most powerful supercomputer, Isambard-AI, based at University of Bristol, to analyse all 172.6 hours of live play across the tournament’s 104 matches.
研究团队使用英国最强大的超级计算机——布里斯托大学的Isambard-AI，分析了赛事104场比赛共计172.6小时的直播内容。

The scale of potential exposure was extraordinary. FIFA reported that nearly six billion people around the world engaged with the tournament, while broadcast records were broken in more than 30 countries.
潜在曝光规模令人震惊。国际足联报告显示全球近60亿人关注赛事，30多个国家的转播记录被打破。

For the UK, the researchers estimated that harmful product advertising generated 267 billion impressions across the 53 broadcasts for which audience data were available.
仅在英国，研究人员估算有害产品广告在53场可获得观众数据的转播中产生了2670亿次曝光。

The analysis counted advertising built into the match footage (e.g. on the hoardings), rather than commercials shown during advertising breaks.
分析统计的是比赛画面中内置的广告（如场边广告牌），而非广告时段播放的商业广告。

Harmful product branding appeared in every match and was visible during 39.3 hours of live play, almost a quarter of all live play.
每场比赛都出现有害产品品牌标识，在39.3小时直播时间内可见，占全部直播时长的近四分之一。

Viewers could not avoid this marketing without also missing some of the football.
观众若想避开这些营销内容，就不得不错过部分比赛画面。

Unhealthy food and drink brands were found to dominate the tournament's sponsorship, representing more than seven in ten (70.4%) of all branding detected.
研究发现不健康食品饮料品牌占据赛事赞助主导地位，占所有检测到品牌标识的70.4%。

This was followed by prediction markets with 9,360 appearances, alcohol with 9,266, gambling with 6,429 and cryptocurrency with 2,632.
其次是预测市场（9360次）、酒精饮料（9266次）、博彩（6429次）和加密货币（2632次）。

Exposure was heavily concentrated among a handful of globally recognised brands.
曝光高度集中在少数全球知名品牌。

A familiar soft drink brand accounted for the highest number of logos (21,893), followed by a major fast-food chain (13,915), a sports drink brand (12,777), and a popular crisps brand (12,087).
某知名碳酸饮料品牌标识出现次数最多（21893次），其次是某大型快餐连锁（13915次）、运动饮料品牌（12777次）和流行薯片品牌（12087次）。

Together, these four brands were responsible for 65% of all harmful commodity logos recorded during the tournament.
这四个品牌合计占赛事期间记录到的所有有害商品标识的65%。

Dr Raffaello Rossi, Senior Lecturer in Marketing and Co-Director of the Bristol Hub for Gambling Harms Research at the University of Bristol, and co-author of the study, said: "As one of the world’s most watched sporting events, reaching billions of people, this year’s FIFA World Cup provided an unparalleled platform for advertising.
该研究合著者、布里斯托大学市场营销高级讲师兼赌博危害研究布里斯托中心联合主任Raffaello Rossi博士表示："作为全球收视最高的体育赛事之一，本届世界杯覆盖数十亿观众，提供了无与伦比的广告平台。

Our findings reveal the sheer scale of branding embedded throughout tournament broadcasts, making exposure to unhealthy food, alcohol and gambling brands both omnipresent and unavoidable for viewers.
我们的研究揭示了赛事转播中植入品牌的庞大规模，使得不健康食品、酒精和博彩品牌的曝光对观众而言既无处不在又难以避免。

“This is particularly concerning given the substantial body of evidence linking marketing for unhealthy products, alcohol and gambling with increased consumption and a range of health, social and financial harms, especially among children and young people.”
"考虑到大量证据表明不健康产品、酒精和博彩的营销与消费增长及一系列健康、社会和财务危害相关，尤其在儿童和青少年群体中，这一现象特别令人担忧。"

Dr Elisa Becker, Researcher, Nuffield Department of Primary Care Health Sciences, University of Oxford, co-author of this study added: “We are facing a global crisis of childhood obesity and diet-related disease, yet the world’s biggest sporting event is saturated with marketing for unhealthy food and drinks.
该研究合著者、牛津大学纳菲尔德初级保健健康科学系研究员Elisa Becker博士补充道："我们正面临儿童肥胖和饮食相关疾病的全球危机，然而世界最大体育赛事却充斥着不健康食品饮料的营销。

Sport should be part of the solution to poor health – not one of the world’s most powerful advertising platforms for the products driving it.”
体育本应是解决健康问题的方案之一，而非助推这些产品的全球最强广告平台。"

A key issue highlighted by the research is that FIFA’s match footage, which featured the same embedded advertising around the pitch, was shared with broadcasters around the world, including in countries where alcohol, gambling or prediction markets advertising may be restricted or illegal.
研究强调的关键问题是：国际足联将带有相同场边植入广告的比赛画面提供给全球转播商，包括那些限制或禁止酒精、博彩或预测市场广告的国家。

Despite existing virtual board replacement technology which enables different advertising to be shown in different countries, findings from this report suggest FIFA’s commercial model failed to adequately account for national laws and cultural norms governing the promotion of harmful commodities.
尽管现有虚拟广告牌替换技术可实现不同国家展示不同广告，但本报告发现国际足联的商业模式未能充分考虑各国关于有害商品推广的法律和文化规范。

FIFA did not use this technology, and distributed World Cup footage containing embedded branding to countries where the use, advertisement, or purchase of some promoted products is not legal.
国际足联未使用该技术，向那些禁止某些推广产品使用、广告或购买的国家分发了含有植入品牌的世界杯画面。

Dr Rossi added: “FIFA’s marketing deals appeared to show little respect for national laws and licensing regimes.
Rossi博士补充道："国际足联的营销协议似乎对各国法律和许可制度缺乏尊重。

The tournament featured around 16,000 gambling and prediction markets logos, with this advertising reaching a host of countries where the promoted brands were unlicensed, or where their legal status was uncertain.
赛事中出现约16000个博彩和预测市场标识，这些广告覆盖多个推广品牌未获许可或法律地位不明的国家。

Indeed, one of the most advertised gambling brands listed licences in around 20 countries, while the World Cup was broadcast across more than 220 territories.
事实上，某个广告最多的博彩品牌仅在大约20个国家持有牌照，而世界杯转播覆盖超过220个地区。

Put plainly, audiences in the overwhelming majority of countries were shown advertising for a gambling operator that was not licensed locally.
简言之，绝大多数国家的观众都看到了未获当地许可的博彩运营商广告。

“What makes this particularly disappointing is that the problem was avoidable.
"尤其令人失望的是这个问题本可避免。

Technology previously used by the UEFA EURO 2024, already allows different countries to see different pitchside advertising, yet it was not used to remove or replace branding where products could not lawfully be advertised or offered.”
欧足联2024欧洲杯已采用的技术本可实现不同国家观看不同场边广告，却未被用于移除或替换那些产品广告非法的品牌标识。"

The researchers call for governments and regulators to ensure that marketing rules apply to commercial branding embedded within live sports broadcasts, backed by meaningful penal
研究人员呼吁各国政府和监管机构确保营销规则适用于体育直播中的商业品牌植入，并辅以有效处罚措施。
