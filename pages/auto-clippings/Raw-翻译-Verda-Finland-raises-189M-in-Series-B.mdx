【文章标题】：Verda (Finland) raises $189M in Series B  
【文章标题】：芬兰Verda公司完成1.89亿美元B轮融资  

【文章正文】：  
Verda became Europe’s latest unicorn today, raising $189 million in new funding.  
Verda今日成为欧洲最新独角兽企业，完成1.89亿美元新一轮融资。  

The oversubscribed Series B was led by Emergence Capital, with MUFG Innovation Partners, Supermicro, Varma Mutual Pension Insurance Company, Lifeline Ventures, 6 Degrees Capital (6DC), byFounders, Tesi (Finnish Industry Investment Ltd), and a group of angel investors including Ola Tørudbakken and Mark Saroufim participating.  
本轮超额认购的B轮融资由Emergence Capital领投，三菱UFJ创新伙伴、超微电脑、Varma互助养老保险公司、Lifeline Ventures、6 Degrees Capital（6DC）、byFounders、芬兰产业投资有限公司（Tesi）以及包括Ola Tørudbakken和Mark Saroufim在内的天使投资人参投。  

That brings our total funding to date to over $450 million across equity and debt.  
这使得公司通过股权和债权融资累计获得超4.5亿美元资金。  

"There's a window right now to build one of the defining compute companies of this generation, and to do so from Europe. It won't be open for long." — Ruben Bryon, founder and CEO  
"当前正是打造这代决定性计算公司的机遇窗口，而且要从欧洲起步。这个机会转瞬即逝。"——创始人兼CEO Ruben Bryon  

Building the AI Cloud of tomorrow  
构建未来AI云  

For the last few years, access to capacity has been a procurement problem.  
过去几年间，算力获取始终是采购难题。  

Teams negotiated for months, took delivery of a fixed environment, and organized their workload around whatever they'd been allocated.  
团队需历经数月谈判，接收固定配置环境，再根据分配资源调整工作负载。  

That worked when capacity was scarce and workloads were predictable.  
这在算力稀缺且工作负载可预测时还算可行。  

It doesn't anymore. Training runs that once took months of advance planning now need to start on infrastructure that's already there.  
如今情况已然不同。曾经需要数月规划的模型训练，现在必须基于现成基础设施立即启动。  

Production inference, where latency and availability aren't negotiable, needs capacity that shows up when the workload demands it.  
生产级推理对延迟和可用性有严苛要求，必须实现工作负载随需随用的算力供给。  

Agentic workloads raise the bar even more, with context that builds across many turns and demand that comes in bursts, spiking the moment a tool call returns.  
智能体工作负载要求更高，需在多轮交互中累积上下文，且突发需求会在工具调用返回时瞬间激增。  

Deciding how much capacity to get and when, increasingly sits with a handful of engineers, not a procurement team working an annual cycle.  
关于算力规模与时机的决策权，正从按年度采购的团队转移到少数工程师手中。  

Verda is building the full stack to meet that need.  
Verda正在构建全栈解决方案应对这一需求。  

We design and build our own data centers, the infrastructure inside them, and the platform that runs on top, enabling customers to get the capacity how, when, and where they need it, while operating it at a lower carbon footprint than the industry average.  
我们自主设计建造数据中心及其内部基础设施，并开发上层平台，让客户能按需获取算力，同时保持低于行业平均的碳足迹。  

This raise deepens all three parts of that stack, with more data center capacity coming online and deeper investment in the platform, the AI Lab, and the features our customers are already using.  
本轮融资将强化全栈三要素：新增数据中心容量上线，加大对平台、AI实验室及现有功能的投入。  

Teams already run their production AI on Verda  
企业级AI已在Verda平台运行  

Verda now powers AI workloads for organizations across 50+ countries, from early-stage startups to enterprises.  
Verda目前为50多个国家的早期初创企业至大型企业提供AI算力支持。  

We reached a $165 million annualized revenue run rate in July 2026, with a team of over 250 people across Helsinki, London, Taipei, and San Francisco.  
2026年7月我们实现1.65亿美元年化营收，赫尔辛基、伦敦、台北和旧金山四地团队超250人。  

Our GPU clusters power Aleph Alpha’s R&D infrastructure, with engineers-in-residence collaborating on the underlying software stack.  
Aleph Alpha的研发基础设施由我们的GPU集群驱动，驻场工程师正共同开发底层软件栈。  

Magnific serves media generation at millions of requests per day on our infrastructure.  
Magnific依托我们基础设施每日处理数百万次媒体生成请求。  

Epsilon Health trains its radiology AI models on a dedicated Verda cluster, at native image resolution.  
Epsilon Health在专属Verda集群上以原生图像分辨率训练放射科AI模型。  

Our AI Lab lives on the platform our customers use  
AI实验室与客户共用平台  

We build our own compilers and serving software, and do our own performance and reliability engineering.  
我们自研编译器与服务软件，自主完成性能与可靠性工程。  

Our AI Lab is a dedicated team that runs real research workloads on the platform, working on solving hard engineering problems like improving GPU utilization, inference optimization, and kernel engineering.  
AI实验室团队直接在平台上运行真实研究负载，致力于攻克GPU利用率提升、推理优化和内核工程等难题。  

That work means customers get more out of their infrastructure, and it’s what shapes our product roadmap, as the Lab runs into what needs building before a customer has to ask for it.  
这些工作既提升客户基础设施效能，也指引产品路线图——实验室总能先于客户需求发现待建功能。  

"What we didn't expect was a team of engineers who could help us design our own data streaming and cluster management, not just hand us a cluster and walk away." - Arjun Karpur, Head of Machine Learning, Epsilon Health  
"没想到他们能协助设计数据流与集群管理系统，而非简单交付集群就离开。"——Epsilon Health机器学习负责人Arjun Karpur  

What’s next  
未来规划  

More capacity, and the power to run it: We will have more than 250 MW of operations in 2027, with data centre capacity live in Finland today and more coming online in Europe, the UK, the US and Asia.  
扩充算力规模与运行能力：2027年运营容量超250兆瓦，当前芬兰数据中心已启用，欧洲、英国、美国和亚洲更多节点即将上线。  

That also means getting the latest infrastructure to our customers as it’s available. We anticipate early deployments of NVIDIA VR200 NVL72 in the coming months.  
这也意味着及时为客户提供最新基础设施。NVIDIA VR200 NVL72芯片将于未来数月率先部署。  

Inference, at scale: The workloads customers run today already look different from a few years ago, longer sessions, context accumulating over many turns, and agentic use spiking demand.  
规模化推理：当前客户工作负载已与几年前大不相同——会话时长增加、多轮上下文累积、智能体使用引发需求峰值。  

For the AI teams, the challenge isn't just serving one of those sessions well, it's serving thousands of them at once without one team's burst becoming another's problem.  
对AI团队而言，挑战不仅在于妥善处理单次会话，更要同时处理数千会话且避免突发流量相互干扰。  

We are investing in providing access to models for inference, tuned for how they're used.  
我们正投资优化推理模型接入方案，使其适配实际使用场景。  

Faster provisioning: We're focused on improving provisioning and setup times so they don’t become bottlenecks for AI workloads.  
加速配置：着力优化资源供给与设置时间，避免其成为AI工作负载瓶颈。  

That means faster spin-up for instances and clusters, quicker storage attachment, and infrastructure that’s fast to respond once it's running.  
具体包括加速实例/集群启动、快速挂载存储、以及基础设施运行时的敏捷响应。  

Platform updates: We continue to broaden our product portfolio to offer AI teams every capability they need across the AI lifecycle.  
平台更新：持续扩展产品组合，为AI团队提供全生命周期所需能力。  

Following the recent release of Container registry, we are on path to release S3-compatible Object storage.  
继近期发布容器注册表后，我们即将推出S3兼容对象存储。

即时集群现已支持通过Slinky工具在部署时预配置Kueue或Slurm调度的Kubernetes，随后将发布万众期待的Ma...
