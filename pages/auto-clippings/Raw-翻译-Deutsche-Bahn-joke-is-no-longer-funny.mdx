【文章标题】：Deutsche Bahn "joke" is no longer funny

德国铁路的"笑话"不再好笑

【文章正文】：

Your Deutsche Bahn "joke" is no longer funny (if it ever was)

你们关于德国铁路的"笑话"已经不好笑了（如果曾经好笑过的话）

Before starting my record attempt between Kemijärvi, Finland and Lagos, Portugal, people told me over and over it would break down in Germany. So much so that I even tried to re-but (pre-but?) some of the likely critique.

在开始芬兰凯米耶尔维和葡萄牙拉各斯之间的破纪录旅程前，人们反复告诫我列车会在德国抛锚。以至于我甚至试图预先反驳（pre-but？）一些可能的批评。

Now, having made the trip, and none of the total delay of 25 hours 22 minutes (making the journey 125 hours 31 minutes, rather than the 100 hours 9 minutes foreseen) was attributable to Germany. 24 hours was Sweden, the rest Portugal.

如今完成旅程后，总计25小时22分钟的延误（使旅程耗时125小时31分钟而非预计的100小时9分钟）没有一分钟是德国造成的——24小时延误在瑞典，其余在葡萄牙。

But the "jokes" keep on coming - just see the comments under this LinkedIn post from The European Correspondent for example. Ha ha, 100 hours of the trip must have been Germany. Oh aren't you funny!

但这些"笑话"仍在继续——看看《欧洲通讯》LinkedIn帖子下的评论就知道。哈哈，100小时延误肯定都在德国。哦，你们可真幽默！

There is a point at 31:27 in the film about the project that underlines the issue. I am in Mannheim, and there are three different trains departing to Basel within an hour.

项目纪录片31分27秒有个场景凸显了问题本质：我在曼海姆，一小时内就有三班不同列车开往巴塞尔。

Between Badajoz and Entroncamento - currently the only train running into Portugal from Spain at all (because Porto - Vigo is currently a bus between Valença do Minho and Vigo) - there are two trains a day, and they both run in the afternoon, and it is a railbus built in 1955 with lousy air conditioning.

巴达霍斯至恩特朗卡门托区间（目前西班牙进入葡萄牙的唯一线路，因为波尔图-维戈段现由瓦伦萨-维戈的巴士替代）每天仅有两班列车，都在下午发车，且是195年制造的空调破旧的轨道巴士。

In northern Sweden it is little better. One third of the country (north of Umeå) relies on a single, single track, electrified line, and places like Haparanda and Kiruna hence have a handful of passenger trains a day.

瑞典北部情况稍好。该国三分之一地区（于默奥以北）依赖单线电气化铁路，哈帕兰达和基律纳等地每天仅有寥寥几班客运列车。

But pretty much no one is complaining about Badajoz, because no one takes that train there. They drive. Rail is not an option to get from Spain to Portugal. As I put it in a recent newsletter, passengers do not complain about trains they cannot take, because those trains do not exist.

但几乎没人抱怨巴达霍斯线路，因为根本没人乘坐——人们选择自驾。铁路不是西葡之间的可行选择。正如我在最近通讯中所说：乘客不会抱怨他们坐不到的列车，因为那些列车根本不存在。

Now, don't get me wrong - German rail has major issues, most notably the investment backlog that means things break down all the time with the tracks, signals, bridges etc., and so reliability is poor. But given the network is so dense, and there are different routes you can take between all the different cities, you will normally get there in the end.

别误会——德国铁路确实存在严重问题，尤其是投资积压导致轨道、信号灯、桥梁等设施持续故障，可靠性低下。但得益于密集的铁路网和城市间的多线路选择，最终总能到达目的地。

And despite it all, the complaints are so numerous because the number of passengers and the number of trains is also so numerous! If a train on a track with 3 trains a day in France gets delayed, there will be no knock on effect on any other train. A delay to a train on a track with 3 trains every 10 minutes in Germany will have a much larger impact.

正因如此，抱怨声才如此汹涌——乘客量和列车班次实在庞大！法国某条每天3班列车的线路延误不会影响其他车次，而德国某条每10分钟3班列车的线路延误就会产生连锁反应。

Ultimately any German who has ever voted for any party other than the Grüne also deserves what they get. The Third Way / Die neue Mitte of Gerhard Schröder from the late 1990s is at the root of all of this, but twelve years of CSU transport ministers left their mark as well, and while Volker Wissing (FDP) redeemed himself as at least being better than his party, the current government looks to be back to where it all was pre-Wissing. So if you're laughing at Deutsche Bahn, and voted for one of those parties that helped cook up the current situation, well, this is at least in part on you.

说到底，任何投票给绿党以外政党的德国人都该自食其果。施罗德90年代末推行的"第三条道路"是问题根源，基社盟执掌交通部长12年同样难辞其咎。虽然自民党的维辛至少比他的党派争气，但现政府似乎又回到了维辛之前的糟糕状态。所以如果你既嘲笑德国铁路，又曾投票给那些造就现状的政党，那么至少部分责任在你。

And if you think this is just rail with a problem, well I have a Berlin-Brandenburg airport, or cracking bridges on German Autobahns to show you.

若你认为这只是铁路的问题，那我还可以给你看看柏林勃兰登堡机场，或是德国高速公路上开裂的桥梁。

Meanwhile my ICE, a Siemens built ICE3neo, is somewhere between Aachen and Liège as I hit Publish on this post. It's well engineered, smooth, quiet, the on board staff impeccable, and I booked it on Europe's best rail website - bahn.de, and today it is even on time. Give me this over the railbus to Entroncamento any day!

此刻当我点击发布时，这辆西门子制造的ICE3neo正行驶在亚琛与列日之间。它工艺精良、运行平稳、环境静谧，乘务人员无可挑剔，我通过欧洲最佳铁路网站bahn.de预订，今天甚至准点到达。比起恩特朗卡门托的轨道巴士，我永远选择ICE！
