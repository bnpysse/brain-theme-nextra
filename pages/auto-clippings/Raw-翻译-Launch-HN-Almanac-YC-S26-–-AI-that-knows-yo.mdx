Proactively gets things done for you
Almanac uses your connected tools and its wiki to notice what needs doing, do it, and tell you when it’s done.
Questions
Asked and answered.
What can it actually access?
Only the accounts you connect. Almanac reads them to keep the wiki current and acts through them to get work done. Every connection is visible, and you can revoke any of them.
Do my teammates see my stuff?
No. An account you connect stays usable only by you. What flows into the shared wiki is the useful understanding: the decision, not your inbox. Shared accounts are added explicitly by the organization.
Can I read and edit the wiki myself?
Yes. It’s a real wiki. Browse it, correct it, add to it. Almanac keeps it current; your edits are part of what it knows.
What if the wiki is wrong?
Every line links back to its source, so you can check the receipt. Correct the page and Almanac works from the correction from then on.
Does it act without asking me?
It works on what you hand it, and it notices things worth doing on its own. But at a login, a payment, or a decision it shouldn't make alone, it pings you first — or hands you the live browser. You can watch every step of a run.
Is this just a chatbot with integrations?
Integrations fetch on demand; they don’t remember. Almanac maintains the wiki continuously and works from a computer of its own, so it starts already caught up and keeps going after you leave.
Why not just run Claude or Codex on my laptop?
You can — until you close the lid. Almanac runs on its own always-on computer, stays signed into your tools, and keeps a wiki of your context that is still there tomorrow. You delegate; it reports back.
Does my laptop need to stay open?
No. Almanac runs on its own machine. Start from the app, Slack, or your texts, close the device, and the finished work finds you.
Does it replace Notion, Linear, or Slack?
No. Your team keeps working where it already works. Almanac connects to those tools, learns from them, and acts across them.

已解答疑问
实际能访问哪些数据？
仅限你授权的账户。Almanac通过读取这些账户更新知识库，并通过它们执行任务。所有连接可见且可随时撤销
同事会看到我的私人内容吗？
不会。你连接的账户仅限你自己使用。共享知识库只包含有效决策信息，而非收件箱内容。组织账户需显式添加
能否手动编辑知识库？
可以。这是真正的Wiki。你可以浏览、修正、补充内容。Almanac会保持更新，你的编辑也会成为它的知识来源
知识库出错怎么办？
每行内容都链接至数据源供核对。修正页面后，Almanac会基于新版本继续工作
会未经请示自动操作吗？
它会处理你交付的任务，并自主发现待办事项。但遇到登录验证、支付或重大决策时，会先征求你的意见——或转交实时浏览器界面。你可以监控每个执行步骤
这难道不是带集成的聊天机器人？
普通集成按需调取且没有记忆。Almanac持续维护知识库并拥有独立计算机系统，因此能保持工作连续性
为何不用本地运行的Claude或Codex？
当然可以——直到你合上笔记本。Almanac运行在永不停机的计算机上，保持工具登录状态，并维护持久化的上下文知识库。你只需下达指令，它会持续反馈
需要保持笔记本开机吗？
不需要。Almanac运行在独立设备上。通过应用、Slack或短信启动任务后，即使关闭设备也能接收完成通知
会取代Notion/Linear/Slack吗？
不会。团队仍在原工具中工作。Almanac负责连接这些工具，从中学习并实现跨平台协作
