【文章标题】：The Dot and the Swarm

【文章标题】：点与群

【文章正文】：

I generally think I have done a good job anticipating the direction and pace of AI over the few years I have been writing this Substack, but I think I recently got something fairly large wrong.

我自认为在撰写这个Substack的几年里，对AI发展方向和速度的预判总体不错，但最近却在一个重要问题上犯了错。

In the last year I have been posting about how I suspected that humans would have to approach working with agents as a manager, deciding how to delegate work to agents and specifying how those agents should be organized.

过去一年我一直在阐述一个观点：人类需要以管理者的身份与AI智能体协作，决定任务分配方式并设计组织架构。

I thought that getting agents to work effectively as a group would take careful construction, akin to building a company, and that this would take time to figure out.

我曾认为让智能体高效协同工作需要精心设计——就像组建公司那样——这需要长期探索。

Nope.

然而并非如此。

I fell prey to The Bitter Lesson, the hard truth, learned over and over again, that things that we thought required elaborate human rules and thinking can be solved with the brute force of better machine learning systems and more AI.

我陷入了"苦涩教训"的陷阱——这个被反复验证的残酷真相告诉我们：原以为需要复杂人类规则和思维的事情，其实可以通过更强大的机器学习系统和更多AI算力粗暴解决。

The Bitter Lesson is everywhere among AI startups and companies adopting AI.

这个教训在AI初创公司和应用企业中随处可见。

A huge amount of effort went into building elaborate computer systems to feed AIs the right information at the right time, but AI systems have learned to seek out information themselves.

人们曾耗费巨资构建精密系统来定时定量给AI投喂信息，结果AI自己学会了主动获取信息。

The same thing happened to prompting.

提示工程也遭遇同样命运。

People built elaborate templates and chains of prompts that walked the AI through a task one step at a time.

人们设计复杂的提示模板和链条，让AI按部就班执行任务。

Then newer models turned out to be better at planning the steps themselves, and, as our research shows, planning steps have much less value.

但新一代模型反而更擅长自主规划步骤——我们的研究表明，人工规划的价值正在锐减。

The history of the Bitter Le—

"苦涩教训"的历史轨迹——

— you know what? I don’t really need to explain the Bitter Lesson, I asked Claude to do it in a music video.

——知道吗？其实不必我来解释，我让Claude用音乐视频演绎了这个概念。

With one prompt, Fable wrote the lyrics and submitted it to Suno; Opus 5.5 did everything else using code alone without any image generation (How did Opus 5.5 pull this off? The Bitter Lesson tells you!).

仅用一条提示，Fable就写好歌词交给Suno；Opus 5.5仅靠代码就完成剩余工作（连图像生成都没用！怎么做到的？"苦涩教训"就是答案！）。

I gave no feedback at all.

而我全程零反馈。

As somebody who teaches managers and has published research on management, I guess I believed that managing agents would be different.

作为管理学教授和研究学者，我曾坚信管理智能体会是例外。

Humans have been working on management for a very long time without fully figuring it out.

人类钻研管理学问数千年仍未参透精髓。

It seemed like the kind of thing that would need to be designed by people, at least for a while.

这看起来像是必须由人类设计的领域——至少短期内如此。

It turns out that organizing work is just one more thing AI can learn to do.

结果证明，工作组织不过是AI能自学的又一项技能。

Which brings us to dots and Muse.

这就引出了dots和Muse的故事。

Dots and Muse

点与缪斯

The number one app in the App Store right now is Meta’s Muse, a personal agent that promises to do work for you.

当前App Store榜首是Meta的Muse——承诺替你完成工作的个人智能体。

OpenAI has now released a competitor tool, called dots.

OpenAI则推出竞品dots。

They aren’t alone: SpaceX’s Grok Bot, Instinct, and Gemini Spark all do similar things, more or less.

同行者众：SpaceX的Grok Bot、Instinct和Gemini Spark都在做类似尝试。

All of these agents draw inspiration from a phenomenon you might remember from earlier this year, OpenClaw.

这些智能体都源自今年初的现象级产品OpenClaw。

The idea of OpenClaw and its successors, which I will call Clawlikes, is that they give an AI agent access to a computer and connect to your accounts (emails, financial records, etc.).

OpenClaw及其后继者（我称之为"类爪"）的核心思路是：让AI智能体接入电脑并关联你的账户（邮箱、财务记录等）。

They analyze and react to that data in real time, even when you aren’t looking.

即使你未主动查看，它们也能实时分析并响应数据。

The trick is that you talk to the model like you would a person, sending it messages on Slack or SMS or WhatsApp, and it also proactively reaches out to you, like a person would.

关键在于你能像与人交流那样通过Slack/SMS/WhatsApp发送消息，智能体也会像人一样主动联系你。

For dots, you can actually jump on a call with your agent as well.

dots甚至支持直接语音通话。

You basically get an infinitely patient personal assistant that looks out for you.

本质上你获得了一个永不疲倦的全能私人助理。

Increasingly, I have discovered that they are finding my mistakes, rather than having me identify theirs.

我越发发现是它们在揪我的错，而非我来指正它们。

As one useful example, one of my personal agents contacted me because an email I sent to our town for a permit had the wrong project number on it.

典型案例：我的智能体曾因发现许可证申请邮件写错项目编号而联系我。

The catch was that I was the one who made the mistake, and I am not 100% sure how the AI spotted the error.

讽刺的是犯错的是我，而且至今不明白AI如何发现这个错误。

Fortunately, it helpfully wrote a draft correcting the issue, so that is good (if a little freaky).

所幸它直接拟好了更正草案——虽然细想有点惊悚。

As another example, Muse noticed that an airline credit of mine was about to expire and, when I asked, contacted American to request an extension.

另一个例子：Muse发现我的航空积分即将到期，经我同意后主动联系美航申请延期。

(As a side effect, every firm's customer service agents are about to be overwhelmed with Clawlikes negotiating for better deals using voice and chat channels made for humans).

（副作用是：各企业客服即将被通过人工沟通渠道讨价还价的"类爪"智能体淹没）

It is tempting to judge these agents by the list of things they can do, like booking travel or canceling subscriptions.

人们容易用"订机票""退订服务"等功能清单来评判这些智能体。

I think the more important thing is what you no longer have to tell them.

但我认为关键在于"你不再需要交代什么"。

You don’t need to type in tons of context, the AI learns it from your messages.

不必输入大量背景信息——AI能从往来消息中自学。

You don’t have to give them a plan, they develop plans themselves.

无需提供计划——它们能自主规划。

They figure it out.

它们会自己搞定。

That would be impressive enough if it were one agent.

单个智能体已足够惊艳。

What actually changed my mind about management is what happens when there are thousands of them.

真正颠覆我管理认知的，是成千上万智能体协同时的景象。

Pre-order my new book!

新书预售中！
