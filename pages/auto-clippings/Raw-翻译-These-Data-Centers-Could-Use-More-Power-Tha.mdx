【文章标题】：These Data Centers Could Use More Power Than Major Cities
这些数据中心的耗电量可能超过大城市

【文章正文】：
How Data Center Power Demand Compares to U.S. Cities
数据中心电力需求与美国城市对比

Key Takeaways
核心要点

Meta’s planned Hyperion data center has a full-power annual energy equivalent of 43.8 TWh, approaching New York City’s electricity use.
Meta规划的Hyperion数据中心满负荷年能耗达438亿千瓦时，接近纽约市用电量

Amazon’s AWS Susquehanna Data Campus has an annual energy equivalent greater than Philadelphia’s electricity consumption.
亚马逊AWS Susquehanna数据园区年能耗超过费城用电总量

Vantage’s Port Washington campus could have an annual energy equivalent roughly four times Madison’s electricity use.
Vantage的Port Washington园区年能耗约为麦迪逊市用电量的四倍

Data centers are drawing increasing scrutiny across the United States as their electricity and water requirements grow alongside the country’s AI infrastructure buildout.
随着美国AI基础设施建设推进，数据中心对水电需求的激增正引发全美范围日益严格的审查

This visualization compares three major planned data centers with six U.S. city electricity benchmarks. Data comes from 2026 corporate and regulatory filings, as well as the latest published consumption figures from city press offices.
本可视化对比了三座规划中的大型数据中心与六个美国城市用电基准数据，数据源自2026年企业申报文件、监管备案及城市新闻办最新公布的用电数据

All figures are in terawatt-hours (TWh), a standard unit of electrical energy. Because the data centers are planned or under development, their figures assume continuous operation at stated future power levels rather than forecasting actual electricity consumption.
所有数据均采用标准电能单位太瓦时(TWh)。由于数据中心处于规划建设阶段，其数值是基于未来满负荷持续运行的假设，而非实际用电预测

Meta’s Manhattan in the Bayou
Meta在沼泽地打造的"曼哈顿"

Facebook parent company Meta is planning a massive data center in Louisiana. Hyperion, as the project has been nicknamed, is set to have an annual energy equivalent of 43.8 TWh at full operation.
Facebook母公司Meta计划在路易斯安那州建设巨型数据中心。该项目代号Hyperion，满负荷年能耗将达438亿千瓦时

That would be nearly eight times the 5.6 TWh of electricity consumed by New Orleans in 2025. Perhaps even more strikingly, Hyperion’s annual equivalent would sit just below New York City’s forecast annual electricity use of 46.9 TWh.
这相当于新奥尔良2025年预计用电量56亿千瓦时的近8倍。更惊人的是，Hyperion年能耗仅略低于纽约市469亿千瓦时的预测年用电量

The New York comparison is particularly fitting. Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced in July 2025 that the $50 billion facility would span nearly the size of Manhattan.
与纽约的对比尤为贴切。Meta CEO马克·扎克伯格2025年7月宣布，这个500亿美元的设施占地面积将接近曼哈顿岛大小

Hyperion, formally known as the Richland Parish Data Center, is expected to partially come online by 2030 before reaching full build-out in 2032.
Hyperion正式名称为Richland教区数据中心，预计2030年部分投入运营，2032年全面建成

Amazon’s $20 Billion Pennsylvania Bet
亚马逊在宾州的200亿美元豪赌

At full capacity, the Amazon Web Services (AWS) Susquehanna Data Campus will reportedly have an annual energy equivalent of 16.8 TWh—roughly 30% more than Philadelphia’s 13.1 TWh of electricity use in 2022.
据报道，亚马逊云服务(AWS)Susquehanna数据园区满负荷年能耗将达168亿千瓦时，比费城2022年131亿千瓦时的用电量高出约30%

The Susquehanna facility is part of Amazon’s larger $20 billion investment in digital infrastructure in Pennsylvania. Governor Josh Shapiro described the investment by the world’s largest company by revenue as the biggest capital investment in state history.
Susquehanna设施是亚马逊在宾州200亿美元数字基础设施投资的一部分。州长Josh Shapiro称这笔全球营收最高企业的投资是宾州史上最大资本投入

Another notable feature is the campus’s proximity to the Susquehanna nuclear power plant. Amazon’s plans to source power from the neighboring facility have faced regulatory scrutiny from federal authorities.
另一显著特点是园区毗邻Susquehanna核电站。亚马逊从邻近核电站购电的计划正面临联邦监管机构的审查

Under an agreement between AWS and the nuclear plant’s operator, the data center would obtain roughly 40% of the plant’s energy output, enough to power more than half a million homes.
根据AWS与核电站运营商的协议，数据中心将获得该电站约40%的能源产出，足以供应50多万户家庭用电

Wisconsin’s New Capital
威斯康星的新"首都"

Vantage’s Port Washington campus in Wisconsin is part of the $500 billion Stargate initiative involving major technology firms including SoftBank, OpenAI, and Oracle. The campus’s 11.4 TWh annual energy equivalent would be roughly four times Madison’s 2.8 TWh electricity benchmark.
Vantage在威斯康星州Port Washington的园区是5000亿美元"星际之门"计划的一部分，该计划参与者包括软银、OpenAI和甲骨文等科技巨头。园区114亿千瓦时的年能耗约为麦迪逊市28亿千瓦时用电基准的四倍

Another useful comparison is the Sacramento area. Port Washington’s annual equivalent would be about 8% higher than the 10.5 TWh of annual electricity sales reported by the Sacramento Municipal Utility District (SMUD), which serves Sacramento and surrounding communities.
另一个有效对比是萨克拉门托地区。Port Washington的年能耗将比萨克拉门托市政公用事业区(SMUD)报告的105亿千瓦时年售电量高出约8%，该公用事业公司为萨克拉门托及周边社区供电

With a total price tag of $15 billion, the Port Washington data center is expected to be completed in 2028. It will add to the United States’ existing global lead in the number of data centers.
Port Washington数据中心总投资150亿美元，预计2028年完工，将巩固美国在全球数据中心数量上的领先地位
