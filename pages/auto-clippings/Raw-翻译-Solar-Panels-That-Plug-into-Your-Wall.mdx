Solar Panels That Plug Into Your Wall Are About to Be Everywhere

插入墙壁的太阳能电池板即将无处不在

California is the latest state to pass a law making it easier to install balcony-friendly solar panels. Renewable energy experts think the new technology is about to take off.

加利福尼亚州是最新通过法律、使安装适合阳台的太阳能电池板更加便捷的州。可再生能源专家认为这项新技术即将腾飞。

On a quiet street in suburban South Salt Lake City, Utah, the stirring of an energy revolution is taking shape. There, outside the spare bedroom of an unassuming, thousand-square-foot midcentury home sit three angled solar panels plugged into a nearby outlet.

在犹他州南盐湖城郊区一条安静的街道上，一场能源革命的萌芽正在成形。在那里，一座不起眼的、占地一千平方英尺的中世纪风格住宅的备用卧室外，三块倾斜的太阳能电池板插在附近的插座上。

Unlike the highly technical and expensive hurdles required to install a large rooftop solar system, the setup on Andrew Leon and Victoria Mauro’s lawn is small, portable, and cheap. The unit generates enough electricity to run laptops, lights, and some appliances, including the refrigerator.

与安装大型屋顶太阳能系统所需的高技术和高成本障碍不同，安德鲁·莱昂和维多利亚·毛罗草坪上的这套设备小巧、便携且便宜。该装置产生的电力足以运行笔记本电脑、灯具以及包括冰箱在内的一些电器。

Plug-in solar, also known as balcony solar because apartment dwellers can use it, is spreading quickly across the U.S. California is the latest state to pass a law reducing the red tape that has previously stymied consumers looking to generate some electricity from a small number of solar panels.

插入式太阳能，也被称为阳台太阳能，因为公寓居民可以使用它，正在美国迅速普及。加利福尼亚州是最新通过法律、减少繁琐手续的州，这些手续此前阻碍了消费者尝试用少量太阳能电池板发电。

Utah was the first state to legalize balcony solar in the spring of 2025, and nine states subsequently passed similar legislation— Colorado, Connecticut, Maine, Maryland, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Vermont, Virginia, and now, California. New York lawmakers approved a bill that is awaiting the governor’s signature, while nearly two dozen other state legislators have introduced them.

犹他州于2025年春季率先将阳台太阳能合法化，随后九个州通过了类似立法——科罗拉多州、康涅狄格州、缅因州、马里兰州、新罕布什尔州、新泽西州、佛蒙特州、弗吉尼亚州，以及现在的加利福尼亚州。纽约州立法者批准了一项法案，正等待州长签署，而另有近二十个州的立法者已提交了相关法案。

Plug-in solar systems, typically comprised of one to three panels to stay below the maximum wattages allowed by the laws, enable users to save money on their power bills and to reduce the energy they use from power plants that generate electricity by burning fossil fuels. “The appeal of being able to make your own electricity, and for less money than you can buy it from a utility, that’s universal for Americans,” says Joshua Pearce, an electrical engineer at Western University in Ontario, Canada, who advanced the merits of plug-in solar a decade ago.

插入式太阳能系统通常由一到三块电池板组成，以保持在法律允许的最大功率以下，使用户能够节省电费，并减少使用燃烧化石燃料发电厂提供的能源。“能够自己发电，而且比从公用事业公司购买更便宜，这种吸引力对美国人来说是普遍的，”加拿大安大略省西安大略大学的电气工程师约书亚·皮尔斯说，他在十年前就提出了插入式太阳能的优点。

For most homes, electricity is generated in faraway power plants and transmitted through power lines. Homes where rooftop solar creates electricity wire the energy into the home through the circuit breaker panel, and excess electricity flows back to the utility. But power from plug-in systems stays in the home. With a small metal box called an inverter attached to the panels and plugged into the outlet, electrical currents are pushed to other outlets throughout the residence. Systems typically contribute 10 to 30 percent of an average home’s use, says Jennifer Eden, who works on renewable energy programs for the policy nonprofit Utah Clean Energy.

对于大多数家庭来说，电力是在遥远的发电厂产生的，并通过输电线传输。屋顶太阳能发电的家庭通过断路器面板将电能接入家中，多余的电量则流回公用事业公司。但插入式系统的电力则留在家庭内部。通过一个称为逆变器的小金属盒连接到电池板并插入插座，电流被推送到住宅内的其他插座。系统通常能满足普通家庭用电量的10%到30%，政策非营利组织犹他清洁能源公司的可再生能源项目工作人员詹妮弗·伊登说。

The set-ups have become especially popular in Europe. Swedish furniture chain IKEA sells plug-in solar kits in Germany, a country where more than a million units have been installed in the past few years.

这种设备在欧洲尤其受欢迎。瑞典家具连锁店宜家在德国销售插入式太阳能套件，该国在过去几年中已安装了超过一百万套。

As more U.S. states approve the technology and as more products become available, “this is going to take off like hotcakes,” Pearce predicts. With widespread adoption, residents could save an estimated $13 billion a year in electricity costs, he calculated in a 2017 journal article, a figure he says is higher now that electric rates have soared. People are especially pleased “there’s now an entry point into solar that doesn’t cost tens of thousands of dollars and can be used by people who don’t own their home,” Eden says.

随着更多美国州批准这项技术以及更多产品上市，“这将像热蛋糕一样畅销，”皮尔斯预测。他在2017年的一篇期刊文章中计算，如果广泛采用，居民每年可节省约130亿美元的电费，他表示随着电价飙升，这个数字现在更高了。人们尤其高兴的是，“现在有了一个进入太阳能领域的切入点，不需要花费数万美元，而且租房者也能使用，”伊登说。

In states with especially high utility rates, residents reap substantial savings. Nick Danz, who lives alone in a modern, two-bedroom luxury apartment in Fort Collins, Colorado, became eager for plug-in solar after reading about its popularity in Germany. Colorado has more than 200 sunny days a year and Danz’s apartment faces southwest, an optimal direction for sun collection. (Directly south is the most ideal.)

在电价特别高的州，居民能获得可观的节省。尼克·丹兹独自住在科罗拉多州柯林斯堡一套现代两居室豪华公寓里，在读到德国插入式太阳能的普及后，他对此变得热衷。科罗拉多州每年有超过200个晴天，丹兹的公寓朝西南，这是收集阳光的最佳方向。（正南是最理想的。）

When, this summer, his state passed a law for plug-in systems, he reached out to the California-based clean-energy nonprofit Bright Saver. To encourage people to adopt renewable energy, Bright Saver sells plug-in systems without markups. Danz paid under $300 for a one-panel unit and affixed it with metal clips over his small, third-floor balcony.

今年夏天，当他所在的州通过了插入式系统法律时，他联系了总部位于加利福尼亚州的清洁能源非营利组织“Bright Saver”。为了鼓励人们采用可再生能源，Bright Saver以无加价的方式销售插入式系统。丹兹以不到300美元的价格购买了一套单板装置，并用金属夹将其固定在三楼的小阳台上。

The system tripped his apartment’s circuit breaker and shut off his computer several times, but a different inverter solved the problem. Danz estimates he has already saved more than $300 on his power bills since August. For the four days a week Danz is away from home working as a train conductor, “the solar panel is essentially running all of my standby items,” he says. He plans to soon move to an apartment with a larger balcony, where he hopes to hang three panels, the most his state’s law allows.

该系统曾多次触发他公寓的断路器并导致电脑关机，但更换逆变器解决了问题。丹兹估计自8月以来他已节省了超过300美元的电费。丹兹每周有四天不在家，担任火车列车员，“太阳能电池板基本上在运行我所有的待机设备，”他说。他计划很快搬到阳台更大的公寓，希望在那里挂三块电池板，这是他所在州法律允许的最大数量。
