【文章标题】：GitHub has not removed malicious imitation software after 3 weeks  
【文章标题】：GitHub三周后仍未移除恶意仿冒软件  

【文章正文】：  
On the 31st August I got an email from a customer, telling me that they had found an imitation of my data wrangling software on Github.  
8月31日我收到客户邮件，告知在GitHub上发现了我的数据清洗软件的仿冒版本。  

I’m not linking to it, but here is a screenshot:  
此处不提供链接，但附上截图：  

It is using our product name and logo, without permission.  
该仿冒软件未经授权使用了我们的产品名称和商标。  

I reported it to Github as an imitation on the same day. I got this reply:  
我当天就以仿冒为由向GitHub举报，收到如下回复：  

A colleague scanned the Mac .dmg file from the repository using virustotal.com and got a whole load of malware warnings:  
同事通过virustotal.com扫描仓库中的Mac.dmg文件后，收到大量恶意软件警告：  

Using Isobuster he found out that they have also changed the background image of the .dmg:  
使用Isobuster分析发现，他们还篡改了.dmg文件的背景图：  

The new image encourages downloaders to ignore any warnings about the malware!  
新图片竟怂恿下载者无视所有恶意软件警告！  

I reported this additional information on the 10th September.  
9月10日我将这些新增证据提交给GitHub。  

As of the 23rd September, I have had no response from Github support beyond the original automated email. 23 days without a reponse. This is pisspoor. Do better Github.  
截至9月23日，除最初的自动回复外GitHub客服再无回应。整整23天杳无音信，简直离谱。GitHub该好好反省了。  

I’m not sure what my next line of attack is. A DCMA takedown request to Github?  
不确定下一步对策，或许该向GitHub提交DMCA下架请求？  

Realistically the only people likely to download the .dmg are those trying to avoid paying for a license for Easy Data Transform.  
实际上会下载这个.dmg的，多半是想盗版Easy Data Transform的用户。  

I don’t have a huge amount of sympathy for them if they get their computers compromised.  
若因此导致电脑中毒，我也不同情他们。  

But I really don’t like bad guys taking advantage of my hard work.  
但绝不能容忍不法分子利用我的劳动成果行骗。  

Ps/ Always download software from the vendor, where possible.  
附：请务必从官方渠道下载软件。  

** Update 24-Sep-2026 **  
**2026年9月24日更新**  

Github finally took the offending page down approximately 10 minutes after this post appeared on the front page of Hacker News. Total coincidence. I’m sure!  
本文登上Hacker News头条约10分钟后，GitHub终于撤下侵权页面——纯属巧合，我信了！  

Moral of the story. If you want even the most basic level of support from Github, you need to get on the front page of Hacker News.  
经验之谈：想让GitHub提供最基础的支持，你得先上Hacker News头条。  

And it seems they are able to do things very quickly, when they want to.  
看来只要他们愿意，办事效率可以极高。
