【文章标题】：Almost Never Use AI to Write Anything Substantive  
【文章标题】：几乎永远不要用AI撰写实质性内容  

【文章正文】：  
I think you should almost never use AI to write -- that is, to do the thing you’re doing when you type words on a page -- whether for a blog post, a research report, a memo, a thoughtful email, a novel, or any other text aimed at conveying an idea, an argument, an analysis, or other substantive1 thoughts.  
我认为你几乎永远不该用AI来写作——即完成你在页面上打字时所做的行为——无论是博客文章、研究报告、备忘录、深思熟虑的邮件、小说，还是其他任何旨在传达观点、论证、分析或其他实质性思想的文本。  

I think this is the case even when you give the AI very detailed bullet points, dictated thoughts, or other context, and even when you edit the AI-written text.2  
即便你给AI提供了非常详细的要点、口述的想法或其他上下文，甚至当你编辑AI生成的文本时，我依然坚持这个观点。  

I think so because (1) the writing process is an essential part of the thinking process, (2) AI writing is vague and wrong in hard-to-notice ways, and (3) writing with AI (and not labeling it as such) is rude and misleading.  
我这么认为是因为：(1) 写作过程是思考过程的重要组成部分，(2) AI写作存在难以察觉的模糊和错误，(3) 使用AI写作（且不标注来源）是粗鲁且具有误导性的。  

I’ll explain these points in more detail below, but first, a few throat clearings.  
下文我会详细阐述这些观点，但首先需要做些铺垫说明。  

As you may know, I’m not anti-AI. I think it makes a lot of sense to use AI for many other parts of the research and writing processes, such as transcribing audio, analyzing data, searching for information, brainstorming, and giving feedback on drafts.  
如你所知，我并不反对AI。我认为在研究写作流程的许多其他环节使用AI非常合理，比如音频转录、数据分析、信息检索、头脑风暴，以及提供草稿反馈。  

I also think using AI for line and copy editing, or for rewriting a passage to make it clearer or tighter, is fine, as long as all the edits are deliberately accepted or rejected by a human.  
我也认为用AI进行逐行编辑、文案校对，或重写段落使其更清晰简洁是可以接受的——只要所有修改都经过人工审慎采纳或拒绝。  

It’s just using AI to write text that I’m against.3  
我只是反对用AI来生成文本。  

And yes, there are various advantages to using AI for writing. For example, it’s less effortful and much faster than writing yourself.  
当然，用AI写作有诸多优势。比如它比亲自动笔更省力且快速得多。  

So the disadvantages of using AI for writing need to be substantial for it to be bad overall. As you may have guessed by now, I think they are.  
因此只有当AI写作的弊端足够重大时，整体上才算是坏事。正如你现在可能猜到的，我认为确实如此。  

And finally, I’m just making a claim about the AI models that exist now and that I expect to exist in the near future.  
最后要说明，我的观点仅针对当前存在及我认为近期会出现AI模型。  

There will likely exist models at some point that are good enough that it makes sense to delegate the writing to them (although at that point it might make more sense to delegate the entire research or writing process end-to-end, since in addition to the writing they will also need to be doing all or most of the thinking).  
未来可能会出现足够优秀的模型，届时将写作任务委托给它们确实合理（不过到那时，或许更合理的做法是把整个研究或写作流程端到端地委托出去，因为除了写作，它们还需要完成全部或大部分思考工作）。  

The Writing Process Is the Thinking Process  
写作过程即是思考过程  

The point of doing any kind of research is to form accurate beliefs about important questions, which you can then communicate to an audience.  
任何研究的核心都是形成关于重要问题的准确认知，进而将其传达给受众。  

One of the best ways of doing that is in my opinion by writing.  
在我看来，写作是实现这一目标的最佳方式之一。  

Paul Graham has written4 that  
保罗·格雷厄姆曾写道：  

Writing about something, even something you know well, usually shows you that you didn’t know it as well as you thought.  
撰写某件事物——即便你自以为很了解——往往会让你意识到认知的不足。  

Putting ideas into words is a severe test. [...] Half the ideas that end up in an essay will be ones you thought of while you were writing it. Indeed, that’s why I write them.  
将想法转化为文字是场严苛考验。[...]最终出现在文章里的观点，半数都是你在写作过程中萌生的。这正是我坚持写作的原因。  

On an episode of Patrick McKenzie’s podcast, Clara Collier says that  
在帕特里克·麦肯齐的播客中，克拉拉·科利尔谈到：  

When I am writing something, something substantive, there’s no part of that writing process in which I am not thinking and changing my mind.  
当我撰写实质性内容时，整个写作过程无时无刻不在思考和修正观点。  

Everything from the outline to turning it into text to just the sentence.  
从列提纲到文本转化，乃至每个句子的斟酌。  

Often I’ll have an experience where I’m trying to turn an outline into a finished product, and I’m playing with a transition, and it’s not working, and I realize, oh, the reason this transition isn’t working is because actually these two points should not be juxtaposed.  
常有这样的体验：当我把提纲完善成稿时，某个过渡段始终不顺畅，最终意识到问题在于这两个论点本就不该并列呈现。  

The thing that I’m trying to do here is wrong. And if I feed the outline into an LLM, it is not going to stop and consider maybe the outline is bad. [...]  
我的处理方式本身就是错的。而若把提纲喂给大语言模型，它绝不会停下来思考或许是提纲有问题。[...]  

Patrick replies:  
帕特里克回应道：  

I absolutely agree that the writing process is the thinking process, and I think we now have empirical demonstration of this, because you make a machine that does writing and thinking just pops out as a side effect.  
我完全认同写作过程即是思考过程，现在我们已有实证：当你造出能写作的机器时，思考能力就作为副产品自然涌现。  

But when writing a multi-page piece, even if you have a strong thesis, the act of forcing yourself to write that down into sentences and paragraphs to structure the argument will show you where the holes are in the argument.  
但在撰写多页文稿时，即便你持有有力论点，强迫自己用句子和段落构建论证结构的过程，仍会暴露出逻辑漏洞。  

It will show you where your research process has been inadequate.  
它会揭示你研究过程中的不足。  

It will surface things like: oh, actually, there is something that is contradictory to the thing that I want to say.  
还会浮现出诸如"啊，其实存在与我观点相矛盾的证据"这类情况。  

And, being a reasonably ethical person, I think I will acknowledge that there are counterarguments, or have to add that the full claimable conclusion is less than the one that I would, quote-unquote, “like to write”.  
作为有基本学术道德的人，我认为应该承认反对意见，或不得不补充说明可论证的结论范围比我"想写的"要窄。  

I basically agree with these takes. When I write something, I must first know what I want to say, i.e., I must think about evidence and arguments and arrive at some beliefs.  
我基本赞同这些观点。当我写作时，必须先明确要表达的内容，即必须思考证据和论证以形成某些观点。  

So writing forces me to do those things, which are necessary for any writing or research project.  
因此写作迫使我完成这些对任何写作或研究项目都必要的步骤。  

For any such project, someone has to have the entire thing in their head -- all the bits of evidence, considerations, arguments, and how these fit together.  
任何此类项目都需要有人在脑中构建完整框架——所有证据、考量、论证及其关联方式。  

Writing with AI makes it easier to avoid the necessary thinking.  
用AI写作让人更容易逃避必要的思考。  

AI Writing Is Vague and Wrong in Hard-to-Notice Ways  
AI写作存在难以察觉的模糊与错误  

When I write something, I make a lot of small decisions about specific wording and phrasing. Together, these small decisions contribute to the overall clarity and correctness of the text.  
当我写作时，会对具体措辞做出大量细微决定。这些微小选择共同构成了文本的整体清晰度和准确性。
