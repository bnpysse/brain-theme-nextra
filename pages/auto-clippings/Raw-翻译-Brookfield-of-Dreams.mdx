【文章标题】：
Brookfield of Dreams  
梦想之布鲁克菲尔德

【文章正文】：
When Jensen Huang delivered his plan to mobilize $500 billion of third-party capital to fund AI infrastructure last month, one of the providers he roped in was Brookfield.  
上月黄仁勋宣布筹集5000亿美元第三方资本投资AI基础设施的计划时，被纳入合作方的机构之一正是布鲁克菲尔德。  

To many observers, it came as a surprise. Huang and his team at Nvidia had been working with Goldman Sachs, Blackstone and Apollo for months, yet days before they shared their announcement, Brookfield, along with KKR and BlackRock, slid into the deal.  
这让许多观察人士感到意外。英伟达团队此前数月一直与高盛、黑石和阿波罗合作，但在官宣前几天，布鲁克菲尔德却与KKR、贝莱德悄然加入了这场交易。  

Flanked by Huang at the CNBC studios, Brookfield CEO Bruce Flatt laid out his credentials:  
在CNBC演播室里，布鲁克菲尔德CEO布鲁斯·弗拉特与黄仁勋并肩而坐，亮出了他的资历证明：  

"We’ve been building out backbone infrastructure since the company started. Originally it started with enormous amounts of power – solar, wind, gas. We moved to data centers. And with Jensen, we’ve now been moving to compute."  
"自公司创立起我们就在建设骨干基础设施。最初是巨量能源——太阳能、风能、天然气，后来转向数据中心。而现在与黄仁勋合作，我们正进军算力领域。"  

Brookfield has interests spanning a range of assets. It manages $1.3 trillion of capital – some its own and some its customers’ – employing more than 5,800 professionals across 50 countries.  
布鲁克菲尔德业务横跨多元资产领域。其管理的1.3万亿美元资本（含自有及客户资金）由遍布50国的5800余名专业人士运作。  

This week alone, executives in its private equity business agreed to buy Australian plumbing manufacturer Reliance as colleagues in London recapitalized Center Parcs, a UK-based holiday park they bought in 2015.  
仅本周，其私募团队刚敲定收购澳大利亚管道制造商Reliance，伦敦同事则对2015年购入的英国度假村Center Parcs进行资本重组。  

Closer to home, Brookfield negotiated a minority stake in American Real Estate Partners, and in Canada, where the firm has its roots, it launched an initiative to invest back in the country via a new Maple Fund.  
在美国本土，布鲁克菲尔德正洽购American Real Estate Partners少数股权；在其发源地加拿大，则通过新设立的枫叶基金启动本土再投资计划。  

To top it off, senior management hosted an investor day at the Conrad Hotel in downtown New York – and I listened in.  
压轴戏是高管们在纽约康莱德酒店举办的投资者日——我全程旁听了这场活动。  

We discussed Brookfield back in 2022 but it’s changed a bit since then.  
我们2022年曾探讨过布鲁克菲尔德，如今它已悄然蜕变。  

I didn’t know it at the time, but a few years earlier Flatt had rebuffed a deal to merge Brookfield with Apollo.  
当时我不知情的是，弗拉特几年前曾拒绝过与阿波罗的合并提案。  

"We were the kings of private equity, credit, and insurance," Apollo’s founder recalled. "They were the kings of real estate and infrastructure. You’d have a firm that would be bigger than Blackstone and more powerful."  
阿波罗创始人回忆道："我们当时是私募、信贷和保险之王，他们是地产与基建之王。合并后将诞生比黑石更庞大、更有影响力的巨头。"  

Instead, Brookfield acquired Oaktree Capital Management to bolster its franchise in credit ("imbecilic," says Apollo’s founder), it transitioned from a conglomerate to an asset manager, and, like several of its peers, pivoted into insurance.  
但布鲁克菲尔德选择收购橡树资本强化信贷业务（阿波罗创始人称此举"愚蠢"），从综合集团转型为资管机构，并像同行那样进军保险业。  

Apollo’s founder is unimpressed: "He’s created a second-tier private equity business. He’s created a second-tier credit operation. Now, he’s creating a second-tier insurance business."  
阿波罗创始人嗤之以鼻："他搞出了二流私募业务、二流信贷业务，现在又要搞二流保险业务。"  

Others in the industry are more complimentary. One of them is Bill Ackman.  
业界也不乏赞誉之声，比尔·阿克曼就是其中代表。  

Through his fund, Pershing Square, Ackman owns a $2.5 billion stake in Brookfield Corporation.  
通过潘兴广场基金，阿克曼持有布鲁克菲尔德集团25亿美元股份。  

"Brookfield is a high-quality, asset-rich, rapidly growing business that has a long-term track record of excellent capital allocation," he writes in his annual letter.  
他在年度致股东信中写道："布鲁克菲尔德是资产雄厚、高速增长的优质企业，拥有卓越的长期资本配置记录。"  

In particular, Ackman reckons Brookfield is poised to benefit from the multi-trillion-dollar wave of AI-related infrastructure investment.  
阿克曼特别指出，布鲁克菲尔德将从数万亿美元的AI基建投资浪潮中获益。  

Flatt has lofty ambitions for Brookfield.  
弗拉特对布鲁克菲尔德怀有远大抱负。  

He has already doubled the firm’s assets under management over five years and reckons he can do it again over the next five.  
过去五年他使公司资管规模翻倍，并自信未来五年能再创辉煌。  

"Our evolution has been about adapting our structure as the opportunity set evolves," he reveals.  
他透露："我们的进化本质是随着机遇演变调整架构。"
