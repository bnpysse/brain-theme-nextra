How Countries Compare on Science Scores in 2026
2026年各国科学教育评分对比

Key Takeaways
核心发现

China led the PISA 2025 science ranking with an average score of 597, followed by Singapore at 560.
中国以597分的平均分领跑2025年PISA科学排行榜，新加坡以560分紧随其后。

Asian education systems dominate the top of the ranking, with six of the seven highest scores.
亚洲教育体系包揽榜单前列，前七名中占据六席。

The United States ranks 13th with an average science score of 502, 20 points above the OECD average of 482.
美国以502分排名第13位，高出OECD平均水平（482分）20分。

How do science skills compare around the world?
全球科学素养如何分布？

This graphic ranks participating countries and economies by the average science scores of 15-year-old students in the latest PISA 2025 study, published in September 2026.
本图表根据2026年9月发布的2025年PISA研究数据，对参与国家/地区15岁学生的科学平均分进行排名。

PISA assesses students in mathematics, reading, and science. Its science assessment goes beyond recalling facts, testing how well students can explain scientific phenomena, evaluate investigations and evidence, and assess scientific information for decision-making.
PISA评估数学、阅读和科学三项能力。其科学评估不仅测试知识记忆，更考察学生解释科学现象、评估研究证据、运用科学信息决策的能力。

More than 760,000 students took part in PISA 2025 across 91 countries and economies, representing roughly 33 million 15-year-olds worldwide.
2025年PISA研究覆盖91个国家/地区，逾76万学生参与，代表全球约3300万15岁青少年。

China in this ranking represents the participating jurisdictions of Beijing, Shanghai, Jiangsu, and Zhejiang.
本排名中"中国"指参与测评的北京、上海、江苏和浙江地区。

Asia Dominates the Top of the Ranking
亚洲领跑排行榜

Behind China and Singapore, Macao, Taiwan, and Japan round out the top five, while South Korea ranks seventh.
中国和新加坡之后，澳门、台湾地区和日本进入前五，韩国位列第七。

Estonia is the only European country among the top seven, with an average science score of 527.
爱沙尼亚是前七名中唯一的欧洲国家，科学均分527。

What Stands Out About Asia’s Top Performers?
亚洲领先教育体系的突出特点

Several of the highest-scoring Asian education systems combine strong academic expectations with learning environments that support consistent performance.
多个高分亚洲教育体系将严格的学术要求与稳定的学习环境相结合。

China, Singapore, Japan, and South Korea all ranked well above the OECD average in science while also performing strongly in mathematics and reading.
中日新韩的科学成绩远超OECD均值，数学和阅读表现同样出色。

One factor may be fewer classroom distractions. In China, Japan, and South Korea, fewer than 10% of students said classmates were distracted by digital devices during most or every science lesson, compared with 28% across OECD countries.
课堂干扰较少是重要因素。中日韩仅不到10%学生表示科学课常受电子设备干扰，而OECD国家达28%。

These systems also produce a larger share of students with advanced skills. In Singapore, 26% of students reached the top two proficiency levels in science, compared with just 7% across OECD countries.
这些体系培养更多高阶能力学生。新加坡26%学生达到科学能力前两级，OECD国家仅7%。

Some high-performing Asian systems also combine strong results with relatively small links between students’ socioeconomic backgrounds and their science performance.
部分亚洲体系在取得优异成绩的同时，学生科学表现与社会经济背景关联性较低。

Outside school, intensive tutoring and strong academic expectations are also common in parts of East Asia.
课外辅导和高压学习在东亚地区较为普遍。

U.S. Scores Above the OECD Average
美国高于OECD均值

The United States scores 502, compared with the OECD average of 482.
美国得分502，OECD均分482。

Canada ranks ninth at 510, while Australia and New Zealand each score 509.
加拿大510分列第9，澳大利亚和新西兰均为509分。

Estonia Leads Europe
爱沙尼亚领跑欧洲

Estonia ranks sixth overall with an average science score of 527, making it the highest-scoring European country in the dataset.
爱沙尼亚以527分位列总榜第6，是欧洲最高分。

The OECD notes that several small score differences are not statistically significant.
OECD指出部分微小分差无统计学意义。
