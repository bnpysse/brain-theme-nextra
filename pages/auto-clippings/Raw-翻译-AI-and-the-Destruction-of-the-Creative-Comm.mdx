【文章标题】：AI与知识共享的毁灭

【文章正文】：
The balance of software copyright protection and openness has always been fraught with minutiae and detail that bores all but the most nerdy of pedants. 
软件版权保护与开放之间的平衡向来充满繁琐细节，只有最迂腐的书呆子才会对此感兴趣。

Yet, through much effort and 40 years of debate we had reached an equilibrium. Now AI has thrown that out the window.
然而经过大量努力和40年争论，我们曾达成某种平衡。如今AI将这平衡彻底打破。

When I was young I remember typing in BASIC programs from magazines into my Commodore 64 and later teaching myself REXX to write games for a BBS I ran. 
年少时，我曾将杂志上的BASIC程序输入Commodore 64电脑，后来还自学REXX语言为我运营的BBS编写游戏。

Without these "open" examples, I would never have been empowered to teach myself the basic tenets of programming. 
若没有这些"开放"范例，我永远不可能自学掌握编程的基本原理。

This was the era when the issue of whether software could be copyrighted was still being debated.
那是个软件是否应受版权保护仍在激烈争论的年代。

First there were shareware and freeware, both closed source. 
最初出现的是共享软件和免费软件，两者都闭源。

Shareware was basically trial-ware; you could try the software and pay a modest fee to register to unlock the full version. 
共享软件本质是试用软件，支付少量注册费即可解锁完整版。

Freeware was totally free, as in beer, but the source was not published. 
免费软件完全免费，但源代码不公开。

Most famously Doom was distributed as shareware, creating a huge market through word-of-mouth copying.
最著名的《毁灭战士》就以共享软件形式发行，通过口碑传播创造了巨大市场。

The forces on the side of openness pivoted and turned copyright onto itself, coining the term "copyleft" and creating licenses like GPL, MPL, and CC-SA, 
开放阵营巧妙转化版权概念，创造出"著佐权"术语，制定GPL、MPL、CC-SA等协议，

forcing those who wish to take advantage of software that was both free as in beer and free as in freedom to cascade those rights onto any further derivative works.
要求任何利用自由软件（既免费又自由）的衍生作品都必须继承相同授权。

The modern internet and cloud could not exist without free and open software. 
没有自由开源软件，现代互联网和云计算根本不可能存在。

Every cloud service and the very backbone of the internet itself are derivative works standing on the shoulders of the previous generation's giants. 
所有云服务和互联网骨干网，都是站在前代巨人肩膀上的衍生作品。

Without that openness, being online would likely look more like AOL and CompuServe, be hundreds of times more expensive, and be even more of an oligarchy than we have today.
若没有这种开放性，网络体验可能仍停留在美国在线和CompuServe时代，费用昂贵百倍，垄断程度比如今更甚。

While there is much discussion of the environmental destruction, the cybersecurity implications, and the misinformation being imposed on all of us by generative AI large language models, 
虽然人们热议生成式AI大模型带来的环境破坏、网络安全威胁和错误信息传播，

I haven't seen nearly as much discussion of the destruction of our foundational openness.
但对基础性开放精神遭受的破坏却鲜少讨论。

First, these LLMs are consuming everything they find online, without regard for copyright or license. 
首先，这些大语言模型吞噬着网上一切内容，全然不顾版权或授权协议。

Any derivative works may or may not reflect the licenses of the original creators and to date there seems to be no appetite for legal enforcement of these obligations.
任何衍生作品都可能无视原作者的授权条款，而至今未见法律强制履行这些义务的意愿。

The social contract has been broken.
社会契约已被撕毁。

I now have every incentive to not share my work, while also being wary of anything I find online that has been shared. 
现在我完全有理由不分享作品，同时警惕网上任何共享内容。

If it hasn't been polluted by slop code, it might have been poisoned with a malicious library, send data off to third parties, or itself be comprised of someone else's stolen work, implicating me in the crime.
即便未被垃圾代码污染，也可能暗藏恶意库、向第三方发送数据，或本身就是窃取他人的作品而使我卷入犯罪。

If I make my code available, AI can be used to more easily discover vulnerabilities to abuse my coding errors, while if I keep my code closed it is far more difficult to find those same mistakes.
公开代码会让AI更易利用我的编码漏洞，而闭源则使这些错误更难被发现。

If I publish my code on a public service like GitHub, I am likely to be inundated with pull requests generated by AI bots and inexperienced users, flooding me with mostly useless slop and taking all of my spare time away just triaging it.
在GitHub等平台公开代码，就会淹没在AI机器人和新手生成的拉取请求中，耗费所有空闲时间处理这些无用的垃圾。

We all have our own set of ethical and moral guidelines, which are also lost once having been absorbed into the colossus. 
我们各自的道德准则在被AI巨兽吞噬后同样荡然无存。

While existing licenses and agreements are imperfect, they have been shown to be enforceable in a court of law allowing me, the creator, power over my own creation.
现有授权协议虽不完美，但至少可法庭执行，保障创作者对作品的掌控权。

AI has turned sharing knowledge from a gift to the world, into a liability for the author. 
AI将知识共享从馈赠世界的礼物变成了作者的法律风险。

The act of openness will be twisted and abused into creating more consolidated wealth and power, without respect or credit for those who did the real labour.
开放行为将被扭曲滥用，成为财富权力集中的工具，真正付出劳动的人却得不到尊重与认可。

This will have incredible negative consequences for the future. 
这将给未来带来难以估量的负面影响。

The past 35 years of openness have created nearly everything we benefit from online, which has transformed the world in a mostly positive and empowering way.
过去35年的开放精神创造了我们受益的几乎全部网络成果，以前所未有的积极方式改变了世界。

We are entering a digital dark age. 
我们正步入数字黑暗时代。

As authors, coders, technologists, and artists we must come together to introduce a new digital Renaissance before too much is lost. 
作为创作者、程序员、技术专家和艺术家，我们必须携手开启新的数字文艺复兴，以免为时已晚。

If we don't, we're likely to end up in some bizarrely twisted version of Fahrenheit 451.
否则，我们终将陷入《华氏451度》般的扭曲未来。
