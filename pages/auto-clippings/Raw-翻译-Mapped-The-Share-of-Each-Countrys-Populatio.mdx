Mapped: The Share of Each Country’s Population Under 18  
【地图解析】各国18岁以下人口占比  

The Central African Republic has the world’s highest share of population under 18, at 56.2%.  
中非共和国以56.2%的18岁以下人口占比位居全球首位。  

African countries account for 28 of the top 30 entries, reflecting the continent’s comparatively high fertility rates and young age structure.  
非洲国家包揽前30名中的28席，反映出该大陆较高的生育率和年轻化人口结构。  

Children make up less than 15% of the population in several East Asian economies, including South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, and Singapore.  
韩国、日本、台湾地区和新加坡等东亚经济体的儿童占比不足15%。
