【文章标题】：可以触摸的《异星工厂》

【文章正文】：
Hello,
  今天我们有个非同寻常的周五要闻。
we have very unusual Friday facts for you.

这一切始于2024年那个炎热的夏天，当时我们正全力开发《太空时代》DLC。游戏已接近完成，但我们希望获得玩家的直接反馈。于是我们决定组织一场试玩活动（FFF-427），让玩家团队从头到尾体验游戏，通过面对面交流为我们提供改进建议，确保最终呈现最完美的作品。
It all started in the hot summer of 2024 when we worked really hard to finish the Space Age DLC. The game was almost ready, but we wanted some feedback from our players. A decision was made to organise a playtesting event (FFF-427), where teams of players could play the game from start to finish and provide us with in-person feedback and criticism to deliver the best product you all deserved.

考虑到我们都是技术宅，便邀请了Prusa Research的朋友们共同设计配套活动方案。他们作为《异星工厂》的粉丝欣然加入，计划就此展开。活动开始时，我们突发奇想决定现场3D打印游戏模型，于是Fearghal迅速创建了来自Gleba星球的标志性生物Wriggler的简易模型。
Since we are all geeks and love to tinker, we asked our friends at Prusa Research to join us and create some accompanying programmes and presentations for all attendees.
Being fans of Factorio themselves, they agreed to join, and the plan was put into motion. When the event started, we realised that it would be cool to print some Factorio models right at the event, so Fearghall quickly created a simple model of the Wriggler from everyone's favourite Gleba.

活动结束后，虽然团队都回归游戏开发，但实体模型的想法始终萦绕心头。在DLC发布后的数月调试期里，我们利用业余时间制作原型。经过多次打印上色后，我们发现缺乏核心理念。经过沉淀，答案其实很简单：传送带——从游戏初期到后期都是工厂的核心要素。
With the conclusion of our event, everyone returned to work on Factorio, but we all wanted to return to the idea of Factorio models in the real world. In the months following the release and subsequent bug-fixing and polishing period, we started working on some prototypes in our spare time. Several models were made, printed, and painted, but they lacked a concept. We let it sit for a while and brainstormed what would be the best direction. The answer was actually very simple: transport belts.
Belts are the core of every factory from the early hours to the very late game.

Jarosław设计了遵循游戏规则的网格系统，随后完成了机械臂初版设计。此阶段我们打印了无数原型，发现每款模型都需要两个版本：高精度卡扣组装版，以及为胶水粘合或打印机兼容性优化的高容错版。
Jarosław started designing the concept of a grid system where you could place every entity closely following the game. The first version of inserters soon followed. During this phase, we were printing countless prototypes. It soon became clear that we needed two versions of almost every model. One set of models is created with high precision, allowing push-fit assembly, and the other is made with high clearance in mind for people who want to glue everything permanently or have clearance issues with their printers.

当Jarosław专注玩家建筑时，Fearghal开始设计虫族单位。从虫巢初版开始，陆续完成了箱子、玩家角色、石炉等模型。最终我们精选了15组共65个模型（247个STL文件），聚焦游戏前期内容。
While Jarosław was busy creating one player-built entity after the other, Fearghall started working on enemies. The first iteration of the biter spawner soon followed. Then came chests, followed by the player character, then a stone furnace… Our model set grew nicely, but we knew that we had to draw a line somewhere, because trying to release every Factorio entity would take too much time. With that in mind, we focused on the early game. We ended up with 15 model sets containing 65 individual models in 247 STL files.

在这个数字时代，实体纪念品日渐稀少。作为软件公司，我们不愿制作昂贵的限量藏品，而是选择开放3D模型文件作为对玩家的感谢礼物。第二个重要原因是：我们期待见证玩家惊人的创造力。
In the modern digital world, there is a decrease in physical memorabilia. Physical releases of games are now almost a relic of the past. We always thought about having something for fans and ourselves as a reminder of times that we spent with the game. We at Wube are a software company, not a toy manufacturer, and we have the best community in the world. Creating some expensive (limited) collector's items didn't sit right with us. We have decided to go with 3D models as a little "thank you for your support" gift so anyone can download them and print them for themselves.
