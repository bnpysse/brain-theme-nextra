How CEO Pay Stacked Up in 2025  
2025年CEO薪酬全景  

Key Takeaways  
核心要点  

Elon Musk’s 2025 compensation package was valued at $132.3 billion, roughly 153 times the second-highest package and nearly 36 times the other nine top-10 packages combined.  
埃隆·马斯克2025年薪酬方案价值1323亿美元，约是第二名薪酬包的153倍，几乎是其他前十名CEO薪酬总和的36倍。  

Nine of the top 10 highest-paid CEOs received compensation packages valued at $100 million or more.  
前十名高薪CEO中有九位薪酬超过1亿美元。  

Median compensation for the 100 highest-paid CEOs reached $39.4 million in 2025, up 35.8% from 2024.  
2025年百强CEO薪酬中位数达3940万美元，较2024年上涨35.8%。  

America’s highest-paid CEOs received some extraordinary compensation packages in 2025, driven largely by major equity awards.  
2025年美国顶尖CEO们获得了惊人的薪酬方案，主要源于巨额股权激励。  

This graphic ranks leading U.S. CEOs by total compensation awarded during the year, and it comes from Equilar and The New York Times.  
本榜单根据年度总薪酬对美国CEO进行排名，数据来源为Equilar和《纽约时报》。  

Compensation includes salary, bonuses, stock and option awards, and other benefits. Stock and option awards are valued at grant date, meaning these figures do not necessarily represent cash received or gains ultimately realized by executives.  
薪酬包含薪资、奖金、股票期权奖励及其他福利。股票期权按授予日估值，因此这些数字未必反映高管实际到手现金或最终收益。

Worth more than all the other pay packages on the list combined, Elon Musk’s $132.3 billion compensation package for Tesla stands out.  
埃隆·马斯克1323亿美元的特斯拉薪酬方案远超榜单其他CEO薪酬总和。  

But unlike a conventional salary or cash bonus, that figure represents the grant-date value of a long-term Tesla stock award. How much Musk ultimately receives depends on Tesla reaching a series of ambitious milestones over the next 10 years.  
但这并非传统薪资或现金奖金，而是特斯拉长期股权奖励的授予日估值。马斯克最终收益取决于未来十年特斯拉能否达成一系列宏伟目标。  

How Musk’s $132 Billion Pay Package Works  
1320亿美元薪酬方案运作机制  

According to regulatory filings, Musk’s performance award is divided into 12 stock tranches. Each generally requires Tesla to hit both a market capitalization target and an operational target.  
监管文件显示，马斯克的绩效奖励分为12批股票，每批都要求特斯拉同时达成市值目标和经营目标。  

Tesla’s targets span both its market value and operating performance. The market cap milestones rise from $2 trillion to $8.5 trillion, while the operational milestones cover vehicles, self-driving car subscriptions, robots, robotaxis, and Adjusted EBITDA.  
目标涵盖市值与经营表现：市值门槛从2万亿美元递增至8.5万亿美元；经营目标包括汽车交付、自动驾驶订阅、机器人、无人出租车及调整后息税折旧摊销前利润。

What Musk Gets for Hitting the Targets  
达标奖励细则  

Each completed tranche represents shares equal to roughly 1% of Tesla’s adjusted share count and gives Musk the ability to direct the voting rights associated with those earned shares.  
每批奖励约占特斯拉调整后总股本的1%，马斯克可获得对应股票的投票权。  

However, earning those voting rights is different from receiving the full economic benefit of the shares. Musk generally must remain in continuous service at Tesla through the applicable 7.5- or 10-year vesting period before the shares vest.  
但获得投票权不等于取得完整经济权益，马斯克需持续任职特斯拉7.5-10年等待股票归属。  

The final tranche requires Tesla to reach an $8.5 trillion market capitalization and complete all 12 operational milestones.  
最后一批奖励要求特斯拉市值达8.5万亿美元且完成全部12项经营目标。  

What the $132 Billion Figure Means for Musk  
1320亿美元对马斯克的意义  

Importantly, the $132.3 billion figure is the grant-date value assigned to Musk’s compensation package, not cash paid to him in 2025.  
关键点：1323亿美元是薪酬方案的授予日估值，非2025年实际支付现金。  

As of September 2026, Forbes ranked Musk as the world’s richest person, with an estimated net worth of roughly $923 billion. His wealth includes significant holdings in Tesla and SpaceX, alongside interests in other businesses.  
截至2026年9月，《福布斯》将马斯克列为全球首富，净资产约9230亿美元，含特斯拉/ SpaceX大量股权及其他业务权益。  

Several CEOs Received Nine-Figure Packages  
多位CEO获九位数薪酬  

Even without Tesla, executive compensation reached extraordinary levels in 2025.  
即便剔除特斯拉，2025年高管薪酬仍达惊人水平。  

Figma CEO Dylan Field received the second-largest package at $864 million, followed by Welltower CEO Shankh Mitra at $821 million and Opendoor CEO Kasra Nejatian at $741 million.  
Figma CEO迪伦·菲尔德以8.64亿美元居次席，Welltower CEO尚克·米特拉（8.21亿）与Opendoor CEO卡斯拉·内贾蒂安（7.41亿）紧随其后。  

Across the 100 highest-paid CEOs, median compensation reached $39.4 million in 2025, up 35.8% from the previous year.  
百强CEO薪酬中位数达3940万美元，同比上涨35.8%。
