How Europe’s Airline Giants Compare  
欧洲航空巨头格局  

Europe’s largest traditional airline groups include Lufthansa, International Airlines Group, and Air France-KLM. Two of the three were formed through major mergers of national flag carriers.  
欧洲最大传统航空集团包括汉莎、国际航空集团与法荷航，其中两家由国家级航司合并组建  

Air France-KLM ($36.0 billion) was created in 2004 through the combination of the French and Dutch flag carriers. International Airlines Group ($39.1 billion) followed in 2011 through the merger of Spain’s Iberia and the United Kingdom’s British Airways. IAG has since added airlines including Aer Lingus and Vueling.  
法荷航（360亿）成立于2004年，由法国航空与荷兰皇家航空合并；国际航空集团（391亿）2011年由西班牙伊比利亚航空与英国航空合并而成，后陆续收购爱尔兰航空等  

Lufthansa ($47.1 billion), Germany’s flag carrier, generates more annual revenue than both IAG and Air France-KLM. Europe also has a significant low-cost airline market, led in this ranking by Ireland’s Ryanair at $18.2 billion.  
德国载旗航空汉莎（471亿）年收入超过上述两家。欧洲廉航市场表现亮眼，爱尔兰瑞安航空以182亿领跑  

China’s Airlines Lead Asia  
中国航司领跑亚洲市场  

China Southern ($25.3 billion), Air China ($23.6 billion), and China Eastern ($19.4 billion) are the three highest-revenue airlines in Asia.  
中国南航（253亿）、国航（236亿）与东航（194亿）包揽亚洲营收前三  

The carriers serve somewhat different markets. Air China has a particularly strong international and long-haul network, while China Southern is one of the region’s largest airlines by fleet size and passenger volume. China Eastern also operates an extensive domestic and international network.
