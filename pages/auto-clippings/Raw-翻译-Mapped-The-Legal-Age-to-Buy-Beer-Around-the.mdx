【文章正文】：
How Old Do You Have to Be to Buy Beer?
购买啤酒需要多大年龄？

Key Takeaways:
核心发现：

Age 18 is the global norm for buying beer across the countries in the dataset.
在数据涵盖的国家中，18岁是购买啤酒的全球普遍标准。

Thirteen countries set the minimum at 16, while Burkina Faso has the lowest listed age at 13.
13个国家将最低年龄设为16岁，而布基纳法索的最低年龄为13岁。

Some countries report no legal minimum age, while others prohibit beer sales altogether.
部分国家未设法定最低年龄，而有些国家则完全禁止啤酒销售。

Around the world, the legal age for buying a beer ranges from 13 to 25, with significant differences between countries.
全球啤酒购买法定年龄从13岁到25岁不等，各国差异显著。

This graphic uses the latest available country-level data from the WHO Global Information System on Alcohol and Health (GISAH). It specifically measures minimum ages for off-premise beer sales, such as purchases from stores, rather than drinking or being served alcohol in bars and restaurants.
本图表采用世界卫生组织全球酒精与健康信息系统(GISAH)的最新国家数据，特别测量的是非现场啤酒销售（如商店购买）的最低年龄，而非酒吧餐厅等场所的饮酒或供酒年龄。
