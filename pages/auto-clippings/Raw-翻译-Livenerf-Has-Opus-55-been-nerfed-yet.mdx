【文章标题】：Livenerf: Has Opus 5.5 been nerfed yet?

【文章标题】：Livenerf：Opus 5.5模型是否已被削弱？

【文章正文】：

A long-running, deterministic-as-possible benchmark for detecting whether a frontier model gets quietly worse after launch.

【文章正文】：这是一个长期运行、尽可能保持确定性的基准测试，用于检测前沿模型在发布后是否悄然变差。

📋 The plan · 📊 Results · 🔬 How it works · 🧪 Pre-registration

📋 计划 · 📊 结果 · 🔬 工作原理 · 🧪 预注册

livenerf is a small, boring, append-only benchmark for one question: does a model get worse after it ships? For months there have been reports that Anthropic "nerfs" models some days or weeks after release. That could mean quantization, a smaller model behind the same name, lower effort, or routing changes. It could also mean nothing happened and people are pattern-matching on noise. Nobody has had a clean day-0 baseline to check against, so every argument ends up as vibes versus vibes. Claude Opus 5.5 came out on 2026-09-22, so this is a chance to start the clock on launch day and keep it running. Right now v0 runs on a Claude Max subscription through headless Claude Code (claude -p), with no API key. You can't make these models deterministic: sampling params are gone and thinking can't be turned off. So livenerf makes everything else deterministic: frozen prompts, pinned CLI, exact graders, raw logs forever. It then measures drift statistically over thousands of samples. It's built on Inspect, the UK AI Security Institute's open-source eval framework. The stats follow Anthropic's own Adding Error Bars to Evals, so there's nothing homebrew to argue about.

livenerf是一个小型、单调、仅追加式的基准测试，只回答一个问题：模型发布后会变差吗？数月来一直有报道称Anthropic会在模型发布数日或数周后"削弱"模型。这可能意味着量化、同名背后的较小模型、更低努力或路由变更。也可能什么都没发生，人们只是在噪声中进行模式匹配。由于缺乏干净的day-0基线作为对照，所有争论最终都沦为感觉对感觉。Claude Opus 5.5于2026-09-22发布，这给了我们从发布日启动计时并持续运行的机会。当前v0版本通过无头Claude Code(claude -p)运行在Claude Max订阅上，无需API密钥。你无法让这些模型完全确定性：采样参数已移除且无法关闭思考过程。因此livenerf确保其他所有环节都是确定性的：冻结提示词、固定CLI、精确评分、永久保存原始日志。随后通过数千样本统计测量漂移。它基于英国AI安全研究所的开源评估框架Inspect构建，统计方法遵循Anthropic自家的《为评估添加误差条》，因此不存在可争议的自制方法。

The series is running. Day 1 was 2026-09-24 22:10 UTC, about 2.5 days after launch. It runs once a day for 30 days: days 1–10 are the baseline, then there are two 10-day windows, so the first possible call is around 2026-10-24. The first Results row lands after day 20. The panel was chosen, confirmed, locked and validated under a pre-registered protocol (v2). Every number below is generated from the logs in docs/CALIBRATION.md.

测试序列正在运行。第1天是2026-09-24 22:10 UTC（发布后约2.5天）。每天运行一次持续30天：第1-10天为基线期，随后是两个10天窗口期，因此首次结论约在2026-10-24得出。首行结果将在第20天后产生。测试组选题按照预注册协议(v2)选择、确认、锁定和验证。下文所有数据均来自docs/CALIBRATION.md中的日志。

Progress (2026-09-29): 6 of 30 days collected (baseline 6 of 10), none missed. All 6 days ran the full 90 samples on the same harness hash (461391b6fce64167) and pinned CLI (2.1.280). Day 5 ran with the budget guard overridden once (see the deviations log).

进度(2026-09-29)：已收集30天中的6天（基线期10天中的6天），无遗漏。全部6天均在同一测试框架哈希值(461391b6fce64167)和固定CLI(2.1.280)下完成90个样本测试。第5天曾有一次预算防护覆写（见偏差日志）。

- The panel. 2,336 GPQA Diamond, MMLU-Pro, competition-math and AIME 2025–26 questions were screened with 4 samples each. Opus 5.5 gets about 93% right on the first try, and 97% of the questions were always right or always wrong. 78 questions are sometimes right, and they form the panel (docs/DESIGN.md).

- 测试组构成。从2,336道GPQA Diamond、MMLU-Pro、竞赛数学和AIME 2025-26题目中筛选，每道题测试4次。Opus 5.5首次尝试正确率约93%，97%的题目要么全对要么全错。78道时对时错的题目构成最终测试组（见docs/DESIGN.md）。

- Selection bias, measured. Questions picked for being "sometimes right" look closer to 50/50 than they are. On fresh samples their pass rate rose from 54.7% to 62.0%, so the power calculation uses the fresh rates.

- 已测量的选择偏差。被选为"有时正确"的题目表面接近50/50分布，实际在新样本中通过率从54.7%升至62.0%，因此功效计算采用新比率。

- What it can detect. One run a day of the whole panel detects an accuracy change of about 7.5 points per 10-day window, for about 3.6% of the weekly plan.

- 检测能力。每天运行全套测试组可在10天窗口期内检测约7.5个百分点的准确率变化，约占周计划的3.6%。

- Validation (docs/VALIDATION.md) passed its pre-registered criterion.

- 验证（见docs/VALIDATION.md）通过预注册标准。

Lower effort shows up much more clearly in tokens than in accuracy:

努力程度降低在token数量上比准确度表现更明显：

- effort low: −62% output tokens, −8.3 ± 4.5 points of accuracy;

- 低努力：输出token减少62%，准确度下降8.3±4.5个百分点；

- effort medium: −26% tokens, −4.2 ± 3.9 points.

- 中等努力：token减少26%，准确度下降4.2±3.9个百分点。

- The limit. Swapping in Opus 5 was not distinguishable from Opus 5.5 at 99% (−3.8 ± 6.3 points, −23% tokens). This instrument can't detect a same-family model swap of that size in a validation's worth of samples. A 10-day window has about 2.5 times as many samples, but that hasn't been shown to be enough.

- 检测极限。在99%置信水平下无法区分Opus 5与Opus 5.5的替换（-3.8±6.3个百分点，-23%token）。本工具无法在验证级样本量下检测同系列模型的此规模替换。10天窗口期的样本量约为2.5倍，但尚未证明足够。

- The questions themselves. A report-only audit of the 78 questions, plus the 2 later excluded, found 8 answer keys that look wrong and 30 ambiguous questions. That's what you'd expect from questions a strong model only sometimes gets "right". Nothing was dropped. A pre-registered sensitivity analysis reruns the result without them.

- 题目本身。对78道题及后来排除的2道题进行只读审计，发现8个疑似错误答案和30道模糊题目——这正是强模型仅有时能"答对"的题目应有的特征。未剔除任何题目。预注册的敏感性分析将在排除这些题目后重新计算结果。

- Serving path. The safety classifier sometimes answers with Opus 5 or refuses biology and some math questions. Those samples are rejected and counted, and questions it touched are excluded.

- 服务路径。安全分类器有时会使用Opus 5回答或拒绝生物类及部分数学问题。这些样本会被拒绝并计数，相关题目将被排除。

The main thing this repo will maintain is a running 10-day table of how Opus 5.5 does on the calibrated benchmark panel relative to its launch-week baseline. A negative delta means worse than launch week. The table reports improvements just as loudly as regressions.

本代码库主要维护一个持续更新的10天周期表格，展示Opus 5.5在校准基准测试组上相对于发布周基线的表现。负Δ值表示比发布周更差。该表格对改进和退步会同等醒目地报告。

The primary metric is the paired per-item score difference against baseline on the calibrated panel, with clustered standard errors, so item difficulty drops out. See PREREGISTRATION.md. The secondary signal I care most about is the output token count per sample. If a model quietly starts thinking less, this is where it shows up first, often before accuracy moves at all.

主要指标是校准测试组中各题目相对于基线的配对得分差异（采用聚类标准误），从而消除题目难度影响。详见PREREGISTRATION.md。我最关注的次要信号是每样本输出token数。若模型悄然降低思考强度，这会最先显现，通常早于准确度变化。
