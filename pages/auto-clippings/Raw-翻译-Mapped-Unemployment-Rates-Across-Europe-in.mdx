Key Takeaways

Finland (10.3%) has the highest unemployment rate in Europe, narrowly ahead of Spain (10.0%).

德国和荷兰的失业率（均为4.0%）不到法国（8.2%）的一半。

Czechia has the lowest unemployment rate in the European Union at 3.2%.

Europe's unemployment picture is changing. Spain has long been associated with high joblessness, but a Nordic country now tops the continent's unemployment rankings.

Europe's Shifting Unemployment Divide

欧洲失业率格局的转变

Finland's position marks a notable shift in Europe's unemployment rankings. Spain held the top spot a year earlier, but Finland has since moved ahead.

芬兰的排名标志着欧洲失业率排行榜出现了显著变化。一年前西班牙还位居榜首，但此后芬兰已超越其上。

Elsewhere, Greece's unemployment rate fell from 8.8% to 7.4% over the year, continuing its labor market recovery. France moved in the opposite direction, with its rate rising from 7.8% to 8.2%.

在其他国家中，希腊的失业率在一年内从8.8%降至7.4%，延续了其劳动力市场的复苏势头。法国则朝着相反的方向发展，失业率从7.8%上升至8.2%。

Russia's low unemployment rate (2.2%) has a different backdrop. Smaller generations entering the workforce, wartime emigration, and the shift toward military production have contributed to labor shortages, intensifying competition for workers.

俄罗斯的低失业率（2.2%）背后有着不同的背景。进入劳动力市场的较年轻世代人数减少、战时移民外流以及向军工生产的转变，共同导致了劳动力短缺，加剧了企业间对工人的争夺。

Germany's Car Industry Cuts Deep

德国汽车业大幅裁员

Germany has one of the lowest unemployment rates among Europe's major economies, but its automotive industry tells a different story.

德国是欧洲主要经济体中失业率最低的国家之一，但其汽车行业却呈现出另一番景象。

The sector shed 42,300 jobs in the year to June 2026, bringing employment to its lowest level since 2005. The contrast illustrates how weakness in a major industry can coexist with relatively low unemployment across the wider economy.

截至2026年6月的一年中，该行业裁减了42,300个工作岗位，使就业人数降至2005年以来的最低水平。这一对比表明，一个主要行业的疲软可以与整体经济中相对较低的失业率并存。

U.S. tariffs and Chinese competition are putting pressure on German automakers. Volkswagen, Europe's largest automaker, has warned that competition is intensifying both within China and as Chinese brands expand into Europe, making previously planned cost reductions insufficient.

美国的关税和中国的竞争正在给德国汽车制造商带来压力。欧洲最大的汽车制造商大众汽车已发出警告，随着中国品牌向欧洲扩张，中国境内及欧洲市场的竞争都在加剧，此前计划的成本削减措施已显不足。

The cuts extend beyond major car brands. Employment at German parts and accessories suppliers fell 7.6% over the year, compared with a 6.1% decline at vehicle and engine manufacturers. The industry's troubles are also affecting businesses throughout the automotive supply chain.

裁员范围不仅限于大型汽车品牌。德国零部件和配件供应商的就业人数在一年内下降了7.6%，而整车和发动机制造商的降幅为6.1%。该行业的困境也波及了整个汽车供应链上的企业。

Spain's Tourism Boom Supports Its Recovery

西班牙旅游业繁荣支撑经济复苏

Spain still has a double-digit unemployment rate, but it has made substantial progress. In 2013, more than one in four people in the country's labor force were unemployed.

西班牙的失业率仍保持在两位数，但已取得了实质性进展。2013年，该国劳动力中有超过四分之一的人处于失业状态。

Yet job creation has not erased longstanding gaps between workers' skills and employers' needs. Northern and central Spain tend to have more diversified economies and lower unemployment than southern regions, where tourism and hospitality play larger roles.

然而，就业岗位的创造并未消除劳动者技能与雇主需求之间长期存在的差距。西班牙北部和中部地区的经济往往更加多元化，失业率也低于南部地区——南部地区旅游业和酒店业所占比重更大。

Tourism is helping drive Spain's recovery, but significant regional and structural challenges remain.
