As the Soviet Union disintegrated in the early 1990s, several of the republics that gained independence represented particularly fertile ground for Western oil supermajors. Partially freed from Moscow’s constraints, flush with world-beating energy resources, and in need of Western technological and financial support, the chaos presented the big energy powerhouses with a generational opportunity. (Some might even speculate that this outcome was the driving motivation behind the Cold War in the first place.)

随着苏联在20世纪90年代初解体，几个获得独立的加盟共和国成为西方超级石油巨头特别肥沃的土壤。它们部分摆脱了莫斯科的束缚，拥有举世无双的能源资源，又急需西方的技术和资金支持，这种混乱为大型能源巨头提供了千载难逢的机遇。（有些人甚至猜测，这一结果正是冷战最初的驱动动机。）

Among the most lucrative moves of the era was Chevron’s entry into Kazakhstan, where it secured a wildly valuable joint-venture agreement in the Tengiz-Korolev area in April of 1993. Later similar deals in Karachaganak and Kashagan with various Western participants doubled Kazakh oil production in the 1990s, setting the country on its way to the 2 million barrels per day (bpd) level achieved in 2025.

当时最有利可图的举措之一是雪佛龙进入哈萨克斯坦，于1993年4月在田吉兹-科罗廖夫地区达成了一份价值极高的合资协议。随后，在卡拉恰甘纳克和卡沙甘与多家西方参与者达成的类似交易，使哈萨克斯坦的石油产量在1990年代翻了一番，为该国产能在2025年达到每日200万桶的水平铺平了道路。

Kazakhstan’s independence from Russia, the legal successor state of the Soviet Union, was nonetheless incomplete—it remains a landlocked country. Construction on the principal oil pipeline providing takeaway capacity for Kazakh crude, operated by the Caspian Pipeline Consortium (CPC), roughly coincided with Russian President Vladimir Putin’s ascension to power in 1999-2000. Through various expansions and other improvements, the CPC pipeline can currently carry roughly 1.4 million bpd of oil to the Russian border and on to the all-important Port of Novorossiysk on the coast of the Black Sea.

哈萨克斯坦虽然脱离了苏联的法定继承国俄罗斯，但独立并不完全——它仍然是一个内陆国家。为哈萨克斯坦原油提供外运能力的主要输油管道由里海管道财团（CPC）运营，其建设时间大致与俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京在1999-2000年上台执政相吻合。经过多次扩建和其他改进，CPC管道目前每天可向俄罗斯边境输送约140万桶石油，并继续输往黑海沿岸至关重要的新罗西斯克港。

Novorossiysk’s strategic relevance goes well beyond Kazakhstan and its Western partners, as the port is also a critical conduit for Russian energy and grain exports. This has made knocking the expansive facility offline a longstanding objective of Ukraine’s drone offensive.
