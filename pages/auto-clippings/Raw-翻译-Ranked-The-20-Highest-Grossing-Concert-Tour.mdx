Key Takeaways

关键要点

- Taylor Swift’s Eras Tour is the only concert tour in history to surpass $2 billion in inflation-adjusted revenue.
- 泰勒·斯威夫特的“时代巡回演唱会”是历史上唯一一场经通胀调整后收入超过20亿美元的巡回演唱会。
- Five tours in the ranking have grossed more than $1 billion, and all began in 2009 or later.
- 排名中有五场巡回演唱会总收入超过10亿美元，且全部始于2009年或之后。
- Ed Sheeran, Coldplay, The Rolling Stones, and Metallica each have two tours among the top 20.
- 艾德·希兰、酷玩乐队、滚石乐队和金属乐队各有两场巡回演唱进入前20。

The Eras Tour: Redefining Concerts

“时代巡回演唱会”：重新定义演唱会

Taylor Swift’s 149-date Eras Tour spanned Europe, Asia, Australia, and the Americas before wrapping in Vancouver in December 2024. With gross revenues of $2.2 billion, it became the first concert tour in history to cross the $2 billion mark.

泰勒·斯威夫特为期149场的“时代巡回演唱会”横跨欧洲、亚洲、澳大利亚和美洲，于2024年12月在温哥华落幕。该巡回演唱会总收入达22亿美元，成为历史上首场突破20亿美元大关的巡回演唱会。

The impact of the Eras Tour, which caused a media sensation dubbed “Swiftmania,” was felt at the economic level. The tour reportedly boosted the U.S. economy by some $4 billion as millions of fans traveled and spent extensively on hotels, food, and merchandise.

引发媒体轰动、被称为“斯威夫特狂热”的“时代巡回演唱会”在经济层面产生了影响。据报道，该巡回演唱会使美国经济增长了约40亿美元，数百万歌迷出行并在酒店、餐饮和周边商品上大量消费。

And the economic impact was abroad as well. Economists predicted a roughly

0.2% boost

to the Singaporean gross domestic product, while Paris reportedly saw a larger economic impact from the tour than from the 2024 Summer Olympics.

其经济影响也波及海外。经济学家预测，新加坡国内生产总值将因此获得约0.2%的提升，而据报道，巴黎从该巡回演唱会获得的经济影响超过了2024年夏季奥运会。

The Billion-Dollar Club

十亿美元俱乐部

While Swift stands alone in surpassing $2 billion from a single tour, four other tours join the Eras Tour in the billion-dollar club.

虽然斯威夫特是唯一一位单场巡回演唱会收入超过20亿美元的艺人，但另有四场巡回演唱会与“时代巡回演唱会”一同跻身十亿美元俱乐部。

Running from 2022 to 2025, Coldplay’s

post-pandemic

Music of the Spheres Tour ($1.6 billion) became the most attended tour in history. The band also prioritized a more sustainable approach, kicking off the tour in Costa Rica due in part to the country’s environmental credentials.

从2022年持续到2025年的酷玩乐队“星际漫游巡回演唱会”（16亿美元）成为历史上观众人数最多的巡回演唱会。该乐队还优先采取更可持续的方式，选择在哥斯达黎加开启巡演，部分原因是该国在环保方面的良好声誉。

Other British artists with billion-dollar tours include Ed Sheeran, whose Divide Tour grossed $1.0 billion, and Elton John, whose Farewell Yellow Brick Road tour reached $1.0 billion.

其他拥有十亿美元巡回演唱会的英国艺人包括艾德·希兰，其“÷巡回演唱会”总收入达10亿美元，以及艾尔顿·约翰，其“告别黄砖路巡回演唱会”也达到10亿美元。

U2’s 360° Tour, which ran from 2009 to 2011, predates the other entries in the billion-dollar club. Adjusted for inflation, it grossed nearly $1.1 billion.

U2的“360度巡回演唱会”于2009年至2011年举行，早于十亿美元俱乐部中的其他成员。经通胀调整后，该巡回演唱会总收入接近11亿美元。

Pop Star or Rock Band?

流行明星还是摇滚乐队？

By and large, the list of highest-grossing concert tours is a mixture of legacy acts and contemporary pop stars. No tour from before 2000 makes the list, in large part as touring has grown to become a larger financial lifeline to artists given

declining music sale

s.

总体而言，票房最高的巡回演唱会榜单是传奇艺人与当代流行明星的混合。2000年之前的巡回演唱会无一上榜，这在很大程度上是因为随着音乐销售下滑，巡演已成为艺人更重要的经济命脉。

Most of the artists in the ranking come from the United Kingdom or United States. Exceptions include Ireland’s U2, Australia’s AC/DC with the Black Ice World Tour ($677 million), and Canadian pop star The Weeknd with the After Hours til Dawn Tour ($736 million).

榜单中的大多数艺人来自英国或美国。例外包括爱尔兰的U2、澳大利亚的AC/DC（“黑冰世界巡回演唱会”，6.77亿美元）以及加拿大流行明星威肯（“午夜时分至黎明巡回演唱会”，7.36亿美元）。

German metal band Rammstein’s 2019-2024 Stadium Tour ($598 million) is the only entry from a non-Anglophone act.

德国金属乐队德国战车2019至2024年的“体育场巡回演唱会”（5.98亿美元）是唯一来自非英语国家艺人的上榜巡回演唱会。
