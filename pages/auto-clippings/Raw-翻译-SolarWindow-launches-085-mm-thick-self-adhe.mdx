【文章标题】：SolarWindow推出0.85毫米厚自粘太阳能薄膜

【文章正文】：
SolarWindow launches 0.85 mm-thick, self-adhesive solar film
SolarWindow推出0.85毫米厚自粘太阳能薄膜

US-based SolarWindow Technologies has announced the commercial launch of ElectroFlex, an ultra-thin, flexible solar product designed to generate electricity on flat and curved surfaces.
美国SolarWindow科技公司宣布推出商用产品ElectroFlex，这种超薄柔性太阳能产品可在平面和曲面上发电。

The product targets applications where conventional PV modules face limitations due to weight, rigidity, or mounting requirements.
该产品瞄准传统光伏组件因重量、刚性或安装要求而受限的应用场景。

The company describes ElectroFlex as a "peel-and-stick" solution that can be applied directly to various surfaces without frames, rigid glass, or conventional support structures. Its size and color can be customized to meet customer specifications.
该公司将ElectroFlex描述为"即剥即贴"解决方案，可直接应用于各种表面，无需框架、刚性玻璃或传统支撑结构。其尺寸和颜色可根据客户要求定制。

The new product consists of a five-layer composite laminate measuring 0.85 mm in total thickness. The stack comprises a 0.10 mm front encapsulant, 0.16 mm high-efficiency solar cells, a 0.04 mm copper cell backing, a 0.30 mm composite laminate and a 0.25 mm rear substrate.
这款新产品采用五层复合层压结构，总厚度0.85毫米，包含0.10毫米前封装层、0.16毫米高效太阳能电池、0.04毫米铜基背板、0.30毫米复合层压板和0.25毫米后基板。

ElectroFlex is 0.85 mm thick and integrates interdigitated back-contact (IBC) cells with a power conversion efficiency of 24.4%, according to the manufacturer. It has a power-to-weight ratio of approximately 73 W/kg.
制造商表示，0.85毫米厚的ElectroFlex集成叉指背接触(IBC)电池，功率转换效率达24.4%，功率重量比约为73W/kg。

SolarWindow said it also supplies wiring, harnesses, electrical systems, and power-balancing components needed to integrate the product and deliver the electricity it generates.
SolarWindow表示还提供集成该产品及输送电力所需的线缆、线束、电气系统和功率平衡组件。

The company is targeting applications in the transportation, marine, aerospace, architecture, agriculture, infrastructure, utility, and specialty vehicle sectors.
该公司目标应用领域包括交通运输、船舶、航空航天、建筑、农业、基础设施、公用事业和特种车辆行业。

Potential applications include data centers and buildings, roofs of commercial trucks and fleet vehicles, curved train surfaces, and drone and aircraft structures, according to the manufacturer.
制造商指出，潜在应用场景包括数据中心与建筑、商用卡车与车队车辆顶棚、列车曲面车身以及无人机和飞行器结构。

ElectroFlex is initially available to Tier 1 original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) in the United States. SolarWindow said it plans to expand sales to selected markets in North America and Asia over the coming quarters.
ElectroFlex初期面向美国一级原始设备制造商供应。SolarWindow表示计划未来几个季度将销售扩展至北美和亚洲精选市场。

The company is also developing LiquidElectricity, a transparent coating designed to turn glass and plastic surfaces into electricity-generating elements. SolarWindow claims the technology, which remains under development, could generate electricity from natural and artificial light, as well as low-intensity, shaded, and reflected light.
该公司同时正在开发LiquidElectricity透明涂层技术，可将玻璃和塑料表面转化为发电元件。SolarWindow宣称这项尚在研发中的技术可利用自然光、人造光以及低强度光、阴影光和反射光发电。
